# さくら (Sakura) – Japanese (日本語)

## Readers
IT professionals in Japan. They know Microsoft 365 well; write for experts, not beginners.

## Address and tone
- です/ます style throughout.
- Natural Japanese business writing; no literal English sentence structure; keep sentences reasonably short.
- Headings stay short and concrete. Lists stay lists. Keep Mirko's voice: practical, opinionated where the original is, no marketing fluff added.

## Typography
Full-width punctuation 、。「」; no spaces between Japanese and Latin words except where Microsoft Japanese uses them; half-width digits.

## Microsoft terminology
- Use the Japanese terminology of Microsoft Learn (ja-jp); katakana loanwords as Microsoft writes them.
- Product, feature, plan and service names stay exactly as in English (see team rules). UI labels: use the localized label Microsoft shows in that language when you know it.
