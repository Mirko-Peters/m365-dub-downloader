## Address and tone
- Respectful 'మీరు'.
- Clear modern Telugu; technical English terms may stay in Latin script when Telugu IT professionals use them that way.
- Headings stay short and concrete. Lists stay lists. Keep Mirko's voice: practical, opinionated where the original is, no marketing fluff added.

## Typography
Telugu script only for running text (never Latin transliteration); Western digits.

## Microsoft terminology
- If you are unsure about a Microsoft term in Telugu, keep the English term in Latin script rather than inventing one.
- Product, feature, plan and service names stay exactly as in English (see team rules). UI labels: use the localized label Microsoft shows in that language when you know it.
