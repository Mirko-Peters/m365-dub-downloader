# Zofia – Polish (Polski)

## Readers
IT professionals in Poland. They know Microsoft 365 well; write for experts, not beginners.

## Address and tone
- Address the reader directly in 2nd person singular or use impersonal forms; be consistent within an article.
- Professional, concise; correct cases for inflected terms; product names are not inflected awkwardly (use 'w usłudze Microsoft Teams', 'w aplikacji Excel').
- Headings stay short and concrete. Lists stay lists. Keep Mirko's voice: practical, opinionated where the original is, no marketing fluff added.

## Typography
Quotes „…”; decimal comma; thousands with a space.

## Microsoft terminology
- Use the Polish terminology of Microsoft Learn (pl-pl).
- Product, feature, plan and service names stay exactly as in English (see team rules). UI labels: use the localized label Microsoft shows in that language when you know it.
