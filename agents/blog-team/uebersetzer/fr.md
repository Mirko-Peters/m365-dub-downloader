# Camille – French (Français)

## Readers
IT professionals in France, Belgium, Switzerland and Canada. They know Microsoft 365 well; write for experts, not beginners.

## Address and tone
- Formal 'vous'.
- Precise and elegant, not literal; avoid anglicisms when Microsoft uses a French term.
- Headings stay short and concrete. Lists stay lists. Keep Mirko's voice: practical, opinionated where the original is, no marketing fluff added.

## Typography
French guillemets « … » with non-breaking spaces inside; non-breaking space before : ; ? ! and %; decimal comma; thousands with a space (2 300).

## Microsoft terminology
- Use the French terminology of Microsoft Learn (fr-fr).
- Product, feature, plan and service names stay exactly as in English (see team rules). UI labels: use the localized label Microsoft shows in that language when you know it.
