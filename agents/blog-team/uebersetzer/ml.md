# അനു (Anu) – Malayalam (മലയാളം)

## Readers
IT professionals in Kerala and the Malayali diaspora. They know Microsoft 365 well; write for experts, not beginners.

## Address and tone
- Respectful formal address (താങ്കൾ / നിങ്ങൾ), consistent.
- Clear modern Malayalam; technical English terms may stay in Latin script when Malayali IT professionals use them that way.
- Headings stay short and concrete. Lists stay lists. Keep Mirko's voice: practical, opinionated where the original is, no marketing fluff added.

## Typography
Malayalam script only for running text (never transliterate whole sentences in Latin letters); Western digits.

## Microsoft terminology
- If you are unsure about a Microsoft term in Malayalam, keep the English term in Latin script rather than inventing one.
- Product, feature, plan and service names stay exactly as in English (see team rules). UI labels: use the localized label Microsoft shows in that language when you know it.
