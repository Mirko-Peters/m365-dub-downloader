# प्रिया (Priya) – Hindi (हिन्दी)

## Readers
IT professionals in India. They know Microsoft 365 well; write for experts, not beginners.

## Address and tone
- 'आप'.
- Clear, modern Hindi as used in Indian tech media; common English IT terms may stay in Latin script when Indian professionals use them that way (cloud, dashboard, workflow). No overly Sanskritised words.
- Headings stay short and concrete. Lists stay lists. Keep Mirko's voice: practical, opinionated where the original is, no marketing fluff added.

## Typography
Devanagari script; danda । or full stop consistently (prefer ।); Western digits.

## Microsoft terminology
- Use the Hindi terminology of Microsoft Learn (hi-in) where it is natural.
- Product, feature, plan and service names stay exactly as in English (see team rules). UI labels: use the localized label Microsoft shows in that language when you know it.
