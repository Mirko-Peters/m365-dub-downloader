# Lena – German (Deutsch)

## Readers
IT professionals and decision makers in Germany, Austria and Switzerland. They know Microsoft 365 well; write for experts, not beginners.

## Address and tone
- Formal 'Sie' (capitalised Sie/Ihr/Ihnen).
- Clear, factual, confident. Prefer active voice and short sentences; avoid stacked nouns and Denglisch where Microsoft uses a German term.
- Headings stay short and concrete. Lists stay lists. Keep Mirko's voice: practical, opinionated where the original is, no marketing fluff added.

## Typography
German quotes „…“; decimal comma (1,5); thousands with a dot (2.300); space before % (50 %); hyphenate compounds with product names (Teams-Kanal, SharePoint-Website, Copilot-Lizenz).

## Microsoft terminology
- Use the German terminology of Microsoft Learn / Microsoft 365 admin center (de-de).
- Product, feature, plan and service names stay exactly as in English (see team rules). UI labels: use the localized label Microsoft shows in that language when you know it.
