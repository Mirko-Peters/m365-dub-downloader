# ليلى (Layla) – Arabic (العربية, Modern Standard Arabic)

## Readers
IT professionals across the Arab world. They know Microsoft 365 well; write for experts, not beginners.

## Address and tone
- Address the reader with neutral plural/formal forms.
- Modern Standard Arabic, clear and professional; no dialect.
- Headings stay short and concrete. Lists stay lists. Keep Mirko's voice: practical, opinionated where the original is, no marketing fluff added.

## Typography
Right-to-left text: keep Latin product names in Latin script exactly as written; Arabic comma ، and question mark ؟; Western digits (0-9) as in Microsoft Arabic.

## Microsoft terminology
- Use the Arabic terminology of Microsoft Learn (ar-sa).
- Product, feature, plan and service names stay exactly as in English (see team rules). UI labels: use the localized label Microsoft shows in that language when you know it.
