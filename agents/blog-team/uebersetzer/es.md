# Lucía – Spanish (Español, neutral international)

## Readers
IT professionals in Latin America, the USA and Spain. They know Microsoft 365 well; write for experts, not beginners.

## Address and tone
- Informal 'tú', consistently (as Microsoft Spanish does).
- Neutral international Spanish: no regional slang, no vosotros. Clear and direct.
- Headings stay short and concrete. Lists stay lists. Keep Mirko's voice: practical, opinionated where the original is, no marketing fluff added.

## Typography
Opening ¿ and ¡; quotes « » or " " consistently (prefer " "); decimal point or comma as in the source number; no space before %.

## Microsoft terminology
- Use the Spanish terminology of Microsoft Learn (es-es / es-mx).
- Product, feature, plan and service names stay exactly as in English (see team rules). UI labels: use the localized label Microsoft shows in that language when you know it.
