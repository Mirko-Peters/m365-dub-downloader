A policy definition is the rule itself and it's written as a JSON document. JSON might sound technical but the structure is actually quite simple. It follows a basic pattern. If this condition is true, then apply this effect. Let me give you a real example. There's a built-in policy that says, "If a storage account doesn't have HTTPS enabled, deny it." That's it. The resource type, the location, a specific setting. Second, the effects. That's the then part. What happens when the condition is met? Do you block the deployment or just flag it? Third, parameters.
