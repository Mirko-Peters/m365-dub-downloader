Let's walk through the two main phases. ingestion and query. ingestion is the setup phase. You head straight to the right section instead of scanning every shelf. The position SW stands for hierarchical navigable small world. The idea is pretty elegant. It builds a multi-layer map of your vectors. The top layer holds a few representative vectors that give you a rough sense of where things are. Each layer below gets more detailed. When you search, you start at the top, find the general neighborhood, then drill down, layer by layer until you hit the exact match. It's like using a city map first. Then a neighborhood map, then a street map. Each one gets you closer. The result is that you can search millions of vectors in milliseconds. That's what powers real-time AI features.
