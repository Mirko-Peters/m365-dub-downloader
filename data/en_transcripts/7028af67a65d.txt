So grab your coffee and let's dive in. What is a model context protocol? Here's the simplest definition. MCP is an open standard that lets AI applications discover and call tools and data sources in a structured way. Think of it like a universal remote. Without MCP, every AI agent needs its own custom wiring to each system, one cable for your CRM, another for your ERP, another for your data warehouse. Every time you add a system, you run another cable. With MCP, you plug in once and get access to everything, one connection, one protocol, one way of working. Before MCP existed, connecting an AI assistant to your ERP was a full project. You had to build custom APIs, maintain connectors, deal with security headaches, and update everything whenever the ERP changed. It was expensive, slow, and fragile. MCP changes all of that. Instead of building a custom bridge every time, you get a standard interface that any MCP compatible tool can use, the model context part is important too. The AI agent doesn't just get raw data. It gets detailed info about what tools exist, what they do, how to use them, and what data is available. So when an agent needs to do something, it can look at the tools, understand which fits the task, and call it correctly. That's how it makes smart decisions because it understands the system.
