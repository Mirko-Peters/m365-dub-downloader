It actively scans your resources and compares them against Microsoft's best practices. The advisor score. The advisor score is your optimization dashboard, a single number from 0 to 100%, that shows how well your workloads follow Azure best practices across all five pillars. Instead of digging through dozens of recommendations and trying to figure out if you're making progress, you get one number broken into five category scores, one for each pillar. So you can see at a glance that your cost score is 92%, but your reliability score is only 65%, and that tells you exactly where to focus your attention. How is the score calculated? It uses the healthy resource ratio, the percentage of resources that follow the recommendations. If you have 100 VMs and 80 of them, follow the cost recommendations your cost score is 80%. Simple. The overall score is the average of all five category scores. So if you're scoring 80 in cost, 70 in security, 60 in reliability, 90 in performance, and 75 in operational excellence, your overall score is 75%, that's your baseline. For reliability, performance, and operational excellence, the scores are broken into subcategories with fixed weights. For example, zone resiliency carries a weight of 30 in the reliability category. Regional resiliency is 25, and data protection and recovery is 20. Each subcategory contributes proportionally to the category score, which means not all recommendations are equal. Some matter more than others, and the score reflects that. The score updates every 24 hours, so if you implement a recommendation today, it takes up to a day for your score to reflect that change. Don't expect instant gratification, but over time, you'll see your score trending upward as you work through the recommendations. It also tracks progress over time, so you can see daily, weekly, and monthly trends. You can look back and say, "Three months ago, my overall score was 62%, now it's 78%, here's the proof that we're improving." This is how you prove to your boss that you're making progress. Instead of saying, "I think we're doing better," you show them a chart. The advisor score gives you a concrete, measurable way to track optimization. If a recommendation doesn't apply to your situation, you can postpone or dismiss it, and those recommendations are excluded from the score calculation.
