The policy reality alignment assessment. The second audit is about the gap between what you're trying to do and what's actually happening on the ground. You likely have a policy stating all customer data must be labeled and you've probably done the training and set the expectations. Now it's time to measure the gap. This gap is your baseline and it represents where you actually are. Not where you think you are. Now you investigate the 30% that is labeled. If the problem is technical and certain systems don't support labeling, you either need to implement a new tool or find an alternative way to govern that specific data. The policy reality alignment assessment isn't about swinging a hammer. It's about understanding why the gap exists so you can design an intervention that actually fits the problem.
