Step two, fix access before scale. Once you make your reality visible the next logical step is cleaning up access. This is usually where organizations start to feel a bit uncomfortable because access mess is almost always tied to history. Co-pilot drastically reduces the friction required to find, summarize and connect every single thing a person can reach. You need to audit who can access what and then ask if that access matches their business responsibility today. Not the org chart from two years ago and not the project structure from a previous transformation. You have to look at the current reality of who owns what and who actually needs the data.
