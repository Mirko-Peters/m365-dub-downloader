Power BI gives you three kinds of gateway, and the one you pick changes how your whole team works. The first is the standard gateway, which is shared and managed centrally. So one installation can serve many people and many data sources at once. It supports the heavier report modes and it can run as a cluster for extra reliability. Microsoft recommends it for anything a team depends on. The second is the personal gateway, and the name tells you most of what you need to know. It works for exactly one user. It only talks to power BI and nobody else can share it. If you're the only person building reports and you just need your own refreshes to run, it can be enough and it's the quickest one to set up. The third is the virtual network gateway and this one is a different animal. Microsoft runs it for you as a managed service, so there's nothing to install and no machine to keep switched on.
