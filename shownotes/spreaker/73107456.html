Modern phishing emails copy real branding and writing styles, so a basic spam filter no longer keeps a business safe. This episode is for Microsoft 365 admins, security teams and business owners. You learn how the five protection layers of Microsoft Defender for Office 365 work together to stop malware, phishing, malicious links and spoofed senders.

In this episode

  • 00:00 – Why email is still the number one threat
  • 01:16 – What Defender for Office 365 is and anti-spam
  • 03:02 – Anti-malware and Safe Attachments
  • 04:42 – Anti-phishing and impersonation protection
  • 06:22 – Safe Links and time-of-click protection
  • 08:12 – Anti-spoofing with SPF, DKIM and DMARC
  • 09:47 – How the five layers work together
  • 11:05 – Common myths and misconceptions

Key takeaways

  • Microsoft Defender for Office 365 is not just antivirus for email but a cloud service with five layers: anti-spam, anti-malware with Safe Attachments, anti-phishing, Safe Links and anti-spoofing.
  • Safe Attachments opens files in a sandbox to judge behavior, and Safe Links rewrites URLs and checks them at the moment of the click, across email, Teams and Office documents.
  • Authentication checks such as SPF, DKIM and DMARC plus impersonation protection stop spoofed and look-alike senders, so check whether your Microsoft 365 plan already includes Defender.
