Your data estate is not broken, it is fragmented: stale dashboards, competing pipelines and isolated datamarts. This episode is for Power BI and data teams who want to see Microsoft Fabric as one ecosystem, from OneLake and domains to Direct Lake, security and Copilot. You get a 30-day governance baseline and a calm migration path from classic datasets.

In this episode

  • 00:00 – The fragmented data estate and the Fabric ecosystem
  • 00:46 – OneLake, domains and workspaces
  • 02:32 – Lakehouse, warehouse and the semantic model
  • 06:09 – Pipelines, Dataflows Gen2, shortcuts and mirroring
  • 11:03 – Power BI, semantic models and Direct Lake
  • 16:34 – Security, compliance and life cycle
  • 22:53 – Copilot as a helpful symbiont
  • 25:47 – Sales analytics path, 30-day baseline and migration

Key takeaways

  • Microsoft Fabric does not replace Power BI; OneLake, domains and workspaces form the governed ecosystem that keeps Power BI reports healthy.
  • A bronze, silver and gold layering with Dataflows Gen2, shortcuts and a certified semantic model on Direct Lake removes import refresh pain and conflicting definitions.
  • Row-level security, sensitivity labels and OneLake security belong in the model and the workspace, so Copilot and every report inherit the same boundaries.

Worth quoting
