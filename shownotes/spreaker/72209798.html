Copilot often feels like a slightly smarter search bar because it runs on a decade-old knowledge architecture. This episode is for IT pros and decision makers who want Copilot to reason over trusted, governed organizational knowledge. Learn how to design metadata, ontology, permissions and ingestion for a scalable Microsoft 365 knowledge graph.

In this episode

  • 00:00 – The 2026 information problem

  • 07:05 – Why Copilot deployments under-deliver
  • 10:18 – Knowledge graphs and metadata as foundation

  • 25:07 – Hallucination, RAG and ingestion pipelines
  • 32:51 – Ontology, entity resolution and security
  • 47:40 – Predictive intelligence, roadmap and ROI

Key takeaways

  • Weak Copilot answers usually stem from the knowledge architecture underneath: unstructured content, missing metadata and overly broad permissions, not the model itself.
  • A governed knowledge graph on top of Microsoft Graph, with a metadata schema, a minimal viable ontology, entity resolution and metadata-first RAG, turns retrieval into trustworthy answers.
  • Governance must run as a continuous operational discipline, starting with a SharePoint Data Access Governance audit and one narrow, measurable use case before scaling.

Worth quoting
