Everyone talks about Microsoft Fabric, but few can explain what it actually is. This episode walks business and IT folks through OneLake, lakehouses, warehouses, data integration, real-time intelligence and Power BI in plain English. You will understand how the pieces fit together and why one copy of data changes everything.

Key takeaways

  • Microsoft Fabric is a unified SaaS data and analytics platform that brings data engineering, integration, warehousing, real-time analytics and BI together under one login.
  • OneLake is the single data lake per tenant in open Delta Parquet format, and shortcuts let you reference external data without copying it.
  • Power BI in Fabric reads OneLake data directly via Direct Lake, while Purview sensitivity labels, DLP and domains provide governance from the start.
