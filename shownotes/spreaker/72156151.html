Enterprise search and AI answers are often hours behind reality because crawlers scan data that never stands still. This episode shows architects how Microsoft Graph delta queries, security trimming, GraphRAG and lineage-aware pipelines deliver fresh, compliant, sub-second discovery.

In this episode

  • 00:00 – The latency chasm of crawler-based search
  • 04:11 – From crawling to event-driven discovery
  • 07:37 – Delta queries and the security trimming trap
  • 11:17 – Sub-second discovery, Graph architecture and scopes
  • 21:00 – Delta tokens, throttling and back-off
  • 26:00 – Scenarios: meeting intelligence and cross-silo context
  • 32:01 – GraphRAG and lazy graph construction
  • 40:07 – Data residency, lineage, infrastructure and roadmap

Key takeaways

  • Periodic crawling leaves enterprise search and Copilot-style AI working on stale data, while Microsoft Graph delta queries and change events enable near real-time discovery.
  • Vector stores do not inherit permissions, so security trimming, least-privilege Graph scopes and throttle-aware design are prerequisites for safe semantic search.
  • Data lineage, EU Data Boundary constraints and cost-aware approaches like lazy GraphRAG should shape the architecture from day one through a phased roadmap.
