Sales, service and marketing often each hold a different page of the same customer's story. This episode explains Dynamics 365 Customer Insights, its Data and Journeys parts and the confusing name change, for anyone working with Dynamics 365. You will learn how unified profiles, segments and triggered journeys help teams respond with the right message at the right time.

Key takeaways

  • Dynamics 365 Customer Insights has two parts: Data builds unified customer profiles and segments, and Journeys turns that picture into planned communication.
  • Journeys can be segment-led or trigger-led, with branches, waits and handoffs to sellers, while consent and frequency caps protect the customer relationship.
  • Clear goals, journey analytics and A/B tests show what works, but clean data and respected preferences matter more than complex branches.

Worth quoting
