Many Excel users want to build apps but think they first need to learn a programming language. This Simply Explained episode shows beginners and citizen developers how Power Fx brings the familiar formula mindset into Power Apps, from properties and button actions to data, variables and reusable rules.

In this episode

  • 00:00 – What Power Fx is
  • 01:50 – From spreadsheet cells to app properties
  • 04:52 – Properties turn formulas into app behavior
  • 07:37 – Working with business data
  • 11:03 – Variables, collections and reusable rules
  • 14:46 – One formula system in an expense app
  • 16:57 – Getting started and limits of low-code

Key takeaways

  • Power Fx is the Excel-like formula language of Power Platform, where formulas live in control properties instead of cells and update automatically.
  • Property formulas describe what a control should show, while OnSelect formulas run actions such as Notify or Navigate, and Filter, Sort and LookUp work with business data.
  • Variables and collections hold short-term context, reusable functions keep rules consistent, and lasting records belong in a proper data source such as SharePoint or Dataverse.
