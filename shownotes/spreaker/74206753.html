Customers repeat their story on every channel because chat, email, phone and social messages live in separate tools. This episode explains for customer service teams and supervisors how Dynamics 365 Omnichannel connects those conversations in one workspace. You will learn how routing, agent tools, customer history and real-time dashboards fit together.

In this episode

  • 00:00 – The broken support journey
  • 00:43 – Why separate channels create bad support
  • 03:27 – The unified agent desktop
  • 07:39 – Routing behind the scenes
  • 11:50 – Tools that help agents respond
  • 15:38 – One customer record
  • 17:38 – What supervisors can see
  • 19:21 – Conclusion: the connected service desk

Key takeaways

  • Dynamics 365 Omnichannel brings chat, voice, SMS and social conversations into one agent workspace with the customer's history beside them.
  • Routing uses queues, rules, skills, presence and capacity to send each request to the right available person, and consults avoid needless transfers.
  • Context only follows the customer when channels are set up correctly and conversations are linked to the right contact or case.
