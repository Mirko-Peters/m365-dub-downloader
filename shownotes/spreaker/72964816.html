Still clicking through the Azure portal to build the same environment again and again? This episode explains ARM templates and infrastructure as code in plain English for Azure admins and developers, and shows how to deploy repeatable environments, make templates reusable and when to move to Bicep.

In this episode

  • 00:00 – Intro: stop clicking through the portal
  • 00:42 – What Azure Resource Manager is
  • 02:20 – Infrastructure as code: the big idea
  • 04:30 – Anatomy of an ARM template
  • 06:50 – Making templates reusable
  • 08:51 – How deployment works
  • 10:55 – The Bicep evolution
  • 13:03 – Common mistakes and how to avoid them

Key takeaways

  • Azure Resource Manager is the control layer behind every Azure request, and ARM templates are its declarative, idempotent JSON blueprints.
  • Parameters, variables, conditions, copy loops and linked templates let one template serve development, test and production environments.
  • Bicep is recommended for new deployments and compiles to ARM JSON, so understanding ARM still helps when troubleshooting deployments.

Worth quoting

“The second approach is declarative, and that's what Arm templates use. Instead of telling Azure how to build something, you just describe what you want the end result to look like.” – Mirko Peters (03:20)

“Arm templates are idempotent. That's a fancy word meaning you can deploy the same template 100 times and get the same result.” – Mirko Peters (04:12)
