Power Pages is usually dismissed as a portal builder, not a foundation for enterprise multi-tenant SaaS. This episode shows architects and Power Platform developers how Dataverse elastic tables, hierarchical partition keys and layered security make scalable, audit-ready SaaS on Power Pages possible, and where the approach stops being the right choice.

In this episode

  • 00:00 – Why standard Dataverse tables break at scale
  • 03:56 – What elastic tables are and the performance gap
  • 10:28 – Partition key design and hierarchical partition keys
  • 18:58 – Storage economics and multi-tenant security foundations
  • 34:01 – Power Pages as a SaaS front end: retrieval and write paths
  • 45:48 – JSON columns and Execute Cosmos SQL Query
  • 53:39 – Reference architecture, migration, throughput and governance
  • 78:31 – Team readiness and the future of low-code SaaS

Key takeaways

  • Use standard Dataverse tables for core relational business entities and elastic tables, backed by Azure Cosmos DB, for high-volume operational data such as logs, telemetry and audit events.
  • Choose the partition key up front, ideally a hierarchical key with tenant ID at level one, and include it in every query to keep performance and tenant isolation intact.
  • Layer tenant isolation across Cosmos DB partitions, Dataverse business units and Power Pages table permissions, and push bulk ingestion to back-end services using CreateMultiple.

Worth quoting
