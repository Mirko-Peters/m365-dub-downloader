Many Microsoft partners still think in Silver and Gold tiers and leave real money on the table. This episode explains to partner owners and alliance managers how membership, Solutions Partner designations and specializations work today, what the Partner Capability Score measures and how incentives and MDF really pay out.

In this episode

  • 00:00 – Why the old Silver and Gold model is gone
  • 02:16 – The membership layer
  • 03:33 – What a Solutions Partner designation requires
  • 05:20 – The six solution areas and the coming consolidation
  • 06:32 – Benefits and incentives of a Solutions Partner
  • 08:53 – Specializations, where the economics change
  • 10:36 – Co-op, MDF and money left on the table
  • 11:58 – Building your partner journey roadmap

Key takeaways

  • The Microsoft partner journey has three layers, membership, Solutions Partner designation and specialization, and the program now measures customer outcomes instead of certifications.
  • A Solutions Partner designation requires 70 of 100 Partner Capability Score points with no zero in any metric, and customer success through usage growth and deployments carries the most weight.
  • Partners should check their capability score and MDF balance in Partner Center, start with one solution area such as Modern Work and then target a first specialization.
