Business Central covers most core processes, but every company has gaps such as special shipping, local reports or bank integrations. This Simply Explained episode is for Business Central users, finance teams and admins who decide which add-ons to install. It explains the types of extensions and what to check on security, data and support before an app becomes part of daily work.

In this episode

  • 00:37 – What an extension actually is
  • 03:58 – The main types of extensions
  • 07:29 – How extensions change your daily work
  • 11:40 – Finding and installing apps
  • 15:05 – Security, data and update questions
  • 18:15 – Conclusion: choose the right add-on

Key takeaways

  • Business Central extensions add fields, pages, reports, permissions and integrations in their own package, so the standard application stays intact and updates cause less repair work.
  • Use Microsoft or ISV apps from AppSource for common needs and per-tenant extensions only for processes unique to your company, with a clear owner for support and testing.
  • Before installing, check publisher, supported versions, pricing, documentation, data flows and permissions, test real cases in a sandbox, and keep a record of every installed extension.

Worth quoting

“standard Business Central already handles a huge amount of normal business work, so extensions should fill a real gap, not become an excuse to rebuild every screen just because the old system looked different.” – Mirko Peters (03:33)
