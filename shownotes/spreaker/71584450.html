Your org chart says the CEO is in charge, but your Microsoft 365 tenant says otherwise: dozens of Global Admins can override any policy with a single click. This episode is for IT leaders, security teams and executives who want to understand who really holds power in their tenant, and how to take it back with a 30-day plan before Copilot exposes the gaps.

Key takeaways

  • In Microsoft 365, real authority sits with whoever holds the Global Admin role, so over-assigned admin rights quietly override executive and security decisions.
  • Copilot does not create new risk; it inherits user permissions and surfaces years of oversharing and broken inheritance at scale.
  • A 30-day plan of inventorying privileged roles, cutting standing Global Admins by at least 80 percent and moving to just-in-time, scoped access realigns permissions with the org chart.
