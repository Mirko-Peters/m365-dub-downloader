A correct bearing warning is useless if it arrives after the safe maintenance window has passed. This episode walks manufacturing, OT and data teams through everything predictive maintenance on Azure needs before the model, from sensors and edge gateways to IoT Hub, Event Hubs and approved work orders.

In this episode

  • 00:00 – Start with the maintenance decision
  • 04:00 – A pump scenario and the fault hypothesis
  • 11:40 – Baselines and simple rules first
  • 19:16 – Sensors, sampling and capture
  • 27:23 – OT access, edge gateway and timing
  • 47:30 – Ingestion, processing and storage on Azure
  • 80:29 – Model selection, inference and monitoring
  • 101:16 – Costs, rollout and readiness gates

Key takeaways

  • Define the maintenance decision, its owner and the warning horizon first, then test whether the target fault produces observable evidence early enough to act.
  • Reliable asset identity, suitable sensors and sampling, safe OT access, store-and-forward gateways, event-time stamps and data contracts matter before any model on Azure.
  • Compare any model against simple rules on maintenance events, route warnings into reviewed work orders, and expand only through explicit readiness gates and shadow operation.
