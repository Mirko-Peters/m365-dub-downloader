Finance teams lose time when budgets, invoices and bank data live in different spreadsheets and systems. This Knowledge Nugget explains Dynamics 365 Finance in plain English for business users, managers and newcomers to ERP, from the first purchase request to the general ledger, budgeting, reporting and controls.

  • 10:52 – Planning ahead: budgets, forecasts and cash
  • 13:43 – Reports and decisions
  • 16:43 – Controls, security and the connected Microsoft platform

Key takeaways

  • Dynamics 365 Finance keeps the official financial record in one place, linking purchase requests, purchase orders, receipts, supplier invoices and payments so nobody retypes data between tools.
  • The general ledger, chart of accounts and financial dimensions give every transaction a category and extra context such as department, location or project, so totals can be traced back to their source.
  • Budgets, forecasts and cash views answer different questions, and controls such as separated approvals, closed periods and a full audit trail protect the records behind every decision.
