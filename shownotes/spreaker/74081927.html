Field service jobs often fall apart because of phone calls, paper notes and a technician who arrives without the right part. This episode explains Dynamics 365 Field Service in plain English for service managers, dispatchers and anyone planning on-site work. Follow one job from request to invoice and see how work orders, scheduling, the mobile app and assets fit together.

In this episode

  • 00:45 – Why businesses need Field Service
  • 02:47 – The work order
  • 06:19 – Scheduling and dispatch
  • 09:54 – The Field Service mobile app
  • 13:12 – Parts, assets, maintenance and billing
  • 16:28 – Copilot and the wider Microsoft connection
  • 17:52 – Conclusion

Key takeaways

  • Field Service, part of Dynamics 365, manages on-site work from the first request through scheduling, the technician visit and parts to the final invoice.
  • The work order is the single source of facts for a visit, and the schedule board matches it to a resource with the right skills, availability and location.
  • The mobile app, customer assets and preventive or predictive maintenance improve first-visit fixes, while Copilot helps summarize but people stay responsible.

Worth quoting

“A first visit fix usually needs three things, the right person, clear instructions, and the correct part, and missing one can turn a simple repair into a second visit.” – Mirko Peters (01:25)

“Customer service can receive the call, but Field Service manages the trip, the work, the parts, and the result.” – Mirko Peters (02:39)
