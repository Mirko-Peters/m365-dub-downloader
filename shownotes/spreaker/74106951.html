Scattered email lists, sales notes and form entries lead to late, repeated or irrelevant customer messages. This episode explains Dynamics 365 Customer Insights - Journeys in plain English for marketing, sales and business teams. You will learn how journeys, segments, channels and triggers work together to send the next sensible message.

In this episode

  • 00:00 – More than a marketing email tool
  • 00:41 – Why businesses need it
  • 03:35 – The journey builder
  • 07:01 – Customer data and segments
  • 10:39 – Channels and content
  • 13:30 – Planned journeys and real-time triggers
  • 16:22 – Copilot, sales connections and analytics
  • 18:50 – Conclusion

Key takeaways

  • Customer Insights - Journeys plans customer communication as visual journeys that decide who enters, what each person receives and when, based on their actions.
  • Segments and connected customer profiles only work with clean data and respected consent, channel preferences and contact limits.
  • Segment-based journeys suit shared schedules, trigger-based journeys respond to customer actions, and Copilot drafts still need human review.

Worth quoting

“A segment is a set of people who meet rules you define. It isn't a spreadsheet someone exported six months ago and forgot to update.” – Mirko Peters (08:30)

“Before you build anything, answer three simple questions. Who should enter? What should they receive? When should each message arrive?” – Mirko Peters (04:19)
