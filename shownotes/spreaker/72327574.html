Organizations are deploying copilots and bots that each wire directly into their own APIs, credentials and logic, and the result breaks as soon as processes span systems and tenants. This episode is for architects and Microsoft 365 and Azure teams who want to turn siloed agents into an orchestrated system with Azure Logic Apps, custom connectors and secretless identity.

In this episode

  • 00:00 – The chatbot mirage and point-to-point integrations
  • 05:07 – Cross-tenant reality and the stateful gap
  • 08:41 – Logic Apps as the nervous system: Standard vs. Consumption
  • 14:41 – Connectors and orchestrator-first architecture
  • 18:33 – Identity: managed identities and workload identity federation
  • 25:30 – Error handling and human-on-the-loop governance
  • 30:10 – A cross-tenant scenario, agent loop and custom connectors
  • 52:10 – Anti-patterns, roadmap, objections and economics

Key takeaways

  • Agents should reason but not execute alone: route them through an orchestrator that owns state, business rules, error handling and the audit trail instead of wiring each bot to every API.
  • Azure Logic Apps Standard is the orchestration layer of choice for enterprise agents, combining stateful and stateless workflows, custom connectors as contracts and the agent loop action.
  • Use managed identities inside a tenant and workload identity federation across tenants so that no client secrets live in the agent layer, then roll out in phases starting with one painful process.
