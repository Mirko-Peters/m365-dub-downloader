Many companies look digital on the surface while their real processes run through spreadsheets, email chains and the memory of a few key people. This episode is for business leaders, IT managers and Power Platform teams who want to recognize the hidden costs of Excel shadow systems and learn how a governed architecture with zoned governance and a Center of Excellence restores speed, trust and control.

In this episode

  • 00:00 – Contoso looks normal from the outside
  • 04:57 – Signals: approval cycle time drift, rework and data inconsistency
  • 16:07 – Key person dependency and why workarounds keep winning
  • 27:20 – The Excel shadow system as architecture and what Power Platform changes
  • 34:35 – The procurement approval process before and after redesign
  • 42:55 – Low-code, governance, zoned governance and the Center of Excellence
  • 60:20 – Business case, economics, security and identity
  • 73:31 – AI amplifies architecture, where to start and the next operating model

Key takeaways

  • Excel files, inboxes and key people often form an unofficial process runtime that hides delays, rework, inconsistent data and concentration risk.
  • Moving a process onto a governed Power Platform architecture with structured intake, rule-based routing, shared reporting and identity-based access turns hidden coordination into observable flow.
  • Low-code and AI only scale safely with an operating model of zoned governance, a Center of Excellence and clear ownership, starting with one high-friction process measured on cycle time, rework and audit pain.

Worth quoting

“Excel isn't the problem on its own. The problem is using Excel as a process runtime. A spreadsheet used for a quick analysis or a temporary calculation is perfectly normal.” – Mirko Peters (27:50)
