Many organizations wrap broken, manual workflows in Power Automate flows and call it transformation, only to scale technical debt. This episode gives Power Platform leaders and makers a framework to measure automation debt with the technical debt ratio, gate what gets built and refactor critical flows in a two-week sprint.

In this episode

  • 00:00 – Industrializing chaos and the 24-month cliff
  • 00:36 – The mirage of speed and creation bias
  • 03:37 – Diagnosing the structural rot: orphaned flows and identity blind spots
  • 06:44 – The technical debt ratio (TDR) framework
  • 09:32 – The ESOAR gating strategy and modular child flows
  • 12:26 – Preempting the Copilot fallacy
  • 15:22 – The two-week automation redesign sprint

Key takeaways

  • Automating a broken process with Power Automate does not fix it; it scales the mess and compounds technical debt.
  • The technical debt ratio, the share of orphaned flows and remediation time versus build time are better health signals than run counts.
  • Eliminate and standardize before you automate, build modular child flows with owners, service accounts and deprecation dates, and do not expect AI to clean up later.

Worth quoting

“When you scale a mess, you don't get efficiency. You get a bigger faster mess. You get a system where errors propagate at the speed of the cloud.” – Mirko Peters (02:24)

“TDR equals the cost to fix your automation debt divided by the total automation investment multiplied by 100.” – Mirko Peters (07:02)
