Hybrid work often means driving to the office only to find your team stayed home. This episode explains Microsoft Places for employees, IT admins and facilities teams: how work plans, desk and room booking, check-in and analytics inside Outlook and Teams take the guesswork out of office days.

In this episode

  • 00:00 – Why hybrid office days go wrong
  • 00:36 – The hybrid work coordination problem
  • 01:35 – What Microsoft Places actually is
  • 02:37 – Setting your work plan
  • 03:38 – Desk booking
  • 05:07 – Room booking with photos and Copilot
  • 06:32 – Check-in and Nearby
  • 07:51 – Analytics, admin and the AI-powered workplace

Key takeaways

  • Microsoft Places lives inside Outlook and Teams and connects your calendar with the physical office, so people know who is coming in and where to sit.
  • Work plans, map-based desk booking, room booking with photos, automatic check-in and Nearby turn office days from guesswork into coordination, with presence sharing always opt-in.
  • Utilization analytics, auto-release policies and a web admin portal help facilities teams, and Places data feeds Copilot and Microsoft Graph for smarter in-office decisions.

Worth quoting

“First, it's not a separate app you install. You don't go to a website, create an account, or learn a new interface. Places lives inside Outlook in Teams, showing up in your calendar and your chat.” – Mirko Peters (01:40)

“The problem isn't the office itself. It's not that offices are bad. It's that you have no information about who's coming in and when.” – Mirko Peters (01:09)
