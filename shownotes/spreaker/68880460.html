Contracts get attached, forwarded and edited until nobody can prove which version was approved, and Copilot happily surfaces whatever was overshared. This episode is for Dynamics 365 and Microsoft 365 architects, admins and compliance owners who want a blueprint that ties Dynamics records to SharePoint libraries, Purview labels, retention and DLP, and Copilot answers with citations.

Key takeaways

  • Document management works when Dynamics 365 holds the events, SharePoint holds the evidence in working, published and records libraries, Purview enforces the law, and Copilot acts as a witness that cites its sources.
  • Content types, required metadata, a small sensitivity label set, auto-labeling, event-based retention and DLP that names its policies turn attachments and copies into governed links with one source of truth.
  • A short monthly cadence of see, decide, adjust and teach, using activity explorer and DSPM for AI, plus time-boxed exceptions with justification keeps the system from drifting back into chaos.
