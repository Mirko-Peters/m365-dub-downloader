Many security teams still fix vulnerabilities in isolation while attackers chain them into paths to domain or global admin. Mirko plays the attacker against Microsoft Security MVP Uros Babic, who shows security teams and decision makers how exposure management, attack paths and Security Copilot change the defense. You learn how to prioritize real risk and measure exposure instead of activity.

Key takeaways

  • Attackers chain misconfigurations, vulnerabilities and excessive permissions into attack paths, so teams should fix what is actually exploitable first rather than everything at once.
  • Zero trust reduces trust, while Microsoft Security Exposure Management shows where an organization is still exposed, especially around tier-zero assets and AI agents.
  • Modern SOC metrics should measure exposure reduction, such as time to remediate critical exposure, instead of ticket and alert volume.
