Leaders who approve every Copilot use case, Teams decision and Power Platform request often become the biggest bottleneck in their own organization. This episode is for executives, IT leaders and Microsoft 365 governance owners who want speed without losing control. Learn how decision boundaries, clear ownership and aligned access let governed decisions happen without leader dependency.

In this episode

  • 00:00 – The hidden cost of being needed
  • 04:56 – Why control fails as a scaling model and the Microsoft 365 governance failure pattern
  • 12:10 – Scenario one: a Copilot rollout under a leader bottleneck
  • 20:20 – Scenario two: decision bottlenecks in Teams
  • 27:05 – Scenario three: Power Platform sprawl versus stagnation and the false choice
  • 42:00 – Redesign: decision boundaries, ownership clarity and access alignment
  • 53:05 – Measuring time to decision, suggestion acceptance and rework
  • 67:40 – The seven-day change and what leaders need to become

Key takeaways

  • When every exception, Copilot use case and Power Platform request flows to a leader, the leader becomes a single point of failure and decision throughput collapses.
  • Scalable governance centralizes standards and observability, decentralizes execution inside explicit decision boundaries, aligns ownership and access, and escalates only true exceptions with evidence.
  • Measure time to decision, suggestion acceptance rate and rework together per decision stream, and start by redesigning one recurring decision loop in seven days.

Worth quoting

“We need to separate two things that are constantly confused, coordination and control. Coordination scales but control does not.” – Mirko Peters (07:29)
