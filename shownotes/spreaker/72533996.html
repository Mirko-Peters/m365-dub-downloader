AI is reshaping hiring, and many tech professionals feel the job market is against them. In this conversation, recruiter and career coach Sarah Jones explains what recruiters and ATS systems look for, how to build a personal brand on LinkedIn without burning out, and what it takes to move into freelancing in the Microsoft ecosystem.

In this episode

  • 00:00 – Sarah's path into tech recruitment and the community
  • 04:47 – Misconceptions about recruiters
  • 08:52 – AI disruption and the skills that matter
  • 16:31 – ATS systems and getting found in a job search
  • 20:23 – Copilot, agents and looking beyond the Microsoft bubble
  • 22:57 – Personal branding, LinkedIn and visibility
  • 37:24 – The freelance course and building a freelance business
  • 51:54 – Women in tech, rapid fire and closing advice

Key takeaways

  • AI skills are becoming expected, but communication, trust and the ability to articulate your value matter even more because clients still want a human in the loop.
  • Make yourself findable: keep your LinkedIn profile and CV keyword-rich and clear, follow up on applications, and engage with the community to build referrals.
  • Freelancing needs a personal brand, a clear pitch, a continuous pipeline and a financial buffer, ideally built through side gigs or contracting first.
