Orders arrive from websites, stores, marketplaces and call centers, while stock sits in many places, and manual handoffs lead to overselling and vague delivery promises. This episode explains for business and IT teams how Dynamics 365 Intelligent Order Management coordinates that journey with orchestration flows, inventory visibility, fulfillment optimization and providers. You will see how one order travels from checkout to billing.

Key takeaways

  • Dynamics 365 Intelligent Order Management is a Dataverse-based coordinator that directs orders across existing sales, warehouse, shipping and billing systems instead of replacing them.
  • Orchestration flows with validation, conditions, splitters and custom actions define the route, while inventory visibility and fulfillment optimization decide where an order ships from.
  • First-party and third-party providers built on Power Automate connectors move order and fulfillment data both ways, and Power BI dashboards show where orders get stuck.

Worth quoting

“Intelligent Order Management doesn't take over every job. It doesn't suddenly become the warehouse workers picking screen, the carriers delivery network, or the finance team's billing system.” – Mirko Peters (04:58)

“If it goes straight to a warehouse and then to a carrier, someone might only spot the problem after a package can't be delivered. A validation step catches the issue earlier, while there's still time to contact the customer or hold the order.” – Mirko Peters (06:09)
