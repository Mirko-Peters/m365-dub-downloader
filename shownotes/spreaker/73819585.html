Many organizations either leave Power Platform wide open or bury makers in approvals. In this interview, Microsoft MVP Michael Roth explains to admins and platform owners how inventory, environments, DLP policies and clearly explained boundaries make governance an enabler for citizen developers rather than a brake on innovation.

In this episode

  • 00:00 – Introduction to Power Platform governance
  • 02:25 – Michael's path into Power Platform
  • 08:30 – Platform, not just a set of tools
  • 15:23 – When harmless apps become critical
  • 19:31 – Governance as traffic rules
  • 30:04 – Clarity, confidence and boundaries
  • 40:01 – Inventory as the foundation
  • 47:55 – Environments, ALM and DLP

Key takeaways

  • Power Platform is an enterprise platform that happens to be accessible, so tenants need deliberate setup instead of open default settings.
  • Governance built on clarity, confidence and explained boundaries speeds up makers and reduces shadow IT, while overly bureaucratic processes drive people away.
  • Start with an inventory, structure environments, and use DLP policies, tenant settings, roles and ALM where solutions become business critical.

Worth quoting

“So it all depends on the context, what is critical or what is not, what is secure, what is insecure, it's never the app, almost never the app, it's almost always the behavior of the people.” – Michael Roth (18:37)

“If I know what will happen, if everything is clear to me, then I can choose my actions and do so. And clarity in Power Platform is exactly that.” – Michael Roth (30:50)
