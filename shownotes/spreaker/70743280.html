Most Microsoft 365 deployments chase adoption first and plan to add governance later, until a Copilot pilot exposes years of oversharing and sprawl. This episode helps IT and security leaders understand why governance timing decides success and how a 90-day identity, classification and enforcement blueprint avoids costly remediation.

  • 70:34 – The 90-day governance blueprint and the entropy principle

Key takeaways

  • Copilot does not create governance problems; it exposes oversharing, permission sprawl and unlabeled data that accumulated because governance was omitted at the start.
  • Governance must follow an immutable sequence of identity, classification, enforcement and audit, because DLP has nothing to match against when 90% of files are unlabeled.
  • A 90-day governance-first blueprint costs far less than a reactive remediation project that can take nine months or more after a Copilot rollout stalls.
