Customer service agents lose time jumping between inboxes, tabs and notes while customers repeat their story. This Simply Explained episode is for Dynamics 365 users, service leads and admins who want to understand how Copilot Service workspace organizes sessions, channels, knowledge, Copilot help and unified routing in one service desk.

In this episode

  • 00:00 – Why the workspace exists
  • 03:06 – Sessions and tabs: the desk drawer analogy
  • 07:04 – One workspace for every channel
  • 10:17 – The productivity pane, Smart Assist and knowledge
  • 11:48 – Scripts, macros, quick replies and Copilot
  • 13:37 – Routing, presence and capacity
  • 16:44 – SLAs, self-service and dashboards
  • 18:50 – Conclusion: one organized service desk

Key takeaways

  • Copilot Service workspace, formerly Customer Service workspace, keeps each customer issue in its own session with up to ten tabs, so agents stop rebuilding context.
  • Omnichannel channels, cases, timelines, the productivity pane, scripts, macros and Copilot help agents find answers fast, but people still review everything before it is sent.
  • Behind the scenes unified routing uses queues, skills, presence and capacity, while SLA timers, escalation rules and dashboards help teams keep promises and spot patterns.

Worth quoting
