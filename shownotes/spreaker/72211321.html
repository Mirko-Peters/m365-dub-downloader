Your CFO wants to know what the organization is actually paying for with Copilot, and the invoice no longer matches the promise. This episode gives IT and finance leaders a framework to expose hidden AI cost layers, from consumption billing and idle tokens to zombie seats, and to redirect that spend toward measurable ROI.

  • 43:17 – Token governance, ROI audit and self-funding AI

Key takeaways

  • Copilot's real cost includes rising base licenses, a parallel consumption billing layer, idle token waste, lazy prompting and human verification time.
  • Zombie seats from blanket rollouts waste budget, while concentrated deployments with deep daily adoption deliver several times the ROI on the same license.
  • Tiered access, token governance with cost-per-outcome metrics, and a hybrid stack that leaves deterministic work to RPA or scripts turn AI spend into a self-funding strategy.
