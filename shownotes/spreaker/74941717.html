A planned completion date looks reasonable until one operation runs late, a material release slips or a resource disappears, and every downstream promise moves with it. This episode shows manufacturing planners and IT/OT architects how to apply critical path thinking to live production, from shared machines and quality holds to MES, ERP, Microsoft Fabric and knowledge graphs, so they can see which dependency controls delivery now.

Key takeaways

  • In production the critical path is the chain of remaining dependencies that controls the earliest completion date right now, and it moves when machines, material, people or quality conditions change.
  • A routing, a status dashboard or a Power BI report shows intent and symptoms, but a live dependency model with explicit links between orders, operations, resources and release conditions is needed to explain why a date moved.
  • Prediction, scheduling and optimization answer different questions, and generative AI should explain schedule results rather than invent the schedule itself.
