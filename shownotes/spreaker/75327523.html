Many Microsoft messaging environments look secure on the surface but still run on defaults, weak identities and misunderstood mail flow. This conversation with Exchange MVP Thomas Stensitzki is for Exchange and Microsoft 365 administrators running on-premises, hybrid or cloud-only email. It covers identity protection, break glass accounts, SPF, DKIM and DMARC, Defender for Office 365 and the operational discipline that keeps messaging secure.

In this episode

  • 00:00 – Introduction to messaging security and the guest
  • 04:41 – Exchange Server, Exchange Online and hybrid differences
  • 10:11 – Identity, admin roles and break glass accounts
  • 15:32 – Legacy authentication and a realistic attack path
  • 23:46 – Mail flow, SPF, DKIM and DMARC
  • 37:37 – Phishing and Defender for Office 365
  • 48:54 – Secure Score, operational discipline and compliance
  • 57:48 – AI, Exchange migrations and rapid fire round

Key takeaways

  • Exchange Online starts with default settings, so mail flow, anti-spam, anti-phishing and anti-malware still need to be configured for the organization's needs.
  • Identity security comes first: protect every user and service account, use PIM for Exchange admins and secure break glass accounts with FIDO keys.
  • Configure SPF, DKIM and DMARC with real policies, use subdomains for third-party senders, and enable Safe Links in Defender for Office 365.
