Many organizations believe more policies, premium licenses and training make their Microsoft 365 governance mature, yet audits still take weeks and Copilot stalls. Drawing on 500 tenants, this episode gives IT, security and compliance leaders a five-level maturity model and a five-question check to find the upgrade that matters most.

In this episode

  • 00:00 – What maturity really measures
  • 04:20 – False signals leaders read as maturity
  • 08:00 – Level 100: reactive governance
  • 11:18 – Levels 200 and 300: managed but fragile, defined but uneven
  • 19:11 – Levels 400 and 500: predictable and optimized governance
  • 28:15 – The five-question maturity check
  • 32:41 – Patterns: audit time, data exposure and Copilot readiness
  • 49:38 – Case study and upgrade paths from level 100 to 500

Key takeaways

  • Microsoft 365 GRC maturity is predictable governance behavior, not the number of policies, licenses or dashboards you have.
  • Audit preparation time, label coverage and data exposure reveal maturity quickly, and Copilot readiness depends on that maturity.
  • Moving up the model means making ownership executable, automating controls that depend on memory and tracking a few trusted outcome KPIs.

Worth quoting

“predictable governance behavior means the right control happens consistently. The right person is held accountable and the evidence exists exactly when it's needed.” – Mirko Peters (02:26)
