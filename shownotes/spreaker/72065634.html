Many Power BI developers struggle with DAX, repetitive model maintenance and finding their place in the community. Microsoft MVP Bernat Agulló Roselló shares how Tabular Editor scripting, calculation groups and DAX UDFs automate semantic model work, and how BI professionals can grow through local user groups.

In this episode

  • 00:00 – Meet Bernat: languages, Japan and global BI communities
  • 05:10 – From Excel macros to BI and Power BI
  • 07:20 – Learning DAX and its biggest misconceptions
  • 11:45 – C# scripting and calculation groups
  • 14:05 – Why Tabular Editor
  • 19:00 – DAX UDFs and what BI teams should automate
  • 26:33 – The future of semantic model automation and AI in BI
  • 37:30 – Building the Power BI and Fabric Barcelona community

Key takeaways

  • DAX only makes sense together with the data model, so investing time in modeling fundamentals and the classic DAX books pays off early.
  • Tabular Editor with C# scripts, calculation groups and DAX user-defined functions removes repetitive model work and protects developers from error-prone copy-paste tasks.
  • Local user groups grow through steady activity, first-time and diverse speakers and annual sponsors, and community work is most rewarding when it is done for enjoyment.

Worth quoting
