Many Microsoft 365 consultants are stuck selling hours, apps and migrations in a market that keeps pushing prices down. This episode is for consultants, architects and IT professionals who want to build a premium practice around governance and risk reduction. You get a framework for assessments, pricing tiers, client acquisition and a 12-month plan.

In this episode

  • 00:00 – Why consultants get commoditized: architectural entropy
  • 06:29 – The control plane model and the three-plane assessment
  • 13:37 – Client scenarios: identity entropy and Power Platform chaos
  • 20:45 – From projects to retainers and from builder to architect
  • 27:14 – Client acquisition: inbound, outbound and the paid assessment
  • 39:33 – Remediation engagements and advisory retainers
  • 46:40 – Competitors, pricing principles and building a team
  • 57:03 – Specialization and the 12-month plan to 100K+

Key takeaways

  • High-value Microsoft consultants sell the reduction of architectural entropy across identity, productivity and infrastructure control planes, not apps and migrations.
  • A paid three-plane assessment creates the baseline and risk report that leads into remediation engagements and recurring advisory retainers.
  • Position against the cost of inaction, such as breaches, failed audits and failed migrations, instead of competing on hourly rates.
