Your Azure OpenAI and Copilot Studio bills keep climbing even when usage looks flat. This episode is for architects, platform teams and FinOps leads running AI agents in the Microsoft cloud, and it shows how to find the context, reasoning and autonomous taxes and remove them with caching, compression, routing and governance.

  • 71:00 – Advanced scenarios, emergency brakes and the weekly checklist

Key takeaways

  • Most AI agent cost comes from architecture, not model pricing: bloated retrieval context, sending every request to the flagship model, and autonomous agents consuming Copilot credits unchecked.
  • Four levers compound when applied together: semantic caching in Azure API Management, prompt compression with LLMLingua, model routing in Azure AI Foundry, and buying capacity correctly with PAYG or yearly PTU only at sustained high utilization.
  • Cost control lasts only with governance: tag every resource, classify agents into gold, silver and bronze cost classes before they are built, and enforce budgets, credit caps and rate limits automatically.
