Your biggest data risk may already be logged in: departing employees, careless sharing and unapproved apps all look like normal work to traditional security. This episode explains for compliance and IT teams how Microsoft Purview Insider Risk Management detects risky patterns, supports privacy-preserving investigations and can be rolled out step by step.

  • 08:46 – Privacy guardrails and compliance
  • 10:19 – Real-world scenarios and getting started

Key takeaways

  • Insider threats come from authorized users, so perimeter tools like firewalls and MFA cannot detect them on their own.
  • Microsoft Purview Insider Risk Management scores patterns of behavior over time using templates, indicators and optional HR or physical security signals.
  • Start small with one or two templates and a pilot group, tune thresholds for about 30 days and train investigators on privacy controls like anonymized user names.

Worth quoting

“Insider threats are hard to detect because they come from authorized users. They're not breaking in. They're logging in. And logging in looks exactly like normal work.” – Mirko Peters (00:44)
