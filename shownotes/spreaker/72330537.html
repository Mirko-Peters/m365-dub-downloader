Your Microsoft 365 Copilot reads SharePoint pages, emails and Teams chats that may hide instructions an attacker planted there. This episode explains for security and IT leaders why indirect prompt injection is an architectural flaw of RAG and how red teaming, dual LLM verification and zero-trust prompting close the gap.

Key takeaways

  • In RAG-based copilots every retrieved document becomes part of the prompt, so hidden text in SharePoint, email or Teams content can act as instructions.
  • Access controls and system prompts do not stop indirect prompt injection; defenses must be architectural, such as dual LLM verification with a non-LLM controller.
  • Zero-trust prompting, continuous red teaming and basic data hygiene of the retrieval corpus are needed before scaling Copilot beyond pilots.

Worth quoting
