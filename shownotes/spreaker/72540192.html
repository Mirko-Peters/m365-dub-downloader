Most SharePoint intranets look successful on paper while quietly failing the employees who use them. This episode is for intranet owners, SharePoint architects and digital workplace leads who want to replace page-view vanity metrics with real telemetry. Learn how Graph, search data, Microsoft Clarity and Knowledge Agent turn a static intranet into a self-optimizing experience platform.

In this episode

  • 04:56 – Publish-and-forget architecture and hidden metrics
  • 17:57 – Microsoft Graph as the structural blueprint
  • 24:15 – SharePoint analytics and search intent
  • 34:32 – Microsoft Clarity and the rage-click diagnostic
  • 47:00 – The optimization loop and Knowledge Agent
  • 57:20 – AI-generated pages and self-optimizing intranets
  • 69:55 – Governance, the four phases and the real question

Key takeaways

  • Page views measure popularity, not usefulness; real intranet quality only shows up in behavioral signals like scroll depth, failed searches and rage clicks.
  • Combining Microsoft Graph, SharePoint analytics, search logs and Microsoft Clarity turns isolated symptoms into a diagnosis and enables a continuous optimization loop.
  • AI can analyze, generate and optimize SharePoint pages, but only on clean metadata and information architecture and with human approval, sensitivity labels and governance in place.

Worth quoting

“This volunteer model is not governance. It's neglect with a name tag. A product requires dedicated ownership. Someone whose job includes keeping the product healthy, not as an afterthought.” – Mirko Peters (07:25)

“If your permissions are messy, AI can't safely retrieve information. Graph doesn't fix these problems. It makes them visible to systems that can. And that visibility is the first step toward automated optimization.” – Mirko Peters (20:59)
