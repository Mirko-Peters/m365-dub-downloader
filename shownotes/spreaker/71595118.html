Many organizations train employees to write better prompts while Copilot keeps pulling stale, overshared or fragmented data. This episode shows IT leaders and Microsoft 365 architects how to replace prompt hacks with a governed architecture built on Microsoft Graph, Purview, Entra ID, Fabric and Copilot Studio, and how to prove its value with decision-focused metrics in 30 days.

  • 03:54 – Replacing the prompt with the decision lattice
  • 07:20 – The architecture beyond the interface: Graph, Purview, Entra ID, Fabric, Copilot Studio
  • 10:45 – Walkthrough: the executive decision brief
  • 14:16 – Measuring success with decision-confident speed
  • 17:10 – The 30-day proof plan

Key takeaways

  • Copilot results fail in production mostly because of fragmented context, mis-scoped permissions and stale duplicate files, not because of weak prompts.
  • A reliable AI coworker is built from layers of signals, curated context via Graph connectors, governed decision logic in Copilot Studio and guardrails from Purview and Entra ID.
  • Success should be measured by time to decision, rework rate and exception count, and the model can be proven on one workflow in 30 days.

Worth quoting
