Customers hate repeating their story every time they switch from chat to phone or get transferred. This episode explains in plain English how Dynamics 365 Contact Center connects channels, routing, agent context and AI help for service leaders and agents. You will learn what the product does, how it fits next to your CRM, and where human judgment still matters.

Key takeaways

  • Dynamics 365 Contact Center brings voice and digital customer conversations into one workspace with shared context, without forcing a company to replace its existing CRM.
  • Unified routing sends each conversation to a queue and an agent with the right skills and capacity, and transfers or consultations keep the collected context.
  • Copilot and AI agents reduce routine work such as self-service, summaries and case notes, but humans must review suggestions and remain responsible for decisions.

Worth quoting

“Think of the CRM as the customer filing cabinet. It stores the customer story over time. The Contact Center is the service desk in front of that filing cabinet where calls and messages arrive and people work through issues as they happen.” – Mirko Peters (01:53)
