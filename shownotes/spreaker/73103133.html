Many organizations roll out Copilot and agents but see little business value because everyone builds their own way of working. In this conversation, Femke Cornelissen, Chief Transformation Officer at Wortell, shares with leaders and adoption teams how strategy, policy, trust and culture make AI transformation stick. You get practical ideas for agent ownership, experimentation and continuous adoption.

  • 52:26 – Quickfire round and closing

Key takeaways

  • AI transformation needs a strategy from the top, an AI team or council and a transformation team, otherwise everyone builds their own isolated solutions without business value.
  • Trust grows when leaders share where the organization is heading, involve employees in solving problems and give a clear framework for which AI tools and data may be used.
  • Adoption is a continuous effort: agents need owners and up-to-date data, and champions help colleagues keep up with constant change.

Worth quoting

“So why you don't have business value is because everyone is creating their own way. You don't have any synergy in your organization in your way of working.” – Femke Cornelissen (10:50)
