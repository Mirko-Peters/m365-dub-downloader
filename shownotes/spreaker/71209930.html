Most Power Platform solutions fail before anything is built, because teams name a tool before they name what is actually broken. This episode gives leaders, architects and makers a simple mental model for the five Power Platform tools and a repeatable sequence for choosing the right first move. Use it to stop building polished apps on top of broken processes.

  • 34:39 – The three forces model: data gravity, process criticality, identity and governance
  • 45:38 – Choosing the first move, the SharePoint and Excel trap and the governed approach
  • 56:36 – Dataverse, cost to serve and the 2026 shifts: agents and managed control
  • 69:53 – A reusable selection sequence and the final reframing

Key takeaways

  • Power Platform projects usually fail at the starting point, when a team picks a tool before naming the structural constraint in the process.
  • Power Apps, Power Automate, Power BI, Power Pages and Copilot Studio each own a different layer, and data gravity, process criticality and identity and governance determine the right build order.
  • AI agents and Copilot Studio amplify whatever foundation exists, so stable data in Dataverse, executable processes and managed environments must come before the assistant layer.
