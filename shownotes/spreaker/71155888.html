When an auditor asks who had access and why, how long does your Microsoft 365 tenant take to answer? This episode is for IT leaders, architects and compliance owners who want to turn governance debt into audit-ready architecture, with retained evidence from Entra and Purview instead of screenshots and heroics.

In this episode

  • 00:00 – The audit notice is not the problem
  • 05:19 – What governance debt really is
  • 12:16 – Three cases: Copilot, regulated firms and AI regulation
  • 23:38 – Audit preparation time as the key metric
  • 33:57 – The retention trap
  • 37:38 – Audit-ready architecture: identity, data and automation
  • 51:18 – The cost and reputation of audit panic
  • 58:51 – Three executive commitments and continuous readiness

Key takeaways

  • Audit panic is a predictable system outcome: controls that exist only in policy language must be proven by manual effort when scrutiny arrives.
  • Measure audit preparation time and treat Entra and Purview log retention as proof duration, because evidence that has expired cannot be recovered.
  • Build identity, data and automation as proof layers, and commit to longer retention, automated evidence collection and quarterly license and access reviews.
