Developers are drowning in Kubernetes, pipelines and compliance checklists instead of shipping features. This episode is for engineering leaders and platform teams who want to understand the DevOps tax and replace it with platform engineering, from golden paths and policy as code to Team Topologies, measurement and AI-ready governance.

  • 20:18 – Golden paths, infrastructure as code and policy as code
  • 31:12 – Team Topologies and the platform product mindset
  • 41:16 – Measurement, developer experience and AI governance
  • 64:31 – Migration path, ROI and objections

Key takeaways

  • The DevOps tax is the hidden cost of making every team run its own infrastructure at scale, paid in cognitive load, tool sprawl, shadow IT and burnout.
  • Platform engineering inverts the model: a platform team treats infrastructure as a product and offers golden paths built on infrastructure as code and policy as code.
  • Success is measured by voluntary adoption, developer experience, DORA metrics and reduced cognitive load, and the same embedded governance lets AI agents such as Copilot operate safely.
