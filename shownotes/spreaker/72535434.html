Most SharePoint files carry no business metadata, leaving organizations unable to find contracts, enforce retention or trust Copilot answers. This episode is for SharePoint architects, compliance teams and Microsoft 365 admins who want to close the metadata gap with a governed taxonomy, document processing models, autofill columns and agents.

In this episode

  • 00:00 – Why SharePoint data without metadata is a liability
  • 08:17 – The metadata foundation: term sets, columns and content types
  • 14:19 – Governance, compliance and Copilot readiness
  • 20:33 – What intelligent document processing means
  • 28:01 – Syntex, SharePoint Premium and Document Processing
  • 34:18 – Model types: structured, freeform and unstructured
  • 40:36 – Agents, skills, autofill and building custom models
  • 69:30 – From metadata to governed action and scaling

Key takeaways

  • The real SharePoint problem is not storage but missing business metadata, which breaks search, retention, DLP, eDiscovery and Copilot grounding.
  • Design the taxonomy first, with term sets, site columns and content types, because document processing models need a target vocabulary to populate.
  • Syntex capabilities now live on as document processing and AI in SharePoint, so inventory existing models, govern skills and autofill carefully, and connect extracted metadata to Purview retention and sensitivity labels.
