SCCM and Intune both manage devices, yet they work in completely different ways and picking the wrong one wastes time and money. This episode helps IT admins and decision makers compare on-premises depth with cloud simplicity, understand co-management and choose the right path for their organization.

Key takeaways

  • SCCM is an agent-based on-premises tool with deep control for Windows, OS deployment and servers, but it needs substantial server infrastructure.
  • Intune is a cloud service that only needs an internet connection, manages Windows, macOS, iOS and Android, and integrates natively with conditional access.
  • Co-management lets you move workloads such as compliance, endpoint protection and updates to Intune step by step while keeping complex tasks in SCCM.
