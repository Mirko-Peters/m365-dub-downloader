Mature Microsoft 365 environments with strong governance and automation can still feel slow and political, and AI often makes that drag more visible. In this episode Mirko Peters shows leaders and platform owners why optimizing parts hurts performance and how to redesign decision, data and interaction flows so Copilot and Power Platform can actually accelerate the business.

  • 08:26 – Why governance doesn't save a misdesigned system
  • 11:08 – The research signal and decision velocity
  • 17:48 – Designing decision flow, data flow, interaction and power
  • 32:31 – Why transformations stall: a case from fragmented to designed
  • 51:47 – Five steps: see reality, identify friction, redesign, align access, layer AI last
  • 69:40 – Microsoft 365, Copilot and Power Platform, metrics and where to start on Monday

Key takeaways

  • Local optimization of tools, governance and automation can make an organization slower, because performance comes from how decision, data and interaction flows and power fit together.
  • Decision velocity, the time from a meaningful signal to a committed action, is a more honest performance metric than model quality, prompt volume or adoption counts.
  • Leaders should redesign one high-friction flow first, align access with accountable roles and only then layer Copilot, Power Automate and AI on top.

Worth quoting

“Governance matters, but it is not an operating model. It is a control framework. It tells us what should be allowed, what should be reviewed, and what should be protected or stopped.” – Mirko Peters (08:38)

“AI is not creating the design problem, it is simply revealing the problem that was already there.” – Mirko Peters (14:30)
