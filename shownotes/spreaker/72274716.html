Most organizations are building chatbots that answer questions but rarely change how work gets done. This episode is for Microsoft 365 and Copilot Studio architects and decision makers who want to codify expert logic into digital twins that diagnose, decide and deliver measurable business outcomes under proper governance.

In this episode

  • 00:00 – Chatbots versus digital twins
  • 04:00 – The logic bot versus chatbot ROI gap
  • 07:27 – The diagnostic boundary and the no-new-privileges principle
  • 10:53 – Copilot Studio as the brain: topics, tools and knowledge sources
  • 14:19 – The four-phase roadmap and the data foundation
  • 21:11 – Governance, safety and the Copilot Studio plus Logic Apps architecture
  • 28:38 – Building, measuring and scaling your first digital twin
  • 43:31 – Governance twin, workflow twin, security and the logic-first future

Key takeaways

  • Chatbots contain cost by deflecting tickets, while digital twins codify expert decision logic and create value by reducing cycle time and errors across a workflow.
  • In Copilot Studio, logic lives in topics, tools and curated knowledge sources, with Logic Apps or Power Automate handling deterministic execution and the agent never getting more access than the user.
  • Follow the sequence instrument, model, simulate and operationalize, put governance and a measured baseline in place first, and start in advisory mode with one high-value workflow.

Worth quoting

“A chatbot answers questions, it sits there, waits for someone to ask it something, and then it generates a response that is useful for basic FAQs. But a digital twin does something completely different. A digital twin makes decisions.” – Mirko Peters (00:16)

“Your digital twin does not get access to data the user does not have, and it does not make decisions the user could not make.” – Mirko Peters (08:47)
