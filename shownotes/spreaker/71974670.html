Most enterprises still rely on employees to apply sensitivity labels, and most of their data stays unlabeled as a result. This episode is for security, compliance and Microsoft Purview architects who want to replace manual tagging with real-time AI classification. You will learn the architecture, the economics and a 90-day roadmap for autonomous governance.

In this episode

  • 00:00 – Why manual tagging fails
  • 03:25 – Building the LLM intelligence layer
  • 06:21 – The latency mismatch and the Guardian agent
  • 09:13 – The economics of autonomous classification
  • 12:24 – Mitigating drift and prompt injection risks
  • 15:15 – The 90-day implementation roadmap

Key takeaways

  • Manual sensitivity labeling fails at enterprise scale because it depends on busy users making correct choices every time, leaving most data invisible to DLP and access controls.
  • A fine-tuned LLM inference layer combined with a real-time Guardian agent can classify content in milliseconds and close the vulnerability window left by slow Purview label propagation.
  • Autonomous classification needs ongoing guardrails: drift detection, a secondary model against prompt injection, hybrid regex-plus-LLM tiers and human-in-the-loop retraining.
