Most finance teams hear Copilot and picture a chatbot, but what does it really do on a Tuesday morning? This episode walks finance professionals and Dynamics 365 Finance and Operations users through collections, month-end close, cash flow forecasting and AP, plus the requirements and adoption steps that decide whether it pays off.

In this episode

  • 00:00 – Introduction and the problem Copilot solves
  • 01:24 – Copilot for collections and AR
  • 02:45 – Financial close and reporting
  • 04:40 – Cash flow and forecasting
  • 05:54 – Expense management and AP
  • 07:19 – Requirements to get started
  • 08:33 – Driving adoption
  • 09:47 – The big picture

Key takeaways

  • Copilot in Dynamics 365 Finance and Operations brings customer summaries, draft emails, variance explanations and forecasts into the finance workflow instead of making people chase data across screens.
  • Collections is the fastest-value starting point, followed by financial close, cash flow forecasting and expense and AP automation.
  • Copilot requires a recent F&O version, Dataverse integration, a cloud-hosted environment and a separate Microsoft 365 Copilot license, and adoption needs targeted pilots and scenario-based training.

Worth quoting
