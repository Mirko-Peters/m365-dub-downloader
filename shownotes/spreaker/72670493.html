Your CRM holds records, but your business runs on relationships that live on LinkedIn. This episode shows sales leaders and Dynamics 365 architects how to connect LinkedIn and Dynamics 365 with Sales Navigator, relationship health scoring, Copilot and orchestrated agents while staying within rate limits, GDPR and good governance.

In this episode

  • 00:00 – Why your CRM is blind

  • 06:07 – LinkedIn APIs, Sales Navigator sync and Power Automate
  • 16:49 – Native integration and relationship health
  • 22:54 – Copilot for Sales and sales agents
  • 29:20 – Orchestration, rate limits and timeouts
  • 41:49 – Governance, roadmap and the changing seller role
  • 64:28 – Multi-agent B2B and the risks

Key takeaways

  • LinkedIn holds the relationship graph and Dynamics 365 holds the process, so modern selling needs an architecture that connects them through official channels such as Sales Navigator CRM sync.
  • Relationship health scoring, Copilot for Sales and orchestrated sales agents turn synced data into insight and action, but they must be designed around API rate limits, timeouts and governance.
  • Roll out in phases, treat LinkedIn data like regulated data and keep humans reviewing agent decisions to manage hallucination, bias and over-reliance risks.
