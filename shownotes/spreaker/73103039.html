Static intranets are a ghost town, and many employees never find the news, tools and policies they need. This episode explains Viva Connections and its 2026 rename to SharePoint for intranet owners, internal communicators and Microsoft 365 admins. You learn its three components, the engagement stats that matter and the first steps to make it work.

Key takeaways

  • Viva Connections is an employee experience front door inside Teams, built on a SharePoint home site, with a news reader, a dashboard of cards and curated resources.
  • In 2026 Microsoft renamed the Viva Connections app in Teams to the SharePoint app, while functionality and admin tools stayed the same and the basic version remains included in Microsoft 365.
  • Adoption depends on pinning the app globally in Teams, keeping news fresh with multiple posts per week and offering a rich dashboard, especially for mobile and frontline workers.
