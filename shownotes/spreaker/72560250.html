Your organization is deploying AI agents under service accounts built for static apps, creating invisible, over-privileged identities. This episode is for identity, security and governance teams who need to bring agents under control, using Entra Agent ID, blueprints, conditional access and an agent registry without slowing teams down.

In this episode

  • 00:00 – Why service accounts were never built for agents
  • 03:24 – The shadow AI explosion and the identity vacuum
  • 08:48 – The cost of the old model and why RBAC fails
  • 16:26 – Three identity types and the blueprint hierarchy
  • 21:07 – Conditional access, agent registry and the agent fabric
  • 30:31 – Least privilege, observability and blueprints in practice
  • 41:04 – Lifecycle, discovery, incident response and unified AI policy
  • 52:48 – Speed vs. security, roles, ROI and the roadmap

Key takeaways

  • AI agents are a third identity type between users and workloads, and forcing them into service principals creates over-privileged God accounts with invisible blast radius.
  • Entra Agent ID, agent identity blueprints and conditional access for agents let you define permissions once per risk tier and enforce them at the identity layer.
  • An agent registry, unified logging and a structured lifecycle from request to retirement turn shadow AI into governed, auditable agents that teams can deploy faster.
