High-intent carts go quiet, churn signals go unheard and consent conflicts silently block outreach while delivery dashboards stay green. This episode is for marketing automation, Dynamics 365 Customer Insights and Power Automate teams who want a forensic way to find why journeys fail and a playbook of guarded triggers, consent resolution, idempotency and monitoring that makes every decision provable.

Key takeaways

  • Customer journeys usually die silently, from missing trigger evaluations, unresolved consent precedence and absent guardrails rather than visible errors.
  • A living journey needs guarded triggers, a consent lattice resolved before channel choice, frequency caps with right of way, idempotency keys, exponential back-off and explicit goal exits.
  • Customer Insights profiles and orchestrates, Power Automate enforces, Fabric preserves lineage and monitors for absence, and Copilot assists while humans approve and own exceptions.
