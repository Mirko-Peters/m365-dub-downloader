Many organizations ask whether Power Platform is secure enough, while the real risk sits in their own governance. This episode is for IT leaders, platform owners and compliance teams running low-code at scale. Five real-world scenarios show how DLP, environments, ownership and Copilot governance fail, and what a defendable operating model looks like.

In this episode

  • 00:00 – Why the question exists: enterprise anxiety around low-code
  • 04:32 – Security vs. governance vs. control plane
  • 12:56 – Where security breaks when governance disappears
  • 17:28 – Scenarios: manufacturing data leaks and financial services audit failures
  • 25:52 – Scenarios: healthcare PHI, retail app loss and Copilot in professional services
  • 41:23 – A real governance operating model: platform team, RACI and environments
  • 50:26 – Metrics that turn governance into a measurable system
  • 55:37 – Zero Trust, Copilot and the blunt answer

Key takeaways

  • Power Platform security is enterprise-grade; data leaks, audit failures and orphaned apps come from missing governance in the control plane.
  • An environment strategy must map to data classification, with tiered DLP policies, designed security roles and a no owner, no app rule for production.
  • Run governance as an operating system with a platform team, measurable thresholds and enforcement, and fix it before Copilot multiplies the gaps.

Worth quoting

“Security is Microsoft's job. Governance is your job. The control plane is where those jobs meet or fail to meet.” – Mirko Peters (05:51)
