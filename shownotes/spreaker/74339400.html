When purchasing, warehouse, sales and finance each keep their own stock numbers, nobody can say how much inventory the business really has. In this Knowledge Nugget, Mirko Peters walks operations and finance teams through how Dynamics 365 Inventory Management tracks every item from supplier receipt to customer shipment, including the money attached to it.

In this episode

  • 00:00 – Intro: the stock you can't see can cost you
  • 00:40 – Why inventory management exists
  • 02:56 – The digital record behind every item
  • 06:48 – From supplier delivery to customer order
  • 10:33 – The money attached to stock
  • 13:51 – Visibility, reordering and daily control
  • 17:16 – Inventory vs. warehouse management
  • 18:19 – Conclusion: one shared stock story

Key takeaways

  • Dynamics 365 Inventory Management keeps one shared record of quantity, location, status, ownership and cost for every item, replacing separate team spreadsheets.
  • Inventory dimensions, item transactions, item groups and costing methods connect each physical stock movement to the general ledger.
  • Inventory management holds the company-wide stock story, while warehouse management guides the physical work of receiving, put-away, picking and shipping.
