Many AI agents look impressive in a demo but fail in production because of governance gaps, poor data and runaway costs. This episode helps Copilot Studio makers, architects and IT leaders move from chatbot experiments to secure, governed enterprise agents with RAG, MCP and multi-agent designs.

In this episode

  • 00:00 – Intro and Elliot's journey into generative AI
  • 06:56 – Why enterprise AI is hard: governance and DLP
  • 10:43 – Selling AI to executive sponsors: ROI and the wow effect
  • 16:17 – Multi-agent systems and orchestration
  • 21:06 – RAG, metadata, versioning and chunking
  • 27:38 – MCP explained
  • 31:05 – Governance, DLP and citizen development
  • 47:46 – The future of agents, model choice and token costs

Key takeaways

  • Enterprises learn AI governance fastest by building small solutions while steadily improving DLP policies, sensitivity labels and monitoring rather than waiting for a perfect model.
  • Good RAG depends on good data: well-described metadata, a clean production knowledge base without outdated versions, and careful chunking keep answers accurate and token costs down.
  • Production agents need clearly communicated tasks, tightly scoped connectors, manual testing and the right model size, because small language models are often enough and endless agent loops can become expensive.
