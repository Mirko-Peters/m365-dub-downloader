You made the sale and sent the invoice, but when does the money actually arrive, and what happens if it doesn't? This plain-English episode is for finance teams, consultants and business users who want to understand Dynamics 365 Accounts Receivable. Follow one customer balance from invoice to payment, credit checks and collections.

In this episode

  • 00:00 – What accounts receivable is
  • 00:35 – The customer account: payment terms, credit limits and posting profiles
  • 03:31 – Creating the invoice: sales orders, free text invoices and subscription billing
  • 06:58 – Receiving and matching payments: settlement and reconciliation
  • 10:33 – Credit management: deciding when to keep selling
  • 12:58 – Collections and disputes
  • 15:54 – One invoice, one connected process
  • 17:20 – Conclusion: keeping money inside under control

Key takeaways

  • Accounts receivable in Dynamics 365 Finance tracks what customers owe from the first invoice until the balance closes, using the rules stored on the customer account.
  • A payment only closes an invoice once it is settled against it; partial payments, payment reconciliation journals and centralized payments keep the open balance accurate.
  • Credit holds, collection lists, promises to pay, disputes and aging reports help finance and sales act on risk early without treating customers as case numbers.

Worth quoting
