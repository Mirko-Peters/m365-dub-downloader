Copilot Credits and autonomous agents replace predictable license costs with variable consumption, while most tenants lack visibility, identity controls and audit trails. This episode helps IT leaders, architects and finance teams understand the credit economy, EU AI Act obligations and the governance, FinOps and procurement steps needed to deploy agents without runaway costs or compliance exposure.

Key takeaways

  • Copilot Credits turn AI from a predictable per-seat license into variable consumption, so budgets must be forecast from usage patterns, task complexity and business value rather than head count.
  • Agents inherit user permissions, act autonomously and can be redirected by prompt injection, which makes distinct agent identities (Agent 365 and Entra ID), scoped tools, approval gates, kill switches and dedicated audit logging essential.
  • With EU AI Act high-risk obligations applying from August 2026, organizations need an agent inventory, risk classification, meaningful human oversight and AI FinOps practices such as showback and chargeback before scaling.
