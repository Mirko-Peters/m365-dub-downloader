A spreadsheet can tell you that 50 units exist somewhere, but it cannot guide a worker to the right shelf. This episode explains Dynamics 365 Warehouse Management in plain English for operations teams, consultants and business users. Learn how receiving, put-away, picking, counting and shipping connect to the rest of the business.

In this episode

  • 00:00 – Introduction: the digital guide on the warehouse floor
  • 00:35 – Why warehouse management exists
  • 02:53 – The warehouse map: sites, warehouses, locations and stock details
  • 05:46 – Inbound work: from delivery truck to the right shelf
  • 09:14 – Outbound work: waves, picking, packing and shipping
  • 12:41 – Daily control: moving, counting, returns and labels
  • 15:34 – One connected platform
  • 17:52 – Conclusion

Key takeaways

  • Inventory management records how much stock the business owns, while Dynamics 365 Warehouse Management knows exactly where it sits and what workers should do with it next.
  • Location directives, work templates and waves turn warehouse rules into step-by-step tasks that workers confirm with scans on mobile devices.
  • Because warehouse work runs inside Dynamics 365 Supply Chain Management, purchasing, sales, production, quality and finance all see the same stock picture.
