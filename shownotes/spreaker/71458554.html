Many companies buy AI licenses and run training, yet the same teams still copy data by hand and rebuild reports every month. This episode is for leaders, finance and operations managers who need to prove Copilot and AI value. Learn a simple measurement model and role-based upskilling approach that turns AI spend into measurable reclaimed time and faster decisions.

In this episode

  • 00:00 – The waste pattern of AI training
  • 00:40 – Why certification metrics don't show ROI
  • 03:24 – The measurement model: reclaimed time, decision velocity and baselines
  • 07:10 – Three roles with high ROI density
  • 10:22 – Proof in the workflow: three before-and-after cases
  • 13:56 – Governance that protects ROI
  • 16:40 – Scaling from pilot to operating model

Key takeaways

  • Completion rates and badges measure exposure, not change; AI ROI shows up only as reclaimed time, faster decisions and fewer errors inside named workflows with a real baseline.
  • Finance analysts, project managers and frontline support teams have high ROI density because their work is repetitive, document-heavy and full of small decisions.
  • Governance with approved data sources, human accountability, use case tiering and shared prompt patterns protects the ROI case, and expansion should follow proven workflows rather than blanket licensing.

Worth quoting
