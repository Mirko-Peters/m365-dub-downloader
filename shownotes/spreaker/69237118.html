Most organizations have AI policies, but nobody with real authority to stop an AI system when it drifts or surfaces sensitive data. This episode is for executives, IT, data and business owners running Copilot, Fabric and enterprise AI in the Microsoft ecosystem. You will get a stewardship model with decision rights, a RACI, risk tiers, escalation and a 90-day plan you can run.

  • 159:13 – Copilot governance, org size and the first 90 days

Key takeaways

  • AI governance documents values, but stewardship gives named people the decision rights and authority to pause, degrade or retire AI systems.
  • Copilot exposure is a substrate problem: overshared SharePoint, open links and unlabeled data must be fixed with Entra groups, Purview labels and DLP.
  • Start with ownership, a risk taxonomy, a use case register and an escalation workflow that resolves in minutes, built over a 90-day plan.

Worth quoting
