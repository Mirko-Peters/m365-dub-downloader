Troubleshooting a blocked sign-in in Entra can mean jumping between logs, policies and risk reports for twenty minutes. This episode explains Copilot in Microsoft Entra ID for identity admins, help desk staff and security analysts: how it turns raw logs into plain-English answers and why it respects your existing permissions. You will see where it saves time and how it stays governed.

Key takeaways

  • Copilot in Microsoft Entra ID is an AI assistant embedded in the Entra admin center that answers plain-English questions by pulling together sign-in logs, audit logs, Conditional Access and Identity Protection data.
  • It shortens sign-in troubleshooting, summarizes risky users with recommended next steps and explains which Conditional Access policies applied and why.
  • Copilot inherits the admin's existing roles and Conditional Access, logs its prompts in audit logs and only suggests actions, so humans stay in control.

Worth quoting

“Co-pilot doesn't just dump raw data on you. It gives you a summary, a narrative, and explanation.” – Mirko Peters (03:22)

“Co-pilot inherits your existing intra roles and permissions. That's the key point. If you don't have permission to view a specific log or resource, Co-pilot can't see it either.” – Mirko Peters (11:40)
