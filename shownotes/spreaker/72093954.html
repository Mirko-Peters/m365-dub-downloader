Most Microsoft 365 governance policies were written before AI agents could find, summarize and act on everything users can access. In this conversation, MVP and Regional Director Christian Buckley shows admins, governance leads and IT decision makers how to rethink permissions, content cleanup and guardrails so Copilot and agents can scale safely.

In this episode

  • 06:36 – Lessons from early SharePoint that still apply
  • 08:40 – Governance mistakes in the age of agentic AI
  • 21:30 – Oversharing and guardrails that enable collaboration
  • 26:50 – First steps for a Copilot pilot and AI readiness
  • 30:30 – Business ownership, champions and making GRC an enabler
  • 43:45 – The future of governance and closing advice

Key takeaways

  • Agentic AI is a governance and operational transformation, not just another productivity feature, because AI instantly surfaces every permission problem across SharePoint, Teams and OneDrive.
  • AI readiness starts with the fundamentals: discover the sprawl, clean up content and permissions, then put guardrails such as templates, labels, lifecycle and external sharing reviews in place.
  • Governance works best when business units own their information, champions spread best practices and policies run in the background so people can collaborate freely within guardrails.

Worth quoting

“I think that the mistake organizations make is treating agentic AI like it's just another productivity feature or roll out. This isn't a new version of Word and PowerPoint.” – Christian Buckley (09:49)
