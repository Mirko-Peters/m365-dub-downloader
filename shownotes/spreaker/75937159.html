Your customer data sits in several clouds and apps, and nobody knows which copy is real. This episode explains Microsoft Fabric shortcuts for beginners and data teams: what they are, where they live, what they can reach, and the trade-offs and mistakes to avoid. You'll learn how to keep one authoritative copy while every team still gets a clean view of the data.

Key takeaways

  • A Fabric shortcut is a pointer to data at a target path, so deleting the shortcut never deletes the source data.
  • Place shortcuts under Tables when the target is Delta and must be queryable in SQL and Power BI, and under Files for raw data you plan to process.
  • Shortcuts trade copies for dependency and first-read latency, so copy data instead when it needs heavy transformation, must stay in a region, or needs full maintenance control.

Worth quoting
