Microsoft has moved its center of investment from canvas apps to Copilot Studio and agents, and many Power Platform makers are still building on the old foundation. This episode is for low-code developers and architects who want to understand the new split between agent judgment and flow logic, the governance and security implications, and the concrete steps to grow into a logic architect role.

In this episode

  • 00:00 – Why the low-code model is breaking
  • 03:46 – What Microsoft actually changed
  • 06:59 – Copilot Studio is not a chatbot
  • 10:24 – Judgment versus logic
  • 23:39 – From maker to logic architect
  • 34:11 – Governance as the new core skill
  • 41:33 – Where Power Apps still wins
  • 57:47 – Three moves that matter now

Key takeaways

  • Copilot Studio agents should own judgment while Power Automate flows own deterministic logic, with a clean line between the two layers.
  • Canvas apps break at scale because business logic lives on screens, but they still win for structured forms, offline work, device integration and dense data grids.
  • Low-code developers stay relevant by moving into logic architecture: data governance, evaluation, human-in-the-loop design, integration depth and AI risk management.

Worth quoting

“Power automate owns the logic. This isn't a limitation of the tool. It is a core design principle.” – Mirko Peters (10:59)

“If you are running a process where the output must be identical every time, like a financial calculation or a compliance check, you do not want an agent making that call.” – Mirko Peters (12:36)
