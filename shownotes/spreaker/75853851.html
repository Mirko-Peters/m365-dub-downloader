Provisioning ten IoT devices by hand is easy, but at 100,000 devices every shortcut becomes a security and support problem. This episode explains Azure Device Provisioning Service for IoT architects and manufacturing IT teams, from device identity and attestation to enrollment groups and IoT Hub allocation. Learn how to build a zero-touch onboarding path that survives factories, suppliers and field replacements.

In this episode

  • 00:00 – Why 100,000 devices change everything
  • 05:23 – What Azure DPS actually does
  • 11:14 – Zero-touch provisioning step by step
  • 17:19 – The identity model behind the device
  • 23:50 – Attestation and TPM
  • 36:30 – X.509 certificates and symmetric keys
  • 59:44 – Enrollments, initial state and first boot
  • 77:55 – IoT hub allocation and capacity planning

Key takeaways

  • Do not bake an IoT Hub connection string into the device image; let Azure DPS assign the hub on first boot after the device proves its identity.
  • Choose TPM, X.509 or symmetric key attestation based on hardware, supplier chain and service process, and plan certificate renewal, revocation and replacement from day one.
  • Design enrollment groups and allocation policies around real trust, data residency and capacity boundaries rather than portal convenience.
