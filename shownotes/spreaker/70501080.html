Most organizations measure Power Platform success by how many apps they build, while abandoned apps, permission drift and ungoverned AI agents quietly drain budgets and widen the attack surface. This episode is for Microsoft 365 and Power Platform architects, CoE leads and consultants who want a concrete control-plane blueprint, from compiled policy and zoned AI governance to a 12-month roadmap, for turning governance into measurable efficiency.

Key takeaways

  • The Power Platform is a distributed decision engine, and the real efficiency gains come from engineering the governance control plane rather than building more apps.
  • Treating authorization, DLP and AI agent governance as compiled, deterministic policy with zoned risk tiers, delegated operations and continuous audit logging turns governance from a bottleneck into an accelerator.
  • A disciplined 12-month roadmap of assessment, authorization design, pilot, rollout and optimization is what turns app rationalization, permission consolidation and faster provisioning into measurable seven-figure savings.
