When a machine stops, the expert who knows it best is often far away, and photos, chats and phone calls leave too much to guesswork. This episode explains Dynamics 365 Remote Assist for maintenance, field service and operations teams. You learn how live shared video and on-screen guidance speed up fixes while people on site stay in control.

Key takeaways

  • Part of Dynamics 365, Remote Assist lets a frontline worker share live video with a remote expert, who can mark the exact part or switch in the shared view.
  • Teams provides the calling and contacts, Field Service adds work order context, and Microsoft 365 with Entra ID controls who can join and access information.
  • Remote Assist fits visual, hands-on work where an expert's view prevents delays or trips, but it never replaces on-site safety rules or physical work.

Worth quoting

“Remote Assist isn't a repair manual that hands you every answer. It's not a robot that diagnoses a machine on its own, and it definitely doesn't replace the trained person who has to do the work safely on site.” – Mirko Peters (03:51)

“Each building block has a simple job. Teams connects people. Remote assist connects that conversation to physical work. Field service connects it to a planned service job and its history.” – Mirko Peters (13:15)
