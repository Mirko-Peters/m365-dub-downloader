Copilot, Cowork and Scout sound like alphabet soup to most Microsoft 365 users and IT leaders. This episode explains in plain English what Microsoft Scout is, how its always-on Autopilot model differs from Copilot, what it needs in licensing and why preparing agent governance now pays off.

Key takeaways

  • Scout is Microsoft's first Autopilot: unlike Copilot and Cowork, it acts proactively on schedules and triggers without waiting for a prompt.
  • Scout runs as a desktop app under its own identity, connects to Microsoft 365 via Work IQ, local files, the web and MCP servers, and gates sensitive actions with approvals.
  • Scout is in private Frontier preview and needs Microsoft 365 Copilot, a GitHub Copilot license and Intune policies, so organizations should start with Copilot, Cowork skills and agent governance now.

Worth quoting

“Co-pilot and co-work both wait for you to initiate. You start the conversation, you give the task, scout doesn't wait. It watches, it monitors, it acts on its own.” – Mirko Peters (01:32)

“When co-pilot acts, it acts as you. It uses your permissions, your access, your identity. When scout acts, it has its own identity. It's a separate digital worker with its own permissions.” – Mirko Peters (04:16)
