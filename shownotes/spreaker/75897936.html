A merger or divestiture can turn two Microsoft 365 tenants into one, and copying files is the easy part. This episode explains in plain language what moves, what breaks and why the domain flips last, for IT leads and project owners who need to plan a tenant-to-tenant migration without surprises.

In this episode

  • 00:00 – What a tenant really is
  • 02:27 – Why companies leave one tenant for another
  • 03:53 – What actually moves
  • 05:36 – The Teams problem
  • 07:14 – The domain move
  • 08:38 – Running both tenants at once
  • 10:00 – Cutover weekend
  • 11:21 – What usually goes wrong and recap

Key takeaways

  • A tenant is your organization's sealed space in Microsoft's cloud, so mergers, divestitures and rebrands force content to be carried from one tenant to another.
  • Identity moves first, then mail and files in waves, while Teams chats, permissions, Planner, Forms and Power Automate connections are the wiring that breaks.
  • The custom domain can live in only one tenant at a time, so coexistence bridges the gap and the domain flips last during a short cutover window.

Worth quoting
