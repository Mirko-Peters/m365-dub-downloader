Manual testing cannot keep up with prompt injections, poisoned RAG pipelines and cross-model infections hitting autonomous AI agents in finance. This episode is for security, risk and compliance leaders deploying agents in banking workflows. You get a blueprint for a multi-model red-teaming gauntlet and an auditable framework ready for the 2026 regulatory deadlines.

  • 10:14 – The Glassbox audit framework and August 2026
  • 13:26 – Proactive warfare versus reactive cleanup
  • 15:53 – The self-healing implementation roadmap

Key takeaways

  • Financial institutions must treat AI agents as identities bound to a human sponsor, because regulators now expect every AI-driven payment decision to be traceable and explainable.
  • Using multiple models is not a safety net on its own, since poisoned knowledge bases and shared blind spots let one compromised model infect the rest of the chain.
  • Continuous, automated multi-model red teaming with reasoning traces and bounded autonomy replaces manual testing and turns security spending into a compounding asset.

Worth quoting

“We have to stop thinking about agents as tools and start thinking about them as identities. If you cannot audit the reasoning, you cannot trust the result and that is the new standard for 2026.” – Mirko Peters (03:06)

“Fine-tuning a single model on insecure tasks doesn't just stay within that model. It actually infects unrelated models in your chain.” – Mirko Peters (04:13)
