Contracts, policies and financial reports now live across SharePoint, OneDrive, Exchange Online and Teams, yet organizations must still prove how long they kept them and why they deleted them. This Simply Explained episode shows compliance teams and Microsoft 365 admins how Purview Records Management uses retention labels, record declaration, disposition review and audit history to build a defensible records lifecycle.

In this episode

  • 00:00 – Intro: from filing cabinets to Microsoft 365
  • 01:27 – What counts as an official record
  • 04:16 – Retention labels are the rule book
  • 07:53 – Record declaration locks the evidence
  • 11:07 – Disposition review: the gate before deletion
  • 14:17 – Audit history provides the proof
  • 16:35 – Putting the pieces together

Key takeaways

  • Microsoft Purview Records Management keeps official records for the required time and disposes of them in a controlled, reviewable way.
  • Retention labels attach item-level rules (trigger, period, end action), record declaration locks official evidence, and retention policies cover broad locations.
  • Disposition review and Purview Audit provide the decision gate and the trail needed to prove why a record was kept or deleted.

Worth quoting

“A record is official evidence that your organization did something, decided something, approved something, or agreed to something.” – Mirko Peters (01:30)

“You can use both approaches in the same connected platform, but they solve different problems. Policies handle broad coverage, while labels handle the records that need individual treatment.” – Mirko Peters (06:39)
