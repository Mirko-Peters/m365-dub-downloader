Many Microsoft partners publish an AppSource listing once and wonder why it never generates pipeline. This episode is for ISVs and partner marketers, and explains a three-layer framework of SEO, generative engine optimization and buyer psychology, plus three concrete moves to make this week.

  • 02:23 – How the AppSource ranking algorithm works
  • 04:14 – The SEO layer: title, keywords and visuals
  • 06:05 – The GEO layer: AI discovery and Copilot
  • 08:02 – Building citations and authority for AI
  • 09:54 – The psychology layer: five triggers that convert
  • 12:10 – Structuring the listing and three moves for this week

Key takeaways

  • An AppSource listing has to work on three layers at once: SEO so buyers find it, generative engine optimization so AI assistants cite it, and buyer psychology so visitors act.
  • Titles that combine brand, function and search keyword, outcome-led first 50 words, captioned screenshots and use-case-specific social proof drive ranking and conversion.
  • AI systems cite brands that appear on trusted external sources such as Reddit, LinkedIn and the Microsoft Tech Community, so partners should test buyer prompts in ChatGPT and Copilot and run App Advisor on their listing.
