Developers who were supposed to gain speed from DevOps now spend days on Terraform, Kubernetes and YAML instead of product code. This episode explains platform engineering, internal developer platforms and golden paths for engineering leaders and developers, and shows how to start small with one path that removes real pain.

  • 02:25 – What Platform Engineering Is
  • 04:08 – The Internal Developer Platform
  • 06:16 – Golden Paths
  • 08:23 – Cutting Cognitive Load
  • 10:21 – Treating the Platform as a Product
  • 12:04 – Why Now: The AI Wave
  • 14:18 – Start with One Golden Path

Key takeaways

  • Shift left overloaded developers with infrastructure, security and operations work, and platform engineering puts a central layer in place to absorb that complexity.
  • An internal developer platform with golden paths makes the secure, tested route the easiest one, while escape hatches keep it from becoming a cage.
  • Run the platform as a product: talk to developers, start with one painful workflow and measure voluntary adoption instead of making it mandatory.

Worth quoting
