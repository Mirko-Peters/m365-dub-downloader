Many companies rush into AI because of market pressure and then stall when results disappoint or the gut feeling says something is wrong. In this conversation, AI strategy advisor Wiebke Apitzsch shows business leaders why responsible AI is good business: start from strategy, protect what must stay human, and design processes that keep hallucinations, bias and data risks under control.

Key takeaways

  • Before choosing tools, organizations should decide who they want to be with AI and which activities must stay human, especially relationships with clients and employees.
  • AI adoption is mostly a culture and process problem: start from business strategy, and design workflows where a knowledgeable human must check AI results, which also contains hallucinations.
  • Responsibility and trust cannot be delegated to a chatbot provider; the company using AI stays accountable, and confidential data needs security measures matched to its sensitivity.

Worth quoting

“So the best thing that companies can do is to say, hey, let me review my strategy and my numbers as always and find out where my problems are and where my opportunities are as always.” – Wiebke Apitzsch (33:49)

“So if you set up the process in a way that a knowledgeable human has a look at the final result in a way that it cannot skip it. You have to avoid that people can become lazy and just say yes, yes, yes.” – Wiebke Apitzsch (40:39)
