Azure architecture is easy when everything works; the real questions start when regions, dependencies and assumptions fail. In this episode, Microsoft MVP Mike Martin helps Azure architects and IT decision-makers tell resilience from high availability, set realistic SLA, RTO and RPO targets, and understand where Microsoft's responsibility ends. You get practical guidance on multi-region design, chaos testing and keeping solutions simple.

Key takeaways

  • Resilience and high availability are different: a resilient system bends and recovers under stress, while high availability fails over to a second instance elsewhere.
  • No solution reaches 100% uptime because the composite SLA is the product of all dependent services, so define RTO and RPO with the business and design multi-region where it pays off.
  • Use the Azure Well-Architected Framework, mission-critical guidance and fault injection with Azure Chaos Studio, and keep designs simple instead of defaulting to Kubernetes.

Worth quoting

“for me, architecture is about knowing which pieces go together, which pieces to avoid, which pieces are necessary and which pieces are not.” – Mike Martin (07:10)

“never take a dependency on a single region. Always make a design for multiple regions. So that way you always have a plan B and even a plan C or a plan D” – Mike Martin (11:56)
