Many organizations buy Copilot licenses and expect instant productivity, only to be disappointed. In this conversation, Microsoft MVP Fabio Bonolo shares with IT professionals and business leaders why change management, data culture and empowering leadership matter more than the tool itself. You get practical tips on champions networks, staying up to date and leading people through AI-driven change.

  • 03:38 – The future of work and the end of the clicking culture

  • 11:50 – Copilot, data quality and data culture
  • 20:29 – Keeping up with updates and building champions
  • 27:59 – Leadership, empowerment and communication
  • 46:02 – Avoiding burnout: find your niche
  • 50:29 – Community events and final thoughts

Key takeaways

  • AI and Copilot projects succeed when organizations invest in change management, training and enablement alongside the technology.
  • Copilot works on your own data, so data quality, regular quality controls and a lasting data culture determine the value it delivers.
  • Internal champions or ambassadors, authentic leadership and a focused niche help people keep up with the fast pace of AI change.

Worth quoting

“the big advantage of co-pilot is you have your own data, but it's also the biggest disadvantage. Because if your data is not well curated, co-pilot doesn't give you good answers.” – Fabio Bonolo (13:45)

“So you have to implement a good data culture and implement or make the employees understand the value of data.” – Fabio Bonolo (17:09)
