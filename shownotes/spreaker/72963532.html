Still running business data on Excel files and SharePoint lists that hit the 5,000-item wall? This episode explains Dataverse in plain English for Power Platform makers and IT pros: tables, relationships, role-based security and how Power Apps, Power Automate and Power BI build on it. You will know when Dataverse is the right foundation and how to start your first data model.

  • 02:32 – Tables: the foundation

  • 06:36 – Many-to-many relationships and intersection tables
  • 08:34 – Role-based security, business units and teams
  • 11:02 – Dataverse versus SharePoint
  • 13:01 – Power Platform integration and AI
  • 15:46 – Getting started with your data model

Key takeaways

  • Dataverse is a managed relational data platform that handles tables, relationships, security and business logic so every Power Platform app, flow and report shares one foundation.
  • Lookup columns, enforced referential integrity and role-based security with business units, teams and column-level security make Dataverse far more robust than spreadsheets or SharePoint lists.
  • Use SharePoint for simple trackers and documents, choose Dataverse for business-critical systems of record, and start with standard tables and a data model planned on paper.

Worth quoting

“Here's something important to understand. Security and dataverse is additive, not subtractive. If a user has two roles, they get the most permissive access from either role.” – Mirko Peters (09:40)

“The golden rule of dataverse security is this, don't assign system administrator to everyone. That role gives full access to everything in the environment.” – Mirko Peters (10:23)
