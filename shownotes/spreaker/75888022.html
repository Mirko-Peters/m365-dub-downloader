Once an Azure tenant grows past a handful of subscriptions, permissions, policies and costs drift out of control. This Simply Explained episode is for admins and cloud teams who need a clear map of the Azure hierarchy. It shows how management groups and subscriptions work together so you can set rules once and see costs in one place.

In this episode

  • 00:00 – Why Azure turns into a mess without a map
  • 02:35 – The subscription is where your Azure actually lives
  • 04:36 – Management groups are the folder above your subscriptions
  • 06:52 – How permissions and policies flow down the tree
  • 09:43 – What a good structure actually looks like
  • 12:05 – Naming, cost boundaries and keeping it sane
  • 14:05 – A quick recap and one question

Key takeaways

  • Subscriptions carry billing, quotas and the link to one Microsoft Entra tenant, while management groups sit above them purely for governance.
  • Access (RBAC) and Azure Policy assigned at a management group are inherited by every subscription and resource below it, and a deny higher up cannot be overridden lower down.
  • Follow the landing zone pattern: keep the hierarchy flat with platform, landing zones, sandbox and decommissioned branches, and use management group views to roll up usage costs.
