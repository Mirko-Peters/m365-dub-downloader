Support agents waste time hunting through old emails and spreadsheets while customers wait, and different agents give different answers. This episode shows customer service teams how Dynamics 365 Knowledge Management turns scattered know-how into approved, searchable articles inside daily case work. You will learn how articles are created, reviewed, found and kept current.

In this episode

  • 00:00 – The problem: answers in people's heads
  • 00:33 – Why teams need a shared knowledge base
  • 03:24 – What Dynamics 365 Knowledge Management is
  • 06:26 – The life of a knowledge article
  • 10:03 – Finding knowledge while helping a customer
  • 13:27 – Keeping knowledge accurate, safe and useful
  • 16:10 – An end-to-end example
  • 17:48 – Conclusion: one shared answer

Key takeaways

  • A shared knowledge base in Dynamics 365 Customer Service gives every support agent the same approved answers, so customers get consistent responses on every channel.
  • Knowledge articles move through a lifecycle of drafting, labelling, expert review, approval, publishing to the right audience and regular updates.
  • Clear titles, an owner per article, permissions and feedback from agents and search misses keep the knowledge base useful.

Worth quoting

“If one agent explains a return policy one way, and another gives a different version, the customer doesn't see an internal knowledge problem. They see a company that can't agree with itself.” – Mirko Peters (01:46)

“A knowledge base becomes the company's working memory, keeping useful know how available when someone takes leave, changes jobs, joins the team, or simply forgets a detail during a busy afternoon.” – Mirko Peters (02:48)
