Generative pages promise AI-built UIs in Power Apps, but how do you get from a quick demo to something an enterprise can maintain? Microsoft MVP Sara Lagerquist explains when to extend model-driven apps with generative pages, why the code-first route beats the maker portal, and how governance and process design keep citizen-built apps under control.

In this episode

  • 00:00 – Intro and Sara's journey from CRM admin to MVP
  • 02:41 – Why Power Platform and model-driven apps
  • 05:22 – Generative pages inside model-driven apps
  • 08:04 – Maker portal vs. coding tools like VS Code
  • 15:14 – Prompting, documentation and maintainability
  • 21:30 – New data sources, form embedding and PCF
  • 26:52 – Governance, citizen development and the CoE
  • 34:56 – Community Sweden, speaking and career advice

Key takeaways

  • Generative pages let you keep the out-of-the-box strengths of a model-driven app and extend only the UI where it falls short.
  • For production-ready generative pages, build them through a coding tool such as VS Code with GitHub Copilot and Microsoft's skill rather than the maker portal, which is best for trying things out.
  • As AI makes app creation fast, the hard parts become process design, adoption and governance, so makers should invest in designing processes first.

Worth quoting
