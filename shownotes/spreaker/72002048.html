.NET developers face a crowded landscape of desktop, web and service technologies, from WPF to ASP.NET Core and gRPC. In this conversation Microsoft MVP Gabor Ruzsinszki helps developers and architects decide when each technology fits, how to approach microservices, and how to use AI assistants without losing core skills.

In this episode

  • 04:46 – Why WPF still matters
  • 08:50 – How modern .NET has evolved
  • 17:51 – Moving to ASP.NET Core and minimal APIs
  • 23:40 – Architecture: documentation, monoliths and microservices
  • 29:44 – Favorite .NET features and performance gains
  • 33:10 – gRPC versus REST
  • 42:50 – AI coding assistants, hot takes and career advice

Key takeaways

  • WPF remains a solid choice for Windows-only desktop apps, while .NET MAUI or other cross-platform frameworks fit when macOS and other platforms must be supported.
  • Start with a well-structured monolith and move to microservices only when scale, headcount or explicit requirements justify the extra effort.
  • gRPC with Protocol Buffers suits fast backend-to-backend communication across languages, while REST stays the natural choice for browser front ends.

Worth quoting

“the most important part of any architecture is documenting your concepts and trying to make it understandable and approachable for the team members.” – Gabor Ruzsinszki (23:50)

“if you are a startup and you are developing an application, then it's an overkill to start with microservices from the beginning because they have a lot of additional effort associated with them.” – Gabor Ruzsinszki (25:55)
