Excel has been around for 40 years, yet Google Sheets now matches or beats it in several areas that many Microsoft-centric teams overlook. In this conversation Excel MVP and comedian David Benaim compares tables, formulas, collaboration, scripting and AI assistants in both tools to help analysts and business users choose the right spreadsheet for each job.

In this episode

  • 00:00 – Meet David Benaim, the Excel comedian
  • 06:47 – Is Excel dead? A short history of spreadsheets
  • 12:34 – Google Sheets vs. Excel: tables, Power Query and collaboration
  • 21:30 – AI in spreadsheets: Copilot, Claude and Gemini
  • 28:11 – Formulas, VBA, Office Scripts and Apps Script
  • 33:08 – From Power Pivot to Power BI
  • 42:35 – Spreadsheet bugs, comedy and the future of spreadsheets
  • 50:49 – Rapid-fire round

Key takeaways

  • Neither tool simply wins: Excel leads with Power Query, Power Pivot and the Microsoft ecosystem, while Google Sheets has more functions, group-by views and friction-free collaboration across organizations.
  • AI is reshaping spreadsheets fast, from Copilot's editing agent and Claude's Excel add-in to Gemini features in Google Sheets such as smart fill and canvas views.
  • Spreadsheets will still exist in ten years, and the most valuable habit to adopt now is using proper tables in either Excel or Google Sheets.

Worth quoting

“spreadsheets in general allow for that flexibility that I think the office employee is going to want for the very, very foreseeable future.” – David Benaim (07:53)

“I think that Excel has power query and power query is absolutely game-changing. And for that reason, until Google sheets has an equivalent, I will favor Excel.” – David Benaim (14:54)
