Turning on Microsoft 365 Copilot for everyone can turn a decade of loose SharePoint permissions into a data exfiltration engine. This episode is for IT leaders and architects who need a practical governance model for Copilot, from prompt injection risks and risk-tiered rollout to Purview, DLP and oversharing cleanup. Learn how to make Copilot secure, auditable and worth its license cost.

  • 50:38 – Roadmap, exceptions, culture and the 90-day plan

Key takeaways

  • Copilot respects permissions, so a decade of oversharing in SharePoint and Teams becomes an AI exposure problem that must be remediated before licenses are assigned.
  • Roll out in risk tiers and enforce boundaries with Microsoft Purview: sensitivity labels, adaptive scopes, label inheritance and interaction-level DLP for prompts and responses.
  • Prove realized value with A/B tests and capacity metrics, govern citizen-built agents and exceptions explicitly, and build a governance-aware culture with champions.
