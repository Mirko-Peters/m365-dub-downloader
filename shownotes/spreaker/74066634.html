Everyone wants Copilot and agents, but fragmented data and weak governance undermine trustworthy answers. Mirko Peters talks with Walter Calcagno about Microsoft Fabric, semantic models, ontology layers, metadata and his AI readiness approach. Data architects, BI teams and decision makers learn what must exist between the lakehouse and a reliable AI experience.

  • 30:20 – Medallion layers and data ready for AI
  • 37:39 – AI readiness assessment and the seven-layer model
  • 48:07 – Governance, Fabric Apps and rapid fire

Key takeaways

  • AI on enterprise data starts with business questions, clear KPI definitions and governance, not with a chat prompt or a specific tool.
  • In Microsoft Fabric, semantic models and an ontology layer above them give LLMs a governed, cost-efficient path to the right data, guided by good metadata.
  • Medallion architecture is flexible: use as many layers as needed, at least two, and prepare specific data for AI to keep token costs under control.
