Managing laptops and phones by hand or through on-premises servers no longer works when people work from anywhere on their own devices. This episode is for IT beginners and decision-makers and explains how Intune enrolls and secures devices, protects data on personal phones and feeds Conditional Access for Zero Trust.

Key takeaways

  • Microsoft Intune is a cloud service that enrolls devices, enforces configuration and compliance policies, deploys apps and tracks device health across Windows, macOS, iOS, Android and some Linux devices.
  • Mobile Application Management protects work data inside apps like Outlook, Teams and OneDrive on personal phones, so IT can wipe corporate data without touching personal content.
  • Intune reports device health to Entra ID, where Conditional Access combines identity and device signals into Zero Trust access decisions.

Worth quoting

“Intune is not a VPN, not antivirus software, and not a remote desktop tool. It's a device management platform.” – Mirko Peters (01:08)

“Intune's answer to this is something called Mobile Application Management, or MAM for short. Instead of managing the whole device, Intune manages only the work apps.” – Mirko Peters (05:47)
