Why do AI agents that shine in demos fall apart on long, real-world tasks? This episode explains the agent harness, the system around the model that adds memory, tools, orchestration and guardrails, and shows how Microsoft Foundry and the Agent Framework provide it. For architects and developers who want to move from clever prompts to reliable production agents.

  • 09:28 – Claw-Style Agents and Hermes
  • 11:15 – Agents in Teams and Copilot
  • 13:16 – Responsible AI and Governance

Key takeaways

  • An AI agent is a model plus a harness, and the harness supplies the agent loop, context management, tools, memory, guardrails and orchestration.
  • Microsoft Foundry delivers the harness as a managed service, including Entra agent identities, enterprise connectors, memory and one-click publishing to Microsoft 365 Copilot and Teams.
  • Guardrails such as step limits, tool restrictions, human approval gates and audit trails turn powerful agents into safe, governable systems.
