Hard-coded business logic and year-long IT backlogs have turned enterprise development into a liability. This episode is for IT leaders, architects and Power Platform teams who want to scale low-code without chaos. You will learn how citizen architects, zoned governance and agentic AI turn low-code into the new enterprise standard.

In this episode

  • 00:00 – The 18-month development cycle as a liability
  • 00:48 – The economic collapse of legacy development
  • 03:37 – From tactical builder to citizen architect
  • 07:03 – The zoned governance strategy: green, yellow and red
  • 10:12 – Agentic AI and the post-application era
  • 13:18 – Retiring legacy debt and the 90-day roadmap

Key takeaways

  • The real cost of traditional development is the waiting: a process stuck in a long IT backlog keeps losing money every month, while low-code solutions can deliver the same requirements far faster and cheaper.
  • Citizen development scales safely only when professional architects build the guardrails, reusable components and secure connectors, and governance is organized in green, yellow and red zones with automated DLP enforcement.
  • With autonomous agents built in tools like Copilot Studio, the architect becomes the human in the loop who defines intent, monitors behavior and keeps business logic exposed through clean data and APIs.
