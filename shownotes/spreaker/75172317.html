Finance teams have ERP data, Power BI reports, Excel everywhere and now AI agents, yet agents do not automatically understand what revenue or margin means in their business. This conversation with Rishi Sapra is for data, BI and finance leaders who want reliable AI answers. It explains how semantic models, ontologies, skills and automated governance in Microsoft Fabric and Copilot Studio ground agents in business context.

  • 38:49 – Persona-driven insights and storytelling with AI
  • 50:10 – Agents, governance and the operating model

Key takeaways

  • Before adding AI agents to financial data, document the business context: personas, decisions and the questions they need answered.
  • Certified semantic models provide deterministic KPI logic, while ontologies, skills, tools and governance add the context agents need to explain why.
  • As agents become more capable, governance has to be automated into the context layer and managed through a hub-and-spoke operating model with a center of excellence.

Worth quoting
