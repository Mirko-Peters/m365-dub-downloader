Employees are pasting proprietary data into public AI tools, and regulations like the EU AI Act and GDPR are tightening the walls around where AI may run. This episode is for architects and IT leaders in Microsoft-centric enterprises who need AI that stays inside their boundary. Learn how private LoRA adapters, quantization and federated training deliver specialized, governable AI at a fraction of the cost.

Key takeaways

  • Shadow AI use of public chatbots leaks proprietary context that Microsoft 365 Copilot's tenant protections do not cover, creating a sovereignty gap that is an architecture problem rather than a compliance one.
  • LoRA adapters on frozen open-weight base models, combined with quantization, make private fine-tuning feasible on a single GPU and let one base model serve a governed library of department-specific specialists.
  • Start with one high-volume, sensitive, narrow task inside your existing Azure boundary, prove it with A/B tests, then grow a governed model zoo integrated with Power Platform, Azure ML and Azure AD.
