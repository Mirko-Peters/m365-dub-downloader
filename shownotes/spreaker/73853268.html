Most professionals use PowerPoint every day but miss features that have existed for years. In this interview, PowerPoint MVP Fiona Walsh shows presenters, template owners and Microsoft 365 users which fundamentals, template practices and Copilot techniques make presentations faster to build and clearer to deliver.

In this episode

  • 00:00 – Intro: why PowerPoint is underestimated
  • 01:00 – Fiona's path from chemistry to PowerPoint
  • 04:38 – What professionals misunderstand about PowerPoint
  • 08:30 – Fundamentals: alignment tools, grouping and the Selection Pane
  • 18:20 – SmartArt, shortcuts and the Quick Access Toolbar
  • 26:40 – Corporate templates that work
  • 33:30 – Copilot in PowerPoint: strengths and limits
  • 45:00 – Delivery tools, storytelling and quick-fire round

Key takeaways

  • Many PowerPoint problems come from using one deck for different purposes: a handout read as a PDF needs a different design from slides that support a live speaker.
  • Long-standing fundamentals such as the alignment tools, grouping, the Selection Pane, SmartArt conversion and the Quick Access Toolbar save time even for experienced users.
  • Copilot and Designer work best with a robust corporate template with many layouts and image placeholders, and Copilot is especially useful for structuring ideas, finding hooks and endings, and anticipating audience questions.

Worth quoting

“People don't tend to realize that one deck doesn't suit all different applications. And that's why PowerPoint gets the bad name.” – Fiona Walsh (03:32)

“If it's a slide that I haven't created, the first thing I'd want to do is click on the selection pane because then I can see how the slide's been put together.” – Fiona Walsh (11:33)
