Training is scattered across SharePoint folders, old portals and email links, so employees never find it and managers cannot track it. This episode explains Viva Learning for HR, L&D and Microsoft 365 admins. You learn where its content comes from, how managers assign courses and five steps to switch it on today.

In this episode

  • 00:00 – Intro: where does training live?
  • 00:37 – The learning problem
  • 01:52 – What Viva Learning actually is
  • 03:52 – Content source 1: Microsoft's own library
  • 05:09 – Content source 2: LinkedIn Learning
  • 06:34 – Content source 3: Your own SharePoint content
  • 08:10 – Manager view: assignments and tracking
  • 11:29 – Your next steps

Key takeaways

  • Viva Learning is a learning hub inside Microsoft Teams that is included in most Microsoft 365 E3, E5 and Business Premium plans and only needs to be turned on.
  • It combines Microsoft's own library, LinkedIn Learning and your own SharePoint training content into one catalog, with up to 1,000 SharePoint files without extra licensing.
  • Managers can assign and track courses directly in Teams, which removes friction and drives much higher completion rates.

Worth quoting

“This fragmentation is the real reason learning programs fail. Not the quality of the courses, not the relevance of the topics, but the sheer friction of getting to them.” – Mirko Peters (01:32)

“The key insight is simple. When learning is easy to find and easy to assign, completion rates go up. Not because the content is better, but because the system stops getting in the way.” – Mirko Peters (09:25)
