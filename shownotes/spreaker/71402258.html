Multi-tenant organizations often assume one vendor and one identity estate mean one AI control story, but Copilot exposes every governance gap between tenants. This episode is for IT leaders, architects and security teams rolling out Microsoft 365 Copilot across several tenants. You will learn why manual per-tenant governance fails and how a hub-and-spoke model with clear rollout gates lets you scale safely.

In this episode

  • 02:59 – Why manual per-tenant governance collapses at scale
  • 05:55 – Why identity does not solve governance
  • 08:52 – The model that works: hub and spoke
  • 13:22 – What leaders should measure before they scale
  • 17:05 – The two patterns leaders keep missing

Key takeaways

  • Multiple Microsoft 365 tenants are separate governance systems: Copilot works within one tenant boundary, and shared identity or cross-tenant settings do not make policies, labels or audit consistent.
  • A federated hub-and-spoke model works best: the hub owns policy design, audit baselines, label standards and rollout gates, while spoke teams remediate inside each tenant.
  • Before scaling Copilot, measure oversharing reduction, observability coverage, time to policy across tenants, label coverage and access drift, and pause any tenant that cannot prove logging, oversharing reviews and label baselines.
