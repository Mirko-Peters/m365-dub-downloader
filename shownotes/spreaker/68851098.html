When a regulator asks for the approved, redacted contract with full lineage, most Microsoft 365 tenants drown in folders and duplicate versions. This episode is for admins and compliance leads who want SharePoint and Microsoft Purview to work as one defense system. You get a practical runbook for labels, DLP, retention and eDiscovery that makes a five-minute audit realistic.

In this episode

  • 00:00 – Red alert: building an audit-ready ECM
  • 00:38 – The problem: document chaos and audit failures
  • 04:07 – The imperial archive pattern: sites, libraries, content types and metadata
  • 09:10 – Law and order: sensitivity labels, retention and DLP
  • 14:27 – The audit crucible: eDiscovery and compliance monitoring
  • 19:58 – Maintenance and future readiness: governance as crew discipline
  • 25:22 – Takeaways and call to action

Key takeaways

  • Replace folder sprawl with a fortress pattern: one site per mission, one library per vault, central content types and a small set of required metadata.
  • Sensitivity labels, retention labels and DLP only protect content when they are tied to encryption and enforcement, rolled out in simulation first and scoped by admin units.
  • Audit readiness comes from routine: pre-staged eDiscovery templates, Compliance Manager evidence, access reviews and a recurring governance sprint.

Worth quoting

“Folder first thinking assumes humans remember paths. They don't. No metadata means no beacons. Broken inheritance leaks permissions down forgotten warrants.” – Mirko Peters (01:47)

“A label without enforcement is a banner in the wind. We tie it to encryption and policy so it defends, not decorates.” – Mirko Peters (09:36)
