Buying Copilot licenses is easy; making people genuinely more effective with them is not. Microsoft MVP Adrian Espes shares what he sees in real adoption projects: prompting habits, data protection, a pragmatic rollout path and why efficiency is a better goal than productivity. For IT leaders and adoption teams planning or rescuing a Microsoft 365 Copilot rollout.

Key takeaways

  • Copilot's biggest advantage is that it lives inside Microsoft 365 tools and works with your emails, meetings and documents under enterprise data protection.
  • Good prompting is learned by practice: be clear about the goal, add context, and tailor prompt guidance to each department.
  • Before rollout, decide whether free Copilot Chat is enough, review tenant settings such as Anthropic models under GDPR, and talk about efficiency rather than productivity.
