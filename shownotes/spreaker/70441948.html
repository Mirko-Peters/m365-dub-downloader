Enterprises drowning in disconnected identity systems, security tools and citizen-built apps don't need another tool, they need someone who understands how the Microsoft stack connects. This episode is for architects, consultants and mid-career IT professionals who want to see why integration with Entra ID as the control plane beats point solutions, and how to prove that thinking with certifications and portfolio work.

Key takeaways

  • The Microsoft advantage is integration, not individual tools: Entra ID is the control plane, and Power Platform, Azure, Fabric, Defender and Purview inherit its governance.
  • Case studies on identity consolidation, Power Platform CoE, Sentinel and Fabric, Intune with Conditional Access and licensing show that consolidating fragmented tools cuts cost and makes governance deterministic.
  • SC-100, MS-102 and PL-600 form one governance architecture; backed by a portfolio of real designs, they signal the orchestration thinking that enterprises pay a premium for.
