As organizations create hundreds or thousands of AI agents, IT faces the question of who owns, secures and governs them. In this interview, Thomas Zou from the Microsoft Copilot Studio team explains for IT leaders and admins how zoned governance, lifecycle management, Entra and Purview help scale agents safely without slowing innovation.

In this episode

  • 00:00 – Intro and Thomas Zou's journey
  • 05:06 – What is agent governance?
  • 07:46 – Where governance begins and readiness
  • 12:09 – Zone governance: innovation vs. control
  • 16:15 – Copilot Studio controls and agent ownership
  • 21:37 – Lifecycle management and monitoring
  • 30:16 – Entra, Purview and securing sensitive data
  • 41:27 – Compliance, adoption and what's next

Key takeaways

  • Agent governance should enable makers to innovate safely rather than block them, with IT acting as an enabling function.
  • Zoned governance with Power Platform environments, Agent 365, Entra and Purview lets organizations match controls to each agent's risk.
  • Successful agent adoption combines an AI center of excellence, executive sponsorship, champions and automated, self-service guidance.

Worth quoting

“It's no longer a realistic expectation for IT to be the gatekeeper to manually approve or deny everything. They cannot have one-on-one meetings with every innovator every day to walk them through the best practices.” – Thomas Zou (26:34)

“The one big best practice is never allow sensitive data to be accessed through agent that is unauthenticated, right? If you want to make sure that you expose sensitive data to specific people, then authentication needs to come in place with a lot of scrutiny.” – Thomas Zou (38:10)
