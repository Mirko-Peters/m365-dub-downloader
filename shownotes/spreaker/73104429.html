Every Microsoft 365 tenant already has email protection, but few admins know where it stops. This episode breaks down Exchange Online Protection's three filtering layers, the zero-hour auto purge safety net and the gaps attackers exploit. Small and mid-sized businesses learn when EOP is enough and when Defender for Office 365 is worth the upgrade.

  • 09:18 – Limits and Defender for Office 365
  • 14:27 – How it all fits together

Key takeaways

  • Exchange Online Protection is included in every Microsoft 365 plan and filters spam, known malware and basic phishing through connection filtering, content filtering, signatures and authentication checks.
  • Zero-hour auto purge retroactively removes messages whose verdict changes after delivery, now also in Teams chats and the Deleted Items folder.
  • EOP has no sandboxing, click-time link scanning or impersonation protection, so organizations with visible executives should add Defender for Office 365 and publish a strict DMARC record.
