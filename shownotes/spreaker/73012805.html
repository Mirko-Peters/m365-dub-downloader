A stolen password should not be enough to walk into your Microsoft 365 tenant. This episode explains Conditional Access in plain English for admins and IT decision makers: the signals it checks, how policies decide, and which baseline policies to deploy first. You will also learn how to roll out policies safely without locking yourself out.

In this episode

  • 01:47 – Why passwords aren't enough

  • 05:23 – How policies work
  • 06:53 – Three baseline policies for day one
  • 09:38 – Risk and persona-based policies
  • 11:57 – From security defaults to custom policies

Key takeaways

  • Conditional Access evaluates signals such as identity, location, device, app and sign-in risk after the password check and turns a flat yes or no into a layered decision.
  • Every tenant should start with three baseline policies: strong MFA for all users, blocking legacy authentication and compliant devices for admins.
  • Create break-glass accounts first, test new policies in report-only mode and with the What If tool, and roll them out in phases.
