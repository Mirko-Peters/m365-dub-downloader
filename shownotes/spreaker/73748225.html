Many teams still treat Copilot as a search box and blame the tool when answers come back incomplete. This episode explains agentic RAG, the orchestration loop behind modern Copilot, for IT decision makers and Microsoft 365 architects. You learn when agentic retrieval is worth its cost, when simple retrieval wins and how to govern agents that act on their own.

  • 05:21 – What agentic RAG is and the orchestration loop
  • 13:29 – Performance data and trade-offs

  • 22:33 – Agents as digital employees

  • 35:38 – Adoption, production reality and ROI

Key takeaways

  • Agentic RAG turns the language model from a passive summarizer into a decision maker that plans, retrieves from multiple sources, evaluates results and queries again.
  • Agentic RAG improves accuracy on complex multi-source tasks but costs more and runs slower, so most enterprises route simple lookups to traditional retrieval in a hybrid design.
  • Autonomous agents need identity in Entra, oversight through Purview, audit trails and enforced grounding before they can be trusted in production.

Worth quoting

“A system that's 10 times more expensive isn't better. It's better for something specific at a cost that has to be worth paying. The real question isn't which is better. It's which task deserves which architecture.” – Mirko Peters (15:28)

“So the more freedom you give the system to reason on its own the more grounding has to be enforced not less” – Mirko Peters (34:40)
