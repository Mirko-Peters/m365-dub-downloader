Attackers rarely break in anymore, they sign in, and over-privileged admin accounts make that easy. In this episode Microsoft MVP Mark Orr shows Microsoft 365 and security admins how Entra Privileged Identity Management, phishing-resistant MFA, Intune device compliance and Conditional Access work together, and how PowerShell automation makes just-in-time access practical.

  • 28:29 – Approvals, auditing, compliance and licensing
  • 38:15 – PowerShell, automation and learning with AI
  • 49:08 – Community tools and rapid-fire round

Key takeaways

  • Protect the identity plane first: move to passwordless, phishing-resistant authentication before investing in advanced device compliance or AI.
  • Entra PIM replaces standing admin rights with just-in-time, task-driven elevation, ideally with justifications, approvals and authentication context that forces MFA on every activation.
  • Combining Intune device compliance, device filtering and Conditional Access with cloud-only admin accounts creates defense in depth, and PowerShell keeps admins working when portals fail.

Worth quoting
