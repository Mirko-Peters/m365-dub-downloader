Business Central users keep hearing about Copilot and agents but rarely know what is free, what costs money and what actually does work for them. This episode walks finance and operations teams through the three layers: built-in features, Microsoft-built agents and custom agents, including Copilot credit costs and security controls.

In this episode

  • 00:00 – What Copilot in Business Central is
  • 01:29 – Free built-in Copilot features
  • 03:22 – Microsoft-built agents
  • 05:20 – Copilot credits and costs
  • 06:42 – Building custom agents
  • 08:31 – From email to order: a scenario
  • 10:13 – Security and governance

Key takeaways

  • Built-in Copilot features in Business Central such as analysis assist, autofill and bank reconciliation are included in the license at no extra cost.
  • Microsoft-built agents like the Sales Order Agent and Payables Agent act in the background but consume Copilot credits and require human approval for critical actions.
  • Custom agents can be created with the Agent Designer from plain-English instructions, and they inherit the same permission model and billing as Microsoft's agents.

Worth quoting

“The built-in Copilot features help you move through tasks faster. They give you information, they fill in fields, they suggest text, but you're still the one doing the work. Agents are different because they can act on your behalf.” – Mirko Peters (01:07)

“They use something called co-pilot credits, which is basically a usage-based currency for AI actions.” – Mirko Peters (05:41)
