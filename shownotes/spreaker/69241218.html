Executives still ping a person instead of opening the dashboard, because they want answers, not canvases. This episode is for BI, data and IT leaders working with Power BI, Fabric and Copilot in Microsoft 365. You will learn why semantic models, verified answers and identity-aware governance matter more than new reports, and how to measure decision velocity instead of adoption.

In this episode

  • 00:00 – Dashboards didn't fail, they expired
  • 02:34 – Visibility versus decisions
  • 05:47 – The hidden assumptions dashboards require
  • 09:38 – Why reporting was rational and what broke
  • 19:55 – The interface shift from canvases to intent
  • 30:50 – Scenario one: an executive question in Microsoft 365
  • 35:20 – Fabric data agents and Power BI as input, not destination
  • 45:36 – Decision velocity and owning the answer lifecycle

Key takeaways

  • Executives ask for decisions, not numbers, so the real cost of a reporting system is decision latency from question to action.
  • Power BI and Fabric semantic models become the governed meaning contract and evidence layer behind Copilot and data agent answers.
  • Trustworthy answers need data contracts, controlled query surfaces such as verified answers, identity boundaries, provenance and observability.

Worth quoting

“The measure of a dashboard is not whether it is viewed, it is whether someone can decide with it.” – Mirko Peters (05:30)
