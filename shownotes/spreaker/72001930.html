Can certifications really build a career in Power Platform, or does only real-world experience count? This episode is for makers, consultants and IT professionals planning their next step in the Microsoft ecosystem. Microsoft MVP and MCT Nathalie Leenders shares how she moved from IT service management to Power Platform consulting and how to learn for exams the right way.

In this episode

  • 00:00 – Introducing Nathalie Leenders, the badger
  • 01:38 – From SharePoint Designer and InfoPath to Power Platform
  • 08:59 – Why the community matters
  • 14:42 – Certifications: trophies or learning?
  • 26:07 – Certification paths, PL-400 and the changing exam landscape
  • 31:01 – Becoming a Microsoft Certified Trainer
  • 36:44 – Canvas apps, Dataverse, Power Automate and Copilot
  • 47:20 – Quotes, rapid fire and final career advice

Key takeaways

  • Certifications are most valuable when used as a reason to learn deeply, combining Microsoft Learn, other sources and hands-on testing, rather than memorizing practice exam answers.
  • Becoming a Microsoft Certified Trainer means learning how to teach rather than just present information, while the MVP award had the bigger career impact through community connections.
  • Low-code is still code: start with canvas apps, understand Dataverse and Power Automate, look at the bigger Microsoft picture and above all stay curious.

Worth quoting

“I think that's been the biggest, the biggest takeaway that I can give to people is be curious because IT is constantly changing.” – Nathalie Leenders (07:47)

“having a certificate itself doesn't mean that you're, that you're 100% skilled in that topic. It just means that you took the exam. But the way that I approach it is I take an exam to learn.” – Nathalie Leenders (16:12)
