Low-code and AI tools make it easy to ship automation that looks like progress while the process underneath stays broken. This episode is for leaders, makers and governance teams working with Microsoft 365, Power Automate and Copilot. You will learn how to spot process debt early and where to add friction back so it creates insight instead of waste.

In this episode

  • 00:00 – Automation scales chaos
  • 00:42 – Why frictionless automation feels right and why it breaks
  • 03:31 – Example 1: Microsoft 365 provisioning sprawl
  • 06:16 – Example 2: Power Automate approval chains
  • 09:55 – Example 3: Copilot on top of bad information architecture
  • 13:07 – Process debt and the exception rate
  • 16:30 – The 30-day fix: inject friction where it creates insight

Key takeaways

  • Frictionless automation removes the effort that used to force questions about ownership, exceptions and process design, so it often hides and scales the mess instead of fixing it.
  • Provisioning sprawl, Power Automate approval chains with dozens of exceptions and Copilot on top of bad information architecture all show the same pattern: organizations automate their inconsistencies and build up process debt.
  • Track the exception rate, give every automation a named business owner, add expiry and review rules, log exceptions by default and review new automation patterns instead of every individual build.
