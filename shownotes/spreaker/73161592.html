CI/CD pipelines were designed for human-speed decisions and are becoming the bottleneck in modern software delivery. This episode is for architects and DevOps leaders who want to understand how AI agents replace linear pipelines with an adaptive fabric, why full autonomy is a myth, and how to build governance, skills and a realistic business case for agentic DevOps.

Key takeaways

  • Traditional CI/CD pipelines are built around human approval gates and brittle scripts, which makes humans the bottleneck once infrastructure moves faster than people can reason about it.
  • Agents operate under policy rather than procedure, coordinated through patterns such as supervisor-worker and multi-agent committees, with tiered autonomy instead of full autonomy.
  • Governance must be built into the architecture through scoped agent identities, policy engines, audit logs and decision-level observability, rolled out in staged low-risk pilots with change management.
