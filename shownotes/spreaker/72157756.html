Copilot is moving from answering questions to doing the work, and many organizations are not ready for that change. In this conversation for Microsoft 365 users, IT pros and adoption leads, MVP Vesa "Vesku" Nopanen explains Copilot Cowork, Work IQ, Copilot Pages and Notebooks, and what it takes to make AI-first collaboration work.

In this episode

  • 00:00 – Introduction and Vesa's background
  • 01:45 – From early AI to AI-first collaboration
  • 08:45 – First Copilot impressions and the need for change management
  • 13:08 – Copilot Cowork as your AI teammate
  • 15:45 – Work IQ, skills and the buzzword flood
  • 20:08 – Governance, responsible AI and human decisions
  • 29:46 – Copilot Pages, Notebooks, Loop and OneNote
  • 44:39 – How Vesa uses Copilot and Cowork every day

Key takeaways

  • Copilot Cowork goes beyond chat: given goals, background and instructions it plans multi-step work and takes actions across Word, Excel, PowerPoint, PDFs, Teams and email.
  • Rolling out AI-first work needs change management, data protection with DLP and sensitivity labels, and responsible AI guidelines, not just licenses.
  • Copilot Pages and Copilot Notebooks build on Loop to turn AI conversations into shared, editable knowledge, while OneNote and Word keep their own roles.
