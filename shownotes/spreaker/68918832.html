When Microsoft 365 Copilot gives inconsistent or wrong answers, the cause is usually the content underneath, not the AI. This episode is for SharePoint, intranet and Copilot owners who want a practical information architecture blueprint covering hubs, content types, the term store, search schema and permissions. You will learn how to give Copilot a map it can ground its answers in.

In this episode

  • 00:00 – Why Copilot answers feel like lies
  • 02:44 – What information architecture really is
  • 05:28 – Why IA decides Copilot accuracy
  • 10:02 – Case file one: overshared sites
  • 13:41 – Case file two: a library without metadata
  • 17:10 – Case file three: hub sprawl and broken navigation
  • 20:59 – The blueprint: structure, semantics, governance
  • 26:35 – Downtown with Viva Connections and the grounding checklist

Key takeaways

  • Copilot accuracy depends on information architecture: hubs, content types, the term store and metadata decide what it can find and prove.
  • Permissions, sharing links and sensitivity labels are part of the map, so overshared sites let Copilot surface content to the wrong people.
  • Build structure first, then semantics, then governance, and use search verticals, refiners and result templates to promote the source of record.
