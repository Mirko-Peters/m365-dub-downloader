Microsoft 365 implementation work is becoming a commodity, and hourly rates keep falling. This episode is for consultants, architects and IT professionals who want to build a scalable Microsoft 365 business. You will learn five outcome-based models across AI agents, Entra security, governance, decision automation and productized tenants, with the pricing and positioning behind each.

In this episode

  • 02:19 – The architectural arbitrage concept
  • 07:40 – Model 1: Agentic workflow factory
  • 15:39 – Model 2: Entra-first security boutique
  • 23:16 – Model 3: Governance as a service
  • 30:20 – Model 4: Decision engine architect
  • 37:49 – Model 5: Industry tenant in a box
  • 46:19 – Pricing, sales motion, traps and the 12-month roadmap

Key takeaways

  • Hourly Microsoft 365 consulting is turning into a commodity; the growing gap between what Microsoft ships and what organizations can govern is where specialized, outcome-based practices earn premium prices.
  • The five models are an agentic workflow factory, an Entra-first security boutique, governance as a service, a decision engine architect and an industry tenant in a box, each combining a build phase with recurring revenue.
  • Success depends on deep specialization in one model and vertical, not underpricing the build phase, active customer success and a real shift from selling hours to owning outcomes.

Worth quoting

“This creates a fundamental problem, hourly consulting incentivizes you to sell more hours. It does not incentivize you to create lasting value or reduce client dependency.” – Mirko Peters (00:44)

“The gap is not technical. It is not a skills deficit. It is architectural entropy. It is the inevitable decay of governance when intent is not enforced by design.” – Mirko Peters (02:00)
