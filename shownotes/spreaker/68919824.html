Your AI agents, Power Automate flows and Copilot deployments may already be outpacing your governance. This episode is for Microsoft 365 and Power Platform admins and security leads who need a concrete 48-hour plan with DLP, PIM, Conditional Access, Defender for Cloud Apps and kill switches to regain control before shadow automation turns into data loss.

In this episode

  • 00:00 – The AI's warning: agents outpacing governance
  • 00:43 – Root cause: human inconsistency and missing guardrails
  • 04:38 – Three failure scenarios: loops, Copilot leakage, shadow automation
  • 09:24 – Minimum viable governance in five phases
  • 13:45 – Ongoing cadence, kill switches and enforcement cuts
  • 20:16 – Mapping the EU AI Act to the Microsoft stack
  • 23:40 – Final plea for structure

Key takeaways

  • Runaway agents, looping Power Automate flows and Copilot oversharing are governance failures caused by inconsistent permissions and policies, not AI rebellion.
  • A 48-hour baseline of Purview DLP connector blocks, secure agent environments, PIM and Conditional Access, red-team runbooks and audit visibility turns chaos into executable control.
  • Pre-staged kill switches, weekly drift and shadow automation reviews, and controls mapped to EU AI Act articles keep agents aligned and auditable over time.

Worth quoting

“The thing most people miss is that agent autonomy is not a switch, it's an accumulation. Every exception you approve, every temporary permission you never revoke, every environment you mark as temporary test and leave running. These become the scaffolding of irreversible behavior.” – Mirko Peters (02:46)
