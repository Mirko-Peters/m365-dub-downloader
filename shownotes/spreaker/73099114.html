Support teams lose time and customer trust when email, phone, chat and CRM live in separate tools. This Simply Explained episode is for service leaders and Microsoft 365 admins who want to understand how Dynamics 365 Customer Service connects cases, channels, Copilot and AI agents, Teams and Outlook, self-service and analytics in one platform.

In this episode

  • 00:00 – Why customer support breaks
  • 02:01 – What Dynamics 365 Customer Service is
  • 03:27 – Case management, the heart of the system
  • 05:38 – Omnichannel: one platform, every channel
  • 07:29 – AI agents and Copilot
  • 10:39 – Integration with Teams and Outlook
  • 13:25 – Self-service and knowledge management
  • 15:23 – Analytics, supervisor tools and recap

Key takeaways

  • Dynamics 365 Customer Service ties every email, chat, call and social interaction to a single case record built on Dataverse, so customers no longer repeat themselves.
  • Omnichannel routing, Copilot summaries and dedicated AI agents for case management, intent, knowledge and quality take routine work off service reps.
  • Deep Teams and Outlook integration, Power Pages self-service portals and real-time analytics turn support into one connected, measurable operation.

Worth quoting

“Most users never think about the back end. But it's what makes everything work. And it's part of the broader Microsoft ecosystem. Teams, Outlook, Power Platform, Co-Pilot, they're all connected.” – Mirko Peters (03:03)

“A case is a single customer issue. It's the central record that everything revolves around. Every email, every phone call, every chat message, it all links back to one case record.” – Mirko Peters (03:31)
