Orders, warehouse work, carriers and freight bills often live in separate spreadsheets, emails and phone calls, and that is where delays and surprise charges start. This episode is for supply chain, logistics and Dynamics 365 teams who want to understand Transportation Management from the ground up. You will learn how loads, routes, rates and freight reconciliation connect one planned movement of goods from order to invoice.

In this episode

  • 00:00 – Why goods, orders and freight bills drift apart
  • 00:50 – Inbound, outbound and transfer order transportation
  • 03:50 – Planning loads with load templates
  • 07:50 – Carriers, routes, rates and the rate route workbench
  • 11:20 – From warehouse door to customer door
  • 14:50 – Freight costs and reconciliation
  • 17:00 – Where Transportation Management fits and what it does not do

Key takeaways

  • Dynamics 365 Transportation Management is the transportation desk inside Dynamics 365 Supply Chain Management, turning sales, purchase and transfer orders into planned movements of goods.
  • Loads, load templates, routes and rate tables let planners group compatible shipments, respect vehicle limits and compare carrier services against delivery promises and cost.
  • Freight reconciliation compares carrier invoices with the expected charge before payment, while live multi-carrier rate shopping or Business Central scenarios need a third-party transportation management system.

Worth quoting

“A load is the digital record for a planned movement of goods. You can picture it as one truck, one trailer, one container, or one collection of shipments that travel together.” – Mirko Peters (04:39)
