Shared inboxes still fill with invoices, receipts and contracts that someone has to open and retype, and simple rules break as soon as layouts change. This episode explains for Power Platform makers and process owners how Content Understanding, Power Automate, Dataverse and Power Apps turn documents into trusted business data with human review where it matters.

In this episode

  • 00:00 – Introduction: documents as business data
  • 00:34 – Why documents break automation
  • 03:04 – Content Understanding simply explained
  • 07:01 – The Power Platform connection
  • 10:32 – Trust, confidence and human review
  • 13:28 – Choosing a first use case
  • 16:11 – Where this changes your work

Key takeaways

  • OCR reads characters, but Content Understanding uses a schema to return the business fields a process actually needs, such as supplier, invoice number and total.
  • Power Automate moves extracted data through the process, Dataverse tracks each record and its history, and Power Apps gives reviewers a place to fix exceptions.
  • Confidence scores and validation rules decide when a document can move on and when a person must check it, and the first use case should be one document type with one clear decision and owner.

Worth quoting
