Microsoft Mesh is surrounded by metaverse buzzwords, yet few people can explain what it does today. This Simply Explained episode is for Teams users and IT decision makers who want to know how Mesh became immersive spaces in Teams, how avatars and spatial audio work, and when a 3D meeting actually beats a video call.

In this episode

  • 00:00 – What Microsoft Mesh does today
  • 00:54 – What exactly is Microsoft Mesh
  • 02:12 – The big pivot from metaverse to Teams feature
  • 03:50 – How to join an immersive space
  • 06:22 – Spatial audio
  • 08:00 – Avatars
  • 09:14 – Custom environments and real use cases
  • 12:10 – The verdict and what's coming

Key takeaways

  • Microsoft Mesh is no longer a standalone metaverse platform; it lives on as immersive 3D spaces inside Microsoft Teams meetings.
  • Avatars and spatial audio create a sense of presence and make side conversations possible, which reduces meeting fatigue.
  • Immersive spaces are capped at 16 people with no custom worlds, so they fit interactive team meetings, onboarding and training rather than webinars.

Worth quoting

“Mesh is not a virtual world you live in. It's a meeting space you enter for specific sessions. No persistent universe, no digital real estate to buy” – Mirko Peters (01:43)

“Companies didn't want another platform to manage, since they already have Teams and Microsoft 365. They didn't want to learn a whole new tool just for a 3D meeting.” – Mirko Peters (02:48)
