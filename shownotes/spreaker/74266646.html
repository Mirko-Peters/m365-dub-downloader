Sales calls, service cases, invoices and orders bury the facts people need under notes, emails and menus. This Simply Explained episode shows business users and decision makers how Copilot in Dynamics 365 summarizes records, answers questions and prepares drafts across Sales, Customer Service, Finance and Business Central, and why human approval stays essential.

In this episode

  • 00:00 – Intro: what Dynamics 365 Copilot is
  • 00:30 – Dynamics 365 as the foundation: CRM and ERP
  • 03:26 – What Copilot actually does: summarize, answer, draft
  • 07:40 – Sales: preparing for the next customer conversation
  • 10:47 – Customer service: from long case histories to next steps
  • 13:39 – Finance, operations and Business Central
  • 16:37 – Data, security and human approval
  • 18:34 – Conclusion: the helper inside each business room

Key takeaways

  • Copilot in Dynamics 365 works inside the business records people already use, summarizing histories, answering questions about permitted data and preparing first drafts.
  • Each Dynamics 365 app (Sales, Customer Service, Finance and Operations, Business Central) has its own Copilot features and task-focused agents, distinct from Microsoft 365 Copilot.
  • Copilot follows the signed-in user's access rules, and people must review drafts and stay accountable for refunds, payments, promises and approvals.

Worth quoting

“Treat a copilot response like work prepared by a junior colleague. It may save you time, but your name still goes on the email, the case update, or the approval.” – Mirko Peters (05:53)

“A copilot helps you ask, understand, draft, and decide. An agent focuses on a defined piece of work that it can carry out on the rules set by the business.” – Mirko Peters (07:01)
