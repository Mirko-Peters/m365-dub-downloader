Many managed security offers are still sold as monitoring and end up priced like a commodity. This episode helps security providers and IT leaders pitch Microsoft security as a strategic business asset that boards will fund. Learn how to tie identity governance and response speed to ROSI, decision latency and protected revenue.

In this episode

  • 00:00 – Why security pitches fail before the second slide
  • 00:42 – The commodity trap and the old model
  • 03:10 – Reframing security as business risk velocity control
  • 06:23 – The numbers executives care about: ROSI, time to decide and revenue at risk
  • 09:58 – Scenario proof: from identity sprawl to controlled continuity
  • 13:20 – The one-page CFO model and the decision ask
  • 16:40 – Packaging and pricing the strategic offer

Key takeaways

  • Security pitches built on alerts, tools and seat counts get treated as overhead, while boards fund controlled exposure that lets the business grow and move faster.
  • Return on security investment, reduced decision latency and protected revenue translate technical metrics into language a CFO can use, ideally on a single page with a simple risk formula.
  • Identity is the control plane, so Entra ID Governance, access reviews and Conditional Access combined with Defender, Purview and Intune signals and automation create measurable continuity and should be packaged as business outcomes.

Worth quoting

“Boards don't fund tooling. They fund exposure inside a growth target and in 2026 that gap gets wider.” – Mirko Peters (00:15)
