Copilot sounds confident but is often wrong because it never sees the systems where your real knowledge lives. This episode is for architects and Microsoft 365 admins planning or rescuing a Copilot rollout. You get a clear framework for choosing graph connectors, plugins or federated MCP connectors, plus a checklist to act on this week.

Key takeaways

  • Copilot hallucinates on business questions mainly because it cannot see line-of-business systems, so closing the grounding gap is an architecture decision, not a prompting problem.
  • Use graph connectors for knowledge and plugins for actions: indexed content inherits Microsoft 365 compliance controls and Entra ID ACLs, while custom RAG middleware adds latency and a parallel security perimeter.
  • Clean up source permissions, design business-readable schemas, migrate off the deprecated Graph Security connector and pilot with a small cohort before rolling connectors out tenant-wide.

Worth quoting

“Plugins are for actions. Connectors are for knowledge. Most teams collapse that distinction early and spend the next year trying to recover from it.” – Mirko Peters (07:26)

“Policies govern behavior. Architecture governs what's structurally possible. A breach that flows through an ungoverned retrieval pipeline doesn't care how good your policy documentation is.” – Mirko Peters (18:22)
