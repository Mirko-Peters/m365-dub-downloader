Organizations invest heavily in Microsoft 365, Power Platform and Copilot, yet many solutions still struggle with adoption. This episode is for app makers, adoption leads and change managers, and shows with MVP Sandra Kiel which game design principles make business apps and Copilot rollouts motivating instead of frustrating.

In this episode

  • 00:00 – Why enterprise apps struggle with adoption
  • 01:00 – Sandra's path from SAP to EventPunks and Microsoft
  • 07:01 – Team building in Minecraft
  • 10:02 – Why nobody wants to use your app
  • 20:04 – Copilot adoption as behavior change and habit loops
  • 25:05 – Game mechanics for adoption programs and game designers
  • 39:08 – Orientation, purpose and feedback in Power Apps
  • 47:09 – Communities, speaking, women in tech and rapid-fire round

Key takeaways

  • Business apps fail on adoption when they ignore the human side; structure, progress visibility and clear feedback borrowed from games improve both motivation and data quality.
  • Copilot adoption is a behavior change that needs habit loops, individualized challenges, points, competition and social elements working together rather than one-off trainings.
  • App makers can start with orientation, objective and progress, explain the purpose of a process, and consider adding a game designer to design the experience.

Worth quoting
