Many organizations rush into agentic AI without knowing which AI is already used in their tenant or what the EU AI Act demands. Microsoft MVP Areti Iles, head of professional services at Telefónica, shares how Dynamics 365 and Power Platform projects succeed, how to govern AI with Defender, Purview and Foundry, and why community and mentorship accelerate careers.

  • 27:07 – Explaining agentic AI
  • 35:05 – AI legislation, EU AI Act and Microsoft tools
  • 51:09 – Community and mentorship
  • 59:38 – UN Commission on the Status of Women and rapid fire

Key takeaways

  • Projects rarely fail because of technology; they fail when business experts lack time, scope creeps and change management starts too late.
  • Agentic AI acts autonomously toward goals, so organizations need inventories, risk classification, human oversight and AI literacy, as the EU AI Act requires.
  • Microsoft Defender, Purview with Compliance Manager and Foundry cover security, compliance and model governance for responsible AI adoption.

Worth quoting

“a lot of the time a project won't fail because of the technology it will fail because of the people. You could have the most brilliantly technologically speaking built system.” – Areti Iles (12:28)

“it's a group of AI systems that have agency which is why we call them agentic it means that they can autonomously make decisions take actions to achieve a goal” – Areti Iles (27:22)
