Copying information between SharePoint, Teams, email and databases by hand is slow and error-prone, and custom integration code takes weeks. This episode explains Azure Logic Apps in plain English for IT pros, business analysts and Microsoft admins, covering triggers, connectors, pricing models, real examples and the shift to agentic automation.

  • 11:30 – Agentic automation and AI agents
  • 13:20 – Getting started with Logic Apps

Key takeaways

  • Azure Logic Apps is a serverless workflow service that connects systems like SharePoint, Teams, Outlook, SQL and Salesforce through triggers, actions and more than 1,400 connectors.
  • Start with the Consumption plan for low or spiky workloads and move to Standard only when high volumes make its fixed monthly price cheaper or you need dedicated compute and network control.
  • Logic Apps is evolving into an orchestration layer for AI, where natural-language workflow creation and AI agents handle exceptions that rigid workflows could not.

Worth quoting
