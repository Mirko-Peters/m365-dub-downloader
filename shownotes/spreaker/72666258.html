Sales reps, controllers and buyers still spend hours copying data between email, CRM and ERP. This episode is for business and IT leaders working with Dynamics 365 and the Power Platform, and it explains how Dataverse-grounded agents take over data entry, reconciliation and routing while staying inside your security and governance rules.

In this episode

  • 00:00 – From system of record to system of action
  • 04:13 – The agent shift and why generic AI fails in the enterprise
  • 10:01 – Sales qualification and account reconciliation agents
  • 16:54 – Customer intent, supplier communications and field service agents
  • 27:41 – Dataverse grounding and the event-reasoning-orchestration pattern
  • 34:16 – Governance with Agent 365, the agent hub and MCP
  • 40:36 – Skills shift, ROI, legacy integration and data quality
  • 55:00 – Change management, security and the four-phase roadmap

Key takeaways

  • Enterprise systems built as systems of record force people to act as manual integration layers; agents that observe events, reason over business data and orchestrate actions turn them into systems of action.
  • Dynamics 365 agents are useful because they are grounded in Dataverse and inherit the same security roles, row-level security, DLP policies, approval workflows and audit trails as the users they work for.
  • Start with one high-volume, painful process, clean the underlying data first, govern agents through Agent 365 with sponsors and approvals, and prepare people to supervise agents and handle exceptions.
