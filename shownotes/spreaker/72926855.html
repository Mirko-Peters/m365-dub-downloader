AI agents are multiplying across tenants with broad access, scattered credentials and no accountable owner. This episode shows identity, security and compliance leaders why service principals fail for AI and how Agent 365 and Microsoft Entra Agent ID, with blueprints, sponsors and conditional access, turn orphaned agents into governed digital workers.

Key takeaways

  • Service principals were built for static applications and cannot handle instancing, credential sprawl, per-agent logging or lifecycle governance for AI agents.
  • Microsoft Entra Agent ID makes agents first-class identities with blueprints that centralize credentials, mandatory human sponsors, access reviews, conditional access and agent-specific risk detection.
  • Organizations should inventory existing agents, assign sponsors, build blueprints and harden them with conditional access, Purview and Defender before auditors or incidents force the issue.

Worth quoting
