A single late shipment can empty a shelf and break a customer promise when every team works from its own spreadsheet. This episode explains Dynamics 365 Supply Chain Management for operations, planning and IT teams in manufacturing, retail and distribution. You learn how one connected record from purchasing to delivery leads to more reliable delivery dates.

Key takeaways

  • Dynamics 365 Supply Chain Management is an ERP system that connects product records, planning, purchasing, inventory, production, maintenance and transport in one shared record.
  • Inventory numbers only become useful with location and status, and barcode scanning on mobile devices keeps the digital record in step with physical movements.
  • Power BI, AI and Copilot help planners spot shortages and risks, but people still review suggestions and make the decisions.

Worth quoting

“A forecast isn't a promise. It's a planned estimate that gives the business a reason to check whether it has enough materials, enough supplier deliveries, and enough time to meet expected demand.” – Mirko Peters (05:35)

“The system brings attention to these exceptions, but a person still decides whether to change the plan, buy from another supplier or speak with the customer.” – Mirko Peters (06:56)
