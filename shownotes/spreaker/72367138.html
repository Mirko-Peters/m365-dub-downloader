Many organizations are stuck between AI experiments and measurable results. In this conversation, MVP Kayode Ajayi shows Power Platform and Copilot Studio teams, architects and IT leaders how governance, adoption and sound solution design turn AI hype into real business value.

  • 34:53 – AI readiness, governance and Copilot Studio security
  • 45:50 – AI costs, local models and quickfire round

Key takeaways

  • Low-code platforms like the Power Platform are enterprise ready when solutions are built with proper discovery, architecture and design for scalability rather than jumping straight into a blank app.
  • Measure AI investments by time saved and real adoption, and plan adoption and change management into every AI implementation because unused tools deliver no value.
  • Every AI conversation starts with governance: Copilot Studio lets you scope agents to specific knowledge sources, while broader Copilot rollouts can expose oversharing in poorly governed organizational data.

Worth quoting

“But when you shut everything down, you realize that people are actually creative and in some cases they will find other ways to do some things that would actually end up landing in the shadow IT world.” – Kayode Ajayi (13:15)

“If you implement an AI solution and people are actually spending more time, doing the same things they were doing without AI and now they take it longer to do it there, that's automatically a failure.” – Kayode Ajayi (24:20)
