Copilot and AI agents in Dynamics 365 speed up approvals, credit holds, sourcing and refunds while your controls stay green on paper. This episode is for Dynamics architects, finance and security leaders who must defend agent-assisted decisions in audits. You will learn where governance quietly drifts and which design controls restore lineage and accountability.

  • 48:20 – Mitigations, executive translation and architect deep dive

Key takeaways

  • Copilot in Dynamics 365 rarely breaks controls; it erodes their meaning by mediating what approvers see and composing actions across apps.
  • DLP, Conditional Access, least privilege and segregation of duties were built for local human actions and miss composite agent pathways through MCP, Power Automate and Graph.
  • Require decision traces, step-up on sensitive tool invocation, and ALM for prompts, tool maps and models, then test one real decision end to end.
