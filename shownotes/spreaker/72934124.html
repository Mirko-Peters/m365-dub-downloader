Copilot drafts and summarizes quickly, but you still spend your time coordinating meetings, emails and follow-ups across apps. This episode explains Copilot Cowork in plain English for Microsoft 365 users and admins: how it executes whole goals, how it differs from regular Copilot, and what you need to start delegating multi-step work.

Key takeaways

  • Copilot Cowork is an AI agent in Microsoft 365 that takes a whole goal, plans the steps and executes them across Word, Excel, PowerPoint, Outlook, Teams and SharePoint in the background.
  • Unlike chat-based Copilot, Cowork is task-based, runs for minutes or hours, picks from built-in skills automatically, supports recurring tasks and asks for approval before sensitive actions.
  • Getting started requires a Microsoft 365 business or enterprise plan, a Copilot license, files in OneDrive or SharePoint and, where needed, admin enablement via the Frontier program.
