Many organizations manage hundreds of projects with Planner, Excel and scattered tools but lack a real portfolio view of resources, budgets and risks. This episode is for PMO leaders, project managers and Power Platform makers. Microsoft MVP Erik van Hurck explains how Planner, the Power Platform and AI come together to build enterprise project and portfolio management.

In this episode

  • 00:00 – Welcome and Erik van Hurck's journey into PPM
  • 04:56 – The biggest project management challenges in organizations
  • 12:00 – Planner, Planner Premium, Project and To Do explained
  • 19:43 – Agile versus waterfall and the multi-tool landscape
  • 22:40 – Projectum xPM on the Power Platform
  • 31:20 – Programs, portfolios and the enterprise PMO
  • 36:00 – How AI is changing project management
  • 47:58 – Governance, quick-fire round and advice for PMO leaders

Key takeaways

  • Microsoft Planner is an excellent task management tool, but enterprise project and portfolio management needs program, portfolio, financial, risk and lessons-learned layers on top.
  • The Power Platform with Dataverse, Power Apps, Power Automate and Power BI provides the flexible foundation for building and tailoring enterprise PPM solutions such as Projectum xPM.
  • AI can draft documents, surface lessons learned and act as a PMO assistant, but project managers stay accountable and AI access should start from a zero-permission mindset.
