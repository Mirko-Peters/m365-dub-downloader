Most people sign in to work apps several times a day without typing a password and never wonder why. This episode explains single sign-on in plain words for beginners and business users, from identity providers and tokens to SAML, OpenID Connect and Microsoft Entra ID. You will understand how one login works and where it becomes a risk worth protecting.

Key takeaways

  • With single sign-on, apps never see your password; they trust an identity provider such as Microsoft Entra ID to vouch for you with signed tokens.
  • SAML is the older XML-based standard common in enterprise software, while OpenID Connect, built on OAuth 2.0, is the default choice for new cloud apps.
  • Because one login opens every door, stolen tokens and misconfigured apps are the main risks, so phishing-resistant MFA, sign-in monitoring and least-privilege access matter.
