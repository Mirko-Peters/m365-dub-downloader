Cloud PCs, Intune and agentic AI are reshaping how enterprise IT manages endpoints. Christiaan Brinkhoff, who helped build Azure Virtual Desktop and Windows 365 at Microsoft, explains for IT pros and decision makers how these platforms fit together and where AI agents, MCP and Graph are taking endpoint management. Learn which decisions keep your foundation future-proof.

  • 53:52 – Intune, Graph and MCP
  • 69:20 – AI hardware, NPUs and cost
  • 77:39 – Rapid fire and closing advice

Key takeaways

  • Azure Virtual Desktop is the PaaS option with full control, while Windows 365 is the SaaS Cloud PC that democratizes VDI, and both can be combined.
  • Endpoint management is moving toward task-specific AI agents with tightly scoped permissions and agentic identities, with admins orchestrating rather than clicking.
  • MCP layered on top of Graph and a unified Copilot experience are seen as the path to more autonomous management, but training and change management are lagging behind.
