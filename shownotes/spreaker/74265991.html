Many sales teams track deals in scattered notes and spreadsheets, so nobody knows what will really close. This episode walks sellers and sales managers through opportunity management in Dynamics 365 Sales, from lead qualification through the four pipeline stages to closing as won or lost. You will learn how to keep a deal record your team and your forecast can trust.

In this episode

  • 00:00 – Lead vs. opportunity
  • 00:26 – What an opportunity actually is
  • 03:10 – How opportunities enter Dynamics 365 Sales
  • 06:14 – The four-stage sales pipeline
  • 11:01 – Building a deal record you can trust
  • 14:37 – Winning, losing and learning
  • 17:54 – Conclusion: keep the deal folder current

Key takeaways

  • An opportunity is one potential sale linked to an account or contact, created by qualifying a lead or directly for existing customers.
  • The qualify, develop, propose and close stages make sure budget, decision maker, stakeholders and internal review are covered before a deal is ready to close.
  • Keeping revenue, close dates and probability current and closing deals promptly as won or lost keeps the forecast honest.

Worth quoting

“A lead is early interest, but an opportunity is a sales conversation with enough shape to track as a possible deal” – Mirko Peters (00:07)

“If those dates are old or the amounts are guesses, the forecast becomes a neat looking screen full of bad information” – Mirko Peters (02:45)
