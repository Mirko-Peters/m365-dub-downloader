Many teams treat Azure Advisor as a passive list of suggestions they ignore. This episode is for Azure admins and beginners and explains its five pillars, how the Advisor score is calculated and how to act on high-impact recommendations to cut costs and improve security and reliability.

In this episode

  • 00:00 – What Azure Advisor is
  • 01:14 – How Advisor works and its five pillars
  • 03:00 – Cost optimization
  • 04:18 – Security
  • 04:58 – Reliability
  • 07:21 – Performance
  • 09:12 – Operational excellence
  • 11:02 – The Advisor score and getting started

Key takeaways

  • Azure Advisor is free and built into the Azure portal; it compares your resources against the Azure Well-Architected Framework and gives personalized recommendations across cost, security, reliability, performance and operational excellence.
  • Recommendations carry high, medium or low impact, and the Advisor score from 0 to 100% shows per pillar where your environment falls short so you can tackle the lowest score and high-impact items first.
  • Pairing Advisor with Azure Policy and a regular weekly or monthly review cadence turns one-time fixes into ongoing compliance.
