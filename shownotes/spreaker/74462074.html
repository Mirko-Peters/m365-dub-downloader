Purchasing often runs through emails, spreadsheets and paper delivery notes, so nobody can say who approved a purchase or whether an invoice is right. This episode explains for beginners how Dynamics 365 Procurement and Sourcing connects requests, vendors, purchase orders, receipts, invoice matching and payment into one traceable process.

In this episode

  • 00:00 – Introduction
  • 00:35 – What procurement and sourcing covers
  • 03:15 – From internal request to the right vendor
  • 06:59 – The purchase order
  • 11:00 – Receiving goods and checking the invoice
  • 15:05 – Controls, visibility and better vendor decisions
  • 18:25 – Conclusion: one connected record

Key takeaways

  • Sourcing chooses the right vendor, while procurement manages the request, approval, purchase order, receipt, invoice and payment in one source-to-pay process.
  • In Dynamics 365 Supply Chain Management a purchase starts as a requisition, can go through an RFQ and becomes a purchase order backed by vendor records, agreements and approval workflows.
  • Product receipts and three-way matching against the purchase order and invoice catch differences before payment, and the connected record enables spend analysis, vendor evaluation and a full audit trail.

Worth quoting

“Procurement and sourcing might sound like a long name for buying things, and it is about buying, but the two words describe different jobs. Sourcing is the work of finding and choosing the right vendor.” – Mirko Peters (00:37)

“A purchase requisition is an internal request to buy something. It tells the company what a person needs, how much, and often where it should go. Notice that this request doesn't promise anything to a vendor yet.” – Mirko Peters (03:34)
