Most companies discover they are overspending on cloud only when the invoice lands. This episode explains FinOps in plain language for beginners, IT pros and finance teams: its principles, its inform-optimize-operate loop and the tools already inside Microsoft 365 and Azure. You will learn how to start with one alert and one owner, and which mistakes to avoid.

  • 09:06 – FinOps tools in Microsoft 365 and Azure
  • 11:37 – Mistakes that sink a FinOps practice
  • 12:58 – What FinOps cannot fix

Key takeaways

  • FinOps is a shared cultural practice between the people who build with technology and the people who pay for it, aimed at getting the most value rather than only cutting costs.
  • The practice runs on six principles and a repeating loop of inform, optimize and operate, with visibility through tagging and showback as the foundation.
  • Microsoft Cost Management, Azure Advisor, commitment discounts and the Microsoft 365 admin center give you the starting tools, but only ownership and habits keep the savings.
