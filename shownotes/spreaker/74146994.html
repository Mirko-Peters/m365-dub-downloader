Sales teams rarely lack data, but they struggle to see which follow-up or deal matters most right now. In this Simply Explained episode, Mirko Peters walks sellers and sales managers through Dynamics 365 Sales Insights, from the assistant and auto capture to predictive scoring, relationship intelligence, conversation intelligence and forecasting.

In this episode

  • 00:00 – Intro: lost follow-ups and the sales coach
  • 00:50 – Why sales teams need insights, not more data
  • 03:07 – Assistant, auto capture and email engagement
  • 06:15 – Predictive lead and opportunity scoring
  • 10:28 – Relationship intelligence and Who knows whom
  • 14:07 – Conversation intelligence and notes analysis
  • 17:58 – Forecasting and the connected sales picture

Key takeaways

  • Dynamics 365 Sales Insights sits on top of the CRM records in Dynamics 365 Sales and turns activity into prompts, scores and relationship signals.
  • Predictive lead and opportunity scoring helps sellers prioritise, but the scores are only as reliable as consistent historical sales data and never replace judgment.
  • Conversation intelligence, relationship analytics and forecasting improve coaching and planning when organisations set clear rules for recording, privacy and licensing.

Worth quoting

“Sales Insights is a group of AI-powered tools inside Dynamics 365 Sales that turns sales activity into guidance by surfacing prompts, scoring leads or opportunities and showing signals about customer relationships.” – Mirko Peters (02:04)

“A high score isn't a promise though. No score can tell you that a buyer has approved a budget, chosen your company, or signed a contract.” – Mirko Peters (08:16)
