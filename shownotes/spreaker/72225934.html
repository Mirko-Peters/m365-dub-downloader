Building a Copilot Studio agent takes minutes, but getting it into production in a bank or government organization takes months of governance work. Microsoft MVP Isha Kapoor shows architects, admins and Power Platform teams how she structures environments, pipelines, DLP, Purview and incident response. Learn how to scale AI agents safely without stopping innovation.

In this episode

  • 00:00 – Intro: enterprise AI beyond five-minute agent demos
  • 06:18 – Designing the ALM strategy: dev, QA and prod environments with DLP
  • 14:26 – Deployment pipelines, approvals and quarterly attestations
  • 21:01 – Incident management with agent quarantine
  • 24:53 – Security: prompt injection, Purview and responsible AI alerts
  • 31:55 – Balancing compliance and innovation with tenant isolation
  • 35:23 – Data residency, Foundry and choosing models
  • 45:33 – Fast release cycles, workflows and advice for getting started

Key takeaways

  • Scaling Copilot Studio in regulated enterprises needs a strict ALM strategy with separate dev, QA and prod environments, DLP and connector policies, pipelines and approvals before agents reach production.
  • Operations teams should be ready to quarantine an agent immediately when an incident occurs, and Purview auditing plus responsible AI checks make agent activity traceable.
  • New AI features such as MCP servers or third-party models should be vetted before they are enabled, while a separate experimentation tenant lets makers innovate without touching production data.
