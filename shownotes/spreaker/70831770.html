Your Copilot rollout looked ready, yet answers feel generic, people double-check everything and adoption stalls after a few weeks. This episode is for leaders and Microsoft 365 owners who want to understand why AI exposes broken permissions, weak classification and unclear ownership instead of fixing them. You get a five-step sequence to clean up the environment before scaling AI.

Key takeaways

  • AI in Microsoft 365 does not fix unclear ownership, messy access or duplicated content; it exposes them at machine speed.
  • Least privilege becomes relevance engineering in the AI era, because broad permissions turn stale and out-of-scope content into decision context.
  • Measure AI success by decision latency without loss of trust, and only reintroduce Copilot where access, ownership and decision flows are aligned.

Worth quoting
