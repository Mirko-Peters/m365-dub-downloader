Most organizations store contracts carefully in SharePoint but still answer every renewal, indemnity or notice question by rereading PDFs. This episode is for legal, finance, compliance and Microsoft 365 teams and shows how document processing, the SharePoint Knowledge Agent, Power BI and Power Automate turn stored contracts into cited answers without migrations or new platforms.

Key takeaways

  • Contracts already stored in SharePoint can become queryable: document processing writes parties, dates, renewal logic and clauses into library columns without moving the files.
  • The SharePoint Knowledge Agent answers natural-language questions with clause-level citations, while Purview labels, retention, DLP, audit logs and security trimming keep governing everything.
  • Start small with one library, a drop-off intake, boring column names and one evidence view, and keep humans in the loop where language is ambiguous.

Worth quoting

“Storage thinking asks, where is the file? Storage thinking optimizes for place. Answer thinking optimizes for precision. One moves boxes better. The other reduces questions.” – Mirko Peters (02:22)

“Why citations matter is simple. Trust doesn't scale on summaries. It scales on reproducibility. When the answer carries its own evidence, audit becomes a review of links.” – Mirko Peters (12:25)
