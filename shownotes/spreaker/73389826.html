Power BI projects often struggle not because of the visuals but because of weak data quality, modeling and performance foundations. In this interview, Fabric Super User and Senior Power BI Developer Thummalacherla Krishnakanth shares practical guidance for beginners and BI developers on star schemas, Power Query versus DAX, optimization, Microsoft Fabric and staying relevant in the AI era.

  • 47:05 – Certifications, community and rapid-fire round

Key takeaways

  • Many Power BI problems start before the visuals: check data quality and data types in Power Query or at the source, and model clean relationships from dimension to fact tables.
  • A star schema with fewer, denormalized dimension tables connected directly to fact tables is the recommended default over a snowflake schema for performance.
  • AI can help analyze and explore data, but strong fundamentals, certifications such as PL-300 via Microsoft Learn and close stakeholder contact remain what keeps data professionals relevant.

Worth quoting

“So in the means we will be having a less number of dimension tables getting directly connected to the fact tables so I would suggest to go with the star schema as a best practice.” – Thummalacherla Krishnakanth (19:24)
