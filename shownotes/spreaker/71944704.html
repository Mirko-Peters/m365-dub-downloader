Most Azure bills are inflated by idle VMs, orphaned disks and forgotten sandboxes that nobody owns anymore. This episode shows cloud and platform teams how to combine Azure Policy, tags, Resource Graph and Logic Apps into an automated cleanup engine that removes waste safely and keeps it from coming back.

In this episode

  • 00:55 – Why cloud waste keeps surviving
  • 03:35 – The governance model: policy, tags and Logic Apps
  • 06:45 – The tagging system that makes deletion possible
  • 09:55 – Building the Logic App cleanup flow
  • 13:52 – What breaks automated deletion and how to design around it
  • 17:10 – Measuring whether the engine works
  • 19:55 – Start narrow and expand

Key takeaways

  • Split the cleanup engine into three layers: Azure Policy sets the rules, tags carry the lifecycle context, and Logic Apps take the action.
  • Keep a small, standardized tag set (owner, environment, cost center, expiry, cleanup action, exception reason) and roll out policy in the order audit, modify, then deny.
  • Check locks, dependencies and soft-delete behavior before deleting, start with low-risk classes like unattached disks in dry-run mode, and track both reclaimed and prevented cost.

Worth quoting

“Policy is the law. Tags are the context. Logic apps are the action. That distinction matters because most governance designs fail by mixing all three together.” – Mirko Peters (03:41)

“The sequence of how you roll this out is everything. Audit first, then modify, then deny.” – Mirko Peters (04:25)
