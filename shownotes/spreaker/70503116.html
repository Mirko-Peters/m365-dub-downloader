Microsoft partners still competing on certifications and deployment skills are watching margins shrink as CSP changes, automation and Copilot commoditize implementation. This episode helps consultants, MSPs and ISP leaders understand why technical excellence is now table stakes and how a tenant stewardship model built on economic outcomes creates defensible, recurring revenue.

In this episode

  • 00:00 – Why deployment expertise no longer differentiates
  • 06:35 – Episodic versus structural revenue
  • 09:27 – The diagnosis: the commoditization curve
  • 13:42 – The three ISP models that are dying
  • 21:21 – The Microsoft economic steward
  • 24:44 – The tenant stewardship model and its three pillars
  • 32:10 – The mandate: what partners must do
  • 43:18 – The choice and the final statement

Key takeaways

  • CSP margin compression, the direct EA transition and automation such as Autopilot, Lighthouse and embedded Copilot are commoditizing Microsoft implementation work.
  • License resellers, migration factories and feature-driven MSPs are losing ground because they sell capabilities customers now assume or can buy anywhere.
  • Partners who build economic telemetry, architectural stewardship and AI value governance move from episodic projects to retainers and own the customer's cost-to-value relationship.

Worth quoting

“When a capability becomes expected, it stops being strategic and that transition is now complete. The market no longer values technical implementation. It values economic stewardship.” – Mirko Peters (03:10)

“If you deploy co-pilot into a company that hasn't redesigned its workflows, adoption will stay low and no value will be created.” – Mirko Peters (19:07)
