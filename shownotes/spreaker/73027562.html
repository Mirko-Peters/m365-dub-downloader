Most in-app search returns the wrong results because it matches words, not meaning. This episode helps developers and architects understand how Azure AI Search combines keyword, vector and hybrid search to power better search experiences and grounded RAG assistants.

In this episode

  • 00:00 – Intro: why app search disappoints
  • 00:50 – Why keyword search breaks
  • 02:11 – Vector search and embeddings
  • 04:46 – Hybrid search and Reciprocal Rank Fusion
  • 06:43 – The RAG connection
  • 08:49 – Real-world use cases
  • 10:52 – Data source, indexer and search index
  • 12:29 – Practical next steps

Key takeaways

  • Keyword search such as BM25 is precise for exact terms but misses meaning, while vector search with embeddings finds semantically similar content but can miss exact matches.
  • Azure AI Search combines both in hybrid search, merging results with Reciprocal Rank Fusion, and serves as the retrieval layer for RAG-based assistants and agents.
  • Its building blocks are the data source, the indexer and the search index, and it delivers the most value on unstructured content such as PDFs, emails and support tickets.

Worth quoting

“Keyword search understands spelling. It understands character patterns, but it does not understand meaning. You get results that have the right letters, but often the wrong content.” – Mirko Peters (01:59)

“An embedding is a special type of vector that captures the meaning of text. It takes a sentence, a paragraph, even a whole document, and turns it into a long list of numbers.” – Mirko Peters (02:29)
