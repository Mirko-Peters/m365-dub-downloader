Manual, ticket-based governance cannot keep up with thousands of low-code apps, so makers move into the shadows. This episode shows Power Platform admins and IT leaders how environment routing, automated DLP guardrails and a modern Center of Excellence let speed and security coexist.

In this episode

  • 00:00 – The cold war between IT control and maker innovation
  • 00:52 – The structural failure of manual governance
  • 03:48 – Environment routing as the foundational lever
  • 06:45 – The logic of the automated guardrail
  • 09:37 – From bottleneck to platform provider
  • 12:34 – The Center of Excellence as a strategic hub
  • 15:06 – Measuring the cultural shift
  • 17:15 – Your challenge: audit, route, build community

Key takeaways

  • Saying no to makers does not remove risk; it pushes work into shadow IT and removes the visibility IT wanted to protect.
  • Environment routing into personal developer environments and DLP connector classification replace manual ticket gates with automated guardrails.
  • A Center of Excellence that acts as a capability, not a committee, uses the CoE Starter Kit and maker recognition to bring shadow solutions into governed paths.

Worth quoting

“Shadow it isn't a rebellion, but a response to a bottleneck created by the system itself. People aren't trying to be malicious, they are just trying to do their jobs and deliver results for the company.” – Mirko Peters (01:59)

“We have to stop trying to fix the default environment and start bypassing it. The solution isn't a better cleanup crew. It's a better architectural starting point.” – Mirko Peters (03:53)
