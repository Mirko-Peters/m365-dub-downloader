Your Dataverse environment works for a hundred users, then falls apart at a thousand. This episode is for Power Platform makers and architects who want to move from building apps to designing systems, using entity mapping, logic delegation and structural security plus proven patterns and a 90-day roadmap.

In this episode

  • 08:03 – From transactional to structural thinking
  • 12:39 – Three pillars: entity mapping, logic delegation, security as architecture
  • 21:51 – Thinking like an architect and skill development paths
  • 34:30 – Patterns that scale: master data, outbox, saga, reference data
  • 41:59 – Case studies and the ROI of relational design
  • 56:56 – The 90-day roadmap and a center of excellence

Key takeaways

  • Most Dataverse problems are architecture problems: duplicated tables, scattered logic and bolted-on security break systems as they scale.
  • Validation and calculation logic belongs server-side in Dataverse, while Power Automate should handle notifications, orchestration and integration.
  • Patterns such as a master data model, the transactional outbox, the saga pattern and canonical reference tables make Dataverse solutions consistent and reliable at enterprise scale.
