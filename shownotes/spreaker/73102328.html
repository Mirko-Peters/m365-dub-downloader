Most businesses buying Microsoft 365 or Azure through a partner are already using CSP without knowing how it works. This episode explains the Cloud Solution Provider program for IT buyers and Microsoft partners: direct versus indirect resellers, how partners earn money, what the new commerce experience changed, and when CSP beats an Enterprise Agreement.

In this episode

  • 00:00 – What CSP is: partners as wholesalers
  • 01:40 – Direct bill versus indirect reseller
  • 03:09 – How partners add value
  • 04:36 – The new commerce experience and term commitments
  • 06:43 – Why customers choose a CSP partner
  • 08:43 – CSP versus Enterprise Agreement
  • 10:24 – Partner requirements and support
  • 12:20 – The big picture

Key takeaways

  • The Cloud Solution Provider program lets partners resell Microsoft cloud services and own billing, support and provisioning for their customers.
  • Under the new commerce experience, monthly, annual and triennial terms carry different prices, and seats can be added but not reduced after the short cancellation window.
  • CSP is a service delivery program rather than a discount program: it suits small and mid-sized businesses, while large, stable organizations should compare it with an Enterprise Agreement.

Worth quoting

“When you buy through a CSP partner, Microsoft is not your billing contact. The partner handles billing, support, and provisioning.” – Mirko Peters (00:57)

“The partner earns the margin on the service layer, not the software. That's the model. The license gets you in the door. The services keep you there.” – Mirko Peters (04:24)
