Most Power Automate estates still depend on people checking failed runs and patching symptoms every weekend. This episode is for makers, architects and platform teams who want automation that observes itself and recovers safely. Learn how telemetry, decision logic and bounded self-correction turn a growing flow estate into a self-healing one.

In this episode

  • 00:00 – Why flows don't manage themselves: maintenance debt
  • 00:44 – The death of manual debugging
  • 03:20 – The telemetry-driven logic layer
  • 06:20 – Building the telemetry nerve center
  • 09:58 – Engineering the recursive loop
  • 13:38 – Cost, scale and platform trade-offs: Power Automate and Logic Apps
  • 17:21 – Governance, AI and autonomy with brakes
  • 19:28 – Implementation path: from brittle flow to self-healing estate

Key takeaways

  • Every new Power Automate flow adds maintenance debt, so failures should be treated as input for a telemetry-driven logic layer that classifies drift and chooses a safe response.
  • A lean telemetry nerve center, reusable child flows and bounded recursive loops with confidence gates, cooling periods and clear termination conditions turn retries into guided re-entry.
  • Self-healing automation needs governance through risk tiers, evidence for every remediation and human approval for high-risk fixes, and heavier recursion belongs in Azure Logic Apps.
