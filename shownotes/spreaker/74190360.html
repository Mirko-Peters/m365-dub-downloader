A Dynamics 365 Sales opportunity can look busy while the customer has quietly stopped replying. This episode explains relationship intelligence for sellers, sales managers and CRM admins: relationship analytics, relationship health and who knows whom, and how to turn those signals into the next useful customer conversation.

In this episode

  • 00:00 – A hidden problem in Dynamics 365 Sales
  • 00:40 – Why sales teams need relationship intelligence
  • 03:01 – What relationship intelligence actually is
  • 06:17 – Relationship analytics: reading the conversation
  • 10:52 – Relationship health: a warning light, not a crystal ball
  • 14:14 – Who knows whom: finding the warm introduction
  • 17:15 – A simple sales day example

Key takeaways

  • Relationship intelligence in Dynamics 365 Sales turns emails, meetings and calls tied to sales records into signals that show whether a customer is still engaged.
  • Basic insights update close to real time from Dynamics 365 activities, while enhanced insights add Exchange data via server-side synchronization and refresh every 24 hours.
  • Relationship health and who knows whom point sellers to the relationships that need attention and to colleagues for warm introductions, but the seller still has to judge the context and have the conversation.

Worth quoting

“Relationship intelligence is like someone opening that file and asking, are we still talking to these people?” – Mirko Peters (01:50)

“The tool can organize communication signals, but it cannot fill in conversations that were never captured. That's just how it works.” – Mirko Peters (04:38)
