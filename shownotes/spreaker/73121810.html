Finding the right business solution for your Microsoft stack used to mean googling vendors and chasing trials. This episode explains Microsoft AppSource for IT pros, business owners, consultants and ISVs: what you can find there, how it plugs into Dynamics 365, Power Platform, Azure and Microsoft 365, and how trials, transactable offers and co-sell work.

In this episode

  • 00:00 – What AppSource actually is
  • 02:04 – The three kinds of offerings
  • 03:40 – How it connects to Dynamics 365
  • 04:55 – Power Platform and Azure
  • 06:24 – Microsoft 365 and Teams
  • 07:41 – Marketplace mechanics: trial, buy, co-sell
  • 09:44 – Common myths and mistakes
  • 11:21 – The big picture and next steps

Key takeaways

  • Microsoft AppSource is a marketplace for business solutions, offering apps and extensions, consulting services, and templates and data services that work with Microsoft products.
  • It connects Dynamics 365, Power Platform, Azure and Microsoft 365 in one search, and since September 2025 it is unified with Azure Marketplace as Microsoft Marketplace.
  • Free trials, transactable offers on existing Microsoft billing, private offers and co-sell make it a low-risk buying channel for customers and a go-to-market channel for partners and ISVs.

Worth quoting

“AppSource is Microsoft's online marketplace for business solutions. Think of it like a mall. But instead of selling clothes or electronics, every store sells software, services, and templates that work with Microsoft products.” – Mirko Peters (00:59)

“Then there's co-sell. This is probably the most powerful feature that most people don't know about. If an offering is co-sell eligible, Microsoft's own sales team can recommend it to their customers.” – Mirko Peters (08:49)
