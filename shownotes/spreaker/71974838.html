When a senior engineer leaves, the Power Automate flows, Logic Apps and Power BI refreshes running on their account often die with them. This episode is for Microsoft 365 and Azure architects who want automation that survives staff turnover. Learn how to move to service principals, managed identities and federated credentials, scope permissions and automate their life cycle.

  • 13:02 – The zero downtime rotation model
  • 15:58 – The economic reality of identity
  • 18:18 – Homework: find the identity rot

Key takeaways

  • Automations tied to personal or shared service accounts break on offboarding, password changes and MFA, so production workloads need their own service principal identities.
  • Prefer managed identities inside Azure and federated credentials via OpenID Connect for external pipelines, and scope every permission tightly to limit the blast radius.
  • Treat identity as infrastructure with a full life cycle: prune orphaned principals, recertify access with business owners and automate secret rotation with Azure Key Vault or certificates.
