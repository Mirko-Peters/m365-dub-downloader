Low-code makers are watching AI agents reshape the Power Platform and wonder what comes next. In this episode, Business Applications MVP Nathan Rose explains to makers, architects and consultants how Copilot Studio, Dataverse, the Dataverse MCP server and business skills fit together, and how to prepare for an agentic future.

In this episode

  • 00:00 – Welcome and Nathan's path into Power Platform
  • 02:53 – From Power Virtual Agents to Copilot Studio
  • 11:02 – Governance in the AI era and Agent 365
  • 15:30 – Low code's expiry date: code apps vs. Copilot Studio
  • 21:32 – Dataverse as the common data layer
  • 24:00 – Dataverse business skills explained
  • 27:45 – MCP explained: Spotify vs. the DJ
  • 46:17 – Change management, shadow AI and what to learn next

Key takeaways

  • Dataverse is a common data layer, and through the Dataverse MCP server agents can reason over its tables while respecting each user's permissions.
  • Dataverse business skills, still in preview, let business users add procedures in natural language that agents discover via MCP, without rewiring or redeploying the agent.
  • Low code is giving way to agentic development, so makers should stay open-minded, get hands-on in sandboxes and keep a human in the loop for production scenarios.
