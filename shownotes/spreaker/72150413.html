Every click on the OneDrive sync button copies enterprise data onto endpoints and away from the controls that protect it. This episode is for Microsoft 365 architects and IT admins who want to understand why syncing and shortcuts fragment their SharePoint data strategy. You will learn the security, metadata and scale limits of sync and how to move teams to cloud-native access.

  • 16:48 – Moving to a cloud-native mindset

Key takeaways

  • Syncing SharePoint libraries replicates data onto endpoints, stripping metadata, versioning context and real-time protections like DLP and sensitivity labels.
  • OneDrive shortcuts avoid per-device replication but introduce deletion cascades and an admin visibility gap that require new governance and user training.
  • Large libraries beyond roughly 300,000 items break the sync model, so organizations should favor browser, Teams and Office app access enforced through Intune and sensitivity labels.
