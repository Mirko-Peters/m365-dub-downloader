Many organizations buy Copilot licenses, see a usage spike after training and then watch adoption drop. This episode is for IT leaders, adoption managers and consultants who need Copilot to stick. Microsoft MVP Edyta Gorzoń shares practical lessons on change management, governance, leadership and champions programs.

In this episode

  • 00:00 – Edyta's path into Copilot adoption
  • 04:39 – Change management and the ADKAR model
  • 14:52 – What successful Copilot adoption looks like
  • 16:43 – Misconceptions and governance first
  • 27:52 – The role of leadership
  • 31:35 – Engaging users and Copilot champions
  • 39:57 – The first 30 days and customer stories
  • 49:14 – Community and final advice

Key takeaways

  • Copilot adoption is a change management challenge, and a structured approach like ADKAR with user sentiment checks works better than a few feature-led trainings.
  • Governance, permissions and documented Copilot policies must be ready before rollout, because Copilot respects whatever access already exists in the tenant.
  • Visible leadership, tailored scenario-based training, short sessions and a champions program turn initial usage peaks into lasting habits.
