Stuck with a messy spreadsheet because a custom app seems to need a developer and months of work? This episode explains Power Apps for business users, citizen developers and IT pros: canvas versus model-driven apps, Dataverse and connectors, security and governance, and how Copilot and AI tools now build apps from plain English.

In this episode

  • 00:00 – Intro: the app you keep putting off
  • 00:22 – What Power Apps actually is
  • 02:30 – Canvas apps versus model-driven apps
  • 04:50 – Dataverse and connectors
  • 07:12 – The ecosystem, security and governance
  • 10:03 – Building your first app without code
  • 12:25 – AI in Power Apps: Copilot, agents and Claude Code
  • 16:54 – When to use Power Apps and next steps

Key takeaways

  • Power Apps is the low-code app builder of the Power Platform, and its real strength is deep integration with Microsoft 365, Entra ID and hundreds of connectors.
  • Canvas apps give pixel-level design control for task-focused apps, while model-driven apps generate their interface from Dataverse tables for complex data.
  • Copilot and external AI tools like Claude Code now generate apps from plain-English descriptions, but preview features still need review before production use.

Worth quoting

“Power Apps is what the industry calls a low-code platform. Low-code means you're writing less code, not zero code. You might still type some formulas, but they're simple.” – Mirko Peters (02:01)

“The real value isn't any single feature. It's the integration. Your app lives inside Teams, pulls data from SharePoint, sends notifications through Outlook, and uses the same security that protects your email.” – Mirko Peters (02:16)
