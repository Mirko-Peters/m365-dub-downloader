Many organizations pay for Microsoft 365 licenses they barely use and struggle to make hybrid meetings and Copilot work for everyone. In this interview, Microsoft 365 MVP Onyinye Madubuko shares how she designs inclusive Teams meeting rooms, improves employee experience with Viva Insights and rolls out Copilot with real adoption. Ideal for modern workplace leads, IT admins and change managers.

Key takeaways

  • Inclusive hybrid meeting rooms depend on basics like eye-level cameras, controlled lighting, cable management and noise assessment, not just devices.
  • Before deploying Copilot, assess oversharing and baseline security such as conditional access and MFA, then roll out in phases with pilot users, training and departmental champions.
  • Track usage in the Copilot Control System, reassign unused licenses, and start adoption from concrete departmental pain points; personal Viva Insights and Copilot Chat are low-cost entry points.
