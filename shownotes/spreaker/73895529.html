Many IT leaders assume a growing company can simply upgrade from Business Central to Dynamics 365 Finance and Operations later. This episode explains why that bridge does not exist, why acquisitions hit the wall hardest, and how finance and IT teams can plan a realistic phased path before a deal forces their hand.

Key takeaways

  • Business Central and Finance and Operations come from different lineages (NAV and AX), so moving between them is a full re-implementation rather than an upgrade.
  • Acquisitions and multi-entity consolidation are what expose the gap, and Dataverse sync, dual-write and batch GL transfers only form a lagging patchwork.
  • Plan early by designing the chart of accounts and master data for consolidation, documenting customizations and following a phased path with a parallel run.
