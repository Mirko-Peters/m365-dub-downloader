Managed service providers and multi-tenant enterprises have long juggled separate logins in every customer tenant. This episode explains how Azure Lighthouse replaces that with delegated, auditable access that the customer controls, and where its limits are. You will be able to explain the handshake, the security model and the naming trap with Microsoft 365 Lighthouse.

Key takeaways

  • Azure Lighthouse lets a provider manage a customer's Azure resources from its own tenant through a registration definition and a registration assignment, with no account in the customer's directory.
  • The customer chooses the scope and built-in role, sees every provider action in its own activity log and can remove the delegation at any time.
  • Lighthouse covers Azure Resource Manager operations only, does not support the Owner role or data-access roles, and is a different product from Microsoft 365 Lighthouse.

Worth quoting

“Azure lighthouse lets one organization manage another organization's Azure resources from its own environment without needing an account inside the other organizations directory.” – Mirko Peters (02:36)
