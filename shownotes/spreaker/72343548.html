Your data map only shows the data someone remembered to register, while forgotten storage accounts, OneDrive copies and AI training sets keep spreading sensitive information. This episode is for data governance, security and compliance teams who want to use Microsoft Purview to discover, classify and trace shadow data before it turns into a breach or a failed audit.

  • 62:19 – Operationalizing remediation and the ROI case
  • 77:13 – AI readiness and the governance roadmap

Key takeaways

  • Shadow data is the unknown copies of sensitive information outside your governance frameworks, and periodic manual audits cannot keep up with how fast it is created.
  • Microsoft Purview works discover-first: automated scans, custom classifiers and lineage reveal where sensitive data lives, how it moves and who created each copy.
  • Discovery only pays off when it is operationalized with labels, retention, stewards and shift-left remediation, which also makes your data estate ready for AI and regulations like the EU AI Act.
