Copilot's real cost isn't the license but the frontier model behind every request, which drives up spend, slows responses and can process data outside the EU. This episode shows IT leaders and architects how small language models, tiered routing and local deployment on Azure Local make Copilot cheaper, faster and more sovereign.

Key takeaways

  • Routing every Copilot request through a frontier model makes AI costly, slow and dependent on cloud processing locations you may not control.
  • Small language models such as Phi-3 handle the high-volume, narrow Microsoft 365 tasks like summarization, classification and drafting at a fraction of the cost and latency.
  • A tiered model mixture with policy and confidence-based routing, local deployment on Azure Local and clear governance lets organizations keep frontier models for the exceptions that need them.
