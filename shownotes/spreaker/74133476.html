When sales, project managers and finance each keep their own version of a customer project, promises, hours and invoices drift apart. This Simply Explained episode walks project-based service businesses through Dynamics 365 Project Operations, from quote and contract to staffing, time tracking and billing, so the whole lifecycle stays connected.

In this episode

  • 00:00 – Intro: one project from first conversation to final invoice
  • 00:32 – Why project-based businesses need a connected system
  • 03:59 – Sales, quotes and project contracts
  • 07:05 – Plans, tasks and day-to-day delivery
  • 10:58 – Staffing: skills, availability, time and expenses
  • 14:13 – Finance: costs, billing and revenue
  • 18:34 – How the connected platform works together
  • 19:30 – Who it fits and what to remember

Key takeaways

  • Dynamics 365 Project Operations connects project sales, planning, staffing, time, expenses, billing and financials around one shared project record.
  • Quotes, contracts, resource bookings and approved time and expense entries flow into billing models such as time and materials, fixed price or milestone billing.
  • It fits service firms that sell customer project work; simple internal task lists are better served by lighter tools like Planner.

Worth quoting

“Here's a simple distinction. A quote says what the company hopes to sell, a contract records what the customer agreed to buy.” – Mirko Peters (05:45)

“Without that visibility, a company can accidentally promise the same person to two full-time projects in the same week. On paper, both projects look staffed.” – Mirko Peters (12:02)
