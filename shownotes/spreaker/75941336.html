SAP S/4HANA sounds intimidating, yet most companies running SAP ECC have to decide where it lives and how to migrate before the 2027 deadline. This plain-language episode is for IT and business people who need to understand what S/4HANA is, how it connects to Microsoft tools, and where projects usually go wrong.

Key takeaways

  • SAP S/4HANA is an ERP system rebuilt on the in-memory, column-based HANA database, which lets analytics and transactions run on one shared copy of the data.
  • Every company on SAP ECC must choose a deployment model and a migration path (greenfield, brownfield or bluefield) before mainstream maintenance ends in 2027.
  • Keeping a clean core and integrating through Microsoft Fabric, Entra ID and Power Platform connectors pays off in upgrades, while data cleanup and process change are the real cost of migration.

Worth quoting
