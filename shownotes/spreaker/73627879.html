Many teams start in Azure by spinning up resources and only later discover unclear ownership, broad access and surprise bills. This episode explains the Microsoft Cloud Adoption Framework for IT leaders and cloud beginners. You learn its six building blocks, what a landing zone really is and why governance cannot wait until the end.

In this episode

  • 00:00 – Why start with a plan
  • 00:51 – Why CAF exists
  • 02:53 – Strategy and plan
  • 06:46 – Ready: the Azure landing zone
  • 11:04 – Adopt: move workloads in
  • 14:27 – Govern, secure and manage
  • 17:50 – Conclusion

Key takeaways

  • The Microsoft Cloud Adoption Framework is guidance, not a product, that walks organizations through strategy, plan, ready, adopt, govern and manage.
  • An Azure landing zone prepares identity, subscriptions, networking, shared services and policies before workloads arrive, and should grow with real workload needs.
  • Governance, security and management run through the whole cloud journey, and every alert, backup and access decision needs a named owner.

Worth quoting
