Many Microsoft partners win deals yet stall because of execution gaps that quietly cost them millions. This episode walks MSPs, CSPs, ISVs and consulting partners through twelve common partnership mistakes, from marketplace expectations and co-sell overload to unused incentives, and shows how Veeam turned its partnership around.

In this episode

  • 00:00 – Why winning deals is not building a business
  • 00:38 – The expectation gap: marketplace is not lead flow
  • 01:35 – The ownership void and sales compensation
  • 02:56 – The readiness gap: finance and messaging
  • 04:44 – The credibility trap: certifications and the one-page plan
  • 06:05 – The data black hole: referrals and co-sell overload
  • 07:52 – Growth bottlenecks, ops and the feedback loop
  • 10:55 – The recovery playbook: lessons from Veeam

Key takeaways

  • Microsoft is an amplifier, not a lead engine: listing on the marketplace or earning a designation only pays off once you bring a clear story and demonstrated value.
  • Partnerships need a dedicated owner, aligned sales compensation, a finance team ready for marketplace and incentive payments, and messaging mapped to specific Microsoft seller roles.
  • Be surgical with co-sell by focusing on a few high-value deals, reinvest incentives instead of letting them disappear into revenue, and keep a weekly rhythm with your partner development manager.
