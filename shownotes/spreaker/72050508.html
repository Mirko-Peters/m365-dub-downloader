Every company wants Copilot, but AI exposes overshared files and weak data governance faster than ever. In this conversation, Danilo Nogueira, Senior Product Manager for Microsoft Purview, explains to security, compliance and IT teams how monitoring, insider risk management and classification help protect data. Learn how to adopt AI without losing control of your information.

Key takeaways

  • AI changes data security from blocking users to monitoring their behavior, and Microsoft Purview with Insider Risk Management supports exactly that approach.
  • Before rolling out Copilot, organizations need to understand permissions, fix oversharing and establish data governance, because AI surfaces everything users can access.
  • Classification with sensitivity labels and posture insights from DSPM help protect confidential data, while a balance between security and productivity needs both perspectives in the company.
