Most organizations still use Copilot like a search box, and productivity stalls once the novelty wears off. This episode is for architects and Microsoft 365 leaders who want to move from prompting to agent orchestration. You get a blueprint for skills, MCP, agent-to-agent delegation, governance and measurement that turns Copilot into an agent fabric owning real workflows.

In this episode

  • 00:00 – Why prompting has hit its limit
  • 03:32 – Defining the Copilot agent fabric and grounding in data
  • 13:20 – MCP and agent-to-agent delegation
  • 20:42 – Anatomy of a skill and the DBS framework
  • 28:18 – Specialist delegation, progressive disclosure and recursive building
  • 44:36 – Governance, schemas and measuring ROI
  • 57:30 – The agent-operated organization and the 100X roadmap
  • 65:05 – Troubleshooting and future-proofing with open standards

Key takeaways

  • Prompt-driven chatbot use plateaus because every workflow depends on the user, while an agent fabric of specialized, skill-based agents coordinated by a parent orchestrator owns outcomes end to end.
  • Agents are only as good as their data and tools, so semantic metadata, MCP for live system access, A2A for delegation and progressive disclosure to protect the context window are core architecture decisions.
  • Scale safely with least privilege at the tool boundary, human approval for high-impact actions, clear agent ownership and portable, vendor-neutral skill files, and measure success by business outcomes rather than minutes saved.
