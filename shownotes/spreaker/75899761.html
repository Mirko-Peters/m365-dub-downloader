Attackers prefer small businesses because fewer people are watching. This episode explains Microsoft Defender for Business for small and medium companies without a security team: what it does, how to set it up, what it costs and where it stops. You will know whether it fits your organization.

  • 02:27 – Why antivirus alone isn't enough
  • 04:06 – The four jobs on every device
  • 06:08 – Setting it up
  • 08:22 – Limits before you buy
  • 10:12 – Defender for Business vs. Defender for Endpoint
  • 11:59 – Cost, licensing and recap

Key takeaways

  • Defender for Business brings the same engine as Defender for Endpoint to companies with up to 300 users, packaged with a setup wizard and default policies.
  • Unlike plain antivirus, its EDR capabilities show what happened, where and what to do next, and can disrupt attacks automatically.
  • Know the limits before buying: the 300-user cap, five devices per user, separate server licensing and no mixing with Defender for Endpoint Plan 2.
