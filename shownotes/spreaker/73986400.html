What happens when AI agents become the whole software team? Mirko Peters talks with Mark Smith (NZ365Guy) about his OpenClaw-based AI software factory, Ralph loops, governance and why context engineering matters more than prompts. A practical look ahead for developers, consultants and IT leaders preparing for AI-first software engineering.

  • 50:19 – Preparing for an AI-first career and rapid fire

Key takeaways

  • Mark Smith runs a nine-agent AI software factory in which each agent fills one gated role in the DevOps loop, from requirements and architecture to coding, security, release and documentation.
  • Owning the agent harness with OpenClaw keeps context and data in his own Azure environment, so models from different providers can be swapped without rebuilding the system.
  • For an AI-first career, Mark recommends moving from prompt engineering to context and outcomes engineering, learning about harnesses and avoiding lock-in to a single vendor.
