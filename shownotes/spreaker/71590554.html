Your Copilot rollout is producing three times more content, but nobody trusts it. This episode is for leaders and Microsoft 365 architects who want to see how unverified AI output turns into digital debt, and how to measure and govern for trusted decisions instead of volume.

In this episode

  • 00:00 – The 3x productivity trap
  • 00:34 – The anatomy of digital debt
  • 03:22 – Scenario A: the document explosion
  • 06:43 – Scenario B: Teams and Loop sprawl
  • 09:31 – Scenario C: Power Automate and silent breakage
  • 12:53 – Decision velocity as the executive metric
  • 15:41 – The 90-day architecture plan

Key takeaways

  • More Copilot output does not mean more productivity when every artifact still needs human validation; unverified content piles up as digital debt.
  • Teams chats, Loop components and AI-built Power Automate flows compound that debt through fragmented context, ownerless decisions and silent breakage.
  • Measure decision velocity instead of time saved, and pay down the debt with kill-switch governance, weekly health checks and a 30/60/90-day plan.

Worth quoting

“Digital debt is the massive accumulation of unverified and unmanaged digital artifacts that eventually overwhelm your human cognitive capacity to make sense of them.” – Mirko Peters (00:53)

“Because in reality, your AI isn't saving you time. It's just moving the work from the creation phase to the validation phase, and that second phase is much more expensive for your business.” – Mirko Peters (03:33)
