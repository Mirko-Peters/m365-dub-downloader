Polling Microsoft Graph every few minutes wastes requests, misses changes and can get your app throttled. This episode explains for Microsoft 365 developers how change notifications, subscriptions, validation and renewal work, and how to build reliable real-time apps with delta queries as a safety net.

In this episode

  • 00:00 – The problem with polling
  • 01:04 – What are change notifications?
  • 02:34 – Creating a subscription
  • 04:16 – The validation handshake
  • 05:16 – Subscription renewal
  • 06:26 – Reliability and safety nets
  • 08:08 – Real-world use cases
  • 09:26 – Choosing the delivery channel

Key takeaways

  • Microsoft Graph change notifications push events to your app through subscriptions, replacing wasteful polling that risks throttling.
  • Every subscription needs a validation handshake within 10 seconds and must be renewed before its resource-specific expiration.
  • Acknowledge notifications fast, use delta queries as a safety net, and choose webhooks, Event Hubs or Event Grid based on volume.
