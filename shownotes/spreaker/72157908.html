Moving from PBX and Skype for Business to Teams Phone and Teams Rooms raises tough choices about routing, devices and security. Teams MVP Carsten Lund Meilbak gives IT and UC leaders a practitioner's view of Direct Routing versus Operator Connect, AI in meetings and voice, and the mistakes to avoid when rolling out meeting rooms.

In this episode

  • 00:00 – Meet Carsten: from PBX engineer to Lync
  • 04:15 – Skype for Business, the cloud journey and early Teams
  • 09:13 – COVID and the forced move to cloud collaboration
  • 12:45 – Teams Phone versus traditional PBX
  • 17:30 – Direct Routing, Operator Connect and hybrid voice
  • 27:38 – AI in voice and meetings
  • 31:11 – Teams Rooms: AI, Android versus Windows and common mistakes
  • 41:06 – Security, compliance and final wishes

Key takeaways

  • Traditional PBX systems are becoming a niche, while Teams Phone is more than enterprise-ready thanks to its integration across Microsoft 365.
  • Direct Routing remains the most flexible way to bring phone numbers into Teams, while Operator Connect is the simpler choice for organizations that just want voice to work.
  • Teams Rooms deployments fail most often on security and compliance configuration, so involve the whole IT organization and treat meeting room devices differently from user devices.
