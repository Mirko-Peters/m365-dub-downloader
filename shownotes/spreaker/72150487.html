Knowledge workers lose a large share of their day hunting through nested folders that force every document into a single place. This episode is for Microsoft 365 architects and IT leaders who want to understand how Microsoft Graph, the semantic index and SharePoint Premium replace static hierarchies with context-driven findability, and how to bring users along.

In this episode

  • 00:00 – Why folders are obsolete
  • 00:42 – The 19% navigational tax
  • 03:46 – Collapse of the static hierarchy
  • 05:04 – Microsoft Graph and the semantic index
  • 08:23 – SharePoint Premium as metadata engine
  • 09:45 – Graph-based UI and cognitive load
  • 10:58 – Overcoming folder nostalgia

Key takeaways

  • Deep folder hierarchies impose a navigational tax because users must recall where someone else once decided a file belongs.
  • Microsoft Graph and the semantic index locate content by meaning, relationships and context instead of by its storage path.
  • Moving beyond folders is a cultural change that needs automated metadata, query-based training and leaders who model the new behaviour.
