Your Copilot answers sound confident, but they are built on only a fraction of your business: the documents in SharePoint and OneDrive. This episode is for Microsoft 365 architects and IT leaders who want AI that sees approvals, tickets and exceptions living in external systems, and shows how Graph connectors, permissions and governance turn a blind assistant into a real-time business brain.

  • 09:01 – Blind spot three: risk and exception exposure
  • 11:56 – The architecture of live ingestion
  • 14:46 – Governance as a competitive advantage

Key takeaways

  • Copilot grounded only in SharePoint and OneDrive documents misses the live system state, such as approvals, tickets and exceptions, that drives real business decisions.
  • Custom Graph connectors bring that external context into the semantic index, but only if identities, schema, ACL-based security trimming and latency targets are designed deliberately.
  • Treat every connector as a governed business asset with a named owner, respect data residency, and start with the one system whose missing context causes the most rework.
