Separate alerts from email, identity and endpoint tools hide the one attack that connects them. This episode explains Microsoft Defender XDR, its five building blocks and how they combine into single incidents with automated response, for IT and security teams using Microsoft 365. You also get three concrete steps to unlock protection you may already be paying for.

Key takeaways

  • Microsoft Defender XDR is a unifying layer that correlates signals from Defender for Endpoint, Office 365, Identity, Cloud Apps and vulnerability management into single incidents.
  • Correlated incidents and automated remediation cut alert noise and can shrink response times from hours to minutes.
  • Start by checking your Microsoft 365 licensing, turn on the unified incident experience and deploy the Defender for Office 365 preset policies before adding more components.
