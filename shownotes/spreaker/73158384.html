Engineering dashboards look greener than ever in the age of AI-generated code, yet incidents, review queues and developer burnout keep rising. This episode helps engineering leaders and IT decision makers see through the productivity illusion and shows which flow, quality, cognitive load and value metrics reveal what is really happening.

  • 34:28 – The governance bottleneck and the burnout signal
  • 47:44 – The diagnostic dashboard
  • 55:48 – The organizational shift and the roadmap

Key takeaways

  • AI has moved the engineering bottleneck from writing code to reviewing, testing and governing it, so activity metrics like deployment frequency look better while stability, incidents and rework get worse.
  • Rework rate, decomposed lead time, flow efficiency and cognitive load reveal system health far better than the classic four DORA metrics alone.
  • Metrics should be diagnostic questions owned by teams and never used for individual performance, with changes introduced as measured experiments through a phased roadmap.
