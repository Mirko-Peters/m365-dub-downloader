Every Azure VM needs disk storage, and the wrong tier means either overpaying or slow performance. This episode explains Standard SSD, Premium SSD, Premium SSD v2 and Ultra Disk for Azure beginners and admins, with a simple decision guide and cost tips.

In this episode

  • 00:00 – Intro: the virtual hard drive for VMs
  • 00:23 – Why disk type matters
  • 01:19 – OS disks vs data disks
  • 02:19 – Standard SSD
  • 03:22 – Premium SSD
  • 04:35 – Premium SSD v2
  • 06:34 – Ultra Disk
  • 08:22 – Choosing the right disk

Key takeaways

  • IOPS, throughput and latency determine disk performance, and the goal is to match them to the workload without over- or under-provisioning.
  • Premium SSD v2 decouples capacity, IOPS and throughput and covers most production databases at a lower price than Ultra Disk.
  • Ultra Disk is reserved for extreme workloads such as SAP HANA or trading platforms, while Standard SSD is enough for dev/test and low-traffic servers.

Worth quoting

“The trick is matching the disk to what your workload actually needs, so you're not over-provisioning and wasting cash, nor under-provisioning and getting slow performance.” – Mirko Peters (01:06)

“With premium SSD V2, you can independently adjust capacity, IOPS and throughput. No downtime, no disk swaps, just change the settings and it happens.” – Mirko Peters (04:45)
