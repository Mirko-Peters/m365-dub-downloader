Connecting cloud AI to SharePoint can expose contracts, HR files and board memos to infrastructure and legal regimes you do not control. This episode is for Microsoft 365 architects and security teams who need private AI, and it walks through a seven-layer, air-gapped RAG architecture with local Llama, Ollama and Qdrant that respects SharePoint permissions.

  • 64:49 – Scaling, model evolution and the complete blueprint

Key takeaways

  • Sending SharePoint content to cloud AI moves it into logs and legal jurisdictions you do not control, while a local stack with Llama, a vector database and on-premises embeddings keeps every query inside your perimeter.
  • Retrieval quality depends mostly on chunking: use heading-aware chunks for Word, page-aware chunks for PDFs, row groups with headers for Excel and slide-level chunks for PowerPoint, and keep source metadata on every chunk for citations.
  • Mirror SharePoint permissions exactly by tagging every vector with a permission tier at ingestion and filtering by the user's Entra ID groups at query time, backed by local audit logs and regular permission reconciliation.
