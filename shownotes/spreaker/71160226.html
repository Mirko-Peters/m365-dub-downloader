Your Microsoft 365 governance looks mature on paper, yet reviews pile up, workspaces sprawl and people route around the rules. This episode is for IT leaders and admins who want governance that scales with Teams, SharePoint, Power Platform and Copilot. You learn how to move control to the point of action and which decision to automate first.

In this episode

  • 00:00 – Why checklist governance fails
  • 05:24 – Cloud scale and why governance is enforcement, not policy
  • 12:37 – Governance as an immune system: detect, respond, adapt
  • 28:14 – Where manual governance breaks: data, workspaces and Power Platform
  • 40:15 – Anchor case: risk went down and the business sped up
  • 50:28 – Why automation fails and moving control to the point of action
  • 58:17 – Microsoft Graph and why AI makes manual governance obsolete
  • 66:05 – Metrics, a seven-day action plan and the system designer role

Key takeaways

  • Manual Microsoft 365 governance is queue-based control: the action happens first, the review later, and the risk lives in between.
  • Effective governance detects, responds and adapts at runtime, using tools like Purview DLP, auto-labeling, provisioning defaults and zoned Power Platform environments.
  • Start by automating one high-frequency governance decision and measure the exposure window and manual governance load before and after.

Worth quoting

“If your control is missing at the point of action, then from an operating perspective, it isn't a control at all. It's just guidance.” – Mirko Peters (02:59)

“So let's make the distinction clearly. Policy tells people what should happen, but governance determines what actually happens. Those are not the same thing.” – Mirko Peters (09:12)
