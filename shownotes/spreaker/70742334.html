Access reviews nobody answers, exceptions that never expire and policies nobody can read: manual admin in Microsoft 365 cannot keep up with machine speed. This episode is for IT admins and architects who want to understand why, and how to move from clicking buttons to defining intent that Entra, Purview, Defender and Agent 365 enforce automatically.

Key takeaways

  • Manual Microsoft 365 administration failed by design: the platform creates identities, teams and permissions at machine speed while governance runs in quarterly human batches.
  • Exceptions, standing privilege and access reviews approved by default turn policy into conditional chaos and compliance theater that no human can keep consistent.
  • The replacement is a governance stack of Entra ID, Conditional Access, Purview, Defender and Agent 365 that enforces defined intent continuously, starting with PIM, deny-first sharing and an agent inventory.
