Developers often spend more time on tickets, permissions and cloud setup than on the feature they were asked to build. This episode explains internal developer platforms in plain English for IT and engineering leaders, covering golden paths, service catalogs, guardrails and how Azure tools fit behind a single self-service request.

In this episode

  • 03:37 – Golden paths
  • 06:53 – The service catalog
  • 09:29 – Guardrails and identity
  • 13:05 – Automation behind Create Service
  • 16:41 – IDP myths and where Azure fits

Key takeaways

  • An internal developer platform is a self-service front door to approved building blocks, run by a platform team that treats developers as customers.
  • Golden paths, a service catalog and guardrails built on Entra ID, Azure Policy and Key Vault let teams move fast without manual approvals or unsafe shortcuts.
  • An IDP is not a single portal product; start with one painful workflow and one golden path, then measure waiting time, tickets and developer feedback.

Worth quoting

“A golden path is an approved, repeatable route for a job that teams do often. It doesn't tell every developer how to write their code.” – Mirko Peters (03:42)

“If the platform says no without a route forward, people will build their own workarounds outside the platform. That creates the exact confusion the platform was meant to reduce.” – Mirko Peters (06:23)
