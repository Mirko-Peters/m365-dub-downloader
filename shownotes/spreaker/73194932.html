Many organizations celebrate early Copilot and chatbot wins, then discover agents borrowing human identities, calling models directly and running long after their projects ended. This episode is for architects, IT and governance leaders who want to scale AI agents safely, explaining how identity, ontologies, a governance hub with a model gateway, observability and Agent 365 form the landing zone that turns scattered chatbots into a managed platform.

In this episode

  • 00:00 – Intro: chat isn't a platform
  • 01:57 – The Copilot illusion and governance debt
  • 06:04 – Identity and reasoning: agent IDs, RAG and ontologies
  • 20:37 – What an AI landing zone actually is
  • 25:13 – The model gateway and the ontology layer
  • 40:26 – Agent 365 as the control plane and the governance hub
  • 50:41 – Data residency, FinOps, multi-cloud and ghost agents
  • 85:07 – Security, transition strategy and autonomous operations

Key takeaways

  • Copilot and chatbots are features, not a platform: scaling AI agents without identity, policy enforcement and logging creates governance debt that compounds with every new team.
  • An AI landing zone combines a traceable identity plane (agent IDs), a governance hub with a model gateway, and a shared ontology-based data plane so agents reason consistently over business relationships instead of text fragments.
  • Agent 365 as a control plane adds registry, policy enforcement, observability and lifecycle management across clouds, enabling cost control, preventive compliance and retirement of ghost agents.
