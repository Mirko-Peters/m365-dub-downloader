Asked to report your company's carbon footprint but buried in utility bills, spreadsheets and supplier data? This episode explains to business leaders and ESG teams how Microsoft Cloud for Sustainability collects that data, calculates emissions with built-in factors and produces reports for frameworks like CSRD.

  • 06:45 – Building block two: calculating your real impact
  • 08:15 – Reporting frameworks, data trail and Copilot
  • 10:05 – Companies using it today
  • 11:52 – The bottom line

Key takeaways

  • Most companies cannot state their carbon footprint because sustainability data is scattered across systems, formats and units.
  • Microsoft Cloud for Sustainability brings data in via Excel templates, Power Query, partner connectors or manual requests and applies built-in emissions factors across scopes 1, 2 and 3.
  • Ready-made templates for frameworks such as CSRD, an auditable data trail and AI-drafted disclosures turn months of manual reporting into a far faster process.
