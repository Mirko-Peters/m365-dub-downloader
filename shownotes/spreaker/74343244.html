When the same customer lives in five spreadsheets and tools, nobody knows which copy to trust. This episode is for business users, makers and Dynamics 365 teams who want to understand what Dataverse is and how Dynamics 365 actually uses it. You will learn how tables, security and rules form one shared foundation that Power Apps, Power Automate and Power BI can build on without extra copies.

In this episode

  • 00:00 – What Dataverse is
  • 00:36 – One shared place instead of separate copies
  • 04:00 – Tables, rows, columns and relationships
  • 08:17 – Identity, security roles and business rules
  • 11:52 – What Dynamics 365 integration with Dataverse really means
  • 16:40 – Power Automate, Power BI, Power Apps and Power Query on the same data
  • 20:10 – When Dataverse fits and what it does not replace

Key takeaways

  • Dynamics 365 Sales and Customer Service already store their data in Dataverse, so integration is mostly about shared tables and access rather than syncing two systems.
  • Dataverse organizes business data in standard and custom tables with rows, columns and relationships, protected by Entra ID identity, security roles, column-level security, business units and business rules.
  • Power Apps, Power Automate and Power BI can work on the same Dataverse rows, while Excel, SharePoint lists and SQL remain better fits for personal analysis, simple tracking or deep developer control.

Worth quoting

“In plain English, dataverse is Microsoft's cloud data platform for business information. It stores that information, in structured tables. So the data has a clear shape, instead of living as random notes, loose files, and disconnected spreadsheet tabs.” – Mirko Peters (01:58)
