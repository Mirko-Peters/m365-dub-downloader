Most Microsoft 365 admins only ever touch three Graph endpoints and miss the reporting, audit, identity governance and security data underneath. This episode is for IT pros and decision makers who want to turn Microsoft Graph into a governance and intelligence layer. You learn which Graph surfaces to build on, from change notifications to Data Connect and hidden gems like Planner and Places.

In this episode

  • 00:00 – What Graph really is
  • 03:44 – Why teams stop at users and groups
  • 07:19 – Reporting APIs and executive dashboards
  • 14:14 – Audit and sign-in logs
  • 17:27 – Delta queries and change notifications
  • 20:49 – Identity governance and guest access
  • 26:58 – Security, risk and compliance APIs
  • 46:58 – Copilot control plane, hidden gems and roadmap

Key takeaways

  • Microsoft Graph is the control plane beneath Microsoft 365, and most organizations use only users and groups while reporting, audit, governance and security APIs sit unused.
  • Combining delta queries with change notifications replaces fragile polling with near real-time, event-driven governance and security automation.
  • Copilot quality and agent readiness depend on Graph maturity, including clean permissions, governed connectors and a documented consent ledger for app-only access.

Worth quoting
