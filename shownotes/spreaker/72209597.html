Only about half of ERP implementations are deemed successful, and the reasons are rarely technical. Microsoft MVP Alicia King shares lessons from more than 100 Dynamics 365 transitions for CFOs, CIOs and project leads on alignment, data quality, global rollouts and how AI agents are changing ERP work.

In this episode

  • 00:00 – Welcome and Alicia King's background
  • 04:31 – How Dynamics 365 Finance and Supply Chain evolved
  • 07:08 – Common mistakes and executive alignment
  • 09:59 – Change management and process redesign
  • 14:43 – Data quality, resources and project roles
  • 28:45 – Global ERP projects and localization
  • 38:27 – Copilot, agents and governance in D365
  • 46:47 – Rapid-fire questions

Key takeaways

  • ERP modernization succeeds through people, leadership alignment and change management, not technology alone.
  • Clean data, dedicated key users and transparent risk management on both the customer and partner side are prerequisites for a successful go-live.
  • AI in Dynamics 365, such as the supplier agent and MCP access from Teams, currently surfaces data and drafts actions under the user's role-based security rather than replacing people.

Worth quoting
