Repetitive work such as sorting leads, updating cases, matching transactions and chasing suppliers eats up time across every business team. In this Simply Explained episode, Mirko Peters shows Dynamics 365 users and decision makers what autonomous agents actually do, where Microsoft applies them, and how guardrails and human oversight keep people in charge.

In this episode

  • 00:00 – Intro: copilot vs. autonomous agent
  • 00:46 – A digital team member with a narrow job
  • 03:31 – Dynamics 365 as the business office
  • 06:21 – Service agents: intent, cases and knowledge
  • 10:27 – Sales, finance, supply chain and field service agents
  • 14:53 – Control, trust and the human team
  • 17:21 – Conclusion: one job, clear rules, human oversight

Key takeaways

  • Dynamics 365 autonomous agents react to business events and carry out narrow, defined jobs, unlike a copilot that waits for someone to ask.
  • Microsoft applies agents across Customer Service, Sales, Business Central, Finance, Supply Chain Management and Field Service, each grounded in the related Dynamics 365 records.
  • The organization sets instructions, data access, permissions and approval points, and people remain accountable for outcomes.

Worth quoting

“Don't ask whether an agent can act, ask who decided what it can do. Every action comes from choices the organization made before it ever touched a customer record and order or a financial transaction.” – Mirko Peters (14:57)

“It's an AI worker with a narrow job, a set of rules and real business data to work from.” – Mirko Peters (03:21)
