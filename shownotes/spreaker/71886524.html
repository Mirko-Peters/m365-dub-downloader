Help desk tickets only measure pain after employees have already lost time and context. This episode shows IT and support leaders how autonomous agents inside Microsoft 365 can detect and fix friction before a ticket exists, and how to govern, measure and roll out this invisible support layer safely.

In this episode

  • 00:00 – Why the ticketing model breaks
  • 02:50 – The invisible employee model
  • 05:39 – The architecture of preemption
  • 09:03 – Governance, agent identity and control
  • 13:01 – The new ROI of invisible support
  • 15:51 – What this means for the support team
  • 18:06 – How to start with one support flow

Key takeaways

  • A ticket is evidence that support arrived late; autonomous agents in Microsoft 365 can use signals from Entra, Intune, Defender and Teams to fix friction before users ask.
  • Govern support agents like employees, with their own identity, a named owner, least-privilege permissions, human approval for high-impact actions and full logging.
  • Measure value through autonomous resolution rate, mean time to resolution, cost per resolution and regained productivity, and start with one high-friction flow.

Worth quoting
