A customer buys online, picks up in one store and returns in another, and suddenly four disconnected systems have to agree. This knowledge nugget explains Dynamics 365 Commerce in plain English for retail, IT and business leaders, covering the customer journey, Store Commerce, the headless Commerce Scale Unit and the data behind it, and what the platform still leaves to people.

Key takeaways

  • Dynamics 365 Commerce connects websites, stores, call centers and back office around one shared view of products, prices, stock, orders and customers.
  • The Commerce Scale Unit is the headless engine behind every channel, so a new app or storefront reuses the same pricing, stock and order rules through APIs.
  • Commerce connects people and processes but does not replace them: clean product data, clear business rules and staff who pick, pack and serve remain essential.

Worth quoting

“Headless simply means the selling engine lives apart from the screen a person looks at. The engine doesn't care if a customer uses a website, a phone app, a self-service kiosk, or a store worker's tablet.” – Mirko Peters (09:18)

“Customers don't care which technical system answered their question. They care that the price on their phone matches the price in the store, and that an item shown as available can actually be ordered.” – Mirko Peters (10:11)
