Most organizations give admins permanent rights they use for only a few minutes a week, leaving the door open for attackers. This episode explains Microsoft Entra Privileged Identity Management in plain English for admins and security owners. You will learn how eligible assignments, just-in-time activation and approvals shrink your attack surface and which pitfalls to avoid.

Key takeaways

  • Standing privileges leave admin rights active around the clock although they are used only briefly, turning every compromised admin account into a potential disaster.
  • PIM makes users eligible instead of permanently active, so roles are activated just in time with a time limit, MFA, justification and optional approval, and every activation is audited.
  • Start by making your global admin accounts eligible, keep activation windows short, require MFA on activation and keep separate break-glass accounts.
