Rolling out Microsoft Copilot exposes every permission and sharing mistake already hidden in your Microsoft 365 tenant. Security, compliance and IT leads hear from MVP Alan Cox how Microsoft Purview, DLP, sensitivity labels, DSPM for AI and Insider Risk Management keep sensitive data out of AI responses without blocking productivity.

In this episode

  • 00:00 – Introducing Alan Cox and governance versus compliance
  • 04:05 – Copilot surfaces existing risk: identity, Defender and insider risk
  • 13:02 – Microsoft Purview explained simply and the road to E7
  • 22:00 – DSPM for AI and discovering AI usage
  • 27:20 – Sensitive data, labeling and Copilot readiness
  • 38:18 – DLP, auto-labeling and adaptive protection
  • 46:26 – Compliance Manager, insider risk and communication compliance
  • 53:47 – Hot takes and final advice

Key takeaways

  • Copilot rarely creates new risk; it surfaces existing oversharing and over-permissioning, so start with a full sharing and permission report.
  • Combine auto-labeling with DLP policies, now creatable in DSPM for AI, to keep Copilot away from sensitive content down to the single site or document.
  • Use Insider Risk Management and adaptive protection to scale controls by user risk, and design governance that makes the right thing easy to avoid shadow IT.
