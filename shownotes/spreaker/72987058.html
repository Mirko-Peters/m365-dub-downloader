Many Microsoft partners win deals but stall because they never connect their work to the partner program's scorecard. This episode is for partner leaders and practice owners who want a practical path through the Partner Capability Score, CPOR, Partner Admin Link, designations, specializations, co-sell, ECIF and MACC so that each customer engagement compounds into incentives and funding.

Key takeaways

  • The Microsoft partner program rewards systems rather than single deals: every onboarded customer, certification and usage metric feeds the Partner Capability Score, and 70 points with at least one point per subcategory earns a Solutions Partner designation.
  • Attribution decides whether your work counts, so file CPOR claims with a properly signed proof of execution and set up Partner Admin Link for every Azure customer you support.
  • Advanced specializations unlock ECIF funding, while marketplace offers that are MACC-eligible and co-sell ready make it easier for Microsoft sellers and enterprise customers to buy from you.
