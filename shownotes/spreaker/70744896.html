Most organizations treat Microsoft Purview as a compliance tool and miss what its data says about the business. This episode is for leaders, architects and security professionals preparing Microsoft 365 for Copilot and AI agents. Learn to read sensitivity labels, DLP violations and insider risk as business intelligence and turn them into an AI readiness plan.

  • 21:29 – Purview as an X-ray: silos, hidden dependencies and operational waste
  • 29:01 – Three audits: classification clarity, policy-reality alignment and behavior patterns
  • 37:09 – AI readiness: data clarity, the 30-60-90 roadmap, data entropy and permissions
  • 45:35 – Purview as the bridge to the boardroom and the strategic imperative

Key takeaways

  • Microsoft Purview is more than a compliance checkbox: label distributions, DLP violations and insider risk signals form a diagnostic map of how the organization really operates.
  • Overclassification, clustered DLP violations and access spikes usually point to unclear priorities, broken processes and overloaded teams, not to reckless or malicious people.
  • AI readiness is a governance problem, so discovery, auto-labeling, permission alignment and AI-ready data zones in a 30-60-90 day plan are what make Copilot safe and valuable.

Worth quoting
