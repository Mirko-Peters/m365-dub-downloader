Many business users think AI needs engineers and months of setup. This Simply Explained episode is for Power Platform beginners who want to use AI Builder inside Power Apps and Power Automate. You will learn which models fit which tasks, how document processing and prediction work, and how credits and licensing affect your plans.

In this episode

  • 00:31 – What AI Builder actually is
  • 02:13 – Ready-made models versus models you build
  • 04:10 – Why this is not just another chatbot
  • 05:17 – What document processing really does
  • 06:50 – How prediction works
  • 09:23 – What credits are and why they matter
  • 11:10 – Where AI Builder fits in real work

Key takeaways

  • AI Builder adds task-focused AI models to Power Apps and Power Automate, so you can automate business chores without writing code.
  • Use pre-built models for common, generic problems and train custom models such as document processing when your documents or data are specific to your business.
  • Building and testing models is free, but running them in a live app or flow consumes credits and makes the flow or app premium.
