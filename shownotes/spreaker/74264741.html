Sales forecasts often live in scattered spreadsheets and hopeful guesses. This episode shows sellers, sales managers and leaders how Dynamics 365 Sales forecasting turns opportunity data into one shared view of expected revenue, confidence and risk. You will learn how forecast categories, role-based views and AI signals work, and why honest updates matter most.

  • 13:50 – AI, data quality and human judgment
  • 17:25 – A simple forecasting rhythm
  • 18:03 – Conclusion

Key takeaways

  • Dynamics 365 Sales builds the forecast from opportunity records, so amount, close date, owner and forecast category must stay current.
  • Forecast categories (pipeline, best case, committed, omitted) give the team a shared confidence language, while won and lost are set automatically on closing.
  • AI scores and rollups support the forecast, but human context and honest weekly reviews decide whether the number can be trusted.

Worth quoting

“Think of the target, often called quota, as the line your sales team aims to cross. The forecast shows the path toward that line based on what the team knows right now.” – Mirko Peters (00:48)

“That doesn't mean the score knows the customer better than you do. It just gives you and your manager another signal to consider before you spend time on the next call or forecast review.” – Mirko Peters (14:40)
