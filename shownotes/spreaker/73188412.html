Many professionals use Excel every day without realizing how far it can take their careers. In this interview, Microsoft MVP Karen Abecia tells Excel users, data enthusiasts and educators how spreadsheets, dashboards and AI tools like Copilot shaped her journey and what makes data presentation truly stand out.

In this episode

  • 00:00 – Welcome: Karen Abecia, Excel educator and Microsoft MVP
  • 02:29 – How Excel changed her life, and the two Excel tattoos
  • 09:44 – Is Excel dead? Answering the critics
  • 11:36 – Good dashboards, the wow effect and data storytelling
  • 22:11 – Copilot, AI and the end of coding barriers
  • 26:47 – Excel and Power BI as partners
  • 35:25 – Teaching, community and becoming an MVP
  • 53:03 – Quick-fire round and wrap-up

Key takeaways

  • Karen Abecia built a global career as an Excel educator without a university degree, driven by continuous learning and a focus on visual, well-presented spreadsheets.
  • In her view, great dashboards come from small details such as alignment, consistent colors and correct text, not just numbers and charts, and Excel and Power BI complement each other.
  • She is still building her working relationship with Copilot in Excel, and she sees the Microsoft MVP award as recognition for years of helping people with data.

Worth quoting

“Excel don't have a lot of things that Power BI have. So they are complementary things.” – Karen Abecia (28:56)
