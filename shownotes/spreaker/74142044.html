HR information often lives in spreadsheets, inboxes and separate tools, so every change creates another outdated copy. This Knowledge Nugget explains Dynamics 365 Human Resources for HR teams, managers and IT, covering employee records, self-service, leave, benefits, workflows and integrations in plain English.

Key takeaways

  • Dynamics 365 Human Resources gives each employee one controlled record with job details, skills, certifications and reporting lines, protected by roles and permissions.
  • Self-service and Microsoft Teams integration let employees check leave balances and submit requests while managers approve them through defined workflows, reducing email chains.
  • The product connects with payroll, recruiting, learning tools and Power BI rather than replacing them, so companies should define their HR processes first and configure the software around them.

Worth quoting

“Still, self-service doesn't replace HR. HR professionals still answer difficult questions, apply policy, handle sensitive cases, and help people through changes that don't fit a standard form.” – Mirko Peters (09:19)

“Records tell you what happened. Workflows help decide what happens next. A workflow is simply a set route for a task.” – Mirko Peters (13:42)
