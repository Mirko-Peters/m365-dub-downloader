Most AI talk targets desk workers, yet the technicians, installers and inspectors in the field still drown in paperwork with little connectivity. Microsoft MVP Fergus Kidd, co-founder and CTO of FieldPal AI, shows builders and IT leaders how agentic AI on Azure AI Foundry, small language models and edge thinking can turn a short voice conversation into a complete, clean report.

In this episode

  • 00:00 – From the ExoMars rover to AI at the edge
  • 06:25 – Why frontline workers deserve more attention
  • 09:20 – Connectivity, voice and wearable devices
  • 13:08 – HoloLens, smart glasses and enterprise hardware
  • 21:08 – Why FieldPal AI was founded
  • 28:06 – Agentic AI on Azure AI Foundry
  • 38:08 – Small language models, RAG and computer vision
  • 53:08 – Rapid fire and advice: go and try it

Key takeaways

  • Frontline workers make up most of the workforce but are rarely the focus of AI, and they need voice-first, offline-capable tools that fit how they actually work.
  • Agentic AI on Azure AI Foundry lets one conversational interface orchestrate specialised agents for knowledge lookup, note taking and report filling, improving data quality at the point of entry.
  • Small language models grounded in curated data via Azure AI Search can be more reliable than large general models, provided the right information and good prompts go in.

Worth quoting

“if we've got the ability to do stuff at the edge, we kind of should, because it's quicker, faster, cheaper, and more resilient in terms of your closer to the person you're trying to help.” – Fergus Kidd (04:48)

“So the beautiful thing about small language models is that they don't always have this kind of inherent confidence that they know everything because their training sets are much smaller.” – Fergus Kidd (39:05)
