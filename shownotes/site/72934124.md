## FAQ

**How is Copilot Cowork different from Microsoft 365 Copilot?**

Copilot Cowork is task-based, while regular Copilot is chat-based. You give Cowork a goal and it plans and executes the steps across Word, Excel, PowerPoint, Outlook, Teams and SharePoint, running for minutes or hours in the background. It can also run recurring tasks, which regular Copilot cannot do on its own.

**What do I need to use Copilot Cowork?**

You need a Microsoft 365 business or enterprise subscription and a Copilot license; personal accounts do not work. Files must live in OneDrive or SharePoint. Depending on availability, an admin may need to enroll in the Frontier program and enable Anthropic as a subprocessor, and in the European Union Cowork is off by default.

**Does Copilot Cowork send emails or change meetings without asking?**

No, Copilot Cowork asks for your approval before sensitive actions such as sending an email, scheduling a meeting or updating your calendar. You see what it plans to do and can change it first. In the Organize My Week example it asks questions about priorities and absences before shortening meetings or booking a one-on-one.
