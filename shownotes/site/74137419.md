## FAQ

**What is Dynamics 365 Customer Insights - Data?**

Dynamics 365 Customer Insights - Data is Microsoft's customer data platform, or CDP. It pulls customer data from sales, service, websites, order systems and other approved sources into one environment, connects records that belong to the same person and builds a lasting unified profile that other business tools and teams can use.

**What is the difference between Customer Insights - Data and Customer Insights - Journeys?**

Customer Insights - Data brings information together, builds profiles and finds patterns, while Customer Insights - Journeys uses those profiles and audiences to send emails, texts and other communications. The episode compares Data to a records room and Journeys to a communications desk: one answers who the customer is, the other what communication should happen next.

**How does Customer Insights - Data match duplicate customer records?**

It matches records using conditions you define after mapping incoming columns. You can match on email address, on a combination of name, phone number and postal details, or on a customer number. Duplicates inside each source are removed first, and records are only joined when there is enough evidence they belong to the same person.
