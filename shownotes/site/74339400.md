## FAQ

**What does Dynamics 365 Inventory Management track for each item?**

It tracks five things for every item: how much you have, where it sits, whether you can use it, who owns it and what it costs. The episode explains how product records, units of measure, product variants and inventory dimensions such as site, warehouse, location, batch and serial number keep those details apart.

**What is the difference between inventory management and warehouse management in Dynamics 365?**

Inventory management holds the company-wide stock record, while warehouse management guides the physical work on the warehouse floor. Inventory answers how much you have, where it is recorded, whether you can use it and what it is worth. Warehouse management directs receiving, put-away, picking and loading, often with mobile barcode scanning and license plate numbers.

**Which costing methods does Dynamics 365 use to value inventory?**

The episode covers FIFO, weighted average, moving average and standard cost. FIFO treats the oldest stock as leaving first, which suits perishable goods. Weighted average blends all available units into one cost, moving average recalculates whenever stock arrives at a new price, and standard cost records differences between a planned cost and the actual price.
