## FAQ

**Why can't ERP build an optimal production schedule?**

ERP plans against infinite capacity. It backward-schedules each order from its due date through the routing without reserving finite machine time against competing orders. That makes its dates useful signals for material and demand planning, but several orders can land on the same machine at the same hour, and capacity reports only detect the overload.

**What is finite capacity scheduling?**

Finite scheduling treats each resource as having only the time it can actually provide. An operation reserves a real slot on a machine and is checked against fixtures, tools and qualified operators in the same window. When no legal slot exists, the schedule moves work or exposes the risk to a customer date instead of overloading the resource.

**Can AI or Copilot create a production schedule?**

Not on its own. Generative AI can explain schedule changes, retrieve procedures and draft explanations, while predictive models estimate durations, failure risk or late deliveries. Choosing a legal sequence needs deterministic optimization that obeys stated constraints. Humans approve changes with operational impact, and assistants need permission-aware access and clear limits on the actions they can take.
