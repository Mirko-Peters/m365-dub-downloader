## FAQ

**How does Microsoft 365 Copilot treat sensitivity labels in its answers?**

Copilot inherits the highest sensitivity of the sources it used, within what the user may access, and marks its answer accordingly. In this design it cites sources with version IDs, cannot lower or remove labels, does not delete versions, and explains a block by naming the DLP policy behind it.

**How do you connect Dynamics 365 records to SharePoint document libraries?**

Each entity form gets a documents pane with working and published views plus a read-only records trace. A flow provisions the document location on a deterministic path using the opportunity, project or case ID, and stage gates refuse steps such as closing a deal when no approved contract sits in published.

**What does a monthly Microsoft Purview governance cadence look like?**

It runs in four beats: see, decide, adjust, teach. Teams review activity explorer, DSPM for AI, communication compliance and insider risk trends, then stewards decide fixes in a short meeting. Admins apply policy changes within the week, and banners and short notes explain the new rules where people work.
