## FAQ

**Why does meeting-room audio matter more than video for Copilot?**

Because Copilot treats meeting audio as real-time data for summaries, action items, documents and emails. Josh Blalock argues that audio which is merely good enough for people can still limit what Copilot understands. If video fails, the meeting continues, but if audio fails, the meeting is over and the AI loses its source.

**What does Microsoft Teams certification mean for meeting-room devices?**

It means a device passed Microsoft's rigorous testing for its category, including quality benchmarks and required features, and room systems are certified for specific room sizes. Non-certified devices can still join Teams meetings, but Microsoft does not support that scenario, so certification also determines whether you get help with audio problems.

**How should organizations plan meeting rooms with difficult acoustics?**

Work with an integrator or consultant and test equipment in your own rooms through a proof of concept instead of comparing specs. Josh recommends judging how audio and transcripts actually sound, considering acoustic panels and furniture, and choosing hardware with onboard DSPs that remove reverb and noise before audio reaches Teams.
