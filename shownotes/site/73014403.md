## FAQ

**What is the difference between DevOps and platform engineering?**

DevOps taught every team to own its own infrastructure, while platform engineering puts a central team in place to absorb that complexity. The platform team builds paved roads, meaning standard, tested and secure ways to handle common tasks, and product teams simply use them. The episode argues the DevOps approach led to developer overload and burnout.

**What is a golden path in platform engineering?**

A golden path is a recommended, pre-built workflow that makes the right thing the easiest thing to do. It is tested, documented and supported by the platform team, but not mandatory. Developers need escape hatches to step off it, and every time they leave the path it signals a missing feature or an unsuitable use case.

**How do you start building an internal developer platform?**

Start with one golden path, not a whole platform. Talk to the developers writing features every day, find the workflow that hurts most, such as creating and deploying a new microservice, and build that path end to end. Then test it with real users, iterate, and measure time to first deploy and voluntary adoption.
