## FAQ

**What is the difference between Customer Service workspace and Copilot Service workspace?**

They are the same agent experience under two names. Microsoft now calls Customer Service workspace Copilot Service workspace, so older videos, documents or your own environment may show either name. It is a newer multisession desk for agents, unlike the older Customer Service Hub, which focused more on one session at a time.

**How many sessions and tabs does Copilot Service workspace support?**

Copilot Service workspace supports up to nine sessions at one time, with up to ten tabs inside each session. Each session keeps one customer issue together, including the case, customer record, product and knowledge articles, so agents can switch between issues without rebuilding context or typing notes on the wrong record.

**How does unified routing decide which agent gets a case?**

Unified routing checks the company's business rules and sends incoming work to the right queue or agent based on skills, presence and capacity. It asks who has the right skill, is available and is not overloaded. Teams choose push assignment, where work is sent automatically, or pick assignment, where agents select from a list.
