## FAQ

**How do you find good use cases for Copilot Studio agents?**

Ask which parts of your day you dislike or feel you should not be doing, and which tasks you would hand to an assistant. Kayode Ajayi explains that AI goes beyond classic automation of repetitive work, because its reasoning can handle scenarios that need intelligence, while humans still make the final decisions.

**How do you measure whether an AI investment delivers business value?**

Start with time saved: if people take longer to do the same work with AI, the project has failed. Kayode Ajayi also tracks adoption, because a tool nobody uses saves nothing, and recommends planning adoption and change management into every AI implementation so the right KPIs and real usage are measured from the start.

**Why start with Copilot Studio when your organizational data is poorly governed?**

Copilot Studio lets you point an agent at specific knowledge sources and turn off Work IQ, so it never touches the rest of your data. Microsoft 365 Copilot draws on organizational data broadly and can expose oversharing, such as HR content reachable through a SharePoint site membership, before governance has been sorted out.
