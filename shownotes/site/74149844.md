## FAQ

**When does Dynamics 365 Guides reach end of availability?**

Microsoft has confirmed that Dynamics 365 Guides and Dynamics 365 Remote Assist will not be available after December 31, 2026. Teams still using Guides should start with an inventory of which guides people rely on, where they connect to work orders or maintenance records, and which training material exists only inside the current setup.

**How do you create a guide in Dynamics 365 Guides?**

An expert builds the step sequence in the Dynamics 365 Guides PC app, adding short instructions, photos, video clips and 3D objects. Then the author wears a HoloLens in the real work area and places each card or model where the worker will need it. Operator feedback is used to update and retest the guide.

**What did Copilot in Dynamics 365 Guides do?**

Copilot in Dynamics 365 Guides let workers point at a component and ask questions in plain language during a task. Announced at Microsoft Ignite 2023, it went into private preview with limited capabilities. It searched company-approved material such as technical documents and expert notes, so answers were only as good as that source content.
