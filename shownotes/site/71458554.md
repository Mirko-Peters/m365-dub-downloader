## FAQ

**How do you measure the ROI of Copilot and AI training?**

Measure reclaimed time, decision velocity and one role-specific quality metric inside named workflows, against a real baseline. The episode's formula is time saved multiplied by hourly cost and adoption rate, plus the value of fewer errors and faster decisions, minus the upskilling investment. Completion rates and badges only show that training happened.

**Which roles benefit most from AI upskilling?**

Finance analysts, project managers and frontline support teams show the highest ROI density, because their work is repetitive, document-heavy and full of small decisions. In the episode's examples, a monthly reporting cycle moves from five days to two, weekly project prep from six hours to two, and support handling time from 12 minutes to seven.

**How should companies scale AI from pilot to operating model?**

Start with three to five workflows per target role, capture a baseline, train people on the task itself and run short pilots with hard success gates. Expand only when reclaimed hours, shorter cycle times and stable quality actually show up, then move into adjacent workflows instead of rolling out blanket licensing across the company.
