## FAQ

**What is the difference between connectivity and integration in manufacturing?**

Connectivity moves signals reliably, while integration connects meaning. OPC UA, MQTT and edge gateways can deliver a machine alarm to every subscriber, but integration links that event to the asset, active operation, work order, material, quality state and customer consequence. Only then can a planner see which shipment is at risk without phone calls and spreadsheets.

**Is a unified namespace enough to integrate a factory?**

No. A unified namespace is a better highway, not the factory map. It gives systems a shared, structured event space, often organized loosely along ISA-95 from enterprise to machine, and removes point-to-point plumbing. But a neat topic hierarchy does not explain what depends on an alarm or what the team should consider next.

**What does Azure Digital Twins add to an industrial data architecture?**

Azure Digital Twins provides a managed graph for operational relationships, modeled in DTDL through properties, components and relationships. Applications can start at an asset and follow links to equipment, signals and process areas. High-volume readings stay in a time series path, and data history can record graph changes in Azure Data Explorer. It doesn't model your factory for you.
