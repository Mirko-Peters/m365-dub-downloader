## FAQ

**Why does Copilot usage drop after the first trainings?**

Usage often drops because a few general, feature-led trainings do not build habits. Edyta sees usage peaks after training sessions followed by a decline. She recommends a change management approach based on ADKAR, checking user sentiment, scenario-based training for each department and flexible project scope that reacts to measured Copilot consumption.

**What does successful Microsoft 365 Copilot adoption look like?**

Successful adoption means most users work with Copilot daily as a habit. Edyta says she starts to be happy around 80% active users, checked in the Microsoft 365 admin center dashboards. Users should also understand how Copilot behaves across apps like Teams, Outlook and Word, and feel motivated to experiment as features change.

**How do you build a Copilot champions program?**

Edyta prefers announcing a Copilot champions program and letting people apply voluntarily, after explaining the role, benefits and responsibilities. Some organizations instead have managers appoint champions. She involves champions from the project kickoff, shares user sentiment and goals with them, and trains them through train-the-trainer sessions so they keep supporting colleagues.
