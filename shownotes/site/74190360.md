## FAQ

**What is the difference between basic and enhanced relationship insights in Dynamics 365 Sales?**

Basic insights use emails, phone calls and appointments recorded in Dynamics 365 and update close to real time once an activity is marked complete. Enhanced insights need the right Sales license, admin setup and server-side synchronization with Exchange, add email and meeting data from there, and refresh every 24 hours.

**How is relationship health determined in Dynamics 365 Sales?**

Relationship health is built from the communication signals around a record: activity, how recent it is, engagement from both sides and the sentiment in those activities. It shows as good, fair or poor on opportunity forms, list views and charts, and administrators can adjust the weights of activity types and the expected level of communication.

**How does who knows whom help find a warm introduction?**

Who knows whom looks for colleagues who already communicated with a lead or contact and shows their name and email address. Basic insights suggest up to five colleagues based on how often they emailed or met the person, and phone calls do not count. Enhanced insights with Exchange data also weigh how recent the contact is.
