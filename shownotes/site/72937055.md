## FAQ

**What do you need to use Copilot in Dynamics 365 Finance and Operations?**

You need a recent F&O version, since older ones are a hard block, Power Platform and Dataverse integration enabled, the relevant features switched on in feature management, a cloud-hosted environment and a Microsoft 365 Copilot license on top of your D365 license. On-premises deployments do not qualify, so those customers must move to the cloud first.

**How does Copilot help with collections in D365 Finance and Operations?**

It summarizes a customer's overdue invoices, payment history, aged balances and risk in the collections workspace, then drafts a personalized reminder email you review and send. Through the Copilot for Finance add-in this also works from Outlook, and collection activity syncs back to F&O automatically, turning a long lookup into seconds.

**Can Copilot post journal entries or approve payments on its own?**

No, Copilot does not make journal entries or approve payments by itself. It suggests accounts and descriptions, flags reconciliation discrepancies and explains variances, but people review and decide. It also respects existing security roles, so a collections agent sees only their own customers while controllers see the full ledger.
