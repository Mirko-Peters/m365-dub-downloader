This episode is for IT beginners and decision-makers and explains how Intune enrolls and secures devices, protects data on personal phones and feeds Conditional Access for Zero Trust.

## Key takeaways

- Microsoft Intune is a cloud service that enrolls devices, enforces configuration and compliance policies, deploys apps and tracks device health across Windows, macOS, iOS, Android and some Linux devices.
- Mobile Application Management protects work data inside apps like [Outlook](https://www.m365.fm/entity/outlook/?utm_source=podcast&utm_medium=shownotes&utm_campaign=microsoft-intune-simply-explained), Teams and OneDrive on personal phones, so IT can wipe corporate data without touching personal content.
- Intune reports device health to Entra ID, where Conditional Access combines identity and device signals into Zero Trust access decisions.

## FAQ

**What is the difference between MDM and Mobile Application Management in Intune?**

Device management controls the whole device, while Mobile Application Management controls only work apps such as Outlook, Teams and OneDrive. On personal phones, MAM blocks copying work data into personal apps and lets IT wipe only corporate data. Personal photos, messages and contacts stay untouched, which makes bring your own device workable.

**How does Intune work with Conditional Access to block unhealthy devices?**

Intune reports whether a device is compliant to Entra ID, and Conditional Access uses that signal in its access decision. In the episode's example, a user enters the correct password and passes MFA, but the laptop is missing a security update, so access to email is blocked until it is installed.

**How do company-owned devices get enrolled in Intune without IT touching them?**

Windows devices use Windows Autopilot, and Apple devices use Apple Business Manager. With Autopilot, a laptop shipped straight to the employee is recognized by its hardware hash after they enter their work email, and Intune applies configuration and installs apps. iPhones bought through authorized resellers enroll before the home screen appears.
