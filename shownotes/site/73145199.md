## FAQ

**What is a delta link in Microsoft Graph?**

A delta link is a saved URL containing a delta token that marks where your last sync stopped. The first delta call returns all data plus this link; calling it later returns only added, updated or deleted items and a fresh link. A next link instead means more pages remain before you are caught up.

**What does the HTTP 410 Gone error mean for a delta query?**

It means Microsoft Graph no longer recognizes your delta token, so you must run a full resync. Graph can invalidate tokens during internal maintenance or tenant migrations, even before they expire. The episode advises treating 410 as a normal part of the lifecycle: catch it, resync and store the new delta link.

**Should I use delta queries or change notifications?**

Use both. Change notifications give near real-time awareness that something changed, and the app then calls the delta query with its saved link to fetch exactly what changed. This avoids wasted polling and keeps the reliability of delta tokens. On their own, notifications suit low-latency alerts and delta queries suit bulk or offline sync.
