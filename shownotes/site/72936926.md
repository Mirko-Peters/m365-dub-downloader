## FAQ

**What can Copilot in Microsoft Entra ID do for sign-in troubleshooting?**

It answers a plain-English question like why a user could not sign in to Teams by pulling sign-in logs, Conditional Access evaluation and device compliance together. Instead of raw error codes, you get a short explanation, such as a policy requiring a compliant device, plus follow-up prompts for more context like recent failures.

**Does Copilot in Entra ID bypass admin permissions?**

No, Copilot inherits your existing Entra roles and permissions, so it cannot see logs or resources you are not allowed to view. Conditional Access applies to it as well, prompts and responses are recorded in the audit logs, and it only suggests actions such as password resets, which an admin must approve.

**How does Copilot help investigate risky users in Entra ID Protection?**

It summarizes a flagged user's risk detections, such as unfamiliar sign-in properties or leaked credentials, and correlates them with recent sign-in activity into a short narrative. It then recommends next steps tailored to those detections, like resetting the password, blocking sign-ins or confirming whether the activity was legitimate.
