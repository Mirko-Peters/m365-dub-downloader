## FAQ

**Why is having many Global Admins in Microsoft 365 a risk?**

Every Global Admin can change any setting, reset executive passwords, read any data and disable security policies with one click. The role was designed as a break-glass emergency lever but is handed out for convenience. The episode cites research that many firms have over 100 Global Admins, creating a shadow leadership layer.

**Does Microsoft 365 Copilot create new data security risks?**

Not by itself, according to the episode. Copilot inherits the permissions of the person asking, so it only surfaces content that person could already reach. What changes is speed: oversharing through broad groups, broken inheritance and anyone links that once stayed buried in deep folders now shows up in seconds as summarized answers.

**How can you reduce Global Admin accounts in 30 days?**

Spend the first two weeks inventorying every standing Global Admin, privileged role admin and service principal with tenant-wide rights. In the final two weeks, cut standing Global Admins by at least 80 percent and move to just-in-time access that is requested, justified and expires. Finish with no more than two break-glass accounts.
