## FAQ

**Should I choose Direct Routing or Operator Connect for Teams Phone?**

Choose Operator Connect if you are new to Teams voice and just want something that works, and Direct Routing if you want full control and flexibility. Direct Routing uses your own provider and a certified session border controller, while Operator Connect has limits, such as numbers tied to the user's country location.

**Should Teams Rooms run on Android or Windows?**

Most large rollouts Carsten sees use Android, because it is simple, cheap and fast to deploy, while Windows is chosen for more complex rooms. New features usually arrive on Windows first, and Windows with Autopilot allows zero-touch provisioning, which Android devices cannot do at the moment, according to Carsten.

**What is the most common mistake when deploying Microsoft Teams Rooms?**

The most common mistake is integrating meeting rooms without fully understanding security and compliance, and not involving the whole IT organization. Room accounts must sign in correctly, handle MFA and fit Intune compliance policies, which often need exclusions because room devices cannot meet rules designed for user devices, such as PIN codes.
