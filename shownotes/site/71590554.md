## FAQ

**What is digital debt in a Microsoft 365 Copilot rollout?**

Digital debt is the build-up of unverified, unmanaged AI-generated artifacts that outgrows people's capacity to make sense of them. Mirko Peters argues that Copilot often moves work from creation to validation, which is more expensive. Multiple drafts, summaries of summaries and conflicting recaps fragment the truth, so meetings turn into debates about which version is correct.

**Why are AI-built Power Automate flows risky?**

AI-built flows can fail silently because their makers often don't understand the underlying architecture and the logic sits in one person's account. The episode describes an invoice flow that broke after a format change and went unnoticed for three weeks, and an archive flow that kept deleting files. Each AI-assisted flow should have a human co-owner.

**What is decision velocity and how do you measure it?**

Decision velocity is the time from a business question to a trusted, logged and actionable decision, and it replaces time saved as the main metric. The episode tracks three signals: decision cycle time between AI output and human acceptance, decision reversals caused by flawed AI input, and confidence lag, visible in how many shadow spreadsheets people keep.
