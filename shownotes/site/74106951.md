## FAQ

**What is the difference between a segment-based and a trigger-based journey?**

A segment-based journey starts at a time the business chooses for a defined group, such as a newsletter, event invitation or product announcement. A trigger-based journey starts when a customer does something, like submitting a form, requesting a demo or abandoning a cart, so the timing follows that action rather than a calendar.

**Which channels does Customer Insights - Journeys support?**

It handles email, text messages and push notifications within one journey. Email suits longer information such as guides or event confirmations, text messages suit short reminders like appointment times, and push notifications reach people who use the company app and allow notifications. Each channel should fit the moment and the customer's agreed preferences.

**Can Copilot write marketing emails in Customer Insights - Journeys?**

Yes, Copilot can draft emails from plain-language instructions, describe audiences and brainstorm journey ideas, but a person still has to review the work. Read content before it goes out, check the customer fields used for personalization and the journey rules, and ask whether the message would make sense after that customer action.
