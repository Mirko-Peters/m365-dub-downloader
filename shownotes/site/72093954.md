## FAQ

**What does AI readiness mean for Microsoft 365?**

For Christian Buckley, AI readiness means three steps: clean up your data, clean up your permissions and put guardrails in place. He also recommends building agents around specific SharePoint environments, such as a single library, so you can strictly control what each agent can and cannot see.

**What is the first governance step before a Copilot pilot?**

Start with discovery of the sprawl. Christian would first assess what exists, including agents visible through Microsoft Agent 365, then clean up content and permissions and put guardrails in place. Instead of simply shutting down unsanctioned agents, he recommends talking with their creators about what they need and how to support them.

**Does locking down Microsoft 365 reduce oversharing risk?**

No, Christian argues that a full lockdown raises risk. People then send files and multiple versions by email, store them in Dropbox or Google Drive and create shadow IT that nobody monitors. He prefers guardrails such as approved Teams and SharePoint templates with labels and lifecycle built in, plus regular external user reviews.
