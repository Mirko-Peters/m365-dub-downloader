## FAQ

**What is Copilot flex routing and can I turn it off?**

Flex routing lets Copilot process inference outside the EU, in the United States, Canada or Australia, when EU capacity is constrained. According to the episode, Microsoft enabled it by default for EU and EFTA tenants in April 2026. Administrators can disable it in the Microsoft 365 admin center under Copilot settings and data location.

**How much cheaper are small language models than GPT-4?**

Hundreds of times cheaper per token at the cloud API rates quoted in the episode. Phi-3.5-mini costs about $0.10 per million tokens, versus roughly $30 for GPT-4 input and $60 for output. Tiered routing architectures reportedly cut total LLM spend by 40 to 90%, and self-hosting can widen the gap further.

**Which Copilot tasks can a small language model handle?**

High-volume, narrow tasks such as email summarization, meeting action items, document classification, routine drafting and simple Excel formula help. These tasks reason over content already in the prompt, so a model like Phi-3-mini performs well. Frontier models remain the better choice for multi-document synthesis, legal drafting and other high-stakes, low-volume work.
