## FAQ

**Can you upgrade from Business Central to Dynamics 365 Finance and Operations?**

No, there is no upgrade path; moving from Business Central to Finance and Operations is a full re-implementation. The two systems come from different lineages, NAV and AX, with different data models, posting logic and dimensions, so data must be extracted, transformed, loaded and validated, and business processes redesigned, at implementation cost and timeline.

**When does it make sense to move from Business Central to Finance and Operations?**

The episode names a threshold: more than three separate Business Central instances feeding one consolidation, or intercompany transactions above roughly 30 percent of BC general ledger volume. Beyond that point, maintaining the integration patchwork tends to cost more than migrating, because every acquisition adds mappings, elimination rules and slower closes.

**Does dual-write connect Business Central with Finance and Operations?**

No, dual-write was built to connect Finance and Operations with customer engagement apps like Sales, not Business Central. Business Central uses its own Dataverse sync, which runs on a schedule, by default every 30 minutes and up to 12 hours when idle, so subsidiaries and headquarters end up stitching two separate integration systems together.
