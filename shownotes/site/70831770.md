## FAQ

**Why does Copilot adoption stall after the first few weeks?**

Adoption often stalls around weeks 6 to 12 because the tool moves from novelty into high-stakes work. Edge cases accumulate, such as summaries pulling stale or wrong documents, and one bad answer under pressure outweighs many good ones. People then verify everything, use Copilot only for low-risk tasks, and stop delegating real confidence to it.

**How does oversharing in SharePoint affect Copilot answers?**

Oversharing lets Copilot pull old, sensitive or out-of-scope content into ordinary answers, because it honors whatever access a user already has. Beyond exposure risk, this degrades relevance and can distort decisions. Mirko Peters therefore treats least privilege as relevance engineering: aligning access with current responsibility narrows the context an answer is built from.

**What should organizations measure instead of Copilot usage?**

Measure decision latency, meaning how long it takes a person or team to move from a question to a confident action. Prompt counts and active licenses only show activity. If a summary arrives in seconds but validation takes much longer, overall latency has worsened. Success means faster decisions without any loss of trust.
