## FAQ

**What is governance debt in Microsoft 365?**

Governance debt is the accumulated cost of postponed control decisions, such as delayed reviews, vague ownership and exceptions that were never normalized. Mirko Peters locates it in five areas: access, lifecycle, logging, ownership and evidence. It looks like progress while it grows, then surfaces as manual effort when an audit, a Copilot rollout or an incident demands proof.

**How long does Microsoft Entra keep audit and sign-in logs by default?**

According to the episode, Entra identity logs may cover only seven days in free tiers or 30 days in paid subscriptions unless you export them. Exporting to Log Analytics or using Purview Audit extends that memory, but neither works retroactively, so any history from before you set them up is already gone.

**How can an organization measure its audit readiness?**

Measure audit preparation time: how long it takes to produce defensible evidence for a serious control question. A few hours suggests evidence is a byproduct of normal operations, while four weeks or more signals manual coordination and dependence on individual people. Tracking this number every quarter shows whether the proof architecture is actually getting stronger.
