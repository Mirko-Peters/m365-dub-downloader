## FAQ

**How should SharePoint sites and libraries be structured to stay audit ready?**

Use one site per mission and one library per vault instead of nested folders. Each site gets a charter, a named owner and a backup owner, and renewal reviews. Libraries are named by business outcome, such as working drafts, published corpus and records, with central content types and five to seven metadata fields.

**Should Microsoft Purview DLP policies start in block mode?**

No, the episode recommends starting DLP in simulation mode, learning the traffic and tuning rules before enforcing. A suggested first step is a baseline credit card policy across Exchange, SharePoint, OneDrive and Teams with user tips, reviewed for seven days, then blocking external destinations with an override that requires business justification.

**How can you hand an auditor an approved, redacted contract in five minutes?**

Run a rehearsed drill. Filter content explorer by the restricted label and the executed contract content type, open the record to confirm headers, watermarks and encryption, show version history and the approver column, check the retention label details, then search in eDiscovery Premium, apply redaction in a review set and export a manifest.
