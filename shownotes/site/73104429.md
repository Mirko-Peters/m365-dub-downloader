## FAQ

**Is Exchange Online Protection included in Microsoft 365?**

Yes, EOP is the built-in email security layer that comes with every Microsoft 365 plan at no extra cost. It is always on, even if an admin never opens the admin center, and filters spam, known malware and basic phishing in the cloud, with no servers to maintain or software to update.

**What does zero-hour auto purge (ZAP) do in Exchange Online?**

ZAP removes already delivered messages when Microsoft's threat intelligence changes their verdict to spam, phishing or malware. It works within 48 hours of delivery and is enabled by default for every Exchange Online mailbox. As of early 2026 it also covers Microsoft Teams chat messages and the Deleted Items folder, where restorable threats used to remain.

**When do I need Defender for Office 365 instead of just EOP?**

You need Defender for Office 365 when targeted attacks such as business email compromise are a real risk. EOP has no sandboxing, no click-time link scanning and no impersonation protection. Plan 1 adds Safe Attachments, Safe Links and impersonation protection, while Plan 2 adds Threat Explorer and automated investigation and response.
