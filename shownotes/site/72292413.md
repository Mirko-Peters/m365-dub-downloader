## FAQ

**Why does Microsoft 365 Copilot hallucinate even with citations?**

Copilot hallucinates because retrieval hands the model conflicting, stale or weakly relevant chunks, and the model is trained to produce fluent answers rather than admit uncertainty. It then blends fragments into a new claim and cites a real document that does not support it, which the episode calls the citation illusion.

**How does permission sprawl make Copilot answers worse?**

Permission sprawl widens what Copilot can retrieve, so outdated or unrelated content enters answers. Users keep access to old sites, guests stay active after engagements end, and service principals reach many sites at once. Copilot has no intention filter, so a stale HR policy from a former role can mix with current guidance in one answer.

**How can you measure and reduce Copilot hallucinations over time?**

You measure them by tracking hallucination rate, citation accuracy and retrieval precision and recall, not just usage. The episode recommends a golden dataset as a regression gate before any change, human review of a small sample of production queries, drift alerts on key metrics, and feeding user-flagged errors back into diagnosis and fixes.
