## FAQ

**Why are Microsoft partners losing margin on implementation work?**

Because implementation has become a commodity. The episode points to CSP margin compression, large enterprise agreements moving directly to Microsoft, and automation such as Autopilot, Lighthouse and Copilot built into the interface. Customers now assume technical competence, compare partners on price, and can buy standardized deployment services from many providers.

**What is the tenant stewardship model for Microsoft partners?**

It is an operating model in which the partner runs the customer's tenant for economic outcomes on a retainer. It rests on three pillars: economic telemetry for cost-to-value visibility, architectural stewardship covering identity, permissions, governance and information architecture, and AI value governance that measures what Copilot actually changes in workflows.

**How should a partner measure Copilot value for a customer?**

By measuring which workflows change and how much time users save, not adoption percentages. The episode suggests looking at drafting, meeting summaries and analysis, calculating time saved per user against the license cost, and spotting departments where adoption stalls. That evidence justifies further investment or shows where the tool is not a good fit.
