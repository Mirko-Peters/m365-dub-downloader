## FAQ

**Is Microsoft 365 Copilot secure out of the box?**

Only as secure as your permissions. Copilot will not show users files they cannot access, but most tenants carry years of inherited rights, everyone links and stale sites. Copilot closes the gap between what people could find and what they actually see, so technically open sensitive documents become functionally public inside the tenant.

**What is a risk-tiered rollout for Microsoft 365 Copilot?**

It sequences licenses by data maturity and user risk instead of enabling everyone at once. Tier 0 is remediation with no licenses, tier 1 covers trusted IT and AI centre of excellence users, tier 2 broader knowledge workers, and tier 3 finance, HR and legal only after DLP and retention controls are proven.

**What is the difference between workload DLP and interaction DLP for Copilot?**

Workload DLP protects files and emails in SharePoint, OneDrive and mail, while interaction DLP evaluates prompts and generated responses. Without the second layer, a user who cannot share a sensitive file can still ask Copilot to summarize it and paste the text elsewhere, which file-based rules never see.
