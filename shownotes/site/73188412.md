## FAQ

**What makes a good Excel dashboard?**

For Karen Abecia, a good dashboard comes down to small details rather than numbers and charts alone. She points to alignment, consistent sizes and colors, title formatting and correct text, noting that most of her Brazilian students make wording mistakes. Her goal is a wow effect where viewers assume the dashboard was built in Power BI.

**Should you use Excel or Power BI?**

Karen Abecia sees Excel and Power BI as complementary partners rather than rivals. She regrets once comparing their learning curves, since companies do not give both tools to everyone. Excel offers a freedom she values, while Power BI has capabilities Excel lacks, so the right choice depends on the project, the company size and the subject.

**How did Karen Abecia become a Microsoft MVP?**

She received a first nomination about ten years ago, but nothing came of it, so she kept creating content and helping people. Six years ago she was nominated again and received the award. She describes it as Microsoft telling her she was doing a good job, and says it brought more respect from fellow teachers.
