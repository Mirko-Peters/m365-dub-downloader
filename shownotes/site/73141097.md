## FAQ

**How does the Microsoft Graph validation handshake work?**

When you create a subscription, Microsoft Graph sends a validation token to your notification URL, and your endpoint must return it as plain text within 10 seconds. JSON or XML responses fail. The check proves the endpoint is alive and under your control; without a successful handshake, Graph rejects the subscription request.

**How long does a Microsoft Graph subscription last?**

It depends on the resource. According to the episode, subscriptions for Outlook messages last up to seven days, Teams chat messages three days, SharePoint files about 30 days and presence information only one hour. Renew with a PATCH request before expiry, ideally whenever a notification shows the expiration is within a day.

**What happens if a Graph webhook endpoint responds too slowly?**

Graph delays or drops notifications. If more than 10% of responses take over three seconds in a 10-minute period, notifications are delayed by 10 minutes; if more than 15% exceed the 10-second timeout, the endpoint is marked as dropped for 10 minutes. Reply quickly with 202 Accepted and process asynchronously.
