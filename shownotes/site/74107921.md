## FAQ

**Do I need to replace my CRM to use Dynamics 365 Contact Center?**

No. Dynamics 365 Contact Center handles the live conversation while the customer record stays where it already lives. It works alongside Dynamics 365 Customer Service, Microsoft provides a Salesforce connector, and other customer platforms can connect through Power Automate, although that route needs careful setup and testing before agents rely on it.

**What is unified routing in Dynamics 365 Contact Center?**

Unified routing applies the same routing rules across voice, web chat, SMS, email and social messages. Each conversation goes into a queue that fits the issue, and the system picks an agent based on availability, skills, current workload and channel. Transfers and consultations keep the context already collected, so customers do not start over.

**What is the difference between Copilot and an AI agent in Contact Center?**

Copilot assists a human agent while they work, by suggesting replies, finding knowledge and drafting summaries and notes. An AI agent handles a defined task on its own, such as answering routine questions, collecting details or creating a case. In both cases people review the output and stay responsible for the service decisions.
