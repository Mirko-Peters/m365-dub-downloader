## FAQ

**Does Dynamics 365 Intelligent Order Management replace my warehouse, shipping or billing system?**

No, it coordinates them instead of replacing them. The online store still captures the order, the warehouse picks and packs, the carrier delivers and finance handles billing. Intelligent Order Management receives the order, checks the route, passes work to the right connected system and watches for updates, so manual handoffs between teams disappear.

**What is an orchestration flow in Intelligent Order Management?**

An orchestration flow is a visual map of what should happen as an order arrives and events come back from connected systems. Actions such as validate order header, send to fulfillment and send to invoice each have one job, while conditions, splitters and custom actions add alternative paths. A published flow must be stopped before it can be edited.

**How does Intelligent Order Management decide where an order ships from?**

Fulfillment optimization compares possible locations against rules the company sets, using live stock data from inventory visibility. Best does not always mean closest: a business may favor speed, lower shipping costs, fewer packages or protecting store stock. The same logic supports ship from store, collect in store and planned split orders when no single location fits.
