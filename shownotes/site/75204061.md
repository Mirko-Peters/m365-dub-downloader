## FAQ

**What is the difference between OCR and Content Understanding?**

OCR reads the characters on a page, while Content Understanding returns the business fields a process needs. OCR can capture a long number without knowing whether it is an invoice number, purchase order or phone number. Content Understanding uses a schema you define, such as supplier name, invoice date and total, to return labeled results.

**How does Content Understanding work with Power Automate?**

A Power Automate flow detects a new email attachment or SharePoint file, sends it to Content Understanding and receives the extracted fields. The flow then checks them against business rules, creates a Dataverse record, routes the item for approval, or assigns it to a reviewer in Power Apps when a field is missing or uncertain.

**Is a high confidence score enough to trust extracted document data?**

No. A confidence score estimates how sure the AI is about one field; it does not promise the value is correct. The episode recommends validation checks such as totals matching line items plus tax, known vendors, matching purchase orders and duplicate detection. High-risk fields like bank details or contract dates may need human confirmation every time.
