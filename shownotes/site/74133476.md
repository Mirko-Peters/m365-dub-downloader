## FAQ

**What is Dynamics 365 Project Operations used for?**

Dynamics 365 Project Operations runs the business side of customer projects. It connects project sales, planning, staffing, time, expenses, billing and financials around one shared project record. Service firms such as consultancies, design agencies, law firms or software companies use it so the promise made in a quote matches the work delivered and invoiced.

**Which billing models does Dynamics 365 Project Operations support?**

The episode covers time and materials, fixed price and milestone billing. Time and materials charges for hours worked and approved expenses, fixed price sets one amount for defined work, and milestone billing follows agreed checkpoints such as design approval or go-live. These rules sit in the project contract and guide a draft pro forma invoice.

**Should a small team use Project Operations or Microsoft Planner?**

For a simple internal task list, Planner is usually the better fit because it keeps the work light. Project Operations makes sense when a project involves customers, contracts, staff schedules, costs and invoices at the same time. The episode also warns that it can require careful setup, especially with differing billing rules, currencies and finance processes.
