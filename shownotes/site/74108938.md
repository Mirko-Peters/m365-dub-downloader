## FAQ

**What is the difference between Excel and Dynamics 365 Finance?**

Excel usually holds a copy of the numbers, while Dynamics 365 Finance holds the transactions that count in the company books. Excel stays useful for exploring numbers, quick models and one-off questions. The official record lives in Finance, together with context such as the supplier, what a payment covers, which part of the business paid and when.

**What does invoice matching mean in Dynamics 365 Finance?**

Matching means comparing the purchase order, the receipt and the supplier invoice before payment. Dynamics 365 Finance links these records, so finance can check that the company ordered, received and was billed for the same thing. If an invoice lists ten laptops but only eight arrived, the team has something specific to investigate instead of an email chain.

**What are financial dimensions in Dynamics 365 Finance?**

Financial dimensions are extra labels attached to an account entry, such as department, cost center, location, business unit or project. The main account says what kind of money movement happened, for example travel, while the dimensions show who spent it and where. Managers can then ask which department or project created specific costs this quarter.
