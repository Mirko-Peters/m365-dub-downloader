## FAQ

**What is Microsoft Entra Permissions Management?**

It is a cloud infrastructure entitlement management (CIEM) solution that discovers every identity and permission across Azure, AWS and GCP and compares granted permissions with those actually used. Microsoft acquired the technology with CloudKnox and folded it into the Entra family. It works in three phases: discover, remediate and monitor.

**What is the Permission Creep Index?**

The Permission Creep Index is a per-identity score showing how far permissions have drifted from least privilege, where higher means worse. It compares what an identity can do with what it actually uses. In the episode's example, one organization cut its average PCI from 80 percent to 25 percent after right-sizing.

**How is Entra Permissions Management different from PIM?**

PIM controls when privileged roles such as Global Administrator are active, while Permissions Management controls which permissions exist across all identities and clouds. PIM is licensed per user through Entra ID P2 or ID Governance; Permissions Management is priced per workload. Using PIM alone leaves non-admin and multi-cloud permission creep invisible.
