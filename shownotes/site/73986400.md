## FAQ

**What is an AI software factory?**

In Mark Smith's version, it is a team of nine AI agents that each fill one gated role in the DevOps loop. Scout gathers requirements, Arc designs the architecture, Cody writes code, Vera verifies, a security role runs red teaming, Rex manages releases and Wiki documents, while Tara orchestrates the hand-offs.

**What is a Ralph loop for AI agents?**

A Ralph loop tells an agent not to stop until the task is actually solved, instead of halting on a minor question. Mark says his system only became autonomous once these loops worked correctly, and he built an exit for agents that keep failing so a runaway loop cannot wedge the virtual machine running everything.

**How does Mark Smith govern his AI agents?**

He feeds Microsoft's published governance content into his system and puts strict gates on what needs his review. Through a CLI into Microsoft Graph his agents can read email and calendar, but none can write email. Only his assistant agent Rook may book appointments, and email drafts go to markdown files he sends himself.
