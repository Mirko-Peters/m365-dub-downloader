## FAQ

**What is the difference between Power Pages and Power Apps?**

Power Apps is for internal employees, while Power Pages builds websites for people outside your company, such as customers, partners, vendors or the public. Power Pages started as Power Apps portals and became a standalone Power Platform product at Build in May 2022, with its own licensing, Design Studio and private-by-default sites.

**How is Power Pages different from WordPress or Squarespace?**

Power Pages connects directly to live business data in Dataverse, while WordPress or Squarespace sites mainly show static marketing content. When a visitor submits a Power Pages form, a record is created in the same database your internal systems use, with no CSV export or manual entry, and Power Automate can trigger workflows from that change.

**How does Power Pages licensing work?**

Power Pages has standalone per-site, per-month licensing based on authenticated users and anonymous visitors. The authenticated plan is paid per user per month; the anonymous plan per site per month by traffic. Sites with both need both plans. A 30-day trial gives full access, and an expired trial site is suspended for seven days before removal.
