## FAQ

**Is Exchange Online secure by default?**

No. According to Thomas Stensitzki, a new Exchange Online tenant works like a default Exchange Server setup: email flows, but transport, anti-spam, anti-malware and anti-phishing settings run on defaults. Administrators still need to configure mail flow and protection for their organization's needs, and identity security for every user and service account comes first.

**How should break glass accounts be secured in Microsoft 365?**

Secure break glass accounts with a FIDO key instead of disabling MFA and relying on a very long password locked away somewhere. Avoid tying them to one administrator's personal Authenticator app, since that person may be on vacation. If admin sign-ins are restricted to certain locations, plan how to reach the tenant when those locations are offline.

**Do SPF, DKIM and DMARC protect my inbox from spoofed email?**

Not directly. SPF, DKIM and DMARC are configured for your own domain to protect the email you send, telling receivers which hosts may send for you and how to handle failures. Use a real DMARC policy such as quarantine or reject, analyze aggregate reports, and move third-party senders to subdomains.
