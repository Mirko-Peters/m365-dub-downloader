## FAQ

**Should I use a lakehouse or a warehouse in Microsoft Fabric?**

Use a lakehouse for unstructured data and large-scale Spark or data science work, and a warehouse for structured data managed with T-SQL. Both store structured data in Delta format in OneLake. Amit Chandak says that up to a few million rows performance is similar, so the team's preferred technology can decide.

**When should I use a OneLake shortcut instead of copying data?**

Use shortcuts whenever possible, because they reference data without making a physical copy. Amit explains that a shortcut also uses the capacity of the workspace it lives in, keeping Power BI reports separate from data engineering workloads, and helps split security. For cross-cloud sources, check egress costs before choosing shortcuts, mirroring or copying.

**Should I use Dataflow Gen2 or Spark notebooks to transform data in Fabric?**

Dataflow Gen2 suits small to medium data volumes and offers more than 200 connectors with Power Query transformations. For large-scale transformations, Amit prefers PySpark notebooks. His teams observed gains of around 80 percent in compute and time when converting Dataflow Gen1 to PySpark, though he has no direct Gen2 comparison.
