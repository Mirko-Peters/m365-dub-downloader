## FAQ

**Why do kaizen events often fail to deliver lasting improvement?**

Mostly because the PDCA check step lacks evidence. After the workshop, production pressure returns and the action tracker drifts away from daily work. Teams close tasks without testing whether a countermeasure held across other crews, product sequences and conditions, so the same loss returns, sometimes moved from setup time into restart, quality checks or waiting.

**Is OEE enough to find the root cause of production losses?**

No. OEE combines availability, performance and quality, so it shows that something changed but not what caused it. A lower score can come from longer stops, slower cycles, rejected units or an outdated target rate. The episode recommends separating the loss modes, asking when each one occurred, and then testing a specific countermeasure.

**How do you measure whether a changeover improvement really worked?**

Measure changeover time, time to stable speed, and units reworked or rejected after restart, broken down by format, crew and tool condition. In the episode's packaging line scenario, average changeover time dropped while rejects rose for one product family, so judging by setup time alone would have shifted loss from availability to quality.
