## FAQ

**Is Google Sheets better than Excel?**

Neither tool wins outright, says Excel MVP David Benaim. Excel leads with Power Query, Power Pivot and the Microsoft ecosystem, so he still favors it. Google Sheets, however, has more functions, a group-by view for tables, the QUERY function and easier real-time collaboration, so he sometimes recommends it to clients for specific jobs.

**Why is real-time collaboration easier in Google Sheets than in Excel?**

Google Sheets lets people from different organizations co-edit without extra barriers, while Excel co-authoring through SharePoint or OneDrive works mainly inside your own organization. David says Excel's co-editing exists on paper, even in the desktop app, but in his experience it is buggy and crashes, whereas Google treats external collaborators almost the same as internal ones.

**How do Copilot, Claude and Gemini compare for working in spreadsheets?**

In David's view the early Copilot in Excel was of little use, until the Claude add-in and then Copilot's editing agent could actually clean data, write formulas and do bulk research. Gemini in Google Sheets adds paid extras such as canvas views like kanban boards and a smart fill handle that researches column values.
