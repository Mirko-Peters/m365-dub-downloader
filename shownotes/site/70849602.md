## FAQ

**Why can optimizing individual teams and tools make an organization slower?**

Because performance comes from how the parts fit together, not from the quality of isolated parts. When one team speeds up, tightens governance or automates a task without redesigning the flow between functions, work piles up in queues, untrusted data and unclear ownership at the boundaries. Local wins then add up to organizational drag.

**What is decision velocity and why does it matter for AI?**

Decision velocity is the time between a meaningful signal and the organization actually acting on it. AI compresses analysis to seconds, so any delay afterwards in approvals, ownership or context becomes visible. Mirko Peters argues it is a more honest metric than model quality, prompt volume or adoption counts, because it covers the full path.

**When should Copilot and Power Automate be introduced in a redesign?**

Last, after the flow is visible, friction is identified, the route is redesigned and access matches accountable roles. AI and automation accelerate whatever already exists, so automating an unstable process shifts manual effort into exceptions and support tickets. Stable, repeatable tasks suit automation, while high-variability work with real consequences suits augmentation.
