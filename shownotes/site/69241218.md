## FAQ

**Will Copilot replace Power BI dashboards?**

No, Power BI gets demoted rather than replaced. In the question era, Copilot and data agents become the first point of contact, while Power BI semantic models supply governed measures, lineage, row-level security and auditability behind each answer. Dashboards become exhibits you open to drill, validate and argue about the model when a decision needs deeper inspection.

**What is decision velocity and how do you measure it?**

Decision velocity is the time from a question being asked to an action being taken. Mirko proposes it as the replacement for adoption metrics like views and clicks. You measure it with timestamps: when the question arrived, when an answer was delivered, when action followed, and whether it proved defensible, alongside escalation and override rates.

**What makes AI answers from Power BI and Fabric trustworthy?**

Trust comes from enforced constraints, not better prompts. The episode lists data contracts with stable semantics, controlled query surfaces such as semantic models and verified answers, identity boundaries enforced in every query, provenance with citations, observability of the answer pipeline, and an escalation path to a named owner when the system cannot answer deterministically.
