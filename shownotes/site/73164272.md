## FAQ

**How many credits does a Copilot Cowork task consume?**

A Copilot Cowork task consumes roughly 70 to 1,500 or more credits, depending on complexity. Light work such as a quick summary runs 70 to 200 credits, medium multi-source work 400 to 600, and heavy reasoning 1,500 or more. At one cent per credit at the time of recording, a heavy task costs about $15.

**What does the EU AI Act require for high-risk AI agents from August 2026?**

From August 2, 2026, organizations using high-risk agents, for example to screen candidates or evaluate employees, need five things: a continuous risk management system, technical documentation, tamper-evident logs kept at least six months, meaningful human oversight under Article 14, and incident reporting. The episode cites penalties of up to 15 million euros or 3% of global turnover.

**Why should AI agents have their own identity in Microsoft Entra ID?**

Agents need their own identity so their actions can be separated from the user's and audited. When Copilot Cowork runs under a user's identity, everything it does looks like the user did it. Microsoft Agent 365 gives each agent a distinct Entra ID identity with scoped permissions, its own audit trail, Conditional Access policies and targeted revocation.
