## FAQ

**Does Microsoft Fabric replace Power BI?**

No, Power BI remains the layer that displays reports and dashboards, while Fabric is the ecosystem underneath it. OneLake, domains, workspaces, lakehouses, warehouses and pipelines keep the data healthy upstream, so Power BI reports stay fresh and consistent. The episode calls this the most common misconception it hears about Fabric.

**What does Direct Lake need to work in Microsoft Fabric?**

Direct Lake needs data stored as Delta tables in OneLake, a compatible and tidy model structure, and access to the right sources. Parquet files without Delta will not work. Wide fact tables and exploding cardinality can still slow it down, so the episode advises trimming unused columns and keeping a clear star schema.

**How do you migrate classic Power BI datasets to Fabric and Direct Lake?**

Start by auditing every dataset, its sources, refresh pain and RLS rules, then land data as Delta in a lakehouse in the right domain. Move complex M logic into Dataflows Gen2, recreate the semantic model, re-implement row-level security because it never migrates automatically, repoint reports and keep the old model read-only briefly.
