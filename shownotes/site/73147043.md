## FAQ

**Do I need to install Microsoft Purview Insider Risk Management separately?**

No, it is a module inside Microsoft Purview, Microsoft's compliance platform, not a new tool you install. If your company already pays for Microsoft 365, you probably already have access and just need to turn it on. It still needs setup: you pick policy templates, choose which users to monitor and set alert thresholds.

**How does Insider Risk Management protect employee privacy during an investigation?**

It hides content and identities by default. Investigators can anonymize user names, so they see a placeholder like user A instead of a real name, and email or document content stays hidden unless explicitly revealed. Every investigator action is logged, policies need admin approval, and alerts are purged automatically unless escalated into a case.

**What can an investigator do after an Insider Risk Management alert fires?**

They can mark the alert as confirmed, mark it as benign, or escalate it to a formal case. The alert shows a risk score and a chronological timeline of the activity behind it. A case adds notes, extra evidence from tools like eDiscovery and a resolution workflow, while the final decision always stays with a person.
