## FAQ

**Does Microsoft 365 Copilot create new data exposure?**

No, Copilot works inside the permission model you already have and does not invent access. It surfaces content made reachable through years of sharing inheritance, loose ownership and forgotten decisions. The episode cites that in some environments over 40% of Copilot responses reference content users did not realize they could access.

**Why do employees bypass Microsoft 365 governance processes?**

Employees bypass governance when the official path is slower than the workaround, not because they are reckless. If a team takes days to provision, people reuse old workspaces, personal OneDrive folders or side channels. The episode calls this structural compensation and argues the fix is redesigning the governed path, not more enforcement or training.

**What is zoned governance in Microsoft 365?**

Zoned governance matches control intensity to risk instead of applying one uniform layer of friction. Low-risk collaboration moves fast with lighter controls, business operations get stronger ownership and lifecycle, and regulated spaces accept more friction. Examples include purpose-based Power Platform environments and starting Copilot testing with up to 100 low-risk sites.
