## FAQ

**What does Dynamics 365 Omnichannel do?**

Dynamics 365 Omnichannel brings chat, voice, SMS and social messages into one agent workspace. Each conversation opens as a session with the customer's contact details, previous cases and earlier activities beside it, so agents do not switch between separate tools and customers are less likely to repeat their story on every channel.

**What is the difference between a transfer and a consult in Omnichannel?**

A transfer moves responsibility for the customer conversation to another queue or person, while a consult only brings a colleague in for help. During a consult the specialist answers the agent's question and the original agent stays with the customer, so the customer avoids another waiting line and an unnecessary handoff.

**Does Omnichannel automatically link every conversation to the right customer?**

No, conversations only connect when channels are set up correctly and the customer can be identified. A signed-in portal user is linked automatically, but an unknown website visitor stays unknown until the agent matches them to an existing contact or creates a new one, then links the conversation to a contact or case.
