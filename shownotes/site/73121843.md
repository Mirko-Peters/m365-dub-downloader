## FAQ

**What is zoned governance for AI agents?**

Zoned governance places agents in environments with different risk tolerance and controls, typically green, yellow and red zones. Thomas Zou describes the green zone as personal experimentation with limited sharing, the yellow zone for testing with more scrutiny, and the red zone for production agents with sensitive business data, which require IT admin approval.

**Who should own an AI agent, the business or IT?**

Ownership should be shared. Business teams know their processes and provide the business logic, IT gives them the knowledge and guardrails to turn that into agents, and executives sponsor the effort and give IT enough resources and influence. Without such a structure, people still build, but they create shadow agents that are hard to secure.

**How can organizations protect sensitive data used by Copilot Studio agents?**

Use a layered approach. Block data connectors in environments where agents must never touch certain data, apply Microsoft Purview sensitivity labels and policies, and put agents that expose sensitive data behind Microsoft Entra authentication and security groups. Thomas Zou's main rule is never to let unauthenticated agents access sensitive data.
