## FAQ

**What is the difference between AI governance and AI stewardship?**

Governance documents values and red lines, while stewardship executes them under pressure. Stewardship is continuous human ownership of AI intent, behavior and outcomes, with named people holding decision rights to approve, pause or retire systems. Mirko argues that policies, model cards and dashboards are instruments, but only owners with stopping power can actually enforce intent when a model drifts.

**Why does Microsoft 365 Copilot surface sensitive data like salary information?**

Copilot reflects the access you already granted rather than inventing it. When SharePoint libraries are overshared, company-wide links never expire and exports stay unlabeled, Copilot can surface that content in an innocent prompt. The episode's fix is at the source: recertify access, kill global links, enforce Purview label inheritance and restrict sensitive joins to managed devices.

**What should the first 90 days of an AI stewardship program include?**

Start with ownership, not an inventory. Month one appoints an executive sponsor and AI steward, approves the risk taxonomy and launches the intake form. Month two builds the use case register, discovers shadow AI and triages risk tiers. Month three activates intake gates, pre-deploy reviews, monitoring and a tabletop exercise measuring time to adjudication and time paused.
