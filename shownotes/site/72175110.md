## FAQ

**Why do Microsoft 365 Copilot rollouts often disappoint?**

Many organizations simply buy licenses and expect results without preparation. Fabio Bonolo says leaders often want too much too fast, so expectations outrun reality. Rollouts work better when companies invest in change management, training and enablement, define use cases with the business around real pain points and treat AI as a long-term investment.

**How does data quality affect Copilot answers?**

Copilot answers from your own company data, so poorly curated content produces poor answers. A one-time cleanup is not enough, Fabio explains. Organizations need a lasting data culture where employees understand the value of data, plus recurring quality controls, for example lifecycle policies in SharePoint Advanced Management, so the chaos does not return within months.

**How can IT pros keep up with constant Microsoft 365 and Copilot updates?**

Block fixed time and focus on a niche. Fabio reserves two hours on Friday afternoons to review Message Center, the admin center and Tech Community blogs, helped by a small AI automation. He also recommends following MVPs, attending events, and building internal ambassadors so colleagues get help without opening a ticket.
