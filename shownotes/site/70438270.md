## FAQ

**What is a three-plane assessment for Microsoft 365 governance?**

It is a paid diagnostic of the identity, productivity and infrastructure control planes, meaning Entra ID, Microsoft 365 and Azure. It costs about $8,000 to $15,000, takes two to three weeks and produces a 20 to 30 page risk report with entropy quantification, risk scores, compliance gaps and a remediation roadmap that becomes the measurable baseline.

**How should Microsoft governance consultants price their services?**

Use three tiers that build on each other: assessment, remediation and advisory retainer. The episode suggests $8,000 to $15,000 for assessments, $80,000 to $200,000 for eight to sixteen weeks of remediation, and $8,000 to $15,000 per month for retainers. Scale within each range by client complexity, and reduce scope instead of discounting for tight budgets.

**Why should consultants charge for discovery instead of offering it free?**

A paid assessment signals that your expertise has value and attracts serious buyers instead of tire kickers. Free discovery calls suggest the opposite. The assessment also uncovers hidden drift the client did not know about, quantifies the risk and creates before-and-after metrics, which makes the following remediation engagement much easier to justify.
