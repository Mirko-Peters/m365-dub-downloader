## FAQ

**Does Copilot in Dynamics 365 break approval controls?**

No, the controls stay intact but their meaning erodes. The approver still clicks and the audit trail still records it, yet the human now validates a compressed narrative Copilot assembled instead of the raw fields. Which variances, receipts or disputes were down-weighted before the recommendation appeared is not captured anywhere.

**Why don't DLP and Conditional Access cover AI agents in Dynamics 365?**

Because both were designed for single events, not agent processes. DLP watches payloads crossing a boundary, while agents synthesize sensitive conclusions inside it and send them out as drafts or Teams posts. Conditional Access evaluates sign-in, but the agent keeps calling tools mid-session, so the episode recommends step-up at sensitive tool invocation.

**How can admins test whether agent-assisted decisions are explainable?**

Pick one real production decision, such as a released credit hold or a goodwill refund, and reconstruct it end to end. List the inputs, identities, run-as chains and connector calls, attempt the lineage, then rerun it in a clone. Two or more flags out of five points to a systemic problem, not one bad record.
