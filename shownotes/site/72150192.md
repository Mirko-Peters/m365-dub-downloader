## FAQ

**What confidence score thresholds should a Power Automate flow use?**

The episode suggests three zones: automate at 0.90 or higher, flag for human review between 0.60 and 0.89, and reject or escalate below 0.60. Thresholds can also be dynamic, for example accepting 0.70 on a small expense report while requiring 0.98 on a large purchase order, so technical logic matches business risk.

**Why should a large language model not calculate totals in a workflow?**

LLMs are good at soft reasoning such as extracting details from messy text, but they are unreliable at arithmetic. The recommended pattern lets the model extract values, then a deterministic flow or rules engine does the addition, tax checks and currency conversion. A mismatch reveals a hallucination and triggers a recovery path instead of a crash.

**What is the difference between character-based and semantic fuzzy matching?**

Character-based matching, often Levenshtein distance, counts the edits needed to turn one string into another, which makes it good for typos. Semantic matching compares meaning instead of spelling, so it recognizes that customer and client refer to the same thing. Combined with confidence scores, both help flows handle inconsistent business data without breaking.
