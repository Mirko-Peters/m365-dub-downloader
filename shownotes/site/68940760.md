## FAQ

**How should the KRBTGT password be rotated to stop golden tickets?**

Rotate it twice, spaced by the maximum ticket lifetime in the domain. The first pass invalidates tickets signed with the oldest key, the second those signed with the interim key. Before rotating, the episode recommends checking replication health, backing up system state and announcing windows, then repeating the ritual twice per year or at significant changes.

**Why is unconstrained delegation a risk in Active Directory?**

Because every user who authenticates to that service leaves a usable TGT in its memory, which an attacker with local admin can reuse to request tickets anywhere that user could reach. The episode recommends replacing it with constrained or resource-based constrained delegation scoped to exact SPNs, denying interactive logon and moving the service identity to a gMSA.

**What is the AD CS ESC1 misconfiguration?**

ESC1 is a certificate template that allows client authentication, lets the enrollee supply the subject name and is open to ordinary users. Anyone can then request a certificate naming an administrator and log on with it through PKINIT. The fix is removing enrollee-supplied subjects, trimming logon EKUs, requiring manager approval and monitoring certificate issuance events.
