## FAQ

**Why does FieldPal AI use a small language model instead of a large one?**

Fergus Kidd says a small language model stays focused and is less likely to answer with false confidence. FieldPal AI only shows it relevant results found through Azure AI Search, so it presents real client data instead of drifting into unrelated topics. The trade-off is more work getting the right information in place first.

**Why did FieldPal AI build on Azure AI Foundry instead of Copilot Studio?**

Its frontline users rarely use Teams, so FieldPal AI needed its own end-to-end platform for headsets and tablets, including recording and offline video uploads. Fergus Kidd says Copilot Studio fits better where workers already live in Teams or Microsoft 365, such as in-home care, and connecting a Copilot Studio agent to their back end stays easy.

**How does voice-based AI improve reporting for field technicians?**

Instead of writing up jobs later, technicians hold a short conversation on site and the AI completes the report. In one example, window installers no longer spend a full day each week on paperwork. The AI also asks for missing photos or serial numbers, so data quality improves at the point of entry.
