## FAQ

**Is Microsoft Mesh still available?**

Yes, but only as immersive spaces inside Microsoft Teams. The standalone Mesh platform was retired by December 2025, the Mesh Toolkit for custom worlds shut down in June 2025, and the dedicated app for PC and Meta Quest is gone. What remains is a 3D view option you can open in any regular Teams meeting.

**How do I join an immersive space in a Teams meeting?**

Join a normal Teams meeting, click View at the top of the screen and choose immersive space 3D. The first time, Teams asks you to set up an avatar, which takes about two minutes, and then to pick an environment such as the outdoor gathering space, the Zen conference room or the boardroom.

**How many people can join an immersive space in Teams?**

Immersive spaces in Teams are limited to 16 people per meeting. That cap, along with the lack of custom environments, makes them a fit for interactive team meetings, onboarding, training and team building rather than large webinars. The episode also notes that you need reasonably current hardware for a smooth experience.
