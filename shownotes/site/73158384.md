- Rework rate, decomposed lead time, flow efficiency and cognitive load reveal system health far better than the classic four DORA metrics alone.

## FAQ

**Why do DORA metrics look good when AI-generated code is causing more incidents?**

Because the four DORA metrics measure activity, not system health. Deployment frequency and lead time improve when AI produces more code, but they ignore rework, review queues and weak tests. The episode cites incidents per pull request rising 243% and review time rising 441% while dashboards still showed green.

**What is rework rate and why is it called the fifth DORA metric?**

Rework rate is the share of code written in the last two weeks that gets rewritten, deleted or rolled back. The episode calls it the truth teller missing from DORA 4, noting it rose from a traditional 3 to 5 percent to roughly 5.7 to 7.1 percent with AI adoption.

**How can engineering leaders measure developer cognitive load?**

Combine three approaches: self-report surveys, behavioral signals and system outcomes. The episode suggests a quarterly one-question mental burden survey, counting context switches per day, tracking 90-minute focus blocks, watching after-hours commits and support tickets, and comparing defect and rework rates for AI-generated versus human-written code, plus satisfaction and attrition trends.
