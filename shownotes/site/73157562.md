## FAQ

**What is the DevOps tax?**

The DevOps tax is the hidden cost of making every team run its own infrastructure at scale. Developers become part-time infrastructure engineers, juggling Kubernetes, pipelines, observability and compliance instead of business logic. It shows up as burnout, turnover, missed deadlines and shadow IT rather than as a line item on the budget.

**What is a golden path in platform engineering?**

A golden path is an opinionated, pre-built workflow covering the 80 percent of use cases that are common. It bundles infrastructure templates, standard CI/CD pipelines, observability and compliance checks, so developers fill in a few fields instead of fifty. The remaining 20 percent goes through a conversation with the platform team.

**How long does it take to move from DevOps to platform engineering?**

The episode puts it at 18 to 24 months across six phases. Teams start with baseline measurement, build a thinnest viable platform, pilot with two or three willing teams, expand adoption with enabling teams, then measure and optimize. Specialized capabilities like cost governance come only in the final phase, once teams trust the platform.
