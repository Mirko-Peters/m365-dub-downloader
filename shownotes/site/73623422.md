## FAQ

**What does Microsoft Defender EASM do?**

Microsoft Defender EASM finds, maps and monitors the parts of your company that face the public internet. It discovers domains, subdomains, hosts, public IP addresses, web pages, certificates and services, then shows how they connect. It does not replace firewalls, patching or endpoint protection; it shows what outsiders can see before you decide what to fix.

**How does Defender EASM discover assets without a login?**

It starts from seeds you already trust, such as domains, IP ranges, host names or ASNs, and follows public connections outward. Microsoft calls this recursive discovery. It uses domain records, certificates, web content and service behavior, but a found connection does not prove ownership, so your team still confirms which assets really belong to you.

**How should I prioritize Defender EASM findings?**

Prioritize by context, not by treating every finding as urgent. Ask how exposed the asset is, whether it has a known weakness or unsafe setup, and how important it is to the business. Then name an owner who can fix, remove or keep watching it, and record that decision so nobody repeats the investigation later.
