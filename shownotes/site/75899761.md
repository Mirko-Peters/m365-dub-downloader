## Key takeaways

- Part of the [Microsoft Defender](https://www.m365.fm/entity/defender/?utm_source=podcast&utm_medium=shownotes&utm_campaign=microsoft-defender-for-business) family, Defender for Business brings the same engine as Defender for Endpoint to companies with up to 300 users, packaged with a setup wizard and default policies.
- Unlike plain antivirus, its EDR capabilities show what happened, where and what to do next, and can disrupt attacks automatically.
- Know the limits before buying: the 300-user cap, five devices per user, separate server licensing and no mixing with Defender for Endpoint Plan 2.

## FAQ

**What is Microsoft Defender for Business?**

It is Microsoft's endpoint security product for small and medium businesses with up to 300 users. It runs on the same engine as Microsoft Defender for Endpoint and protects Windows, Mac, iOS and Android devices, but it adds a setup wizard, default policies and a simpler dashboard for teams without a dedicated security department.

**How much does Microsoft Defender for Business cost?**

At the time of recording, the standalone plan costs three dollars per user per month when paid yearly, covering up to five devices per user with no minimum device count. It is also included in Microsoft 365 Business Premium. Server protection is a separate add-on at three dollars per server instance, and a one-month trial exists.

**What is the difference between Defender for Business and Defender for Endpoint?**

The difference is mainly scale and packaging, not protection strength. Defender for Endpoint is the enterprise product in Plan 1 and Plan 2, with advanced hunting and Microsoft Threat Experts. Defender for Business includes everything in Plan 1, parts of Plan 2 and small business extras, but it is capped at 300 users and cannot be mixed with Plan 2.
