## FAQ

**What can you find on Microsoft AppSource?**

Microsoft AppSource lists three kinds of business offerings: apps and extensions, consulting services, and templates and data services. Apps range from full applications built on Dynamics 365 to small add-ins, partners offer workshops, assessments and implementations, and templates cover industry scenarios such as a patient feedback app built with Power Apps.

**Are AppSource and Azure Marketplace still separate?**

No. In September 2025 Microsoft unified AppSource and Azure Marketplace into a single platform called Microsoft Marketplace. Azure infrastructure solutions and consulting services now appear alongside business apps, one listing can show up in both storefronts, and buyers can search across Dynamics 365, Power Platform, Azure and Microsoft 365 with consistent billing.

**What is co-sell in Microsoft AppSource?**

Co-sell means Microsoft's own sales team can recommend an eligible offering to their customers. For a small software vendor, Microsoft's field sellers effectively extend its sales force. The episode also covers free trials, transactable offers billed through existing Microsoft agreements that can count toward an Azure consumption commitment, and private offers with custom pricing.
