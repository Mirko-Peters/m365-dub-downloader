## FAQ

**What is SharePoint Premium and does it replace SharePoint?**

SharePoint Premium is an AI-powered add-on for SharePoint Online and does not replace it. Your existing SharePoint stays where it is, and Premium adds three pillars on top: content experiences such as the Business Documents app, content processing with AI models and OCR, and content governance with reports, access reviews and lifecycle management.

**How much does SharePoint Premium cost?**

SharePoint Premium is not included in any Microsoft 365 enterprise plan, not even E5; it is billed pay-as-you-go through Azure. At the time of recording, Microsoft offered limited free monthly capacity through December 2025. You can enable it per site, so the episode suggests starting with one library, setting an Azure budget and checking the first bill.

**Why does Copilot need SharePoint Premium?**

Copilot needs structured metadata, classified documents and governed access to answer well, and SharePoint Premium provides them. If invoices lack vendor names and amounts as metadata, Copilot can only guess. Premium extracts such fields into columns, so Copilot can filter precisely. Microsoft calls preparing content for AI the primary use case for SharePoint Premium.
