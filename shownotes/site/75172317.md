## FAQ

**What is the difference between a data model, a semantic model and an ontology?**

A data model is the structure of tables; for analytics, transactional tables are denormalized into Kimball-style facts and dimensions. A semantic model adds measures, logic and security to deliver deterministic KPIs. An ontology uses less rigid, verb-based relationships between business entities and connects to lakehouses, so AI can explore why numbers changed.

**Is Power BI separate from Microsoft Fabric?**

No. Rishi Sapra says Power BI is one of the workloads in Fabric, so there is no reason to keep one semantic model in Power BI and another in Fabric. Fabric provides the governed data and logic foundation, and data agents built on it can be added to Copilot Studio and used in Teams.

**How should governance work for AI agents on financial data?**

Through automation rather than long documents nobody reads. Rishi Sapra builds access, permissions and policies into a context layer and writes employment contracts for agents that force them to apply those policies. A hub-and-spoke operating model with a center of excellence certifies skills and datasets before they become available across the organization.
