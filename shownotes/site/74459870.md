## FAQ

**What is a load in Dynamics 365 Transportation Management?**

A load is the digital record for one planned movement of goods, such as one truck, trailer, container or group of shipments that travel together. It lets planners group compatible shipments from the same warehouse and timeframe, while load templates check weight, volume and height limits before anyone starts physical work at the dock.

**Does Dynamics 365 Transportation Management offer live multi-carrier rate shopping?**

No, the built-in module works with rate tables your company enters and maintains in Dynamics 365. It does not pull live prices from carrier feeds. For multi-carrier rate shopping, live booking links or deeper shipment tracking, companies normally connect a third-party transportation management system alongside Supply Chain Management.

**How does freight reconciliation work in Dynamics 365?**

Freight reconciliation compares the carrier's invoice with the expected charge and shipment details already recorded during planning. When the invoice fits the company's accepted rules, it moves forward; when it does not, the difference goes to someone for review. This lets finance and transport teams catch mismatches before the freight bill is paid.
