## FAQ

**Why do different teams get different revenue numbers in Microsoft Fabric?**

Because each team clones the shared semantic model and adjusts the DAX for its own needs. In the episode's finance scenario, finance, sales and operations all read the same ERP data but built three revenue measures with different filters and date logic, so the board saw three defensible yet incompatible figures.

**What is the difference between data security and semantic governance in Fabric?**

Security decides who can touch an object; semantic governance decides what that object means. Entra, workspace roles, OneLake security and Purview handle access, labels and audit. Nothing in them flags five measures called total revenue or a churn window quietly changed from 90 to 30 days of inactivity.

**What should happen in the first 90 days of Fabric governance?**

Day 0 freezes new chaos by restricting workspace creation and unreviewed production models. By day 30 every heavily used dataset and model has a named owner and the top KPIs have authoritative definitions. Day 90 enforces domain boundaries, archives unclaimed workspaces and limits Copilot and data agents to certified semantics.
