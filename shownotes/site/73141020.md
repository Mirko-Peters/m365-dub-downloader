## FAQ

**What is Microsoft Cloud for Sustainability?**

Microsoft Cloud for Sustainability is a connected platform, built on Power Platform and Dynamics 365, that centralizes a company's sustainability data. It follows a cycle of record, report and reduce: it records emissions, reports on them accurately and helps cut impact over time. Beyond carbon, it now also covers water, waste, social and governance data.

**How do you get data into Microsoft Cloud for Sustainability?**

There are four ways: Excel templates, Power Query, partner connectors and manual data requests. Templates suit small teams, Power Query pulls data on a schedule through over 40 connectors such as SAP and Salesforce, partner connectors link ESG data providers, and a built-in portal collects readings from colleagues. Everything lands in one standard data model.

**How much does Microsoft Cloud for Sustainability cost?**

At the time of recording, Essentials costs $4,000 per month and Premium $12,000 per month. Essentials covers carbon, water and waste tracking plus calculations for scope 1, 2 and some scope 3 emissions. Premium adds full scope 3 coverage, AI-powered insights, Copilot integration, product carbon footprint management and more storage for complex reporting needs.
