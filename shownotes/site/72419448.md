## FAQ

**What are Dataverse business skills?**

Dataverse business skills are a preview feature that lets business users write procedures in natural language inside Dataverse. Agents discover them through the Dataverse MCP server, so a new or changed procedure, such as one for gold-tier customers, does not require rewiring, retesting and redeploying the agent. Nathan compares them to microservices.

**How is MCP different from a regular API?**

Nathan explains that an API is like Spotify and MCP is like a DJ. An API expects a defined input and returns a defined output, so it fails on anything unexpected. MCP brings an LLM's reasoning into the process, reads the context, picks the right tools and can adapt inputs, such as converting text to a number.

**Should enterprises use Dataverse business skills in production yet?**

Not yet, according to Nathan, because business skills are still in preview. He recommends evaluating them in a sandbox environment, since preview features can change or disappear without notice. He still considers them capable of enterprise-grade tasks such as assembling a bill of materials or working out a salesperson's next best action on an opportunity.
