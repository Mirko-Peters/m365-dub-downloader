## FAQ

**How many leads can Sales Navigator CRM sync push into Dynamics 365?**

According to the episode, Sales Navigator CRM sync is capped at 1,000 leads and contacts per user per month, not per day. The sync runs daily and is mostly one-directional from LinkedIn, while lead source, lead status and configured custom fields sync both ways. The cap pushes teams to treat LinkedIn as a targeting system.

**Why does the Power Automate 100-second timeout matter for sales agents?**

When an agent calls a Power Automate flow synchronously, the flow must finish within 100 seconds or it is terminated and the agent gets nothing back. The episode recommends keeping that part fast: accept the request, queue it and return a tracking ID, then let an asynchronous flow handle slow enrichment and write results to Dynamics 365.

**Can you scrape LinkedIn profiles into Dynamics 365 under GDPR?**

No, scraping is the risky path the episode warns against. It violates LinkedIn's terms, and GDPR requires a lawful basis because profiles are personal data; being publicly visible is not enough. Instead, use official channels such as Sales Navigator CRM sync, restrict connectors with DLP policies and rely on native Dynamics 365 audit logging.
