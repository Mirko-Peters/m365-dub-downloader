## FAQ

**When should I use Dataverse instead of SharePoint lists?**

Use Dataverse for business-critical systems of record with related tables, enterprise security and automation; use SharePoint lists for simple trackers and documents. SharePoint lists hit a 5,000-item view threshold and do not enforce lookups, while Dataverse handles millions of rows, enforces referential integrity and supports column-level security.

**How does security work in Dataverse?**

Dataverse uses role-based security: you assign permissions to roles, then add users or teams to those roles. Control applies at table, record and column level, with access levels such as user, business unit and organization. Security is additive, so a user with two roles always gets the most permissive access from either.

**What license do I need for Dataverse?**

Dataverse requires a Power Apps premium license, which the episode puts at about $20 per user per month at the time of recording. SharePoint lists, by contrast, come with a Microsoft 365 subscription at no extra cost. The episode argues the premium is worth it for scale, enforced relationships and finer security.
