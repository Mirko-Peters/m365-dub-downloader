## FAQ

**Why do Microsoft Purview rollouts often fail?**

Because Purview is already in the tenant, so teams turn it on without planning. They scan every source, hand out access to whoever asks and end up with thousands of unlabeled assets and scattered collection admins. Business users open the catalog once, find noise and never return. The episode calls this a rollout problem, not a tooling problem.

**What are the four permission layers in Microsoft Purview?**

The tenant level, the data map, the unified catalog and the governance domain. The tenant level controls who configures the platform, the data map holds collections and scanned metadata, the unified catalog manages business concepts and catalog-wide roles, and governance domains are where business owners, stewards and data product owners run their data. Each layer has its own roles.

**How should the Purview administrator role be protected?**

With just-in-time access through Microsoft Entra Privileged Identity Management instead of standing membership. The group stays empty by default and users are only eligible. Activation requires justification, MFA and an independent approval, lasts at most four hours and is logged. The episode stresses this because the role can raise its own permissions.
