## FAQ

**What is the difference between exposure and a vulnerability?**

A vulnerability is an individual issue, while exposure is how attackers chain several issues into a path to valuable assets. Uros gives the example of a misconfigured service account, a device vulnerability and excessive permissions that are each minor alone but together lead to domain admin. Exposure management finds and prioritizes such chains.

**Why should AI agents be treated as identities in security?**

AI agents act, not just answer: they call Microsoft Graph, read SharePoint and query Exchange using delegated or application permissions granted at integration time. That makes them comparable to service principals or managed identities in access scope, so Uros recommends identifying, protecting with Conditional Access, monitoring and automatically blocking risky agents like any other identity.

**Which KPIs should a modern SOC track?**

A modern SOC should measure exposure reduction rather than activity volume. Uros argues that counting alerts, incidents and closed tickets says little about security outcomes, since dashboards can be green while attack paths stay open. Better metrics include time to remediate critical exposure and exposure affecting tier-zero assets, combined with MITRE ATT&CK mapping.
