## FAQ

**What is Dynamics 365 Remote Assist used for?**

Dynamics 365 Remote Assist lets a frontline worker call a remote expert and share live video of the equipment in front of them. The expert sees the real scene, asks for closer views and marks the exact part to check. Typical uses include factory maintenance, field service visits, inspections and remote coaching during training.

**Do you need a mixed reality headset to use Remote Assist?**

No, a supported phone or tablet works for many everyday jobs such as quick visual checks during a visit, inspection or repair. Supported mixed reality headsets fit tasks where the worker needs both hands free, because guidance appears where they are looking. The episode says headsets are not the starting point for most teams.

**How is Remote Assist different from a normal Teams video meeting?**

A Teams meeting connects people, while Remote Assist focuses that connection on the physical work scene. The expert can circle a connector, place an arrow or add a note inside the worker's view instead of describing locations in words. Calls still run through Teams contacts, and experts can bring in a second specialist without restarting.
