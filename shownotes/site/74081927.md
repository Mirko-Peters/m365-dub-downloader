## FAQ

**What is Dynamics 365 Field Service used for?**

Dynamics 365 Field Service manages work done at customer locations, from the first request through scheduling, the technician visit and parts used to the final invoice. It brings people, appointments, customer information and service details into one place, so dispatchers plan with current information and technicians see the job details on site.

**What is the difference between Dynamics 365 Customer Service and Field Service?**

Customer Service handles the incoming request, such as a call where an agent records the issue, while Field Service takes over when solving it requires a visit. Field Service manages the trip, the work, the parts and the result, often starting from a work order an agent creates from the service case.

**Does the Field Service mobile app work without an internet connection?**

Yes, Field Service Mobile supports offline work, so technicians in basements, plant rooms or rural sites can still open job information and record updates. When the device reconnects, the changes sync back to the system. The app also shows bookings, customer details, equipment history, service tasks, photos, barcode scanning and customer signatures.
