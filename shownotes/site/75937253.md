## FAQ

**What license do I need to use Direct Lake in Power BI?**

Direct Lake needs a paid Microsoft Fabric capacity, sold in sizes called F SKUs. It is not available on a free account, on Pro or on Premium Per User alone. Viewers still need a Pro or per-user license unless the capacity is an F64 or larger. A Fabric trial lets you test it for sixty days.

**What is the difference between Direct Lake on SQL and Direct Lake on OneLake?**

Direct Lake on SQL silently falls back to DirectQuery when it cannot read the files, so problems appear as slow reports. Direct Lake on OneLake connects straight to Delta tables, can combine tables from several Fabric items and never falls back, so problems appear as errors. Microsoft recommends the OneLake version for new models.

**Why is my Direct Lake report slow?**

Usually the Delta tables underneath are untidy rather than Direct Lake itself being slow. Overwrites erase the table history and force a full cold reload, while many tiny files, small row groups or partitioning on a column with thousands of values drag performance down. Too little capacity memory also causes paging that feels like slowness.
