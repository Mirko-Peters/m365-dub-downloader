This episode explains Microsoft Purview Audit for IT admins, compliance officers and security analysts, from the unified audit log to Audit Standard vs. Premium and a practical audit plan.

## Key takeaways

- Microsoft Purview Audit collects activity records from [Microsoft 365](https://www.m365.fm/entity/microsoft-365/?utm_source=podcast&utm_medium=shownotes&utm_campaign=microsoft-purview-audit-simply-explained) services into one searchable unified audit log, capturing who did what and when, but not the full content of files or messages.
- Audit answers what happened, while eDiscovery, Content Search, Insider Risk Management and Compliance Manager each cover different parts of an investigation or compliance programme.
- Audit (Premium) adds longer retention and richer detail, but retention cannot recover events that were never recorded, so plan retention, roles and a tested investigation process before an incident.

## FAQ

**What does Microsoft Purview Audit record?**

Purview Audit records actions, not content. Each activity record shows who acted, when, which Microsoft 365 service was involved and which item was touched, such as a file, mailbox, site or policy, sometimes with an IP address. It does not store the full text of emails, chats or documents, only that an action happened around them.

**What is the difference between Purview Audit and eDiscovery?**

Audit tells you that something happened, while eDiscovery helps you find and manage the content itself. Audit shows that a person opened, shared or deleted a document at a certain time. eDiscovery locates the emails, chats or files for a case, can place them on hold, supports review and exports them as evidence.

**How long does Microsoft Purview Audit keep records?**

Audit (Standard) keeps many records for up to 180 days by default. Audit (Premium), available with Microsoft 365 E5, E5 Compliance or a suitable add-on, keeps eligible records for one year and allows custom retention policies of up to ten years. Longer retention cannot recover events from before it was set up.
