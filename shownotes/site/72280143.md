## FAQ

**Why do Copilot and AI pilots stall in Microsoft 365?**

They stall because AI amplifies existing governance gaps rather than creating new ones. In the simulation, identity blind spots, abandoned workspaces, ungoverned automations, unclear ownership and paper-only compliance broke adoption at predictable points. Copilot only surfaces what users can already access, but it does so at conversational speed, so hidden oversharing quickly turns into visible incidents.

**How was the synthetic market of 100 companies built?**

It was built with Azure AI Foundry as 100 synthetic company personas with differing governance, identity, collaboration and automation practices. A GraphRAG knowledge graph mapped relationships between identities, permissions and policies, and multi-agent simulations let CISO, architect and business unit agents negotiate trade-offs across more than 1,000 iterations of AI adoption scenarios.

**How does the 90-day AI readiness assessment work?**

You score five domains from zero to three: identity, data, collaboration, automation and governance, for a total out of 15. In the simulation, scores of 12 to 15 reached 70 percent or higher adoption, while scores below 9 stalled at 20 to 30 percent. You then start work in the weakest domain, scoring honestly.
