## FAQ

**How can game design principles improve business app adoption?**

Game design improves adoption by structuring apps around the user's journey instead of an endless screen. Sandra Kiel recommends breaking processes into visible steps, recognizing progress and giving clear feedback. She stresses this is not about making apps childish but about borrowing game mechanics, and in her experience a better experience also produces better data quality.

**Why don't trainings alone drive Microsoft 365 Copilot adoption?**

Trainings alone fall short because Copilot adoption is a behavior change, and behavior change comes from habit loops. Sandra Kiel compares this to the core loop that keeps players in a game. Instead of handing people working prompts from a library, let them start small, experiment, see what does not work and improve their own prompts.

**Which game mechanics work best in an adoption program?**

No single mechanic works alone; Sandra Kiel recommends combining several as a framework. She names points for actions, challenges, individualized paths with different difficulty levels and roles, competition and social elements. Using only points, only leaderboards or only rewards does not work, and she suggests adding a game designer to shape the overall experience.
