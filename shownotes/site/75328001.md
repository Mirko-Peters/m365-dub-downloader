## Key takeaways

- Copilot readiness is a [Microsoft 365](https://www.m365.fm/entity/microsoft-365/?utm_source=podcast&utm_medium=shownotes&utm_campaign=paul-keijzers) project: start with sharing settings, permissions, retention, sensitivity labels and DLP, including Power Automate DLP for connectors.

## FAQ

**What should you check first to prepare a Microsoft 365 tenant for Copilot?**

Start with the sharing settings. Paul Keijzers first checks whether sharing allows everyone, which is common in new or small tenants, and then asks what the business really needs. After that come permissions, retention, sensitivity labels and DLP, the basics companies most often forget when they simply buy Copilot licenses and start.

**Why does Copilot seem to hallucinate when it uses old SharePoint content?**

Often it is not hallucinating but answering from your own outdated files. Content migrated from old file servers sits unarchived next to newer versions, so Copilot may use version one instead of version thirty. Archiving old sites or restricting them from Copilot keeps that content out of the results while it is still retained.

**How can you find oversharing across SharePoint, Teams and OneDrive?**

Use the oversharing reports in the SharePoint admin center, which Paul says become available once you have a Copilot license. They show how sites are used and where oversharing happens across SharePoint, Teams and OneDrive. Review the findings first, because some flagged sites, such as marketing data shared with partners, are shared on purpose.
