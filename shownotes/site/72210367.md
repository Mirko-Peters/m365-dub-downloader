## FAQ

**When should I use Copilot Studio instead of Power Automate?**

Use Copilot Studio for judgment and Power Automate for deterministic logic. Agents fit messy inputs, exceptions and natural-language requests where the right action depends on context. Flows fit structured, high-volume, auditable work such as provisioning accounts, assigning licenses or routing approvals. Keep financial calculations and compliance checks out of agents, since they need exact reproducibility.

**Why do sub-agent citations disappear in Copilot Studio multi-agent setups?**

The orchestration layer summarizes sub-agent responses before they reach the master agent, often stripping citations and document links. This is deliberate safety behavior against prompt injection, not a bug. The workaround is exposing the logic through a Power Automate flow with an HTTP trigger that returns the raw response, links intact, in a structured format.

**Are Power Apps canvas apps still worth building?**

Yes, canvas apps still win for complex validated forms, offline work, device features such as barcode scanning and signatures, and dense data grids. A 2026 community survey cited in the episode found 88% of practitioners plan to build the same number or more. The emerging hybrid lets Copilot Studio handle triage while Power Apps completes the transaction.
