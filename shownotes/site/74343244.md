## FAQ

**Do I need a connector to integrate Dynamics 365 with Dataverse?**

For apps like Dynamics 365 Sales and Customer Service, usually not, because their data already lives in Dataverse. Integration then means building on the same tables, relationships, rules and access controls. The question shifts from how to sync two customer lists to which people and apps should work with a record and what each may do.

**How does security work in Dataverse?**

Security in Dataverse sits with the data, not with individual apps. Microsoft Entra ID identifies the user, security roles define who can read, create, update or delete rows in each table, and row-level access limits which records someone sees. Column-level security protects sensitive fields, and business units create boundaries between regions, divisions or subsidiaries.

**When should I use Dataverse instead of Excel, SharePoint lists or SQL?**

Choose Dataverse when several people and apps need connected business data with clear access rules and repeatable processes. Excel remains a good fit for personal analysis and quick lists, SharePoint lists work for simple team tracking, and SQL stays a strong choice for developers who need deep control. Dataverse fits when data spans apps, automation and reports.
