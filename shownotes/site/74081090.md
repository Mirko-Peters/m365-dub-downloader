## FAQ

**Does Microsoft Purview store my data?**

No. Microsoft Purview works around data that stays where it already lives. The data map collects metadata such as table names, columns, owners and classifications without copying customer records or files into Purview. Your Azure database stays in Azure, Fabric data stays in Fabric, and supported external sources remain in place while Purview adds rules and visibility.

**What is the difference between the Purview data map and the unified catalog?**

The data map is a technical inventory of connected sources, recording systems, tables, reports and how they connect. The unified catalog is the business-friendly layer where people search for data products with a description, a named owner, approved uses and access rules, supported by a business glossary and governance domains such as finance or sales.

**How do sensitivity labels and DLP work together in Microsoft Purview?**

Sensitivity labels identify information and describe how to handle it, for example with a watermark, encryption or forwarding limits. DLP checks actions as they happen, such as an upload to personal storage, and can warn, block or record them. Policy tips explain the rule, and teams often start by recording activity or showing warnings before blocking.
