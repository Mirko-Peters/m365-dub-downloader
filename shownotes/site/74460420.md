## FAQ

**What is the difference between inventory management and warehouse management in Dynamics 365?**

Inventory management is the stock record: how much stock the business has, with quantities, costs and availability. Warehouse management answers where exactly that stock sits and what a worker should do with it next, turning the record into guided physical work such as receiving, put-away, picking and shipping, confirmed with scans.

**What is a location directive in Dynamics 365 Warehouse Management?**

A location directive is a set of rules that decides where an item should go or come from. It can consider the product, its size, storage needs, warehouse zone and space limits, so heavy stock lands on ground-level racks and cooled goods only go to approved cold storage. Workers simply see the resulting instruction.

**What is a wave in Dynamics 365 warehouse picking?**

A wave is a planned group of outbound orders that the warehouse processes together, for example orders leaving on the same truck or sharing a delivery date or route. Releasing a wave creates work such as picking, moving items to packing and staging at the dock, so workers spend less time walking.
