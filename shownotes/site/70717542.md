## FAQ

**What are the five Microsoft 365 business models beyond hourly consulting?**

The episode outlines an agentic workflow factory, an Entra-first security boutique, governance as a service, a decision engine architect and an industry tenant in a box. Each targets the gap between what Microsoft ships and what organizations can govern, and each replaces billable hours with a build or licensing phase plus recurring revenue.

**How should an agentic workflow factory be priced?**

Mirko suggests three revenue streams: a flat build fee of 50,000 to 150,000 dollars for the orchestration architecture, usage fees of two to five dollars per agent decision, and a monthly optimization retainer of 10,000 to 30,000 dollars for monitoring agent behavior, refining decision criteria and adding new agents over time.

**What are the biggest mistakes when moving to outcome-based Microsoft 365 consulting?**

The most damaging trap is discounting the build phase, because that is where reusable intellectual property is created. Mirko also warns against assuming 90 percent utilization, treating retainers as passive income, building one generic tenant for every industry, hiring too early, neglecting customer success and mixing hourly with outcome-based work.
