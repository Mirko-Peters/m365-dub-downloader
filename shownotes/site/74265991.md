## FAQ

**What is the difference between a lead and an opportunity in Dynamics 365 Sales?**

A lead stores early interest, while an opportunity is a potential sale linked to an account or contact. When a lead shows a real need, possible budget and willingness to talk, you qualify it; Dynamics 365 Sales creates an opportunity, carries the lead details over, keeps both linked and closes the lead record.

**What are the four stages of the sales process in Dynamics 365 Sales?**

The standard business process flow has qualify, develop, propose and close. Qualify covers contact, account, timeframe, budget, buying process and decision maker. Develop records customer need, proposed solution, stakeholders and competitors. Propose adds the internal team, proposal and internal review. Close covers the final proposal, decision date, a thank you and a debrief.

**Why should you close lost opportunities in Dynamics 365 Sales?**

Closing a deal as lost keeps the forecast honest, because open opportunities count as possible future revenue. A lost opportunity stays in the system with its history, and the close form can record a useful reason, such as budget removed or a competitor selected, so sales leaders can later look for patterns across many deals.
