## FAQ

**Why do companies struggle to get value from Copilot licenses?**

Mostly because of human behavior, not governance, says Jess Stratton. Many admins already handle the risks and safeguards well, but employees are rarely shown how Copilot helps their own work. Training is shifting from listing features to explaining why and how Copilot saves time in each person's job, with real productivity gains.

**How can I make Copilot write in my own voice?**

Ask Copilot to analyze your recent sent messages in Outlook and create custom instructions based on your writing voice. Jess Stratton tried this and the next email sounded like her immediately. Brand kits in the Create tool, PowerPoint templates and output settings in apps like Excel and Outlook also help avoid generic AI content.

**Who makes a good Copilot champion in a department?**

The most enthusiastic sharer, not the biggest expert. Jess Stratton recommends finding the person in each department who loves showing others what Copilot can do, then putting them in the same room as colleagues with similar job functions who struggle. Peer conversations between people doing similar work are where adoption really takes hold.
