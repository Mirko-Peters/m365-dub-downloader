## FAQ

**Is Viva Engage the same as Yammer?**

Viva Engage replaced Yammer in 2023, and most Yammer features are still there with data migrated automatically. The difference is integration: Yammer was a standalone social tool, while Engage is part of the Viva platform and connects to Viva Connections, Viva Insights and Viva Learning, with a cleaner interface.

**What is the difference between Viva Engage and Microsoft Teams?**

Teams is for real-time work in small groups, while Viva Engage is a company-wide space for asynchronous conversation. Teams channels handle chat, meetings and files for a project, whereas Engage posts are typically visible to the whole organization, so anyone can answer a question. The two tools complement each other rather than compete.

**Which Viva Engage features need a premium license?**

Advanced analytics, campaigns and leadership corner require an additional license, while communities, conversations, storylines and basic analytics come with most Microsoft 365 business plans. Premium is available through the Viva Employee Communications and Communities license, which covers Engage, Amplify and Connections, or through the full Viva suite license that includes every Viva app.
