## FAQ

**How does Power Platform governance prevent shadow IT?**

It uses zoned environments so speed and control match the risk. Green zones hold auto-approved citizen apps, amber zones need IT review for business-critical workflows, and red zones are built only by IT. DLP policies restrict connectors, RBAC defines who builds what, and a Center of Excellence of four to six people sets policies and templates.

**When is Power Automate per flow licensing cheaper than per user licensing?**

Per flow licensing usually wins for shared, high-volume processes used by many casual users. In the episode's example, a shared app serving 400 people costs about $200 a month per flow, compared with $8,000 at $20 per user, at the time of recording. Organizations should also check Power Apps capacity already included in Microsoft 365 E3 and E5.

**How do you build an ROI business case for Power Platform automation?**

Start by documenting the cost of the current manual process. Measure labor hours at a fully loaded rate, cycle time, error rates and rework, then track real post-deployment results from system data. Compare total cost of ownership against the manual baseline and a pro-code alternative, targeting at least 300 percent first-year ROI and payback within six months.
