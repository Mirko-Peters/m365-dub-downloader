## FAQ

**What is the Up next work list in Sales Accelerator?**

Up next is the daily work list inside Sales Accelerator that shows the leads and opportunities needing attention, each linked to a call, email or due follow-up. It ranks items using predictive scoring, company rules and recent customer activity, keeps overdue work visible, and lets sellers check the customer record before acting.

**How many records can be added to sequences in Dynamics 365 Sales?**

It depends on the license. According to the episode, Dynamics 365 Sales Enterprise includes up to 1,500 records per month in sequences, while Sales Premium supports unlimited records. Teams that handle a high number of leads or opportunities should check which license they have before building their follow-up plans around sequences.

**What is a sequence in Dynamics 365 Sales Accelerator?**

A sequence is a planned series of sales actions attached to a lead or opportunity, such as emails, calls, tasks and wait periods. It gives every rep a repeatable follow-up path, while the rep can change the wording, pick a different action or pause it. The episode recommends starting with a few proven steps.
