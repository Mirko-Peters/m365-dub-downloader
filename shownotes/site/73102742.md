## FAQ

**Do I need a separate app to use Microsoft Places?**

No, Microsoft Places is not a separate app. It lives inside Outlook and Teams, so work plans appear in your calendar and presence shows up in chat. You do not visit a website, create an account or learn a new interface; Places connects your calendar data with the buildings, floors, desks and rooms of your office.

**Can my employer see my location in Microsoft Places?**

Only if you opt in. Sharing your work plan and your in-office presence is optional, and according to the episode there is no way for an admin to turn it on for everyone. Places detects check-in automatically, for example when you connect to a desk monitor or corporate Wi-Fi, but visibility remains your choice.

**What happens if I book a desk in Microsoft Places and don't show up?**

Places automatically releases the desk after a set time so someone else can book it. Facilities teams configure these auto-release policies, for example releasing a desk if nobody checks in within 30 minutes. Utilization reports also show the gap between booked and actually used spaces, which helps decide which office space is worth keeping.
