## FAQ

**What is the Microsoft Cloud Solution Provider program?**

The Cloud Solution Provider program, or CSP, lets partners resell Microsoft cloud services such as Microsoft 365, Azure and Dynamics 365. The partner buys at wholesale, adds its own services and becomes the customer's contact for billing, support and provisioning, so Microsoft is no longer the billing contact.

**Can I reduce license seats under the new commerce experience?**

No, not after the seven-day cancellation window. Under the new commerce experience, a commitment becomes binding after that window, and seats can be added but not removed mid-term. The episode advises planning headcount carefully, over-buying slightly when unsure, or choosing the monthly term, which costs a 20% premium over the annual list price.

**Should I choose CSP or an Enterprise Agreement?**

CSP is the likely choice for organizations under 500 employees, while larger ones should run the numbers on an Enterprise Agreement. According to the episode, EA pricing can be 20% to 35% lower per seat at 500 or more users, but new EA enrollment stopped in October 2024, so most businesses now default to CSP.
