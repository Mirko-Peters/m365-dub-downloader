## FAQ

**What is a Microsoft 365 tenant-to-tenant migration?**

It is moving users and their content from one Microsoft 365 tenant to another, usually after a merger, divestiture, spin-off or rebrand. A tenant is the organization's sealed space in Microsoft's cloud, and two tenants share nothing by default, so mailboxes, OneDrive files, SharePoint sites and teams have to be carried across piece by piece.

**What breaks in a tenant-to-tenant migration?**

Mostly the wiring around the data, not the data itself. Teams chat history may not come across cleanly, permissions must be rebuilt, apps such as Planner, Forms, Loop, Whiteboard and To Do often need manual handling, and Power Automate flows and Power Apps lose their connections, so someone has to reconnect them in the new tenant.

**Why is the domain moved last in a tenant migration?**

Because a custom domain can live in only one tenant at a time. It must be released from the old tenant before the new one accepts it, which creates a gap where mail has to be caught and rerouted. Teams therefore run both tenants in coexistence and flip the domain during a short, quiet cutover window.
