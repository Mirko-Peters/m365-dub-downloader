## Key takeaways

- At millions of pages, plugging Copilot into data is not enough: a tiered selective-activation model plus recursive, structure-aware chunking with overlap beats expensive semantic chunking on cost and accuracy.
- Precision comes from orchestration: an intent-based router, hybrid BM25 and vector retrieval fused with reciprocal rank fusion, cross-encoder reranking, rich metadata and knowledge graphs.
- Governance and measurement must be built in from day one, including chunk-level permission trimming, [sensitivity labels](https://www.m365.fm/entity/sensitivity-labels/?utm_source=podcast&utm_medium=shownotes&utm_campaign=i-engineered-copilot-for-3-5-million-pages-the-epstein-files-challenge), retention, audit logs, latency budgets and gold-standard evaluation sets.

## FAQ

**Is semantic chunking better than fixed-size chunking for large RAG systems?**

Not in the benchmarks cited. Recursive 512-token chunking reached 69 percent accuracy on document-level retrieval, while semantic chunking scored 54 percent with chunks averaging only 43 tokens. Semantic chunking also adds 1.5 to 3 times the preprocessing overhead. Start with recursive, structure-aware chunking and 10 to 15 percent overlap, then measure.

**How do you make millions of documents searchable without embedding everything?**

Use selective activation with three tiers. High-value documents such as depositions get recursive chunking, rich metadata, embeddings and cross-encoder reranking. Bulk evidence like emails gets keyword-only BM25 indexing. The deep archive stays in cold storage with on-demand extraction. This shrinks the embedding budget and vector database considerably while keeping key content precise.

**How do you prevent Copilot or RAG from showing documents a user should not see?**

Attach permission metadata to every chunk at ingestion and trim results at query time based on the user's identity, before reranking and before the LLM sees anything. Sensitivity labels express policy, audit trails log each retrieval, and permissions must be refreshed regularly, because stale cached credentials can leak restricted content silently.
