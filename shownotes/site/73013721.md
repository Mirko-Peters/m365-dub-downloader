## FAQ

**Is Microsoft Defender XDR a separate product I have to buy?**

No, Defender XDR is not sold as a separate line item. It is a unifying layer over Defender products you may already own and is included with Microsoft 365 E5 and the E5 security add-on. Business Premium or E3 with certain add-ons may already provide some of its capabilities.

**What are the five components of Microsoft Defender XDR?**

The five components are Defender for Endpoint, Defender for Office 365, Defender for Identity, Defender for Cloud Apps and vulnerability management. They protect devices, email and collaboration, on-premises Active Directory and cloud apps, and they prioritize missing patches. Defender XDR groups their related alerts into single incidents with a timeline.

**How do I turn on Defender XDR in my tenant?**

Go to security.microsoft.com, open the settings section and turn on the unified incident experience, which the episode says takes about ten minutes. First check your licensing in the Microsoft 365 admin center. Then deploy Defender for Endpoint, enable the Defender for Office 365 preset security policies and review vulnerability management.
