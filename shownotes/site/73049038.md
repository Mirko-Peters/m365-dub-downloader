## Key takeaways

- Azure Advisor is free and built into the Azure portal; it compares your resources against the Azure Well-Architected Framework and gives personalized recommendations across cost, security, reliability, performance and operational excellence.
- Recommendations carry high, medium or low impact, and the Advisor score from 0 to 100% shows per pillar where your environment falls short so you can tackle the lowest score and high-impact items first.

## FAQ

**Is Azure Advisor free to use?**

Yes, Azure Advisor is completely free and built into the Azure portal. There is nothing to install, enable or configure. It scans your resources, compares them against the Azure Well-Architected Framework and gives personalized recommendations across cost, security, reliability, performance and operational excellence, although it needs at least seven days of data first.

**How is the Azure Advisor score calculated?**

The Advisor score uses the healthy resource ratio, meaning the percentage of resources that follow the recommendations. Each of the five pillars gets its own score, and the overall score is their average. Reliability, performance and operational excellence use weighted subcategories. The score updates every 24 hours, and dismissed or postponed recommendations are excluded.

**Where do Azure Advisor security recommendations come from?**

Azure Advisor security recommendations come from Microsoft Defender for Cloud and are based on the Microsoft Cloud Security Benchmark, which aligns with standards such as CIS and NIST. Typical items include enabling MFA for privileged accounts, restricting network access to storage accounts and encrypting data at rest and in transit, each raising your secure score.
