## FAQ

**What is an authorization compiler in Power Platform governance?**

It is a framework that translates business policy into enforceable configuration once, instead of reviewing each access request manually. Intent such as finance users accessing customer data without exporting it becomes Entra ID roles, Conditional Access rules and DLP policies. Apps, flows and agents then inherit that policy automatically, so enforcement happens without waiting on an administrator.

**How does the zoned governance model for AI agents work?**

It assigns agents to one of three zones based on risk. Zone one covers personal productivity with no production data and minimal overhead. Zone two covers team collaboration with pre-approved connectors, data classification and audit logging. Zone three covers enterprise production agents with full monitoring, compliance enforcement, human oversight and the ability to terminate them instantly.

**How long does a Power Platform governance transformation take?**

The episode lays out twelve months. Months one and two cover assessment and policy definition, three and four authorization architecture, five to seven a pilot with refinement, eight to ten the enterprise rollout, and eleven and twelve optimization, delegated operations and measuring financial impact. Compressing the schedule, the episode warns, mostly piles up architectural debt.
