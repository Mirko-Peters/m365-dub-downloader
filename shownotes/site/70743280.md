## FAQ

**Why do organizations pause their Microsoft 365 Copilot rollouts?**

They pause because Copilot exposes permissions that were never governed, not because the technology fails. Copilot inherits each user's access, so a routine query can surface confidential emails or financial forecasts from overshared sites. Once security and legal teams see that exposure, regulated organizations in finance, healthcare and government stop the rollout to remediate.

**Why don't DLP policies protect data that has no sensitivity label?**

DLP policies need a signal such as a sensitivity label to match against, so unlabeled files pass through unnoticed. The episode argues that most files in a typical tenant carry no label, and Copilot summaries of confidential documents start unlabeled too. Governance and classification must therefore come before DLP can act as an enforcement mechanism.

**What does the 90-day governance-first blueprint for Microsoft 365 include?**

It runs in three 30-day phases: identity, then classification, then enforcement and monitoring. Days 1-30 define roles in Entra ID, enforce naming conventions and set access reviews. Days 31-60 apply sensitivity labels at the source and test DLP outside production. Days 61-90 pilot Copilot on low-risk sites and form an AI governance board.
