## FAQ

**Does Microsoft 365 Copilot create new security risks?**

According to Alan Cox, Copilot does not really introduce new risk; it surfaces existing risk such as oversharing and over-permissioning. Users may have access to content they never knew about, and Copilot can bring it up. That is why he starts every rollout with a full sharing and permission report and fixes what he finds first.

**How do you block Copilot from processing sensitive documents with Purview?**

Create a label, auto-apply it to a sensitive information type, a SharePoint site or even a single document, then add a DLP policy saying Copilot may not process content with that label. Copilot then will not even summarize it. In DSPM, the same policy can now be created in one or two clicks.

**What is adaptive protection in Microsoft Purview?**

Adaptive protection, part of Insider Risk Management, applies DLP controls based on each user's risk level. Alan builds one policy with three rules: a policy tip for minimal risk, a tip or blocked action for moderate risk, and blocked access for users with a history of risky behavior. It also works with Conditional Access.
