## FAQ

**What is CPOR in Microsoft Partner Center?**

CPOR, claiming partner of record, tells Microsoft you are the partner driving usage at a customer. You file it in Partner Center under Incentives and Customer claims, choose a role, list workloads and upload a signed proof of execution. CSP partners who also file CPOR get double points for customer success metrics.

**Why do CPOR claims get rejected?**

Claims usually fail for three reasons. The proof of execution must be electronically signed, not a scanned wet signature. It must be less than 12 months old, otherwise you need fresh confirmation from the customer. And each workload has to be listed separately with the actual work described, instead of a generic managed services statement.

**How can a Microsoft partner get ECIF funding?**

You need an advanced specialization first; without one there is no ECIF. There is no public portal: your partner development manager or a field seller nominates the deal with scope, expected Azure consumption and your credentials. Microsoft reviews it in roughly 30 days, and payout follows about 45 days after approval and proof of execution.
