## FAQ

**Do I need an extra license for Viva Learning?**

No, for basic functionality most organizations need no extra license, because Viva Learning is included in most Microsoft 365 E3, E5 and Business Premium plans. It only has to be turned on in the tenant. A Viva Learning or Viva Suite license becomes relevant if you exceed the limit of 1,000 SharePoint files.

**Where does the content in Viva Learning come from?**

Viva Learning does not create its own content but pulls it from several sources. The episode covers Microsoft's built-in library with Microsoft Learn and academies such as Copilot Academy, LinkedIn Learning if your company has licenses, and your own training files in SharePoint. Third-party learning management systems can be connected too, all appearing in one catalog.

**How do managers assign and track courses in Viva Learning?**

Managers assign courses directly in Teams by browsing the catalog, picking a course and setting a due date for a person or an entire team. Employees then see it in their My Learning tab. Managers can track who finished, who is in progress and who has not started, and can share courses in chats or channels.
