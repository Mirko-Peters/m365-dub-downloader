## FAQ

**How do you give Microsoft 365 Copilot persistent memory?**

You give it a structured vault of skill.md files that the system reads at the start of every session. Each file holds a name, a description, step-by-step instructions, examples of good output and constraints, so Copilot follows your actual working rules instead of starting from zero and asking you to re-explain your context every time.

**What is the difference between Copilot Cowork and regular Copilot chat?**

Copilot Cowork orchestrates multi-step work, while regular chat drafts content that you then execute manually. You describe a goal, such as recapping a meeting and scheduling a check-in, and Cowork plans the steps, uses Microsoft Graph to touch mail, calendar and files, then pauses so you can review, change or approve the plan.

**Why do agent flows need to finish within about 100 seconds?**

Agent flows run in a synchronous window of about 100 seconds because Cowork needs the result to chain the next step. That rules out heavy computation or slow queries inside the flow. The episode recommends background jobs that prefetch, cache and summarize data, so the flow reasons over prepared information and avoids Microsoft Graph throttling.
