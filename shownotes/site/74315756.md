## FAQ

**What is the difference between a copilot and an autonomous agent in Dynamics 365?**

A copilot waits for someone to ask, then answers, finds information or helps write. An autonomous agent has a specific job, watches for work such as a new case or order, and acts on its own within rules the organization sets. Unlike fixed automation flows, it reads the context before choosing an allowed action.

**Which autonomous agents are available in Dynamics 365?**

The episode names the customer intent, case management and customer knowledge management agents in Customer Service, the sales qualification agent in Sales, the sales order agent in Business Central, the account reconciliation agent in Finance, the supplier communications agent in Supply Chain Management and the scheduling operations agent in Field Service.

**How should a company start with Dynamics 365 autonomous agents?**

Start with repeatable work where a mistake has a clear way back, such as drafting follow-up messages for review or filling routine case details. Decide what the agent may read, update or send, and where a person must approve. Then review its actions, feedback and touched records, adjusting instructions, knowledge or permissions as needed.
