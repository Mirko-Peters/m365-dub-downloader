## FAQ

**Is the Microsoft Cloud Adoption Framework a product you install?**

No, the Microsoft Cloud Adoption Framework is guidance, not a product, a certification or a one-click setup. It gives organizations a way to think through cloud adoption in six connected parts: strategy, plan, ready, adopt, govern and manage. These parts help teams ask better questions before quick decisions turn into an expensive rebuild.

**What is an Azure landing zone?**

An Azure landing zone is a prepared part of Azure where applications and data can live safely. It covers identity with Microsoft Entra ID, management groups, subscriptions, networking, shared services and Azure Policy rules. A platform landing zone holds shared services, while application landing zones give individual workloads their own space under the same rules.

**Which workload should we move to Azure first?**

Pick a workload that matters enough to teach you something but is simple enough that people understand its owner, its data and what success looks like. Avoid starting with the oldest, least understood application, because fragile, poorly documented systems with hidden dependencies can turn a first move into a long rescue job.
