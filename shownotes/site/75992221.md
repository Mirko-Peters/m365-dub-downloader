## FAQ

**Do Power BI report viewers need a Pro license on a Fabric capacity?**

It depends on the capacity size. On F SKUs below F64, viewers still need a Power BI Pro license to open Power BI content. From F64 upward, a free license is enough for viewers, while creators still need Pro or Premium Per User to build and publish. Other Fabric items, such as lakehouses and notebooks, follow their own rules.

**Why does a Fabric capacity slow down days after a heavy job?**

Because of smoothing and carry forward. Fabric lets a heavy job burst beyond the capacity size, then spreads its cost over time, with background work smoothed across 24 hours. The overspend becomes debt that must be paid down, so new work competes with old work and throttling can hit unrelated users later.

**Does row-level security in a semantic model protect the data in OneLake?**

No, it only protects people who read through that model. Anyone with a workspace role that reaches the lake can open the Delta files directly and bypass the filter. OneLake security defines roles on the lakehouse itself so rules apply across engines, but admins, members and contributors can still bypass it.
