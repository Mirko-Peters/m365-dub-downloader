## FAQ

**What are the alternatives to Project Online after its retirement?**

Microsoft offers three routes: Microsoft Planner, an on-premises return to Project Server, or Dynamics 365 Project Operations. Joe advises against going back on premises and finds that organizations relying on time entries and complex work breakdown structures get a better long-term fit from Project Operations, because they do not have to build that functionality themselves.

**How do you migrate hundreds of projects into Dynamics 365 Project Operations?**

You automate the migration with scripts that target the schedule API, because Project Operations does not allow direct writes to its project, task and assignment tables. Single MPP files can be imported in the tool, but bulk moves need repeatable scripts. In one migration Joe describes, his team ran several test iterations before migrating all projects successfully.

**What does the time entry agent in Project Operations do?**

It drafts each person's weekly time entries from their resource assignments and Outlook appointments, then asks them to review and submit. It can also chase people who have not submitted. Joe adds that further AI capability can approve or reject submitted entries against a policy document, which removes a laborious, manual process that is critical for invoicing.
