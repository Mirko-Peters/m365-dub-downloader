## FAQ

**Why is hard-coded custom software considered technical debt?**

Every line of custom code becomes a future maintenance anchor that slows down change. When business logic is buried in code only one person understands, even a discount rule or shipping workflow needs regression tests and a deployment window. The episode argues this freezes how the business worked at one moment in time.

**What are the green, yellow and red zones in low-code governance?**

They are environment tiers for different levels of risk. The green zone is for personal productivity with strict DLP policies. The yellow zone hosts departmental apps after a center of excellence review and ownership plan. The red zone holds mission-critical logic with full application lifecycle management, separate environments and professional oversight.

**How can an organization start retiring legacy code with low-code in 90 days?**

Use a three-step roadmap. In the first 30 days, audit the backlog and pick high-value, high-friction processes instead of moving everything. By day 60, establish technical readiness with APIs, security layers and initial multi-agent orchestration. By day 90, launch the first pilot, ideally with a fusion team of business users and architects.
