## FAQ

**Why does Microsoft 365 Copilot adoption stall in many organizations?**

Copilot adoption often stalls because leaders insert themselves into every use case, so people treat it as a supervised experiment instead of a daily tool. Workflows and approval routes stay unchanged, and early pressure for visible ROI pushes people toward safe showcase tasks rather than honest experimentation that builds trust through repeated local use.

**What are decision boundaries in Microsoft 365 governance?**

Decision boundaries define who may decide what, under which conditions, without asking upward by default. The episode sorts decisions into local, cross-functional and executive classes, judged by risk class, business impact, reversibility, required evidence and time expectation. Low-risk, reversible choices stay local, while high-risk or irreversible ones escalate with context attached.

**How can leaders measure whether their governance creates decision flow?**

Leaders should track time to decision, suggestion acceptance rate and rework together, per decision stream rather than as company averages. Fast decisions with high rework signal noise, low rework with slow decisions signals over-control, and acceptance rates show whether Copilot output survives real work. Read in isolation, each metric invites misleading stories.
