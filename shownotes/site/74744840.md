## FAQ

**What is the Me, We, Us model for Microsoft 365?**

It is Tracy van der Schyff's way of showing employees where work belongs. Me covers personal tools such as Outlook, OneNote, OneDrive and To Do. We covers teamwork toward a common goal in Microsoft Teams and Viva. Us covers company-wide publishing, such as intranet sites built on SharePoint.

**What is ontological design?**

Ontological design is the idea that what we create ends up shaping us in return. Tracy compares it to cooking a dish you then eat: the intent and ingredients change you. Applications, social media posts and AI-generated content work the same way, so people should put purpose and thought into what they create.

**What happens when Copilot is rolled out into a messy Microsoft 365 environment?**

Copilot exposes the mess instead of fixing it. Tracy describes it as a spotlight that surfaces years of poor storage habits, oversharing and unclear permissions. Her advice is to draw a line from a set date, teach people where to store files and why, and treat data hygiene as the foundation for AI.
