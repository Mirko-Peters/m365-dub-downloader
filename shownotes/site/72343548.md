## FAQ

**What is the difference between shadow data and shadow IT?**

Shadow IT is the unauthorized applications employees use, while shadow data is the information itself: unknown, hidden or overlooked copies of sensitive files and records outside your governance frameworks. You can block every unauthorized app and still have major shadow data exposure, because much of that data lives in authorized tools that nobody is monitoring.

**How does Microsoft Purview scan for shadow data without slowing production systems?**

Purview reads and indexes metadata instead of copying the data, so sensitive content stays in your environment. The episode recommends managed identities, narrow scan scopes, custom scan rule sets, incremental scans after the first full scan, and off-peak, staggered schedules so the Data Map does not hit its throughput limits and start throttling.

**Why does data lineage matter for finding shadow data?**

Lineage shows where data came from and where it went, which is how ungoverned copies become visible. Purview captures lineage from sources such as Azure Data Factory and Power BI, revealing hops from production to dev environments and personal OneDrive folders, and traces each copy back to the pipeline, team or person that created it.
