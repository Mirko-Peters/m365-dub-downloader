## FAQ

**Is Microsoft 365 Copilot safe to use with company data?**

Yes, as long as you sign in with your enterprise account. Adrian Espes explains that this gives you enterprise data protection, so what you share in the chat is not shared with other people or companies and is not used to train AI models. He contrasts this with consumer tools, where training settings should be checked.

**How should an organization start a Microsoft 365 Copilot rollout?**

First decide whether you need paid licenses at all, around 28 euros or 30 dollars per user at the time of recording. Adrian recommends trying the free Microsoft 365 Copilot Chat first. Then review tenant settings, especially Anthropic models for European companies under GDPR, since processing happens on US servers, and options such as web grounding.

**Do employees need to become prompt engineers to use Copilot?**

No, prompting skills grow through practice rather than formal training. Adrian advises stating the main goal clearly and adding context; a role is optional because Copilot can infer it. For accurate results he tells Copilot not to invent information it does not have, and he tailors examples to each department's goals.
