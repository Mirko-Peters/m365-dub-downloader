## FAQ

**When should a Dynamics 365 Finance and Operations integration be asynchronous?**

Choose asynchronous when volume is high: Anitha Eswaran's rule of thumb is more than 10,000 records per five minutes, while under 1,000 can be synchronous. She first studies the process, its frequency and its load, then decides between real-time, near real-time and asynchronous patterns, including how failures will be detected and alerted.

**What are the limitations of OData in Dynamics 365 Finance and Operations?**

OData throttles under high volume, so it is not suited for high-throughput integrations. It is real-time and easy to consume for small transactions, but pushing large record volumes through it in a short window triggers throttling. For bulk data, DIXF fits better, and custom services handle complex logic such as multiple joins.

**Why use middleware like Azure Service Bus or Logic Apps with D365 F&O?**

Middleware takes the communication load off the AOS, which otherwise slows down when custom code calls external systems. F&O publishes a business event, Service Bus or Event Grid receives it, and downstream systems react. This gives loose coupling, high scalability and near real-time delivery, plus retry options such as dead-letter queues.
