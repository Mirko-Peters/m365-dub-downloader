## FAQ

**Why do so many generative AI pilots never reach production?**

Most fail because they do not lead with value first, meaning a clear return on investment and a defined outcome. Leon Gordon adds that many lack a governed, AI-ready data foundation, while rushed implementations skip quality testing against edge cases. A lack of business trust in the answers, including fear of hallucinations, also keeps projects stuck.

**Why are semantic models important for AI agents in Microsoft Fabric?**

Semantic models hold the business logic agents need: fact and dimension tables, relationships, measures and descriptive context. Leon Gordon calls the semantic model one of the highest priority objects in a data environment, because agents use it to understand calculations and data relationships. Fabric ontologies and Fabric IQ offer further routes to this intelligence layer.

**How should data quality anomalies be handled in a Fabric pipeline?**

Quarantine them instead of stopping the load. Leon Gordon's pattern is to define quality rules and tolerances with the organization, such as checks for nulls or value ranges, let compliant data load, and move anomalies into a quarantined environment where a human in the loop reviews them and reprocesses them if needed.
