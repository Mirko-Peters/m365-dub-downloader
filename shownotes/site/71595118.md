## FAQ

**Why does Copilot give wrong or outdated answers even with good prompts?**

Copilot usually fails because of the data underneath, not the prompt. The episode names three causes: context collapse when Teams chats, Loop components and SharePoint files diverge; mis-scoped permissions that either block everything or expose old overshared files; and stale duplicate files that Copilot treats as current, turning it into a hallucination amplifier.

**What is a decision lattice for Copilot?**

A decision lattice is a shared, structured framework that replaces individual prompting with four layers. Signals are raw feeds such as emails and meeting transcripts; the context layer uses curated libraries and Graph connectors; the decision node applies corporate rules in Copilot; and the action is a governed output you can trace back to its sources.

**How can you prove a Copilot architecture works in 30 days?**

Pick one messy, frequent workflow and measure it before changing anything. In weeks one and two, baseline time to decision and rework loops. In week three, define approved SharePoint sources, remove stale duplicates, map a Graph connector and set logic in Copilot Studio. In week four, compare time to decision, rework rate and exception count.
