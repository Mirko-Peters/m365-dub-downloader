## FAQ

**What is Microsoft Defender for Office 365?**

Defender for Office 365 is a cloud-based security service that protects email, calendar and documents inside Microsoft 365. It is not a separate app and not just antivirus for email. It uses five layers, anti-spam, anti-malware with Safe Attachments, anti-phishing, Safe Links and anti-spoofing, that check every incoming email within seconds.

**How does Safe Links protect against malicious URLs?**

Safe Links rewrites URLs and checks the destination at the moment you click, not when the email arrives. This catches links that were harmless when sent but changed later, known as time-of-click threats. It also follows redirects and shortened URLs, and it works across email, Teams chats and Office documents such as Word files.

**How does Defender for Office 365 stop spoofed emails?**

Defender checks email authentication with SPF, DKIM and DMARC to confirm a message came from an authorized server. SPF verifies the sending server, DKIM checks the digital signature, and DMARC tells the receiving server what to do on failure. Impersonation protection also catches look-alike domains and fake messages from executives such as the CEO.
