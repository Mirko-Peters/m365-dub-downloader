## FAQ

**What is Dynamics 365 Knowledge Management?**

It is a single place where a support team creates, organizes, reviews, finds and improves help articles while working with customers. Each article has a title, subject and labels, and it is embedded in Dynamics 365 Customer Service, so agents search for guidance from the same screen where they handle the customer case.

**How does a knowledge article get approved and published?**

A draft gets categories and keywords, then a subject expert checks that the advice is correct and the article owner decides whether it is ready and who can read it. After approval it is published to the right audience, either employees only or a customer self-service site, and later reviewed, updated or retired.

**Can customers see the same knowledge articles as support agents?**

Only if you publish them that way. Permissions control which articles agents can read and which appear on a customer self-service site. Internal articles can include staff-only steps such as account checks, while the public version uses customer-friendly words and safe instructions. The same question may therefore need two separate versions.
