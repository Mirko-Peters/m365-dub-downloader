## FAQ

**How does the AppSource ranking algorithm decide which listings to show?**

It weighs measurable signals, and the title is the single most important factor. Descriptive keywords in the title matter more than the brand name. Engagement signals such as click-through rate, installs, leads and retention also feed back into ranking, so listings that convert well get surfaced more while weak ones stay buried.

**What is generative engine optimization (GEO) for an AppSource listing?**

GEO is the practice of making your content citable by AI systems such as Copilot, ChatGPT and Perplexity, so your solution is referenced when buyers ask an AI tool a question. AI weighs entity strength, factual depth and citations, so use FAQ-style content, specific verifiable claims and genuine mentions on Reddit and LinkedIn.

**What should the first 50 words of an AppSource description say?**

They should answer who the solution is for, what problem it solves and what outcome buyers can expect. According to Microsoft's guidance, the first 30 to 50 words appear in search results, so they are your chance to earn a click. Lead with the outcome, such as a faster month-end close, not the feature.
