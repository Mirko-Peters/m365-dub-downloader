## FAQ

**What is Dynamics 365 Commerce used for?**

Dynamics 365 Commerce connects selling across websites, physical stores, call centers and back office teams around shared products, prices, stock, orders and customer records. It is not just a website builder or a cash register system, but supports both and links them to the wider business, so staff and shoppers work from the same information.

**What is the Commerce Scale Unit in Dynamics 365 Commerce?**

The Commerce Scale Unit is the headless engine that applies the same selling rules for every channel. Websites, Store Commerce and custom apps send requests through APIs for prices, availability or new orders. It can run in Microsoft's cloud or as a self-hosted unit closer to a store with unreliable internet, or as a mix of both.

**Can a customer return an online order in a different store?**

Yes. Because the order and customer record travel with the purchase, staff in another branch can find the original online order in the Store Commerce app and process the return there. The customer does not have to contact the website first, which removes the common frustration of being told an item belongs to a different system.
