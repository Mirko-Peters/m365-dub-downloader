## FAQ

**What is the difference between Customer Insights - Data and Customer Insights - Journeys?**

Customer Insights - Data brings customer information together into unified profiles and segments, while Customer Insights - Journeys uses that information to plan and send communication. A simple way to remember it: Data helps you see the customer, Journeys helps you respond. Both now sit under the Dynamics 365 Customer Insights name and make most sense together.

**What happened to Dynamics 365 Marketing?**

Microsoft renamed Dynamics 365 Marketing to Customer Insights - Journeys in September 2023, when the original Customer Insights product became Customer Insights - Data. People still say D365 Marketing, real-time marketing or just marketing, so in meetings it helps to ask whether someone means the Data side, the Journeys side or both.

**How does Customer Insights match records from different systems into one customer profile?**

It uses matching rules that the business sets up instead of guessing that similar records belong to the same person. Raw records from sales, purchase, website and service systems are joined into a unified customer profile when they refer to the same customer. Good rules reduce duplicates without merging different people who share details.
