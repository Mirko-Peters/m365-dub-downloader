## FAQ

**What is an industrial data model?**

An industrial data model is a shared description of factory objects, their identities, states and relationships, so ERP, MES, maintenance and quality data mean the same thing. It does not replace those source systems, which keep ownership of their data. It lets people, planning tools, simulation and AI answer what a single event, like a machine stop, affects.

**What does PPR stand for in manufacturing data models?**

PPR stands for product, process and resource. It links what the factory builds, how the work must happen and what can perform it, such as machines, tools and qualified people. Those links turn isolated records into context, so a planner can check whether an alternate resource can run an operation for a specific product revision with an approved tool.

**Can Microsoft Fabric create an industrial data model for you?**

No. Microsoft Fabric can ingest, store, govern and present data from ERP, MES, quality, maintenance and IoT sources, and its semantic models support shared reporting definitions. But it cannot decide what a resource capability means in your plant or whether a machine can process a product revision. People who own the process must define those relationships.
