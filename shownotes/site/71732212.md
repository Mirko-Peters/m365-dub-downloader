## FAQ

**Does Microsoft 365 Copilot create new access to files?**

No, Copilot does not grant new access; it works with the permissions you already have. What changes is visibility: oversharing that used to sit unnoticed, such as broad Everyone group access or an M&A folder set to internal, becomes conversational. A junior analyst asking for a strategy summary could receive content about an unannounced merger.

**What is Restricted SharePoint Search used for in a Copilot rollout?**

Restricted SharePoint Search acts as an emergency brake that excludes high-risk sites, such as board papers, payroll data or legal strategy, from being used as grounding for Copilot. It can be applied before any sensitivity label work, giving teams room to fix their permission debt without shutting down the whole AI deployment.

**Why do rigid sensitivity labels lead to more rework with Copilot?**

When a label blocks Copilot from the system of record, it falls back to whatever it can see, like an old draft in a public Teams channel. Users then spend time checking and correcting the output. The episode estimates that around 40 percent of the time saved with AI is lost to this kind of rework.
