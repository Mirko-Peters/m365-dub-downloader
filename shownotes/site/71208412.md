## FAQ

**What are the five levels of Microsoft 365 governance maturity?**

The model runs from level 100, reactive governance driven by incidents and heroics, through level 200, managed but fragile, and level 300, defined but uneven across workloads. Level 400 is predictable governance with executable ownership, automation and trusted metrics, while level 500 is optimized governance that keeps tuning controls against business risk.

**How can I quickly check my organization's governance maturity?**

Ask five questions: does every critical workspace and dataset have a clear owner, do you know what share of sensitive data is labeled, are key controls automated, can you produce audit evidence in days, and does governance make the right behavior easiest? Mostly yes suggests level 400; mostly no points toward level 100.

**Why does more security awareness training not fix governance problems?**

Training improves awareness but cannot fix a design flaw. If broad access is the default, labels are easy to skip and the approved path is slower than the workaround, people under pressure take the shortcut. Mirko Peters recommends safer defaults, enforced flows and fewer optional controls first, so training reinforces a working system.
