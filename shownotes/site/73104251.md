## Key takeaways

- OneDrive sync is a continuous two-way mirror between devices and the cloud, backed by version history, not a one-time upload.
- Files On-Demand keeps all files visible as online-only placeholders, while selective sync decides which folders appear on a device at all.
- Most sync problems come from long paths, full storage, locked files or unsupported characters, and pausing and resuming sync fixes many temporary glitches.

## FAQ

**Why is my OneDrive not syncing and showing a red X?**

Usually a sync rule was broken. The episode names three main causes: file paths over 400 characters, a full storage quota, and files locked by an open app such as Word or Excel. Unsupported characters in file names and very large files or libraries also cause problems. Pausing sync for 30 seconds and resuming fixes many glitches.

**What is the difference between Files On-Demand and selective sync in OneDrive?**

Selective sync decides which folders appear on your device at all, while Files On-Demand shows every file but keeps most as online-only placeholders. Unchecked folders in selective sync live only in the cloud. With Files On-Demand you can mark files as always keep on this device or free up space to return them to online-only.

**How do I move my OneDrive folder to another drive?**

You unlink and relink your account instead of dragging the folder. In OneDrive settings, open the account tab and choose unlink this PC, after copying your files somewhere safe. When you sign back in, click change location and pick a folder on the other drive. This only changes local copies, not your cloud storage.
