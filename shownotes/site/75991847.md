## FAQ

**How do you build a cloud cost baseline for a manufacturing data platform?**

Start by naming the billing boundary: which subscriptions, services and billing period the review covers, checked against the invoice. Use the rates your organization actually pays, keep credits separate, never mix actual and amortized cost views, and observe normal shifts plus shutdowns, maintenance windows and period-close reporting peaks before judging any change.

**How does Microsoft Fabric capacity billing work?**

A Fabric capacity is billed by its purchased size and billing arrangement, not by adding up each item's consumption. Workloads consume capacity units over time, recorded as CU seconds, and smoothing spreads interactive and background usage across different windows. The Fabric Capacity Metrics app helps link utilization and throttling to the items creating pressure.

**What is the difference between showback and chargeback?**

Showback shows a plant its attributed cloud costs without moving money out of its budget, while chargeback makes an actual internal budget transfer. The episode suggests starting with showback to discover incentives, and only moving to chargeback once plant owners understand which charges they can influence and how disputed amounts get corrected.
