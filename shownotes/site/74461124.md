## FAQ

**What does settlement mean in Dynamics 365 Finance accounts receivable?**

Settlement means matching a customer payment to the invoice it pays, so the invoice no longer shows as unpaid. Without settlement, an open invoice can sit on the account even though the cash already arrived. Partial payments can be settled too, leaving only the unpaid remainder open so finance sees exactly what is still due.

**What happens when a sales order goes on credit hold in Dynamics 365?**

A credit hold pauses the sales order for review before more goods ship; it does not reject the order. Dynamics 365 can trigger it based on the credit limit, open invoices, overdue days and the new order value. Authorized staff can release the order when there is a sound reason, such as proof that payment is on the way.

**How does the collections page in Dynamics 365 Finance help with overdue invoices?**

The collections page gives the team one shared place to see overdue balances and all follow-up activity. Agents work from customer lists built with collection criteria, then send reminders, collection letters or emails, or schedule calls. They can record promises to pay and flag disputed invoices, so the next agent knows what was already agreed.
