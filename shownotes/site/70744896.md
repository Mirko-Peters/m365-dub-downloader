## FAQ

**How can Microsoft Purview be used as a business intelligence tool?**

Purview can show how the organization really operates by reading its signals as behavioral data. Sensitivity label distributions reveal what teams value, DLP violations point to broken workflows, and insider risk signals highlight stressed or understaffed teams. Access patterns also expose silos, hidden dependencies on single people and manual work such as repeated reconciliation.

**What does it mean when most files are labeled highly confidential?**

It usually means people do not know what is truly sensitive, so the labels lose their signal value. The episode describes a financial services firm where 85% of labeled data was confidential or highly confidential. Overclassification creates friction for permissions and points to unclear priorities, so leaders should clarify what actually needs protection.

**How do you prepare Microsoft 365 data for Copilot in 90 days?**

Use a 30-60-90 plan that moves from visibility to taxonomy to readiness. First run discovery and baseline the critical data types. Next apply auto-labeling, run DLP in audit mode and complete the three audits. Finally refine policies, create AI-ready data zones for departments like finance and align permissions before scaling Copilot.
