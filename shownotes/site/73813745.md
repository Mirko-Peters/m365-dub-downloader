## FAQ

**What is the difference between the Azure Well-Architected Framework and the Cloud Adoption Framework?**

The Cloud Adoption Framework prepares the company-wide Azure foundation, while the Well-Architected Framework looks at one workload at a time. CAF covers shared governance, security, networking, subscriptions and landing zones. WAF asks whether a specific customer portal or order system can do its job well, much like one plane relying on a prepared airport.

**What are the five pillars of the Azure Well-Architected Framework?**

The five pillars are reliability, security, cost optimization, operational excellence and performance efficiency. Reliability keeps promises when parts fail, security controls who gets in and what they reach, cost optimization removes waste, operational excellence makes daily work repeatable, and performance efficiency helps the workload meet demand when users need it most.

**How do you run a first Azure Well-Architected review?**

Pick one workload with a clear business purpose and run the Azure Well-Architected review, which asks roughly 60 questions across the five pillars. Treat it as a discussion, not an exam. Turn the recommendations into a prioritized backlog, give every action an owner, record the trade-offs and repeat the review after major changes.
