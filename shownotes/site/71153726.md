## FAQ

**What is structural debt in Microsoft 365?**

Structural debt is the collection of design choices in a tenant that keep producing risky behavior even when nobody intends harm. It includes permissions granted quickly and never pulled back, workspace creation without lifecycle logic, unclear ownership and connectors approved one request at a time. It compounds quietly through convenience rather than through dramatic incidents.

**Why are Microsoft 365 default settings not neutral?**

Defaults are product decisions that signal which path is normal, so they shape behavior faster than any policy document. They are built as a generic starting state for many organizations and cannot know your regulatory environment, partner model or sensitive departments. Inheriting them as governance means inheriting generic enablement without business context.

**How can leaders reduce shadow IT in Microsoft 365?**

Leaders reduce shadow IT by making the governed path faster than the workaround, not by adding restrictions. The episode suggests a 30-day pilot in one high-pressure area: workspaces in minutes from templates, owners and labels built in from day one, clear open, controlled and sensitive zones, and measuring waiting time, exceptions and off-platform work.
