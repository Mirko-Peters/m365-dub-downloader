## FAQ

**What is the difference between sourcing and procurement?**

Sourcing is finding and choosing the right vendor, while procurement manages the actual purchase once the company decides to buy. Procurement covers the request, approvals, purchase order, delivery, invoice and payment. Together these steps form the source-to-pay process, which Dynamics 365 handles inside Supply Chain Management with links to finance and accounts payable.

**What is three-way matching in Dynamics 365?**

Three-way matching compares the purchase order, the product receipt and the vendor invoice before payment. If a vendor bills 20 helmets but only 10 were received, the difference shows up so the company can investigate. Price differences, extra quantities or unordered lines can be caught while there is still time to ask a clear question.

**What is a purchase requisition and how does it differ from a purchase order?**

A purchase requisition is an internal request to buy something and does not promise anything to a vendor. It states what is needed, how much and where it should go, then passes through approval workflows. A purchase order is the formal document sent to the vendor with items, quantities, agreed prices, delivery date and payment terms.
