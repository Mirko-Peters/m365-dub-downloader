## FAQ

**What is the difference between Windows Update for Business and Windows Autopatch?**

Windows Autopatch is not a new update engine but a managed service on top of Intune for policy and Windows Update for Business for delivery. Microsoft takes over ring creation, monitoring and remediation, such as pausing a failing rollout, while policy decisions, exclusions and accountability stay with your organization.

**How do quality and feature update deferrals differ in Intune update rings?**

Quality update deferrals run from zero to 30 days because monthly patches close holes attackers study, while feature update deferrals can reach 365 days because the current version stays supported. Each type also gets its own deadline of zero to 30 days from when the update is offered, plus a grace period of up to seven days.

**When should I use an expedited quality update in Intune?**

Use an expedited quality update when the risk is already real: confirmed active exploitation, or a flaw reachable from the internet or needing no user action in something your estate runs. It skips the ring deferral for one named update but forces a restart, so warn users first and remove the policy once the fix has landed.
