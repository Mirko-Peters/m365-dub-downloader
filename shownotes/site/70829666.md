## FAQ

**Does high Microsoft Teams activity mean collaboration is healthy?**

Not necessarily, because high Teams activity often signals coordination pressure rather than healthy collaboration. Mirko Peters points to team and channel sprawl, multiplying private channels, decisions moving into one-to-one chats and heavy message density. These patterns usually mean people are rebuilding clarity in real time because ownership and shared context were never designed into the environment.

**Why does Copilot give inconsistent answers in a well-governed Microsoft 365 tenant?**

Copilot reasons over the content, permissions and context that actually exist, not over your intended design. When SharePoint and OneDrive hold contradictory versions, weak metadata and drifting access, Copilot surfaces that ambiguity faster and in fluent language. The episode frames weak output as an organizational memory problem first, not primarily an AI quality problem.

**How can leaders find the real organization behind the org chart?**

Start by tracing one real, recurring decision from trigger to outcome instead of running a broad maturity assessment. Then follow the files and versions it depends on, identify the hidden owners everyone checks with, and measure friction such as handoffs, delays and version counts. Finally, simplify team spaces, content locations and permissions before scaling anything.
