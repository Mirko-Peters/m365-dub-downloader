## FAQ

**What is Dynamics 365 Supply Chain Management used for?**

Dynamics 365 Supply Chain Management is a cloud-based ERP system for managing the physical movement of products and materials. It connects product records, demand planning, purchasing, inventory, warehouse work, production, equipment maintenance and transport, so purchasing, production, warehouse and customer service teams work from one shared record instead of separate spreadsheets and old reports.

**Why is a total stock number not enough in Dynamics 365 Supply Chain Management?**

A total stock number hides what the business can actually use. The episode's example shows 500 units, of which 300 are reserved, 150 are blocked for inspection and only 50 are ready. Location and status, kept current by barcode scans on mobile devices, show what can really be picked, promised and shipped.

**Does AI or Copilot make supply chain decisions in Dynamics 365?**

No, AI and Copilot support people but do not run the business. AI can estimate demand, flag unusual patterns and highlight possible supply issues, and Microsoft had a procurement agent in preview at the time of recording. Planners, buyers and warehouse managers still check suggestions, apply their own knowledge and take responsibility for decisions.
