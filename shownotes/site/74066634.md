## FAQ

**What is an ontology layer in Microsoft Fabric and why does it matter for AI?**

An ontology layer sits above several semantic models and relates them through a graph model instead of one giant semantic model. Walter explains that an LLM can be given access to this layer to answer business questions, while security still applies, so users only reach data they are allowed to see and sensitive data can be anonymized.

**Does medallion architecture always need bronze, silver and gold layers?**

No. Walter argues you need at least two layers and can use three, five or seven as needed. The first stores historical data without worrying about duplicates, the second holds cleaned, consistent data. A further layer contains data prepared for a specific purpose, such as machine learning or AI, which helps keep token costs under control.

**What does Walter Calcagno's AI readiness assessment measure?**

It measures how ready a company is for AI adoption across five dimensions: governance, technology, internal political decision, human resource readiness and training. The result is a picture of what to fix first, for example governance before any AI project, because adopting AI without that groundwork, in Walter's words, throws money into the trash.
