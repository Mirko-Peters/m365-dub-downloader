## FAQ

**What is FinOps in simple terms?**

FinOps is a shared practice between the people who build with technology and the people who pay for it. The name combines finance and DevOps. It is not a product or a single job title, and its goal is getting the most value from technology spending rather than only shrinking the bill, sometimes by spending more.

**Which Microsoft tools help with FinOps in Azure and Microsoft 365?**

Microsoft Cost Management is the main tool, reachable from the Azure portal and the Microsoft 365 admin center. It offers cost analysis, budgets, anomaly and scheduled alerts, and exports to Power BI. Azure Advisor recommends resizing or shutting down idle resources, while savings plans, reservations and Azure Hybrid Benefit lower rates for steady usage.

**What is the difference between showback and chargeback?**

Showback shows each team what it spends, while chargeback actually bills each team for its usage. Showback is the gentler place to start because it builds awareness before budgets move. Neither is meant to punish anyone; both make the cost of a decision visible to the person making it, so the next choice is smarter.
