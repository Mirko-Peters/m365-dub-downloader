## FAQ

**What role does Microsoft Fabric Real-Time Intelligence play in a factory?**

It sits in the middle of the pipeline as a shared plane for data in motion. Eventstream ingests and routes events, Eventhouse and KQL support fast investigation, and Activator triggers defined actions. It does not replace the machine controller, the MES or an APS system, and it does not calculate feasible production schedules.

**Can Copilot reschedule production after a machine stops?**

No, Copilot should explain rather than schedule. With governed access to trusted data it can summarize the event sequence, retrieve approved procedures and draft shift handover notes. Calculating a feasible plan needs an APS system, a constraint solver or defined business rules that test capacity, tooling, material and labor, followed by human approval.

**How do you stop Fabric Activator from flooding people with alerts?**

Design conditions around production context and add protection against noise. Use debounce logic so a stop must persist before it counts, suppress duplicate cases for the same incident and suppress escalations during approved maintenance states. Set priority by bottleneck, active work and shift, and build the rules with the people who receive the work.
