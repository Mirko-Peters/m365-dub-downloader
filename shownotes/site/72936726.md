## FAQ

**Why does Copilot in Power BI give wrong answers?**

Copilot in Power BI matches words in your question to words in your report, such as column names, page titles and chart labels, rather than understanding meaning. When your wording differs, it guesses and can confidently pull the wrong figure. It is also non-deterministic, so the same question can produce different answers in different sessions.

**How should I phrase questions to Copilot in Power BI?**

Use the exact terms already in your report instead of everyday language. If a chart is labeled order volume, ask about order volume, not total orders. Be specific about visual types, fields and time periods, and refine results in follow-up messages, for example asking Copilot to switch to quarterly or sort from highest to lowest.

**Can Copilot in Power BI forecast numbers or explain why something happened?**

No, Copilot in Power BI cannot forecast future numbers or explain causes. It only works with data already in your model and can show historical patterns, but explaining why something changed needs human business context. It also cannot fix bad source data, and for major formatting changes it gives manual instructions instead of applying them.
