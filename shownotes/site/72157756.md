## FAQ

**What is the difference between Copilot Cowork and regular Microsoft 365 Copilot?**

Copilot Cowork plans and executes multi-step work, while regular Copilot is meant for simpler question-and-answer tasks. You give Cowork goals, background and instructions, much like briefing a new employee, and it reasons over the data, plans, then acts across Word, Excel, PowerPoint, PDFs, Teams and email, following constraints more closely.

**What is Work IQ in Microsoft 365?**

Work IQ is the intelligence layer between your requests and the information AI retrieves from Microsoft 365. It builds on Microsoft Graph, which Copilot has long used to fetch data, but reasons over that data better, so the AI understands what you work on, who you message and which meetings are coming up.

**When should you use Copilot Pages, Copilot Notebooks or OneNote?**

Use Copilot Pages to turn a Copilot conversation into a shared, editable Loop page, and Copilot Notebooks to collect pages, links and documents on one topic for AI to reason over. OneNote keeps its strengths, such as structured sections and easier sharing with external users, so many organizations use both.
