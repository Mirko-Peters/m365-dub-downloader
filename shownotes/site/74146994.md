## FAQ

**What is Dynamics 365 Sales Insights?**

Dynamics 365 Sales Insights is a group of AI-powered tools inside Dynamics 365 Sales that turns sales activity into guidance. It sits on top of existing CRM records and surfaces prompts, scores leads and opportunities, and shows relationship signals, so sellers spend less time searching and more time deciding what to do next.

**How does predictive lead scoring work in Dynamics 365 Sales?**

Predictive lead scoring compares current leads with past leads that turned into opportunities or customers and gives each one a score. It can use fields such as lead source, company details, job role and activities. The score helps sellers sort a crowded list, but a low score does not mean a lead belongs in the bin.

**Does auto capture save every email to the CRM automatically?**

No, auto capture finds emails and meetings connected to customers and brings them to the seller for review. The seller decides what belongs with an account, contact, lead or opportunity, which keeps private or unrelated threads out of the record. The premium version can also suggest creating a new contact for an unknown colleague in a thread.
