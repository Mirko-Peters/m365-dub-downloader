## FAQ

**What is the difference between a Fabric warehouse and a lakehouse?**

A Fabric warehouse supports full DML with insert, update, delete and merge, while a lakehouse SQL endpoint is read only. Both store data as Delta tables in OneLake. The episode recommends the lakehouse for raw, still-changing data and exploration, and the warehouse for cleaned, structured data with a strict relational schema used for reporting.

**How does time travel work in Microsoft Fabric Warehouse?**

Time travel lets you query a table as it existed earlier by adding FOR TIMESTAMP AS OF with a UTC date and time to a SELECT statement. It relies on the Delta transaction log and keeps history for 30 days by default. That makes it useful for recovering accidentally deleted rows without restoring a backup.

**What is Direct Lake mode in Power BI and why does it matter?**

Direct Lake lets Power BI read Delta files in OneLake directly, instead of importing data or sending live DirectQuery calls to the warehouse. Import leaves data stale between refreshes, and DirectQuery costs compute on every click. With Direct Lake, reports stay fresh and fast without a refresh schedule or per-query compute charges, according to the episode.
