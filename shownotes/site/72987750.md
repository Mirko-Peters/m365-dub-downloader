## FAQ

**Does listing on Microsoft Marketplace generate leads automatically?**

No, a marketplace listing does not equal lead flow. The episode describes Microsoft as an amplifier, like a microphone: nothing happens unless you bring a story and demonstrated value. Leads are a mature output of the partnership, arriving after you have shown you can win and that Microsoft field time invested in you pays off.

**How many deals should a Microsoft partner register as co-sell?**

Only the few deals where you truly need Microsoft's help; the episode suggests three to five high-value use cases. Flooding Partner Center with every deal marked as co-sell dilutes your signal, makes you look desperate rather than strategic, and leaves Microsoft sellers unable to tell which opportunities actually need their involvement.

**Why do Microsoft partners lose incentive payments?**

Often because their own finance team is not prepared. Finance may treat an unexpected incentive payment as a mistake and send it back, or let it disappear into general revenue. The fix is to brief finance proactively on private offers and incentive payouts, then reinvest the funding in events, co-marketing and partnership headcount.
