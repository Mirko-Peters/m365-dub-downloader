## FAQ

**Why does shadow IT grow in Power Platform tenants?**

Shadow IT grows because manual, ticket-based governance cannot keep pace with low-code development, so makers bypass it to deliver results. Mirko Peters describes makers using personal connectors when business ones are blocked and sharing apps with everyone because security group requests take weeks. Saying no removes IT's visibility without actually removing the risk.

**What is environment routing in Power Platform and why does it matter?**

Environment routing automatically sends new makers into their own personal developer environment instead of the shared default environment. The system checks predefined rules and creates the environment on the fly if needed, in seconds rather than weeks. This limits the blast radius of mistakes, clears noise from the default environment and gives makers a safe place to experiment.

**How should a Center of Excellence handle unmanaged apps?**

A Center of Excellence should invite the makers of unmanaged apps to make them enterprise-ready rather than treating them as rule breakers. The episode describes using the CoE Starter Kit to find orphaned and unmanaged apps, then offering help instead of warnings. Makers who feel recognized, it argues, are far more likely to follow compliance standards voluntarily.
