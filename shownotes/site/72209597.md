## FAQ

**Why do so many ERP implementations fail?**

Most fail because users never adopt the change, not because of the technology. Alicia King points to searches showing only about half of ERP implementations are deemed successful and links that to weak change management. Leaders must make users feel safe and capable, and teams can personalize Dynamics 365 screens to hide unneeded fields.

**How should a company staff an ERP project on its own side?**

Free up dedicated key users and subject matter experts by offloading part of their daily work before the project starts. Adding the implementation on top of 40 to 50 hour weeks leads to burnout and resistance. Both customer and partner also need strong project managers who share risks openly on the RAID log.

**Does AI in Dynamics 365 Finance and Supply Chain respect security roles?**

Yes, AI and MCP access in Dynamics 365 run under the user's role-based security permissions. When Alicia asks Copilot in Teams to create a purchase order, the system checks whether her role may write that data. Governance gets trickier once external sources such as SharePoint files or other applications are pulled in.
