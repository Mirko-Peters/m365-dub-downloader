## FAQ

**Which Microsoft expert certification should I take first?**

Pick the credential that matches your current role. The episode maps Azure infrastructure administrators to AZ-305, security engineers to SC-100, Power Platform developers to PL-600, Azure developers to AI-102 and Microsoft 365 administrators to MS-102. Career changers should start with fundamentals such as AZ-900, SC-900 or PL-900 and build real experience first.

**What is the difference between AZ-104 and AZ-305?**

AZ-104 teaches you to operate Azure, while AZ-305 teaches you to decide what Azure should look like. The administrator exam validates executing a design someone else made. The architect exam tests trade-offs between cost, performance and resilience in unfamiliar scenarios, so it cannot be passed by memorizing commands or procedures alone.

**How do I calculate whether a Microsoft certification is worth it?**

Compare the realistic salary premium with the full cost, which is mostly your time. Direct costs are small, but six to nine months of study means roughly 400 to 600 hours. Then adjust the expected premium for your geography, career stage, employer recognition, market demand and the risk of failing the exam or seeing no promotion.
