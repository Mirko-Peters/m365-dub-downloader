## FAQ

**What makes a Microsoft Teams Rooms meeting space inclusive?**

A camera at eye level of the people in the room, not mounted above the screen, is the starting point. Onyinye Madubuko also checks lighting and sunlight reflection, tidy cable management and a noise assessment covering roads, footsteps and glass walls, so engineers can choose devices with suitable noise cancellation.

**What should an organization do before deploying Microsoft 365 Copilot?**

Assess oversharing first, because Copilot exposes it. Review file owners, anyone-with-the-link sharing, access duration and sensitivity labels in OneDrive and SharePoint, then confirm baseline security such as conditional access, MFA and blocking legacy authentication. Only then deploy licenses in phases to pilot users, backed by training and departmental champions.

**How can you track Copilot adoption and deal with unused licenses?**

Use the Copilot Control System in the admin center to see who uses Copilot and in which apps. Low usage signals a need for more training. If users still do not engage after being contacted, reassign their licenses to colleagues who will use them, since Copilot licenses are costly.
