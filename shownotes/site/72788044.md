## FAQ

**What is subscription vending in an Azure landing zone?**

Subscription vending automates subscription requests: a short form triggers a Bicep deployment that creates the subscription, places it in the right management group and applies policies, RBAC, logging and networking. Teams get a usable, compliant environment in minutes instead of weeks, and the governance decisions are encoded once in code rather than repeated manually.

**How should Bicep modules be versioned and consumed at scale?**

Publish modules to a registry with semantic versioning and have teams reference a specific pinned version, never latest or main. Major versions signal breaking changes, minor versions add options and patches fix issues. Upgrades happen through a reviewed pull request, published versions stay immutable, and teams consume modules instead of forking copies that drift.

**Should Azure Policy start in deny mode or audit mode?**

Start in audit mode, then enforce. Audit policies log and flag violations without blocking deployments, so you can collect data for several weeks and ask teams why they deviate. Turn on deny only for validated policies, once a time-bound exception process exists and the governed path is faster than the workaround.
