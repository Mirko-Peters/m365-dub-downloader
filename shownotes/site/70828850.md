## FAQ

**Why do high performers often feel isolated at work?**

High performers often become the hidden integration layer that holds a fragmented system together. They carry context, translate between teams and smooth over unclear ownership, so leaders see strong output instead of strain. Because few colleagues understand the full load they carry, their work feels essential but invisible, which feeds loneliness, burnout and eventually protective withdrawal.

**How do private channels in Microsoft Teams affect collaboration?**

Used as a default habit, private channels split the organization into small pockets of trust that others cannot see. Work inside them moves fast, but decisions become hard to reconstruct, weak ties disappear and new employees lose entry points into the real work. The episode recommends open-by-default spaces and shared decision logs instead.

**Is remote work the cause of loneliness at work?**

No, the episode argues that remote work exposed weak connection design rather than creating it. Offices once supplied accidental contact and weak ties for free, and many organizations never built a digital replacement. Research cited in the episode shows hybrid and remote workers can report higher psychological safety, so better connection architecture matters more than mandated presence.
