## FAQ

**What is a case in Dynamics 365 Customer Service?**

A case is the central record for a single customer issue, and every email, call and chat links back to it. It holds the description, product, priority and an SLA timer. If a case is about to breach its SLA, escalation rules notify a supervisor or reassign it, and Copilot can summarize its full history.

**What AI agents are included in Dynamics 365 Customer Service?**

The episode covers four: the case management agent, the customer intent agent, the customer knowledge management agent and the quality evaluation agent. They create and close cases, spot new issue patterns, draft knowledge articles while checking for duplicates, and score interactions with coaching suggestions. They feed into each other, alongside Copilot in the agent workspace.

**How does Dynamics 365 Customer Service integrate with Teams and Outlook?**

Teams is embedded in the Dynamics workspace, so agents chat, call and open swarming channels linked to a case without switching apps. In Outlook, an agent turns an email into a case with one click and the case fields fill automatically. External experts can view the case in Teams without a Dynamics license.
