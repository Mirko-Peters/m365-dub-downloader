## FAQ

**Why do Dynamics 365 and Power Platform projects fail?**

They usually fail because of people, not technology. Areti Iles sees business experts without enough time for discovery, testing and UAT, scope creep that turns an MVP into a long project, end users kept at arm's length until go-live, and change management that starts too late. Unclear ownership and underestimated running costs add further risk.

**What should organizations do first to prepare for the EU AI Act?**

Start with an AI inventory that shows which AI is used in your tenant and for what. Then classify each use case by risk, put human oversight, logging and data loss prevention in place, and provide AI literacy training. Iles also recommends an acceptable use policy so shadow AI becomes visible and can be managed rather than banned.

**Which Microsoft tools help govern AI use in an organization?**

Iles names three: Microsoft Defender, Microsoft Purview and Foundry. Defender discovers AI apps and agents and detects threats such as prompt injection. Purview logs prompts and responses, classifies sensitive data and offers Compliance Manager scoring against regulations. Foundry matters once you build or customize models, governing allowed models, guardrails, safety filters and documentation.
