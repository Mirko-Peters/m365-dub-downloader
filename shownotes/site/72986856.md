## FAQ

**What replaced Microsoft Silver and Gold partner levels?**

The Microsoft Cloud Partner Program replaced them in October 2022 and was rebranded the AI Cloud Partner Program in July 2023. Instead of counting certifications and competencies, it measures customer outcomes such as consumption and usage growth. The journey now has three layers: membership, Solutions Partner designation and specialization, each unlocking different benefits.

**How many points do you need for a Solutions Partner designation?**

You need 70 of 100 Partner Capability Score points, with at least one point in every metric. Performance is worth 20 points, skilling 25 and customer success 55. Customer success covers usage growth and deployments, where a workload only counts when more than 40% of licenses are actively used, so unused licenses do not help.

**How do Microsoft partner backend incentives and MDF get paid out?**

Backend incentives accrue in six-month periods. Below 10,000 dollars per period, Microsoft simply pays by check. Above that, 60% is paid as a check and 40% goes into a co-op bucket of market development funds, which must be spent within six months on approved activities. Unspent funds are taken back by Microsoft.
