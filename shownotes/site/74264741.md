## FAQ

**What are the forecast categories in Dynamics 365 Sales?**

Sellers choose between pipeline, best case, committed and omitted. Pipeline is the default for new, early deals; best case means real progress without commitment; committed means a verbal or contractual commitment; omitted removes the deal from all forecast totals. Won and lost are set automatically when the opportunity is closed through the close opportunity process.

**How is a sales forecast different from a quota?**

A quota is the target a seller or team aims to reach, while the forecast is a working estimate of the path toward it. The forecast is built from open opportunities, their expected amount, expected close date and the team's confidence, so it shows whether current deals offer a realistic route to the number.

**Can AI predict which deals will close in Dynamics 365 Sales?**

No, the opportunity score works as a second opinion, not a prediction. It looks at signals such as recent customer contact, meetings, emails, stage movement, deal amount and lead source, and compares them with past sales patterns. A low score is a reason to ask questions, while the seller adds context the data cannot see.
