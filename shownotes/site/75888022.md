## FAQ

**What is the difference between an Azure management group and a subscription?**

A subscription holds your resources and carries billing, quotas and the link to one Microsoft Entra tenant, while a management group holds no resources at all. Management groups sit above subscriptions purely for governance, so you can apply access and policy to many subscriptions at once and roll up their usage costs.

**Can a lower-level Azure Policy override a deny set on a management group?**

No, a deny assigned higher up the tree cannot be overridden by a friendlier rule lower down. Azure Policy works as a deny system, so to loosen a rule for one scope you have to exempt that scope. This design stops a subscription from quietly undoing a company-wide guardrail.

**Why does the management group cost total differ from the billing account total?**

Because the management group view rolls up only usage costs from the subscriptions beneath it. Purchases such as reservations and third-party Marketplace items are left out, so both totals can be correct while counting different things. Management group scopes are also not supported in Cost Management for Microsoft Customer Agreement subscriptions.
