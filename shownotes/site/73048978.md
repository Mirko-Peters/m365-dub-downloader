## FAQ

**Does Azure Cost Management cost extra?**

No, Azure Cost Management is a built-in service included with your subscription at no extra cost. It lets you monitor, analyze, control and optimize spending at the billing account, subscription or resource group level. Cost data refreshes about every four hours, and finalized billed charges are available within 72 hours after the invoice is issued.

**Which budget alert thresholds should I set up first in Azure?**

Start with one budget at the subscription level and alerts at 50, 75, 90 and 100 percent. Route them to an email list that includes finance and engineering leads, and review the budget monthly. Optionally, add an action group that shuts down non-production resources automatically when spending reaches 90 percent of the budget.

**Should I choose reserved instances or an Azure savings plan for compute?**

For most organizations the savings plan is the better choice, unless workloads are very stable and predictable. Reserved instances offer up to 72 percent off but are tied to a specific VM family and region. The savings plan offers up to 65 percent off and applies to any compute service across any region.
