## FAQ

**Do I need to code to use AI Builder?**

No, AI Builder needs no code and no data science team. You pick a model type, connect your data, let training run on its own and then use the result. It is built directly into Power Apps and Power Automate, so the AI appears inside the apps and flows where the work already happens.

**How many documents do I need to train AI Builder document processing?**

You only need five documents to get started, not thousands and not a prepared labeled data set. You train the model by showing it which information you want back, publish it to your apps and flows, and then it reads each new document in the background, including layouts specific to your suppliers.

**How much does AI Builder cost?**

Building, training and testing models is free; credits are only spent when a model runs in a live app or flow, which also requires a premium license. At the time of recording, Microsoft had historically sold about a million credits for roughly 500 dollars a month. Custom document processing costs around 100 credits per page.
