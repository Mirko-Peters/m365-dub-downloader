## FAQ

**Is Microsoft Power Platform secure enough for enterprise use?**

Yes. According to the episode, the platform's security is enterprise-grade, with Microsoft Entra ID authentication, encryption at rest and in transit, regional isolation, customer-managed keys, DLP and Purview auditing. The weak link is governance: the environments, DLP policies, security roles and ownership rules that customers must design and enforce in the control plane themselves.

**What does the no owner, no app rule mean in Power Platform?**

It means no app or flow runs in production without a named business owner and a defined lifecycle. The episode calls it the only realistic business continuity policy for citizen-built solutions, illustrated by a retail app that broke after its creator left and an audit where flows belonged to departed employees.

**How should Copilot be governed in Power Platform?**

Restrict it by default and enable it first in a few well-understood, non-regulated dev and test environments with good telemetry. Tag every AI-assisted asset, keep prompt logs for regulated workloads, define AI-specific DLP rules for where Copilot may read and write, and treat agents as non-human identities with least privilege.
