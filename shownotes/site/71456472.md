## FAQ

**How do you calculate return on security investment (ROSI)?**

Return on security investment equals exposure before minus exposure after minus security cost, divided by security cost. Exposure itself is probability times impact. The episode argues this is enough for a CFO: it does not prove perfection, it shows whether the spend reduces expected loss enough to justify itself against other uses of capital.

**What is decision latency in cybersecurity?**

Decision latency is the time between a security signal and confident executive action. It includes detection and response, but also the hesitation that happens when the incident picture is fragmented. Mirko recommends adding it as a multiplier in the CFO model, because paused approvals, delayed customer communication and idle teams make incidents more expensive every hour.

**How should managed security services be packaged and priced?**

Package them around business outcomes rather than seats, tools or tickets. The episode suggests three layers: a control plane foundation for identity governance and policy, resilience acceleration for response flow and automation, and board-ready risk reporting. Reporting should then show exposure down, decision time down, downtime avoided and control adoption up.
