## FAQ

**Where do I find the alignment tools and Selection Pane in PowerPoint?**

Both sit on the Home tab under the Arrange drop-down menu. Position Objects there lets you align content horizontally or vertically and distribute it evenly, while the Selection Pane lists every object on a slide, shows grouping and lets you select items you cannot click. Fiona recommends adding both to the Quick Access Toolbar.

**How can Copilot in PowerPoint help with a presentation?**

Fiona finds Copilot most useful before and after building slides rather than for creating them. Dictate a brain dump of your ideas, audience and goal and it helps structure the flow. With a finished deck it can suggest hooks, smarter endings and the questions a specific audience, such as a finance team, might ask.

**Can PowerPoint show live subtitles or translation while presenting?**

Yes. In Presenter View you can switch on live subtitles in the subtitle settings, an accessibility feature that can also translate your speech. Fiona notes it can translate from English into 72 languages. Because it reorders each sentence for the target language's grammar at the end, speaking a little slower helps the audience read along.
