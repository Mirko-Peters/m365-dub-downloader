## FAQ

**Why are deep folder hierarchies bad for finding files?**

Folders force every document into a single location and make users remember where someone else once filed it. The episode cites research that users spend 19% of their day hunting for information. Because a contract matters to legal, sales and project teams at once, people duplicate files, which creates conflicting versions of the truth.

**How does the Microsoft Graph semantic index find documents without folders?**

It represents content as vectors, mathematical representations of concepts, rather than matching keywords. A search can then find related documents even when different words are used, such as agreement instead of contract. Combined with relationships between people, meetings and chats, the Graph surfaces relevant files by context while respecting existing security boundaries.

**What does SharePoint Premium do with document metadata?**

SharePoint Premium extracts metadata automatically when a document arrives, so employees no longer tag files by hand. In the episode's invoice example it reads the vendor, total amount, tax ID and due date. Mirko says processing 500 invoices once took 1,500 minutes of manual entry, and automation turns filing into a background process.
