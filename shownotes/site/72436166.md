## FAQ

**When should you use Power Pages instead of Power Apps?**

Use Power Pages when external users need to work with your data, and Power Apps for internal users, because Power Apps should not be exposed externally. Michel says Power Pages pays off most when a company already runs business processes on Dataverse, since a site can be spun up quickly with the same security model.

**How do you secure the Power Pages Web API?**

Secure it in three layers: enable only the tables and fields that are really needed, add table permissions with global, account or contact scope, and configure column permissions to control which fields users can read or update. Michel stresses that anything JavaScript can reach is also reachable through browser developer tools.

**Will AI replace Power Platform developers?**

Michel does not think so; in his view AI can replace developers who don't use AI. He uses it to speed up bug fixes, prototypes and proofs of concept, but results on existing apps can be unpredictable, humans still need to review the output, and good developers spend tokens only where they make an impact.
