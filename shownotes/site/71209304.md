## FAQ

**What is an Excel shadow system?**

An Excel shadow system is an unofficial process runtime where files act as databases, inboxes act as workflow engines and people act as the middleware. It captures inputs, routes work and stores state, but without governance, clean access control or a reliable audit trail, so delays, rework and key person dependency stay hidden.

**Why doesn't banning Excel stop shadow systems?**

Because the workaround solves a real constraint: spreadsheets are fast, familiar and already available, while formal IT delivery moves through long backlogs. Banning Excel removes the compensation before fixing the underlying constraint, so people rebuild the same shadow path elsewhere, in Teams chats, SharePoint lists, personal notes or small apps nobody oversees.

**What is zoned governance in Power Platform?**

Zoned governance defines lanes based on risk, visibility and business impact instead of one rule for everything. A green zone lets business teams experiment with approved connectors and clear ownership, while solutions touching sensitive data or wider audiences get more review and security involvement. A Center of Excellence defines and monitors these zones.
