## Key takeaways

- Copilot in Dynamics 365 works inside the business records people already use, summarizing histories, answering questions about permitted data and preparing first drafts.
- Each Dynamics 365 app (Sales, Customer Service, Finance and Operations, Business Central) has its own Copilot features and task-focused agents, distinct from Microsoft 365 Copilot.

## FAQ

**What is the difference between Dynamics 365 Copilot and Microsoft 365 Copilot?**

Dynamics 365 Copilot works close to business processes and the records inside Dynamics 365 apps, such as leads, cases and orders. Microsoft 365 Copilot works across Teams, Outlook, Word and Excel. The two can connect, bringing business context into email and meetings, but they are separate products, not one product under different names.

**Can Dynamics 365 Copilot see records I don't have access to?**

No. Copilot follows the access rules attached to the signed-in user, so if you cannot open a customer record, it should not pull details from that record into an answer. Dataverse keeps business records and access rules in place behind many Dynamics 365 customer apps, helping the right people work with the right records.

**What does Copilot do in Dynamics 365 Customer Service?**

It creates case summaries covering the main issue, what the customer already tried, work done so far and recorded next steps, and it summarizes chat and transcribed voice conversations. It also drafts replies and brings relevant knowledge content closer to the case. Service agents still check every draft for accuracy, tone and promises before sending.
