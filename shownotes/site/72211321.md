## FAQ

**What is a zombie seat in Microsoft 365 Copilot?**

A zombie seat is a paid Copilot license assigned to someone who rarely uses it and produces almost no measurable value. The episode cites about 35.8% active usage among licensed employees, so in a 5,000-seat rollout roughly 3,750 licenses sit idle, often because nobody tied the license to a specific task or outcome.

**How many minutes per week does Copilot need to save to pay for itself?**

About 36 minutes per week, according to the episode, assuming a fully loaded labor cost of $50 per hour and the $30 monthly license. The episode recommends business cases that lead with a conservative 30 to 60 minutes saved weekly and treat three to four hours at maturity as an upper bound.

**When should I use RPA instead of AI agents for automation?**

Use RPA or deterministic scripts for stable, high-volume, rules-based execution such as posting entries or updating CRM records. AI costs scale with every token, while a deployed bot costs almost nothing per run. The episode reserves LLMs for judgment work like classifying emails or extracting meaning from unstructured documents, with humans handling exceptions.
