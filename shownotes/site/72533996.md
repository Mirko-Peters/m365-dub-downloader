## FAQ

**How do I get my CV past ATS and AI screening?**

Make sure the keywords for the role are clearly in your CV and LinkedIn profile, because screening by AI or a non-technical recruiter starts with a search. Sarah also recommends following up with the hiring manager or talent team when you apply and again after three to five days, in case your application slipped through.

**When is the right time to start freelancing?**

Ideally when you have enough money saved to cover about six months without income. Sarah suggests building that buffer, plus first clients and case studies, through contracting or side gigs alongside a permanent role. Many freelancers she interviews only made the move after a redundancy, having put it off for years.

**What is the difference between contracting and freelancing in the UK?**

A contractor usually gets work through a recruiter, who handles business development and payment risk in exchange for a margin, and works fixed hours on three- or six-month contracts. A freelancer bills a day rate through their own limited company, finds their own clients and manages their own time across several projects.
