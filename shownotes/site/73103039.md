## FAQ

**Is Viva Connections now called SharePoint?**

Yes. Between May and July 2026, Microsoft renamed the Viva Connections app in Teams to the SharePoint app. Functionality, management tools and the admin experience stayed the same, and tenants that had already customized the app name and logo saw no change. The rename reflects that the app was always powered by a SharePoint home site.

**Do I need a Viva license to use Viva Connections?**

No. The basic version is included with a Microsoft 365 subscription and offers the news reader, dashboard, resources and audience targeting via Entra ID groups. The Viva Suite version adds targeted push notifications, premium analytics and AI features through Copilot, which matters most for frontline workers who need urgent alerts on their phones.

**How do you increase Viva Connections adoption?**

Pin the app globally in Teams first, because organizations doing so see 75% of their weekly active usage come from that single action. Then publish fresh news several times per week, since engagement drops sharply after a week, and build a rich dashboard: those with 10 or more cards get three times the engagement.
