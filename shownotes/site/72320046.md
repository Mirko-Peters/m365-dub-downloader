## FAQ

**What happens when seeded AI Builder credits are removed on November 1, 2026?**

Agents that relied on them start billing immediately or stop working. Seeded AI Builder credits included with Power Apps premium and Dynamics 365 licenses disappear with no automatic conversion. Without Copilot Studio capacity packs or pay-as-you-go billing, agents stop; with uncapped pay-as-you-go, they keep running and billing without limit.

**Should I buy PTU or stay on pay-as-you-go for Azure OpenAI?**

Stay on pay-as-you-go unless utilization is very high and steady. The episode says a yearly PTU for GPT-5 global breaks even at roughly 86% sustained utilization, hourly PTU never wins and monthly PTU rarely does. Start on PAYG, collect 30 to 90 days of telemetry, then model PTU against actual usage.

**How does semantic caching in Azure API Management reduce AI costs?**

It returns a stored answer for semantically similar prompts, so no LLM call is made and no tokens are consumed. An inbound lookup policy embeds the prompt and queries Azure Cache for Redis; an outbound policy stores new responses with a time to live. Similarity thresholds of 0.92 to 0.96 are typical.
