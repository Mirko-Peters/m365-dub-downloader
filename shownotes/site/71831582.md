## FAQ

**What is a telemetry-driven logic layer in Power Automate?**

A telemetry-driven logic layer is a supervisory control plane that watches signals across flows, classifies failures and decides on a safe response. It runs a loop of desired state, detection, decision, remediation and learning, kept separate from the business logic. Mirko Peters stresses that the decision, not the remediation itself, is the smart part.

**Where should Power Automate failure telemetry be stored?**

Storage depends on maturity: a SharePoint list or database table is enough to start, Azure Table Storage suits structured records at larger scale, and Log Analytics or Application Insights fit larger estates needing deeper search and analytics. The choice depends on volume, query needs, retention and who must access the evidence, not on prestige.

**When should self-healing logic move from Power Automate to Azure Logic Apps?**

Move it when recursion gets deep: heavy state handling, high-volume processing, queue-driven work or orchestration across multiple systems. Power Automate fits supervised recursion close to the business process, such as approvals, notifications and SharePoint or Teams actions. In Logic Apps Standard, fixed compute can become more economical once action volume is high and predictable.
