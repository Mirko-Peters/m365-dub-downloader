## FAQ

**How do you stop a Power Automate flow from looping on itself?**

Add a trigger condition so the flow only fires when the watched field really changes, and set a concurrency limit of one. The episode also recommends lowering service protection limits below the default for that environment, turning off personal-scope flows and moving the flow into a managed solution, so loops fail closed at the platform boundary.

**Is Microsoft 365 Copilot leaking data when it shows sensitive content?**

No, Copilot operates within the effective permissions users already have in Entra ID and SharePoint. When it surfaces finance or HR content, the cause is inherited permissions, mislabeled folders or labels applied only to documents. The fix is site-level sensitivity labels, enforced sharing and download restrictions, and information barriers between groups such as HR and finance.

**What kill switches should admins pre-stage for AI agents?**

The episode recommends three pre-staged controls: a DLP policy blocking all external connectors across environments, disabled until needed; a Conditional Access policy denying sign-in for high-risk service principals behind an approval gate; and a Power Platform environment variable that pauses agent execution, wired to an admin-only flow. Teams should practice activating them and time each step.
