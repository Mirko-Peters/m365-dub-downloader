## FAQ

**Should business logic live in Power Automate or in Dataverse?**

Validation and calculation logic belongs server-side in Dataverse, using business rules, calculated or rollup fields and plugins. Power Automate should handle notifications, approvals routing, orchestration and external integration. In one example, moving a credit-limit approval from flows into a plugin cut processing from 45 seconds to four, and the rule then lives in one place.

**What is the transactional outbox pattern in Dataverse?**

It is a way to make integrations reliable by writing an entry to an outbox table in the same transaction as the record change. A downstream service, such as a flow or Azure Function, reads unprocessed entries, handles them and marks them complete. Failed entries stay pending and are retried, so events are not silently lost.

**How do you start moving a Dataverse environment toward relational design?**

Start with a 90-day proof of concept rather than a full rebuild. Spend two weeks auditing tables and duplication, two weeks designing one process such as order to cash with stakeholders, four weeks piloting one solution, then expand to further processes while measuring development time, data quality and integration reliability against your baseline.
