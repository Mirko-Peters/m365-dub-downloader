## FAQ

**Why do AI-generated characters change faces between shots?**

Character drift happens because each shot is generated independently and every model uses its own identity embedding. If you swap a reference image, reword a character descriptor or push motion too high, the model recalculates the face. A locked master reference pack with front, back, side and close-up views, reused with the same @ tags, keeps identity stable.

**What motion strength should I use in Higgsfield or Seedance?**

Keep motion in a moderate range, roughly 3 to 5 on a mental scale of 1 to 10. Below 3 the footage feels frozen; above 5 texture crawling, limb warping and temporal flicker appear. Gentle presets such as arcs, slow dollies and pans are low risk, while crash zooms and orbits carry much higher artifact risk.

**How much does a short AI corporate video cost to produce with this workflow?**

In the episode's 60-second example with 12 shots, generation costs about 24.30 dollars at the time of recording: roughly 6.30 for Seedance and 18 for Higgsfield. Adding 40 hours of labor at 50 dollars an hour brings the total to about 2,024 dollars. Weak references or aggressive motion settings multiply costs through extra iterations.
