## FAQ

**Can employees request leave in Microsoft Teams with Dynamics 365 Human Resources?**

Yes. Microsoft puts time off balances and leave requests directly into Microsoft Teams, so employees can check available leave, pick dates and send a request without opening a separate HR website. The manager receives it through the approval process, and the system applies the company's configured leave rules, such as working days and public holidays.

**Does Dynamics 365 Human Resources replace payroll and recruiting systems?**

No. It holds the employee record, compensation information and HR rules, but payroll has local tax rules and country-specific requirements. Many companies connect it with finance or a separate payroll tool, and keep their own recruiting or learning platforms. Human Resources shares the people data those tools need so records do not have to be re-entered.

**What does an employee record in Dynamics 365 Human Resources contain?**

An employee record holds far more than a name and job title. It can include job details, contact information, work history, skills, certifications, interests, accomplishments and training. It also links to departments, jobs, positions and reporting lines, while roles and permissions control who can view or change each area of this sensitive data.
