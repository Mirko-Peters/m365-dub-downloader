## FAQ

**What is the difference between cloud flows and desktop flows in Power Automate?**

Cloud flows run on Microsoft's servers and connect modern services through APIs and connectors, so your computer does not need to be on. Desktop flows use robotic process automation to control the mouse and keyboard, clicking and typing in legacy applications that have no API. Many organizations end up using both kinds.

**What are the three types of flows in Power Automate?**

Power Automate has automated, instant and scheduled flows, and they differ only in the trigger. Automated flows start on an event such as a new email, instant flows start when you click a button in Teams, SharePoint or on your phone, and scheduled flows run on a timer. After the trigger, everything works the same.

**How do I build my first flow in Power Automate?**

Go to make.powerautomate.com, sign in with your Microsoft 365 work account, click Create and choose an automated cloud flow. Use "When a new email arrives" as the trigger, then add a Teams action that posts a message to yourself as Flow bot. Save, click Test and send yourself an email.
